Fulbright Graduate Student Award 2027-28

Natalia Kozłowska
9 February 2026

Fulbright Graduate Student Award supports graduate degree studies at an accredited higher education institution in the United States. Applications are accepted in all fields of study, except for medical studies requiring direct contact with patients, LLM and MBA programs. Grantees may receive up to $47,000 to cover the cost of their first year of study, with the exact amount depending on the location of the host institution, the student’s financial need, and available program funding.

Scholarships are awarded for the first year of studies carried out in the 2027–2028 academic year, with the possibility of extension for a second year.

The call for applications is open from February 9 to April 27, 2026, until 3:00 PM (Polish time). Three scholarships are planned within this competition.

Eligibility requirements

We welcome applicants who meet all of the following criteria:

  • Hold Polish citizenship and do not possess dual Polish–U.S. citizenship or a U.S. permanent resident card (Green Card).
  • Hold a master’s degree or will obtain it no later than the end of June in the year the scholarship begins.
  • Demonstrate very good English proficiency (minimum B2 level).
  • Meet the formal requirements for a J-1 student visa.
  • Are not residing in the United States at the time of application, and whose total stay in the U.S. during the six years preceding the application has been shorter than five years. Preference is given to candidates whose total stay in the U.S. within the past five years has not exceeded nine months.

Applicants are not eligible to apply if they:

  • Hold a doctoral degree or will defend their doctoral dissertation before the start of the scholarship, in accordance with the scholarship timeline.

More information and the full call for applications are available on the program website in Polish.
