YSD Undergraduate Excellence Scholarship

About

Yayasan Sime Darby invites qualified undergraduate students for the September 2026 intake and first-year undergraduate students to apply for an excellence scholarship to pursue/complete their academic dreams at a reputable higher learning institution in Malaysia.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Malaysian.
  • Age 25 years or below.
  • Completed STPM/ Pre-University/ Foundation/ Local Matriculation/ Diploma with a minimum CGPA of 3.30.
  • Total monthly gross household income of RM11,000 and below.
  • Strong leadership qualities through various leadership positions in school, national and/or global competitions and/or sporting events.
  • Active participation in community service.

Accepted Fields of Study

Business / Social Science related courses:

  • Accounting & Finance
  • Economics

Engineering and Technology related courses:

  • Data Science
  • Data Analytics
  • Electrical & Electronic Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Civil Engineering

Architecture & Built Environment related courses:

  • Architecture / Structural / Building
  • Environmental Science
  • Urban & Town Planning
  • Sustainability
  • Quantity Surveying
  • Real Estate

Accepted Universities

YSD welcomes candidates with firm or conditional offer from the following list of Universities:

  1. Monash University Malaysia
  2. Universiti Malaya
  3. University of Nottingham Malaysia
  4. Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia
  5. Universiti Putra Malaysia
  6. Universiti Sains Malaysia
  7. Universiti Teknologi Malaysia
  8. Universiti Teknologi Petronas
  9. Taylor’s University
  10. UCSI University

Value of Scholarship

Tuition Fees

Monthly Allowance
(Living, subsistence, accommodation)
Laptop Allowance
Book Allowance

Scholarship recipients will have the opportunity to undergo YSD THRIVE – Future Leaders, a comprehensive learning experience for YSD scholars towards producing high quality and well-rounded graduates. The initiative offers tailored soft skills training, access to an e-learning platform, volunteering opportunities, mentorship and internships at Sime Darby Berhad and Sime Darby Property Berhad.

Note: Scholarship monies will be disbursed upon execution of scholarship agreement.

Service Bond Period

3 years at Sime Darby Berhad or Sime Darby Property Berhad upon completion of studies.

Application

Scholarship is now open.

YSD Special Needs Bursary

About

Yayasan Sime Darby invites promising People with Disability (PWD) undergraduate or diploma students for the September 2026 intake and first-year students to apply for a bursary to pursue tertiary education at any recognised Malaysian higher learning institutions.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Malaysian.
  • Aged 25 years or below.
  • Students with disabilities who are registered OKU cardholders.
  • Obtained a minimum five (5) credits in SPM for Diploma applicants.
  • Completed STPM / Pre-University / Foundation / Local Matriculation / Diploma with a minimum CGPA of 2.50 for Undergraduate applicants.

Accepted Fields of Study

YSD accepts any fields of study except for medicine.

Accepted Universities

YSD welcomes candidates who are currently studying; and those who have obtained firm or conditional offer from any recognised Malaysian higher learning institutions that are approved by Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA).

Value of Scholarship

Tuition Fees

Monthly Allowance

(Living, subsistence, accommodation)
Book Allowance

This bursary award is up to a maximum amount of RM15,000 per year.

Scholarship recipients will have the opportunity to undergo YSD THRIVE – Rising Talent, a wholesome learning experience specially designed for our Bursary and TVET scholars, including tailored employability skills training, wellness support, mentoring, industry meetups, volunteerism opportunities and access to an e-learning platform.

Note: Scholarship monies will be disbursed upon execution of scholarship agreement.

Service Bond Period

Bursary recipients are not required to serve a scholarship bond at Sime Darby Berhad, SD Guthrie Berhad and Sime Darby Property Berhad.

Application

Application closed.

For Employees & Employees' Children

About

Employees who are currently studying and those who are interested in pursuing diploma, undergraduate or postgraduate studies locally on a part-time basis are invited to apply for a scholarship.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Malaysian.
  • Employee must receive an approval from their respective Head of Department at SD Guthrie Berhad, Sime Darby Berhad and Sime Darby Property Berhad (three companies).
  • A full-time employee at any three companies or its subsidiaries in Malaysia only. This privilege will not be extended to employees in associated corporations (“Associate Companies” means companies in which the three Companies above shareholding is less than 50%).
  • At least 2 years of service within any three companies.
  • A minimum CGPA of 3.30 and above and/or its equivalent in his/her most recent academic qualification.

Accepted Fields of Study

Fields of study that are related to three companies businesses, subject to approval from the respective Human Resource Department of three companies.

Accepted Universities

YSD welcomes candidates who are currently studying; and those who have obtained firm or conditional offer from; any recognised Malaysian higher learning institutions that are approved by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA).

Value of Scholarship

Tuition Fees

Thesis Allowance

Book Allowance

Service Bond Period

2 years at any of the three companies upon completion of studies.

About

Yayasan Sime Darby invites employees’ children who will commence tertiary education in September 2025 and first-year students to apply for a bursary to pursue their academic dreams at any recognised Malaysian higher learning institutions.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Malaysian.
  • Minimum CGPA of 2.50 for STPM/ Pre-University/ Foundation/ Local Matriculation/ Diploma; or minimum of 5 credits for SPM.
  • Employee (child’s parent) must be a full-time employee at SD Guthrie Berhad, Sime Darby Berhad, Sime Darby Property Berhad (three companies) or its subsidiaries in Malaysia only. This privilege will not be extended to employees in associated corporations (“Associate Companies” refer to companies in which the three Companies above shareholding is less than 50%).
  • Employee (child’s parent) who is currently seconded to the company’s subsidiary outside Malaysia is eligible for this sponsorship.
  • Employee (child’s parent) per capita income must not exceed RM3,000 per month.

Accepted Fields of Study

YSD accepts any fields of study except for medicine.

Accepted Universities

YSD welcomes candidates who are currently studying; and those who have obtained firm or conditional offer from any recognised Malaysian higher learning institutions that are approved by the Malaysian Qualifications Agency (MQA).

Value of Scholarship

This bursary award covers tuition fees, living and book allowances up to a maximum amount of RM15,000 per year.

Successful employees’ children will have the opportunity to undergo YSD THRIVE – Rising Talent, a wholesome learning experience specially designed for our Bursary and TVET scholars, including tailored employability skills training, wellness support, mentoring, industry meetups, volunteerism and access to an e-learning platform.

Note: Scholarship monies will be disbursed upon execution of scholarship agreement.

Service Bond Period

Bursary recipients are not required to serve a scholarship bond at SD Guthrie Berhad, Sime Berhad and Sime Darby Property Berhad.

About

YSD invites YSD Donor Companies to nominate high potential employees to apply for a scholarship to pursue Executive MBA Programme on a part-time basis at Asia School of Business (ASB) in collaboration with MIT Sloan Management.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Malaysian.
  • Employee is nominated by the HR department of SD Guthrie Berhad, Sime Darby Berhad and Sime Darby Property Berhad (three companies) from high-potential talent pool.
  • A full-time employee at any three companies or its subsidiaries in Malaysia only. This privilege will not be extended to employees in associated corporations (“Associate Companies” means companies in which the three Companies above shareholding is less than 50%).
  • At least 2 years of service within three companies.

Accepted Fields of Study

Master of Business Administration (MBA).

Accepted Universities

Asia School of Business (ASB).

Value of Scholarship

The scholarship covers course fees inclusive of academic fees, enrolment deposit, book allowance, thesis allowance, on-campus accommodation for the residency weeks, 3-week MIT Sloan immersion, as well as flight, meals and other related expenses for the MIT Sloan immersion.

Service Bond Period

2 years at any of three companies upon completion of studies.

Application

Please contact your respective HR for further details.
