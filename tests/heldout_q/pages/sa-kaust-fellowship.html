KAUST ACCELERATES IMPACT THROUGH SCIENTIFIC EXPLORATION

Pursuing Fellow-Led Research

A unique opportunity for early-career researchers to design and lead their own research project, expanding on innovative ideas from day one. Fellows build their own career path and transition toward a competitive, independent research career, supported by world-class experts, top-tier mentorship, and an exceptional international community at KAUST.

Research Facilities

KAUST provides easy access to all the necessary research tools and equipment Fellows need to fulfill their research goals, with unparalleled laboratory facilities available on campus. Fellows are also supported by their experienced colleagues and can rely on the expertise of their host Principal Investigator.


Three years of funding

Global Fellows receive up to $40,000 in dedicated research funding for their research proposal, along with a yearly living stipend of $75,000 plus a comprehensive benefits package, including medical insurance, on-campus accommodation, schooling for up to three children, relocation allowance, and annual travel assistance. This package, allows Global Fellows to focus on their research full-time with financial security.

A Global Network

KAUST has an extensive network of international research partners that facilitate networking opportunities for the KAUST research community to meet a global set of external organizations operating in academia, industry, government agencies, funding bodies, and other external partners. This extensive network of research and industry leaders helps Fellows to build successful international partnerships for their future careers.

Personal & Professional Development

The KAUST Global Fellowship Program offers a variety of learning and development programs designed to support and improve Fellows' existing skills while nurturing and developing new skills. These programs include the KAUST Mentoring Program, the Harvard HE Teaching Certificate, and Leadership programs/ certificates. Each Fellow's learning experience is enhanced further through networking opportunities with other Fellows and successful alumni of the Fellowship program.

A sense of community

KAUST attracts some of the brightest minds from across the globe, with more than 110 nationalities living, working, and studying on campus, creating a dynamic international environment. KAUST is more than a university; it is a unique ecosystem where education, research, innovation, and industry converge. This supportive intellectual environment enhances our researchers' scientific endeavours, as well as their personal and professional development, and offers networking opportunities that will help to kick-start your budding research career.

 

The KAUST Global Postdoctoral Fellowship accelerates University's impact by empowering early-career researchers to drive innovation and scientific advancement. Aligned with Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030, the Fellowship fosters global and local partnerships, supports commercialisation efforts, and enhances economic and societal development through transformative research.

 

To be an internationally recognised fellowship that empowers outstanding early-career scientists and engineers to reach their full potential. Through world-class support and mentorship, the program enables Fellows to translate their discoveries into real-world solutions, shaping the future for the benefit of the Kingdom and the world.
