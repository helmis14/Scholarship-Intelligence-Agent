Italian University just a click away!

Universitaly is the Italian Ministry of University and Research's online portal, specifically created to accompany Italian and international students on their study path and to provide them with the broadest description of all the components of the Italian university system

You can consult Universitaly in order to access all the information needed to find the university course you are interested in, to check the terms and conditions for the admission to the various faculties, to discover the opportunities for facilitations and scholarships, and to verify the formalities and obligations required for your entry and stay in Italy.
