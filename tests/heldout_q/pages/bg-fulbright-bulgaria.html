Core Grants

l

Graduate Study Grants

MA or PhD degrees in any accredited US university available for all fields of science and arts. Funding up to $66,000.



Visiting Scholar Grants in the U.S.

This grant allows Bulgarian scholars and university teachers to work in their field of studies for 3-5 months in the US.

U

Programs for Doctoral Students

This program is for Bulgarian researchers in advanced stages of their PhD studies, who can work in elite US universities.



Bulgarian Leaders Grant

For self-placed students who hold a BA. Funding up to $100,000. Candidates must return to Bulgaria after the completion of their program.



Hubert H. Humphrey Fellowships

This program presents experienced specialists with the opportunity to enhance their professional qualifications in elite institutions in the USA.



Grant for School Teachers

Fulbright Teaching Excellence and Achievement (FTEA) – a six-week program for Bulgarian middle and high school teachers.
