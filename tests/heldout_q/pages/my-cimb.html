OUR PROGRAMMES

#SeKODlah TecHive

Shaping Digital Futures

 

Designed to prepare Malaysian youth for the digital economy, #SeKODlah TecHive entered its third year of empowering young talent, bringing together 35 participants for a 26-day bootcamp covering programming, cybersecurity, data analytics, data visualisation, generative AI, soft skills, and financial literacy.

 

Participants also visited CIMB for industry sharing sessions with colleagues from Group Technology and Group Data & AI, as well as CIMB Foundation CEO, En. Shahriman Mohd Shariff. The programme concluded with the #SeKODlah TecHive Hackathon, where participants applied their knowledge to solve real-world challenges and pitch innovative solutions.

 

With the completion of its third cohort, #SeKODlah TecHive has empowered 94 young Malaysians with future-ready digital skills, strengthening the pipeline of digital talent for Malaysia's evolving economy.

Pottery Apprenticeship

Crafting Brighter Futures Through Pottery and Craft

 

Pottery Apprenticeship is a three-month, full-time apprenticeship programme that equips participants with pottery, design, and business skills to create and market pottery products. Through employment pathways, retail partnerships, and microbusiness opportunities, the programme empowers participants to build sustainable livelihoods. A total of nine students successfully completed the programme.

Past Programmes
CIMB MicroBiz Ready Entrepreurship Programme – Taylor’s Education Sdn. Bhd.

Empowering Micro and Nano-Entrepreneurs

 

CIMB MicroBiz Ready Entrepreneurship Programme, which began in 2020, is a specialized entrepreneurship initiative centered on digitalisation skills enhancement and financial access education to facilitate the growth and development growth of micro and nano-entrepreneurs. This year, a total of 120 micro and nano-entrepreneurs of low income backgrounds from Klang Valley, Perlis and Johor will receive various support to foster their business development and sustainability.

Komuniti Tukang Jahit Upskill Sewing Programme

Supporting Women To Create and Grow Home-Based Businesses.

 

In collaboration with Komuniti Tukang Jahit, a community-based network of local tailors, the Upskill Sewing Programme empowered women from underserved and low-income communities in the Klang Valley with advanced sewing skills, exposure to design innovation and opportunities for a new income stream.

 

The programme onboarded 34 participants, of whom 22 completed the training. Among the graduates, 18 continued to take sewing orders, with an average monthly earning of RM494, nearly double the initial target. The majority of graduates can now produce commercial-grade products and a few are ready to expand into home-based enterprises.

 

 

Empowering Special Needs Youths with Digital Tech Skills - Infinite Minds Academy Sdn. Bhd.

Inclusive Growth for 10 Special Needs Youths

 

Our collaboration with Infinite Minds Academy empowers 10 special needs youths from B40 backgrounds with essential digital skills in graphic design, animation, and more, to enable them to gain employment opportunities and financial independence.

#seKODlah TECHIVE

Strengthening Skills Through Real-World Tech Learning

 

In partnership with the Forest Interactive Foundation, CIMB supported the #seKODlah TecHive Digital Technology Upskilling Programme to develop Malaysia’s talent pool for the technology and innovation sectors. This initiative included workshops and activities such as TechBoot Camp and Hackathons, combining coding, data analytics and problem-solving with exposure to real industry practices.

 

In 2025, the programme benefitted 34 participants, providing them with technical and soft skills for the digital economy, with participants reporting greater motivation and a sense of purpose after applying their new skills to real-world projects. Following the programme, 23 out of 34 participants received job offers and half are now employed in the digital sector.

 

Festive Celebration at The Spastic Children Association of Selangor & Federal Territory

Supporting Inclusive Learning and Social Engagement

 

Embracing the spirit of togetherness and giving during the Chinese New Year celebration, CIMB volunteers partnered with TNG Group to bring festive cheer to 68 participants at the Spastic Children Association of Selangor & Federal Territory (SCAS&FT).

 

Through a variety of interactive activities, including arts and crafts, sensory play, storytelling, and festive-themed workshops, volunteers spent meaningful time engaging with students and fostering an inclusive and joyful environment. As part of the initiative, CIMB Foundation also provided festive contributions to support the students and the centre. The programme reflected the values of inclusion, compassion, and community support, making the Chinese New Year celebration more meaningful for all involved.

 

Festive Celebration with PPR/PA Angkat MADANI

Celebrating Ramadan Through Learning and Giving

 

This Ramadan, CIMB Foundation organised a Ramadan Shopping Programme for children from PPR Bukit Jalil, bringing together CIMB leaders and volunteers to create a meaningful and enriching festive experience.

 

Beyond preparing for Hari Raya, the programme introduced children to basic financial literacy concepts through guided shopping activities, helping them learn the importance of budgeting, distinguishing between needs and wants, and making informed spending decisions. The day concluded with festive contributions that added to the spirit of celebration, reflecting CIMB Foundation's commitment to empowering communities through education, inclusion, and meaningful engagement.

 

Festive Celebration at Sekolah Angkat MADANI

Bubur Lambuk Preparation, Distribution & Berbuka Puasa 

 

The Bubur Lambuk and Buka Puasa programme at SK Bukit Jalil brought together CIMB volunteers in the spirit of compassion, unity, and giving during Ramadan.

 

Volunteers worked hand in hand to prepare and distribute bubur lambuk to students, while contributing through the Gerobok Rezeki community pantry initiative and duit raya distribution to help spread joy and support those in need. The programme reflected the importance of community collaboration and the enduring values of generosity and togetherness during the holy month.

 

Santuni MADANI: Back to School at PPR/PA Angkat MADANI

Supporting Students for a Strong Start to the School Year

 

The Back to School programme helped 700 students from underserved communities in PPR Lembah Subang, PA Seri Selangor, and PPR Seri Alam prepare for the new academic year by providing essential school supplies and uniforms. By reducing the financial burden on families, the initiative ensured that students had the resources they needed to begin the school year with confidence and focus on their education.

 

Through support that promotes equitable access to learning, the programme reflects a commitment to empowering young people, encouraging school participation, and creating opportunities for brighter futures through education in these communities.

 

Santuni MADANI: Liver Screening at PPR/PA Angkat MADANI

Strengthening Community Health Through Early Fatty Liver Disease Detection

 

Metabolic dysfunction-associated fatty liver disease (MAFLD) affects millions of Malaysians and can lead to serious health complications if left untreated. Through a partnership with ReGen Rehab Hospital, CIMB Foundation is providing free health screenings to residents of PPR Seri Selangor to support early detection and intervention.

 

The initiative provides fatty liver screening, health assessments, and follow-up support, including professional consultations and complimentary supplements for individuals diagnosed with fatty liver disease. By improving access to preventive healthcare, the programme aims to empower communities to take charge of their health and well-being.

 

Satu Pemimpin Satu Kampung Santuni Madani

Supporting Inclusive Gowth in Underserved Communities

 

The Santuni MADANI, Satu Pemimpin Satu Kampung initiative is a Malaysian community-centred programme under the broader Malaysia MADANI framework, designed to strengthen socio-economic development in underserved and rural communities. The initiative encourages leaders and agencies to adopt specific villages or communities, engage residents in dialogue to understand their socio-economic needs and work collaboratively on lasting solutions.

 

CIMB has pledged RM3.6 million in support of community programmes and services over two years, aiming to benefit around 9,000 residents across four public housing areas in the Klang Valley — PPR Pinggiran Bukit Jalil, PPR Lembah Subang, Perumahan Awam Seri Selangor, and PPR Seri Alam.

 

We have started engaging local leaders in these communities to understand their needs and how we can support them through our programmes developed with partners. We aim to address multiple community needs holistically through 11 programmes that range from entrepreneurship and income generation training (including iTEKAD Program Keusahawanan CIMB Islamic-Taylor, iTEKAD CIMB Islamic Rider Enterpreneur Programme and CIMB MicroBizReady), to upskilling in sewing and micro-enterprise development and education support through free tuition, teacher training and literacy initiatives.

With a two-year shelf life, these nutritious meals are stockpiled for rapid deployment, reducing food waste while providing efficient, life-saving relief during crises.

Empowering Senior Citizens Through Financial Knowledge and Confidence

 

Supporting seniors with the knowledge and tools to navigate everyday financial matters, the Financial Literacy for Senior Citizens programme was conducted at PAWE Paya Jaras, PAWE Setia Alam, and PAWE Lembah Pantai, benefiting a total of 90 senior citizens across the three centres.

Closing The Gap

Expanding Access To Higher Education for Underserved Youth

 

Developed in partnership with Yayasan Tunku Abdul Rahman, the Closing The Gap (CTG) programme supports Malaysia’s goal of equitable access to higher education. The initiative focuses on enabling high-potential youth from disadvantaged backgrounds to overcome systemic barriers and pursue ambitious academic pathways. Through structured mentoring, guidance and experiential learning, CTG equips students with the confidence, skills and resources to navigate their post-SPM journey (Sijil Pelajaran Malaysia, Malaysia’s national secondary school examination) — transforming aspirations into attainable goals.

 

In 2025, 65 scholars participated in the University Residential Camp at the University of Nottingham Malaysia. The immersive five-day programme exposed students to eight academic streams and offered 32 tutorial sessions that simulated real university classes. Participants also engaged in practical workshops covering résumé writing, scholarship interviews and presentation skills. By the end of the camp, 92% of students reported feeling better prepared to navigate their post-SPM pathways.

 

Beyond academic exposure, the camp also focused on strengthening essential life skills, helping students to develop critical thinking, teamwork and stress-management skills and equipping them with the confidence and resilience needed to thrive in higher education and their future careers.

 

Advancing Financial Literacy Through Be$MART

Shaping Future Malaysian Leaders Across Universities in Malaysia

 

Launched in Malaysia in 2016, Be$MART is our flagship financial literacy initiative designed for university and college students, equipping them with essential financial planning skills. Delivered as a just-in-time programme, it engages youth at a pivotal transition point as they prepare to enter the workforce. Through engaging, interactive workshops, the programme equips participants with practical skills in budgeting, saving, investing, debt management, financial protection and fraud awareness.

 

Over the years, the programme has reached more than 23,000 students, including over 2,000 individuals across seven cohorts in 2025.

 

Be$MART was also replicated in Indonesia as a dedicated financial literacy programme for CIMB Niaga’s local university scholarship awardees. 41 awardees from 22 universities participated in the Be$MART Advance Level programme, which introduced investment concepts and money-laundering awareness, with support from the Indonesian Financial Transaction Reports and Analysis Center. Meanwhile, 42 awardees from 21 universities joined the Be$MART Basic Level programme, focusing on future financial planning and an introduction to the financial services industry.

CIMB Chevening Scholarship

The Chevening Scholarship Programme is a UK government flagship global scholarship initiative administered by the UK’s Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office (FCDO) and which UK government fully / jointly funds selected individuals to pursue a master’s degree in the UK. The UK government also partners with some corporates which fully / jointly fund the selected individuals to study for a UK master’s degree, often in specific areas that align with the organisational objectives and providing an opportunity to form long-lasting, positive relationships with emerging talent and influencers in the industry.

 

To date, CIMB has funded 32 scholars under the CIMB-Chevening programme.

Cultivating Resilience Through Community Gardening via Employee Volunteerism

Group Technology (GT) Kebun Komuniti: Growing Food, Nurturing Communities

 

What started as a simple volunteer effort in 2024 has blossomed into a thriving community garden, where CIMB Group Technology staff come together to cultivate fresh, healthy produce while creating a meaningful impact.

 

In collaboration with Kebun Komuniti Hartamas (KKH), GT Kebun Komuniti has produced hundreds of kilograms of vegetables and fruits, including spinach, chilies, okra, brinjal, bananas, and papayas. These harvests are donated to charity homes, helping to strengthen food security and support communities in need.

 

Beyond growing food, the initiative nurtures connections, encourages sustainable living, and provides a fulfilling avenue for employees to volunteer, learn, and give back. More than just a garden, GT Kebun Komuniti demonstrates how collective action and shared purpose can cultivate lasting positive change, one harvest at a time.

Restoring Reefs, Strengthening Stewardship via Employee Volunteerism

Supporting Coral Reef Restoration Through Partnership and Environmental Action at Pulau Perhentian

 

Bringing together environmental education, conservation and collective action, the ERL ESG Coral Restoration Project at Pulau Perhentian provided participants with an opportunity to contribute directly to the protection and restoration of Malaysia's marine ecosystems. 

 

Conducted in collaboration with Express Rail Link (ERL) and Alunan Resort, the three-day programme combined hands-on coral restoration activities, reef observation exercises and a beach clean-up initiative, fostering a deeper understanding of the vital role coral reefs play in sustaining marine biodiversity and supporting healthy coastal environments. 

 

Through meaningful partnerships and active participation, the initiative highlights how collective stewardship can help strengthen ecosystem resilience, promote environmental awareness and contribute towards the long-term preservation of Malaysia's natural heritage.

 

Past Programmes

Empowering Local Communities as Future Marine Conservation Leaders

 

The Building Marine Conservation Leaders program aims to empower local communities and increase their involvement in marine resource management by training future conservation leaders. This initiative promotes shared responsibility for sustainably managing marine resources.

Thinking Big with Management of Elephants and Grasses – BORA

Thinking Big with Management of Elephants and Grasses

 

CIMB Foundation supported the expansion of managed grasslands and mineral sources at the Tabin Wildlife Reserve in Sabah, aiming to encourage the presence of Bornean elephants within the reserve and reduce elephant-human conflicts. This effort was undertaken in partnership with the Borneo Rhino Alliance Bhd (BORA).  We are encouraged by the increased presence of elephant herds in the area proving the success of this active and targeted management of natural habitats for wildlife conservation.

CIMB Mangrove Conservation Initiative - UKM

Mangrove Conservation Initiative

 

The CIMB-UKM Mangrove Conservation initiative includes the restoration and rehabilitation of mangroves through planting and nursing of 7,000 mangroves to protect the marine and coastal ecosystems, as well as the coastline. The initiative involves three-pronged areas which are education and awareness on mangroves, mangrove habitat restoration and enhanced research and development dedicated to the protection of mangroves.
