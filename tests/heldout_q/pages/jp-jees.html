JEES INTERNATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP（STUDY SUPPORT）

JEES International Scholarship (Study Support)

  1. TOP
  2. JEES奨学金
  3. JEES International Scholarship (Study Support)
about

概要

This scholarship is aimed at providing financial support to self-funded international students enrolled at Japanese universities and technical colleges who are outstanding both academically and in character. It is intended to contribute to reducing financial worries and enhancing learning achievement.

Monthly amount 50,000 JPY　
※The monthly amount for students selected in 2025 will be 40,000 JPY until the end of the scholarship period.
Duration Up to 2 years
Applicant's nationality Not specified
Qualifier ・Undergraduate students (second year or above), graduate students, and students of technical colleges (fourth year or above)
・Privately financed international students
・Academic high achievers
・Students with a record of volunteer activities or international exchange activities, or those who demonstrate a strong interest in such activities
※For further details, please contact the scholarship office at your home university during the application period.
Application Period Submission Deadline: Wednesday, May 20, 2026
※Please note that, in addition to the application period, your home university may establish its own internal deadline for the on-campus selection process. Applicants are advised to check with their university in advance.
※Applications for 2026 have closed.

Applications for our scholarship require a recommendation from your current university. Those wishing to apply or with any enquiries should contact your university's scholarship office. We do not accept direct applications or enquiries from students.
