FOUNDERS & TEAM
Founders
Martin Moshal
Founder and Philanthropist
Entrepreneur, investor, philanthropist, and business leader with a track record of building successful ventures (and many failures...). He is a prominent supporter of major educational, technological, and national initiatives, including the development of North High-Tech Park, investment in the Beresheet 2 lunar mission, and serving as a major benefactor of Yad Vashem. In recognition of his impact, he received an Honorary Doctorate from the Technion and an Honorary Fellowship from the Hebrew University. ​Through the Moshal Program, Martin enables pathways to higher education and economic mobility for thousands of talented students from underprivileged backgrounds. He believes in creating a legacy of shared impact, offering his students and alumni a clear message: "You don’t need to pay us back but you do need to pay it forward" Martin Moshal
Yael Lavie
Co-Founder and Program Chairperson
Business executive, entrepreneur, and social impact leader with decades of international experience in finance, real estate and large-scale initiatives. She co-founded the Moshal Program in 2009 and has led its growth from concept to a leading international program advancing economic mobility through higher education. Yael also Chairs North High-Tech Park, a regional development project creating employment and growth opportunities. She brings a business and investment mindset to the social world. Guided by the belief that talent is widely distributed while opportunity is not, Yael works to expand access to life-changing opportunities and unlock human potential at scale. “There is nothing more rewarding than seeing talented young people discover their potential, achieve their goals, and create a lasting impact far beyond their own lives.”
Team Leaders
Ohad Gross
CEO
Global CEO Following a 25-year career in high-tech across Israel and the United States – where he led and grew multiple businesses. Ohad now steers the Moshal Program toward its strategic objectives and next phase of significant growth. He sees Moshal students and alumni as exemplars of determination, motivation, and capability – the future leadership of society and industry. His mandate: give them the ideal support to break through the glass ceiling.
Meena Thomas
Country Managing Director
Meena brings more than 20 years of leadership experience from Amazon and Shell, where she led large teams and complex operations. She combines this experience with a genuine passion for developing people, an authentic leadership style, and a strong belief in the power of quality education to transform lives. Meena joins the Moshal Program as the Country Managing Director.
