Scholarship

Overview

Education Scholarships are a key initiative of the Putera Sampoerna Foundation. We firmly believe that this scholarship program stands as a crucial milestone in shaping high-caliber prospective leaders for Indonesia.

Fundraising

From its inception, the primary focus of the Putera Sampoerna Foundation
through this program has remained unwavering: to provide easy access to
education for academically talented yet financially disadvantaged
students. To this day, the scholarship program continues to be a
cornerstone of our efforts, having nurtured over 43,000 promising and
qualified future leaders of the nation.
