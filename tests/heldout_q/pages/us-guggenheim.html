Applying for a Guggenheim Fellowship

The Guggenheim Fellowship is a mid-career award supporting the work of individuals who have made notable contributions to more than fifty fields across the arts, humanities, natural sciences, social sciences, and interdisciplinary areas.

Each year, roughly 200 Fellows are selected through a rigorous competition that assesses both past accomplishments and future plans. Recent success rates have averaged around 5%. 

For more information, including detailed instructions about the application itself, find the 2027 Guggenheim Fellowship Application Guide here.

The 2027 United States and Canada competition is now closed.

Eligibility

Applicants must:

  • Be citizens or permanent residents of the United States or Canada

  • Be mid-career professionals who have made notable contributions to their field 

  • Propose a significant project and demonstrate that they are prepared to complete it at the highest level of excellence

  • Apply as individuals or in pairs (i.e., two individuals working on the same project)

The following are not eligible to apply:

  • Individuals who have already received a Guggenheim Fellowship 

  • Students currently enrolled in secondary, undergraduate, graduate, or postgraduate programs 

  • Organizations, institutions, or other established groups 

To learn more about the individuals we support, we recommend consulting the list of recent Guggenheim Fellows here.

Rigor, fairness, and confidentiality

The Guggenheim Foundation draws upon a network of several hundred advisers to carefully assess each application. Multiple layers of review ensure that evaluations are impartial, comprehensive, and of the highest possible standard. Advisers’ identities are confidential, guaranteeing a fair and meticulous review and ensuring that the focus stays squarely on the merit and originality of the work submitted. 

We regret that we are unable to provide feedback on applications.  

Please note that the Guggenheim Foundation charges no fees and has no affiliated application consultants or application advisors. No third-party service has inside information on, or influence over, our selection process. Please do not hesitate to contact us with any questions or concerns.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • When you fill out your application, you will be invited to choose the one field that you deem most representative of your proposed project.

    Please note that we have recently introduced several new interdisciplinary areas reflecting developments in the academy and beyond. We encourage you to browse the full list of options before you select your field.  

  • Work examples are required in certain fields. If this applies to your field, the application portal will make that clear. Work examples should be uploaded to the application portal or postmarked two weeks after the application portal closes. (For the 2027 application cycle, this date is September 29, 2026.)  

  • In scholarly fields, Guggenheim Fellowships support digital projects (e.g., e-books, multimodal works, and other emerging forms of publication) that:

    • Produce original knowledge;

    • Undergo successful peer or editorial review;

    • Articulate plans for long-term preservation.

  • During the application process, you will be prompted to submit the names and contact information of a minimum of three (and up to four) references that have agreed to write letters on your behalf. Please be sure to contact your references well in advance and confirm their preferred email addresses before completing the “Reference” section of your application. Once your application is submitted, reference letter requests will be automatically sent to the addresses you provided. Your references will be prompted to upload their letters there.  

  • Our competition is not organized in terms of rounds. All applications that meet our eligibility requirements are sent to readers or jurors for full consideration.

  • The deadline to apply for the 2027 application is September 15, 2026. Late applications are not accepted.

  • For the 2027 application cycle, applicants will find out if they have received a Fellowship in late April.
