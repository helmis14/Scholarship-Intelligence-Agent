Temasek Foundation – Union Scholarships



The Temasek Foundation – G Muthukumarasamy Scholarship, Temasek Foundation – RKS Nachiappan Scholarship, and Temasek Foundation – Nithiah Nandan Arumugam Scholarship honour the legacies of three late unionists - Mr G Muthukumarasamy, Mr Nachiappan RK Sinniah, and Mr Nithiah Nandan s/o Arumugam - who believed in the value of education and advocated for lifelong learning. Learn more about the unionists here.

Offered to students of all ages including mature students, these scholarships are offered on a merit basis to individuals from lower income backgrounds, who demonstrate academic excellence. 

Features

  • Two components:
    a) Living expenses for the student recipient
    b) A family recognition award for one or more family members to receive, in appreciation of the role of families in contributing to the success of the student and/or other family members
  • No age limit for applicants
  • Bond-free
  • Tenable for the entire course of study, and renewable yearly subject to the recipients meeting the criteria

Eligibility

  • The scholarships are given based on merit and income criteria
  • Eligible applicants must be Singaporeans with per capita income of not more than S$1,875.
  • Students of all ages are welcome to apply, including mature students

 

Application

Applications for the Temasek Foundation Scholarships will be opened yearly. Mid-course applications are also accepted. The public may contact the respective Institutes of Higher Learning for more information:
