    Becas_Ayudas_MH_Titulo

    ENROLLMENT OF HONOR

    It supposes a reduction of 16% on the amount of enrollment in the first year of Degree for those who have obtained it in 2nd year of high school diploma (certificate accrediting). This discount cannot be combined with other discounts, such as large family. In the case of international students, it is necessary that the center educational certifies that their average is in the top 5% of their promotion in the course taken in the year of access to the University, in addition to the certificate of enrollment of honor. 

    During the Degree, those who obtain the grade of enrollment of Honor in any subject obtain a discount in the following enrollment of approximately 16% of the value of the same issue of credits in which they obtained that grade in the last course. The system makes the discount automatically.
