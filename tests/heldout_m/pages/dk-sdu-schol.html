SDU Study at SDU Fees and Funding Scholarships

Scholarships

SDU offers a limited number of scholarships for highly qualified non-EU/EEA citizens. Scholarships can only be awarded for specific programmes offered on our campuses in Vejle and Sønderborg.

Danish Government Scholarship

The Danish government offers a limited number of scholarships through the Danish Government Scholarship programme to highly qualified applicants in recognition of the important investment that overseas students make during their education.

Who can receive the scholarship?

To be eligible for the scholarship, you must be a non-EU/EEA citizen enrolling in one of the following master programmes at our campus in Sønderborg or bachelor programmes at our campus in Vejle:

  • MSc in Engineering – Electronics/Electrical Engineering (Sønderborg)
  • MSc in Engineering - Mechanical Engineering (Sønderborg)
  • MSc in Engineering – Mechatronics (Sønderborg)
  • BSc in Artificial Intelligence (Vejle)
  • BSc in Computer Science (Vejle)
  • BSc in Interactive Technology Engineering (Vejle)
  • BSc in Software Engineering (Vejle)

NOTE: If you are eligible to receive the Danish State Grant, you cannot be considered for the scholarship. 

Full or partial tuition fee waiver

The scholarship is granted as a full tuition fee waiver and will cover the tuition fee for two years (MSc programmes) or three years (BSc programmes). 

Note that fee waiver covers 30 ECTS per semester. If the number of courses exceed 30 ECTS they are not covered by the fee waiver. 

To keep your scholarship, you must demonstrate satisfactory academic progress each semester. As a guideline, this means completing at least 30 ECTS per semester. If you are not considered to be making satisfactory progress, the scholarship funding may be discontinued for the following semester.

For Terms of Payment and tuition fee rates, please see Tuition fee.

Application and selection criteria – bachelor level

To be considered for the scholarship you simply apply for admission to one or more of the relevant programmes. No separate scholarship application is needed. 

To be eligible for a scholarship, please note: 

  • You must submit your application for admission no later than March 15 
  • Applicants with the highest score in the online admission test (uniTEST) will be invited to an interview. 

Selection criteria

  • The scholarships are awarded to the best-qualified candidates based on your result in the online admission test (uniTEST) and an online interview with key people from the study programme. 

Interviews will take place in early June.

You will receive an invitation to the interview by email with short notice in late May, once we have received your uniTEST score, so remember to check your email regularly in May. 

If you have applied for more than one of the four programmes, you will only be invited to one interview. 

The interview consists of a few mathematical questions and a brief talk about your motivation for studying at SDU. It does not require any specific preparation on your part. 

Application and selection criteria – master level

To be considered for the scholarship you simply apply for admission to one or more of the relevant programmes. No separate scholarship application is needed.

To be eligible for a scholarship, please note:

  • You must submit your application for admission no later than February 1 - even if you have a Danish residence permit
  • Your application must include all necessary documentation at the time of submission (i.e. it must not be necessary for SDU to ask for further documentation)
  • If you have applied for more than one programme at SDU, you will only be considered for the scholarship if the master’s programme is your highest priority

Your application must include:

  • Application deposit fee receipt
  • Official Bachelor’s transcripts and diploma in English, Danish, Swedish or Norwegian – must include grades and ECTS/units/credits for each course
  • Course descriptions for the courses on your transcripts (verified)
  • Self-assessment form (Electronics and Mechatronics)
  • Proof of English language proficiency

Please note: All documents must be uploaded to your online application at the time of application. We do not accept any documents sent by email.

Interview

The scholarships are awarded to the best-qualified candidates based on the grade point average of your bachelor’s degree and an online interview with key persons from the master’s programme. 

You will receive an invitation to the interview by email once we have processed your application for admission. If you have applied for more than one of the programmes, the interview will be for your highest priority. 

Interviews will take place between February 19 and 27.

You will receive the invitation with short notice after February 15, so remember to check your email regularly in February. The interview consists of a few technical questions related to the entry requirements and does not require any specific preparation on your part. 

Contact information

For further information about the scholarships or the admission process, please contact the admissions office.

Realise your potential and prepare for the future with a degree from The University of Southern Denmark

Every year, SDU welcomes highly qualified and motivated students who contribute to their education and the study environment and pursue high levels of professional expertise and brand-new perspectives. Challenge yourself and apply for a university programme in Denmark. Test-based admission in quota 2 is an entrance examination which is now used for nearly all our programmes. We recommend that everyone apply through quota 2 at SDU, even those with a grade point average sufficient to apply through quota 1.
