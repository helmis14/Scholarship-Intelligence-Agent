Every gift makes a difference

Operating under the auspices of the Fondation de France, the ESSEC Foundation mobilizes individual and institutional philanthropy to power the School's strategic priorities.

Donor generosity is the catalyst that unites academic excellence with social inclusion through an ambitious scholarship program. It drives world-class research, builds a state-of-the-art campus, fuels student entrepreneurship, and secures the School’s long-term financial independence.

You may choose to direct your support toward specific initiatives or allow the Foundation to allocate your gift where it is needed most.
