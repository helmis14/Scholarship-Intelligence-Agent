International Scholarships

Available scholarships

Flinders University offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate scholarships for 2027 and beyond, to support international students to pursue their educational and career goals. 

 

Search our range of Flinders, government and privately funded scholarships offered for commencing and graduating international students.

Academic scholarships

Choose from a range of full undergraduate and postgraduate scholarships valued up to 50%.

Learn more

Alumni scholarships

If you are a Flinders University Alumni, you could be eligible for a 20% full duration scholarship to study another undergraduate or postgraduate degree at Flinders.

Learn more

External scholarships

If Flinders University works with a range of Australian and foreign government and industry scholarship programs to provide opportunities for eligible international students to have their studies at Flinders sponsored.

Learn more

Higher degrees by research scholarships

Flinders University offers a range of limited place scholarships for students to seeking to study a PhD or Master by Research degree.

Learn more

Financial aid

You may be eligible for financial support or a student loan to study at Flinders if you are from the USA or Canada.

Learn more

English for Academic Purposes Express Scholarship

This scholarship is offered to new Flinders University Academy students and provides a 30% tuition fee reduction.
