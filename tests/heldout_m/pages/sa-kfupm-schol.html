KFUPM Scholarship

 

Distinguished graduate applicants who demonstrate exceptional qualifications and performance with a high GPA can be admitted as Full-time MS or Ph.D. students with a full scholarship. We offer scholarships for all PhD programs and PhD - Direct Entry Programs. Accordingly, they receive the following benefits:

  • Stipend according to category.
  • Free-furnished air-conditioned on-campus housing, with utilities and maintenance.
  • Air tickets are only for International students.
  • Medical/ dental care.
  • Subsidized meals.

Other benefits:

  • Opportunity to become RA-PhD.
  • Free study, No tuition fee.
  • Funding for participating in conferences under certain conditions.
  • Opportunity to work on research projects and get financial benefits.
  • Main library and department resources center.
  • Safe & secure environment.
  • Recreational facilities (including a private beach).
  • Free textbooks.

Important Note: The duration of the direct PhD program is five (05) years. Full scholarship will be provided for the first four (04) years of the direct PhD program.
