Vice Chancellor's Postgraduate International Scholarship

£7,000 tuition fee waiver for international postgraduate students studying a Masters degree.

Key facts

Fee status

International (including EU)

Your country/region

All international

Level

Postgraduate (taught)

Number of awards

September 2027 and January 2028: 100 awards

Value of awards

£7,000

Deadline

Applications for January 2027 are now closed.

Applications for September 2027 and January 2028 are now open.

We are seeking students who are prepared to inspire, eager to shape their future careers and ready to ‘Be the difference’ both in Stirling and globally within their communities.

As part of our commitment to attracting the very best students to join our diverse international community, we are delighted to offer the Vice Chancellor's Postgraduate International Scholarship.

This scholarship is awarded in the form of a £7,000 tuition fee waiver for new students in the first year of a Masters degree and is open to all full-time, self-funding students who are classed as International for tuition fee purposes.

Eligibility and availability

  • You must have received a conditional or unconditional offer for an eligible postgraduate taught course at the University of Stirling, any application without a valid offer will not be assessed.
  • You must pay the Scholarship deposit of £1,000 to secure the scholarship by the deadline outlined on your scholarship offer letter (see ‘how to apply’ for deadlines).
  • Please ensure that you have read the terms and conditions before submitting your application.
  • You must be a new student starting at the university in September 2027 or January 2028.
  • You must be classed as an International student for tuition fee purposes.
  • You must be a self-funded student (those receiving external sponsorship or other scholarships will not be eligible).
  • You must enter directly into a full-time postgraduate degree programme delivered at the University’s Stirling campus.
  • Online, MRes or MPhil degrees are not eligible.
  • This scholarship cannot be combined with any other Stirling scholarship or award. If offered more than one University of Stirling award, then the highest value award will be applied.
  • Students progressing from an INTO Pre-Masters course are not eligible to apply but may be eligible for INTO Progression scholarships. 

How to apply

Applications must be submitted via the Vice Chancellor's Scholarship Application 2027-28.

Applications will be assessed and added to a wait list as scholarship funding becomes available. If your scholarship application is successful and a scholarship offer is made, you will be required to pay a £1,000 deposit to secure the scholarship by the relevant deadline outlined below.

We aim to inform all applicants of the outcome either way by 15 July. In the meantime, please proceed with your application on the basis of other available scholarships you may be eligible for.
