We have International Excellence Scholarships available to overseas students who have demonstrated academic excellence or show potential to do so during their time at Swansea University.  

Scholarships are awarded as a discount on tuition fees worth up to £5,000 for the first year of study. Undergraduate students who receive the full £5,000 in their first year of study will be awarded an additional £2,000 in their second year if they achieve a minimum average of 70% in their first year.   

Applicants must have received an offer to study an undergraduate course or a postgraduate taught course at Swansea University to apply.  

Please complete the scholarship form that best represents the area of your country of ordinary residence. Your country of ordinary residence is the country where you have been living for the last 5 years and excludes countries in which you reside purely for the purpose of studying.  

China

Click here to apply for the International Excellence Scholarship (China) for January and March 2027 entry. 

If you are unable to use the Google form above, we also accept applications via this form: 

International Excellence Form China January & March 2027

Please read the full terms and conditions to determine if you are eligible to apply. 

Please note that only one application per student will be considered. 

Remember, you need to include transcripts of previous studies and provide proof of your average score/GPA issued by the institution (if no average score or GPA is on your transcript) so we can process your application. 

Applications for this scholarship will close at 12:00 GMT on the 27th November 2026. 

Rest of the World

Click here to apply for the International Excellence Scholarship (Rest of the World) for January and March 2027 entry. 

Please read the full terms and conditions to determine if you are eligible to apply. 

Please note that only one application per student will be considered. 

Applications for this scholarship will close at 12:00 GMT on the 27th November 2026.
