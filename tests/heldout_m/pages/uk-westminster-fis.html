                              Scholarships

                              Postgraduate scholarships

                              International Postgraduate Excellence Award – January 2027

                              Amount: £3,000 – £5,000

                              Deadline: 30 November 2026

                              The International Postgraduate Excellence Award is designed to support international students who have achieved academic excellence and show passion for their chosen field of study. We're offering scholarships for high-achieving international students, covering £3,000 towards their full-time Master’s tuition fees. Students who enrol on the 2 year Master's with Integrated Professional Year Experience programme could receive £5,000 towards their tuition fees. 

                              Find out more about the International Postgraduate Excellence Award. 

                              Chevening Scholarship

                              Amount: Fully funded - see terms and conditions on Chevening website.

                              Deadline: 6 October 2026

                              Chevening Scholarships are the UK government’s global scholarship programme for overseas fee-paying students. This fully-funded scholarship covers flights, accommodation and course fees to undertake a one-year Master's course at any UK university.

                              Find out more about the Chevening Scholarship and apply on the Chevening website.

                              Home Postgraduate Part Fee Scholarship

                              Amount: £2,000

                              Deadline: 2 November 2026

                              One off £2,000 Tuition Fee Award paid directly to the University towards your tuition fees in your first semester. Students on two-year postgraduate or part-time courses will receive the award for their first year only.

                              Find out more about the Home Postgraduate Part Fee Scholarship.

                              International Postgraduate Excellence Award - September 2026

                              Amount: £3,000

                              This scholarship is now closed.

                              The International Postgraduate Excellence Award is designed to support international students who have achieved academic excellence and show passion for their chosen field of study. We're offering scholarships for high-achieving international students, covering £3,000 towards their full-time Master’s tuition fees.

                              Find out more about the International Postgraduate Excellence Award.

                              Postgraduate Sanctuary Scholarship

                              Amount: Full tuition fee plus up to £12,000

                              Deadline: 2 November 2026

                              The University offers two Postgraduate Sanctuary Scholarships to support students seeking asylum and are currently living in the United Kingdom. It covers full tuition costs, plus a maintenance grant of £1,000 per month for up to 12 months.

                              Find out more about the Postgraduate Sanctuary Scholarship.

                              Undergraduate scholarships

                              Home Undergraduate Bursaries

                              Amount: Between £1000 - £3000

                              Deadline: Open to current students

                              The University provides a number of bursaries and financial support available to current students such as the Estranged Student Bursary, Care Leaver Bursary and Students with Caring Responsibilities. Students can apply for this support once they have enrolled in their course. 

                              Find out more about Bursaries and Awards for current students.

                              Chris Wilkinson Scholarship

                              Amount: £5,000 grant each year for the second and final year of study

                              Deadline: 2 November 2026

                              The Chris Wilkinson Scholarship is a prestigious maintenance scholarship for second-year architecture students, which also covers their third year of study. Established in loving memory of Chris Wilkinson OBE, a celebrated Westminster alumnus and founder of WilkinsonEyre, this scholarship honours his legacy of innovation and excellence in architecture. 

                              Find out more about the Chris Wilkinson Scholarship.

                              ECCEDU International Business and AI Programme in Madrid

                              Amount: Full (1) or half (2) scholarship 

                              Deadline: 27 September 2026

                              Over the course of six weeks, you will be immersed in Madrid, broadening your international perspective while connecting with global opportunities. A guaranteed, four-week professional internship placement in a European company aligned with your specific field of interest plus high-impact lectures and academic conferences led by international experts.

                              Find out more about the ECCEDU International Business and AI Programme.

                              International Undergraduate Excellence Award (IEA) - September 2026

                              Amount: £2,500

                              This scholarship is now closed.

                              This scholarship supports international students who have achieved academic excellence and show passion for their chosen field of study. 

                              Find out more about the International Undergraduate Excellence Award.

                              The O+T Foundation Bursary

                              Amount: Up to £10,000 per year for each year of study

                              Deadline: Applications are currently closed

                              The O+T Foundation Bursary supports talented undergraduate students from underrepresented backgrounds to study Real Estate or Quantity Surveying and Commercial Management at the University of Westminster.

                              Find out more about the O+T Foundation Bursary.
