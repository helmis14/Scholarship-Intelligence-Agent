International fees and funding

You'll find Coventry a welcoming place to live and study, with lots of support available.

  • How to pay your tuition fees
  • Scholarships
  • Applying for your visa
  • Coming to the UK
  • Cost of living hub

Tuition fees

We calculate tuition fees per year of study and they can vary depending on the course you want to study. Please refer to our course pages for the latest and most accurate information regarding detailed entry requirements and tuition fees, or contact us.

You may be able to get a Tuition Fee Loan and help with living costs if you’re from an EU country, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway or Switzerland. Check if you're eligible for undergraduate funding. The UK Council for International Student Affairs (UKCISA) also provide guidance on funding and eligibility for international students, including postgraduate study.

Our scholarships

We believe in encouraging students to fulfil their potential. That is why we continually invest in a range of financial awards to help students with their tuition fees.

All scholarships are subject to availability, visa requirements, meeting specific criteria and competitive application.

See our international student scholarships

Payment plans

We offer payment instalment plans for self-funded international students, please see Tuition Fees Terms and Conditions for more information.

Cost of living

As well as your course fees, you’ll also need to consider the cost of living at university. Your cost of living will depend on your lifestyle and spending habits, but you could find your money goes further in Coventry than in other parts of the UK. For example, Coventry is ranked as the 4th Student City in England for Affordability (Coventry) in the QS Best Student Cities Index 2027.

Alumni discount

All Coventry University graduates can receive a 20% discount from September 2026 from the tuition fees payable for their postgraduate programme.

Find out more about the alumni discount
