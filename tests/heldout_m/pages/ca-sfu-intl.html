Undergraduate Scholars Entrance Scholarships

Eligibility

If you’re a current high school student who demonstrates excellence in academic and extracurricular activities, you may be eligible for this scholarship.

It is available to both domestic and international students admitted for the fall term to any undergraduate degree.

Academic Requirements

To be considered, applicants must have a minimum average of 90% or 31 IB points (excluding bonus points) in courses required for admission to their primary program.

Value

From $20,000 up to $60,000 for domestic students, and $140,000 for international students.

Further funding, in the form of a living allowance, may be offered to support exceptionally talented students with demonstrated financial need.

In addition to the funding, the program includes perks such as:

  • Priority access to residence
  • 'Front of the line' course enrolment priority

Learn more

Process 

You must submit an application to be considered for an entrance scholarship.

Applications open

October 1, 2026

Application deadline

December 15, 2026
