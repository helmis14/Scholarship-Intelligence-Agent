Taylor's University Scholarship Application Process for:

  • Merit Scholarship
  • Talent Scholarship
  • Sports Scholarship
  • Community Scholarship 
  • PPE Exclusive Scholarship
  • Postgraduate High Achiever Scholarship 

1. Discover

Visit our website to discover our wide range of scholarships and specific details for each scholarship, including eligibility criteria and deadlines.

2. Application

Send your application form with the required documents by the deadline at the link provided by the counsellor. Any submission after the deadline will not be processed.

3. Processing

Applications will be processed within approximately 2-3 weeks after the deadline. Look out for notifications from the Scholarship Management Team in your email inbox (and Junk/Spam folders).

4. Shortlisting

Shortlisted candidates will be guided on the next step(s) via email.

5. Assessment

Following the directions in the email, complete an assessment* by a given deadlines and/or participate in one or more interview sessions.

6. Review

The Scholarships Management Team will review your application and conduct a final vetting process.

7. Selection

Successful applicants will receive an exclusive offer letter via email. Unsuccessful applicants will also be notified via email. Kindly refer to the Application Outcome dates on our website to ensure you do not miss any email notifications.

What does Taylor's Scholarship cover?

What does Taylor's Scholarship cover?

Taylor's Scholarship covers the Tuition Fee of the programme only for the standard duration. All other fees including repeat fees to be borne by students.
What types of scholarships are available?

What types of scholarships are available?

We provide various scholarship opportunities based on criteria that include, but are not limited to, merit, talent, financial needs, and athletic achievement. 
How can I apply for scholarships?

How can I apply for scholarships?

What are the eligibility criteria for scholarships?

Do I need to maintain a certain CGPA/score/grades to continue my scholarship?

Do I need to maintain a certain CGPA/score/grades to continue my scholarship?

Scholarship recipients are required to maintain satisfactory academic results as outlined by the minimum CGPA/score/grades specified in their scholarship offer letter. 
Are international students eligible for scholarships?

Are international students eligible for scholarships?

Selected international applicants may apply should they meet the terms of the scholarship. Please refer to our Terms and Conditions for a detailed explanation of whether the requirements are met.

Unfortunately, we are unable to provide individual feedback to applicants as we receive high volume of applications.

What should I do if I'm awarded multiple scholarships?
