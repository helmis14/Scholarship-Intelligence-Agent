About us

What we do

International Office provides information and support in the following areas:

 

1. Exchange Programmes

  • Incoming and outgoing students and staff
  • Erasmus+, CEEPUS, Freemover, government scholarships, and other mobility programmes
  • Cooperation agreements and partnerships
  • Mobility statistics and reporting

2. Degree Programmes Accredited in English

  • Support in the development of new English-taught degree programmes
  • Marketing and promotion of English-taught programmes
  • Admissions procedures
  • Student support services

3. Project Support

  • Erasmus+, CEEPUS, IVF, AKTION, and other projects
  • Creation and registration of projects in the ISEP system
  • Administrative support for project implementation and management

Student and Staff Mobility (Incoming & Outgoing)

  • Coordination of incoming international students and academic staff within the CEEPUS, AKTION, DAAD, Compatriots, Freemover, and interfaculty agreement programmes (Asia)
