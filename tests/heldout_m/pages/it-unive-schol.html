Welcome to Ca’ Foscari University of Venice’s online application portal for applicants in possession of an international entrance qualification who wish to apply for admission to a bachelor’s or master’s degree programmes or to Ca’ Foscari’s Foundation Year programme.

APPLYING FOR ADMISSION IN 2027/2028
If you are interested in applying for admission to a bachelor’s or master’s degree in 2027/28, be sure to register now so that you receive our email newsletter as well as updates about when it will be possible to apply for admission based on your citizenship and your programme of interest.
If you are interested in applying for admission to the Foundation Year programme, write to foundation.cfsie@unive.it to request to receive updates about procedures and deadlines.

GETTING STARTED
You can register on the platform at any time so that we can begin sending you helpful information about the Ca’ Foscari student experience. When registering, provide a personal email address that you check regularly, as it will be used for all communication moving forward.

You can also learn more about the overall application procedure and the documents you need to provide by consulting the dedicated web page either in English or in Italian, and the articles appearing on this homepage provide lots of useful information.

If you have any questions you would like to ask about studying at Ca’ Foscari, you can contact us by writing to prospective.students@unive.it. We also encourage you to check out what our students post on Instagram at @studentlifeinvenice!

Thank you for your interest in Ca’ Foscari!

BA / MA APPLICANTS NOT REQUIRING A STUDY VISA

You belong to this category if you have citizenship from a Schengen Area country, Ireland or the Republic of Cyprus. You may also belong to this category if you have a non-EU citizenship but are already residing in Italy and in possession of an Italian residence permit that allows university enrolment. To ensure that you choose the correct applicant category, please FILL OUT THIS DEDICATED QUESTIONNAIRE.

BA / MA APPLICANTS REQUIRING A STUDY VISA

You belong to this category if you are currently living outside Italy, do not already possess an Italian permit of stay that allows university enrolment, and only have citizenships from countries that DO NOT appear in the following list: Schengen Area countries, Ireland, Republic of Cyprus. To ensure that you choose the correct applicant category, please FILL OUT THIS DEDICATED QUESTIONNAIRE.

FOUNDATION YEAR APPLICANTS

You belong to this category if – regardless of your citizenship – you wish to apply for the Ca’ Foscari Foundation Year. This propedeutic year-long programme is designed to prepare international students for undergraduate studies at an Italian university.
