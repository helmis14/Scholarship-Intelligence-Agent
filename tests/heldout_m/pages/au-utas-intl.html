International scholarships

We offer international students a range of scholarships across all areas of study. Explore our scholarships and learn how to apply.

  1. Home
  2. Study with us
  3. Scholarships, fees and costs
  4. International scholarships

We’re pleased to support international students to study with us. We believe that university study should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their background.

Our generous scholarship offerings provide full fee-paying international students with a range of opportunities to go towards living and study costs. Some are based on academic merit, and others provide you with support to access university studies. We also provide scholarships for our alumni and for family members of current students.

For many of our International Scholarships, you will be assessed at the time of submitting your International Student Application so there is no need to apply separately. Offer letters will notify applicants if they have been awarded the scholarship.

You are also eligible to apply for a domestic scholarship if:

  • You've successfully completed a 100% enrolment load in any study period with us in an undergraduate or postgraduate course.
  • You're completing years 11 and 12 onshore in Australia.

    Please note, this does not apply to international candidates applying to undertake a Higher Degree by Research (HDR).
