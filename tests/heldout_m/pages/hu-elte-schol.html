Scholarships

Most of the degree programs - in Hungarian and in English - of the Faculty of Primary and Pre-School Education are available through different Scholarship programs.
Be aware, that the Faculty of Primary and Pre-School Education (as an institute) is only responsible for the available degree programs themselves, scholarship applications and its regulations are announced by various organizations! Find out more about their special requirements on the websites and read their 'Call for applications' documents carefully!  

Available scholarships:

  • Stipendium Hungaricum - for international applicants

  • Stipendium Hungaricum Subprogramme – Students at Risk Subprogramme for students fleeing the war in Ukraine

  • Scholarship for the Hungarian Diaspora - for foreign applicants with Hungarian identity 

  • CEEPUS Scholarship
