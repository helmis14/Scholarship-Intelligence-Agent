College.Fully Funded.

For more than a century, Berea College has covered tuition for every student. Now, we're going further. Every Berea student receives a fully funded educational experience—including tuition, housing, meals, and transformative opportunities like internships. This is more than financial aid. It's a reimagining of what higher education can be.

Academics

Find what moves you. Then go further.

At Berea, you won't just choose a major — you'll discover what you're capable of — inside and outside of the classroom. Small classes and close faculty mentorship give you room to ask bigger questions, explore across disciplines and turn what you learn into something meaningful.

Whether you're working in a lab or on the farm, creating in a studio, or taking your learning into the community, your education is meant to be used. It prepares you to lead and serve in a changing world while keeping people, purpose, and impact at the heart of what you do.

Practical and deeply human, it's built to prepare you not just for a career, but for a life of purpose.

At Our Core

The Heart of Berea

Berea College was founded in 1855 by an ardent abolitionist. We encourage values such as inclusion and impartial love, and it shows in everything that we do.

01—Inclusion

Impartial Love

Founded on the belief that all people are made of one blood, Berea welcomes students of every background without regard to race, gender, or creed.

Learn more→

02—Opportunity

Fully Funded

Every student receives a fully funded degree—covering tuition, housing, food, and fees, without loans and without limits.

Learn more→

03—The Work Program

Work to Learn

Students work a minimum of 10 hours each week in one of 130+ work positions, building skills, earning wages, and developing a lifelong sense of purpose.

Learn more→

04—Community

Serving Appalachia

Deeply rooted in the mountains of Kentucky, Berea serves the people of Appalachia while welcoming students and perspectives from across the globe.
