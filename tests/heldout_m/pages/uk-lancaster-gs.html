Scholarships and bursaries

At Lancaster, we are investing in the future of our students by enabling access to a world-class education.

Find scholarships and bursaries

You will be automatically considered for Lancaster's major scholarships when you apply - we take all the information we need from your application so you don't have to do anything extra.

Scholarships for undergraduate students starting in 2027

If you're starting a undergraduate programme at Lancaster in 2027 then these opportunities may be available to you.

  • Undergraduate EU Scholarship

    A £3,000 fee reduction scholarship, per year of study, for international students from the European Union, starting an undergraduate course in 2027.

  • Undergraduate International Excellence Scholarship

    £3,000 fee reduction per year of study for high-achieving international undergraduate students who make Lancaster their firm choice.

  • Undergraduate Home Excellence Scholarship

    A £1,000 scholarship per year of study, for up to three years, for high-achieving home undergraduate students who make Lancaster their firm choice.

  • Lancaster Bursary

    £1,000 in each year of study for home undergraduates with an assessed household income under £35,000, paid in two instalments.

  • Undergraduate India Scholarship

    £3,000 tuition fee reduction, each year of study, for international students from India starting an undergraduate degree.

  • Undergraduate International Regional Scholarship

    £3,000 tuition fee reduction, each year of study, for international undergraduates from one of 12 eligible countries.

  • Lancaster Sanctuary Scholarship

    A scholarship for students who are refugees or have humanitarian protection, with support and resources to help them integrate into our community here at Lancaster.

  • Cowrie Foundation Scholarship

    A scholarship for a UK student of Black African or Caribbean heritage starting undergraduate study in 2027.

Scholarships for postgraduate students starting in 2027

If you're starting a postgraduate programme in 2027 at Lancaster then these opportunities may be available to you.

  • Early Acceptance Scholarship

    Eligible students will receive a £2,000 scholarship if you accept your place at Lancaster before 23:59 GMT on 7 April 2027.

  • Postgraduate India Scholarship

    £10,000 tuition fee reduction for students from India starting a taught master's degree.

  • Postgraduate International Regional Scholarship

    £10,000 fee reduction scholarship for international students from 12 underrepresented countries, starting a taught postgraduate course in 2027

  • Master's Excellence Scholarship

    A 15% fee reduction scholarship, valued at up to £4,635, for students who achieve a 2:1 or first-class degree in their undergraduate studies.

  • Alumni Loyalty Scholarship

    10% off fees for all Taught Master's degrees if you are a Lancaster graduate.

  • Lancaster Sanctuary Scholarship

    A scholarship for students who are refugees or have humanitarian protection, with support and resources to help them integrate into our community here at Lancaster.

  • Master’s Scholarship for Black British Students

    This scholarship will provide a full fee waiver and stipend of £9,000, for living costs to Black British students.

  • Management School Progression Scholarship

    A 10% fee reduction for applicants with a 2:2 classification to support progression to postgraduate study in LUMS.

  • Management School MBA Scholarships

    For students studying Lancaster's MBA programmes.

External scholarships for postgraduate students

Scholarships from external funders for postgraduate students at Lancaster University.

  • ESRC Studentships

    Lancaster University forms part of the Economic and Social Research Council's (ESRC) North West Social Science Doctoral Training Partnership (NWSSDTP).

  • GREAT Scholarships

    Support for students from France, Ghana, Indonesia and Spain.

  • China Scholarships Council

    Funding for tuition fees, living costs and flights for Chinese PhD students, following a successful application.

  • Commonwealth Scholarships

    Full funding for international postgraduate students, depending on eligibility criteria.

  • Higher Education Scholarships for Palestinians

    A joint award from the British Council's HESPAL scheme and Lancaster University for Palestinian Master's students.

  • Chevening Scholarships

    Full funding for international postgraduate students, depending on eligibility criteria.

  • LPDP Scholarship

    Financial support for Indonesian citizens pursuing postgraduate study in the UK as LPDP listed universities.
