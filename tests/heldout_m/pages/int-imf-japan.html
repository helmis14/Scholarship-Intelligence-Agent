Mandate and mission

  • The International Monetary Fund (IMF) works to achieve sustainable growth and prosperity for all of its 191 member countries.
  • The IMF supports economic policies that promote financial stability and monetary cooperation to increase productivity, job creation, and economic well-being.
  • The IMF is governed by and accountable to its member countries.
