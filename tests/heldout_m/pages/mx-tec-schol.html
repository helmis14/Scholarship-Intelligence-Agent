Scholarships Campus Monterrey

Gallagher Foundation

It supports the visions of young leaders, in countries such as Mexico, South Africa and Turkey, with a commitment to their country and provides an academic community that shares a similar commitment.

navigate_next
  • Scholarship that covers 100% tuition.
  • Provides additional supports for support, books, lodging and transportation.
  • Applies to all professional careers in traditional mode (except for MC, MO and ARQ).
  • Annual call (August).
  • Be a student admitted to first entry to professional career.
  • Demonstrate a high socioeconomic need (carry out the socioeconomic scholarship application process).
  • Have a cumulative high school average equal to or greater than 95.
  • Obtain 1,400 or more points on Academic Aptitude Test (PAA).
  • Hand in personal and family photograph
  • Write an essay of intention and a curriculum (in English), in which you mention the main academic and co-curricular activities that you have carried out.

ZABER Foundation

Agreement with the Zaber Foundation that seeks to support students with high academic standards and high economic need.
 

navigate_next

Together, the Zaber Foundation and the Tecnológico de Monterrey, provide a scholarship that covers 100% of tuition.
Annual call (August).

  • Be a student admitted to first income.
  • Demonstrate a high socio-economic need (carry out the socioeconomic scholarship application process).
  • Have a high school average equal to or greater than 90.
  • Obtain 1,360 * points or more on the Academic Aptitude Test (APA).
  • Register to the call of the Zaber Foundation and be selected.
  • Obtain letter of support from the foundation.

Scholarship Fund Domínguez-Rivas

It grants scholarships of tuition to the students who are studying the career of Engineer in Nanotechnology and Chemical Sciences (INCQ) and who demonstrate a high academic performance.

navigate_next
  • Aditional 10% de scholarshio on tuition fee.
  • Apllies for current and first year students
  • Limited availability.
  • Be a student admitted or pursuing an INCQ career at the Tecnológico de Monterrey, Monterrey Campus. Being an admitted to INCQ Carrer or a current student at Tec de Monterrey
  • Have a cumulative grade average equal or higher than 90  (Highschool or professional) 
  • Obtain 1,300* points or more on the Academic Aptitude Test (PAA). This requirement doesn’t apply to current students.
  • Have a  60% or more Socio-economic Scholarship.
  • Hand in an essay letter directed to professor Dr. Javier Rivas Ramos.

For more information contact: 

Dr. Marcelo Videa
mvidea@itesm.mx

Assistance houses of CEMYD

Its social task is to provide accommodation for talented and economically constrained female students. As part of its philosophy and in support of the values that the Tec promotes in its students, CEMYD emphasizes the practice of citizenship competencies and professional development, as well as raising awareness of the responsibility to reward the support received by this Club.

navigate_next
  • Hosting support
  • Applies only to women
  • Admission to Tecnológico de Monterrey, Monterrey Campus
  • Demonstrate socio-economic need.
  • Cumulative average equal to or greater than 85.
  • Obtain 1,300 * points on the Academic Aptitude Test (PAA). Only for students of first income.
  • Be a scholarship student, with at least 60% scholarship

The FAMBD-CEMYD opens call for female scholarship holders in two periods during the year. Each call precedes the semester in which de entry will occur.

For the February-July semester the call opens from October 15 to November 15. For the August-December semester the call opens form April 15 to May 15.

Process:

  1. The communication can only be made by the interested student.
  2. You must sent an email requesting information to carolina.morales@tec.mx. With this, the application process begins.
  3. If you already have a student email, please communicate from that email.
  4. You must apply for the immediate semester in which you wish to start your studies.
  5. Any questions will be resolved during the period in which the call is open. This is for your own safety. If you send the mail too early, there is a risk that it will be “misplaced” and you will not receive a response.

Dr. Carolina Morales CAROLINA.MORALES@ITESM.MX

Brotherhood Ricardo Guajardo

It is a Private Charity Association composed of former Tecnológico de Monterrey scholars who lived in the Ricardo R. Guajardo Suárez residence building, and by their Fraternity graduates since 1994. Under the motto "Gratitude in Action", he works to help the integral formation of the Fellows of the Fraternity, so that they are professionals in their area of ​​knowledge, leaders generators of change and citizens committed to the development of their community, urgently required in Mexico
 

navigate_next
  • It provides moral and economic support to outstanding foreign students, who have serious economic limitations to pay for their support in the city of Monterrey.
  • Subject to availability of places.
  • Admission to Tecnológico de Monterrey, Monterrey Campus.
  • Demonstrate socio-economic need.
  • Cumulative average equal to or greater than 85.
  • Obtain 1,300 * points on Academic Aptitude Test (PAA). (Only for first year students)
  • Have at least a 60% socioeconomic grant.
  • Fill out the application application.
  • Curriculum vitae.
  • Prepare an essay no longer than 2 pages, in which you explain why you want to get this support of lodging.
  • Letter signed by the Race Director in which you propose to obtain support.
  • Go to the scheduled interview session by appointment.

Dr. Jesús Eugenio García Gardea
jegarcia@itesm.mx

Apoyos Económicos por Grupos Estudiantiles Monterrey

En este apartado podrás encontrar información sobre los diferentes apoyos 
económicos complementarios que ofrecen los Grupos Estudiantiles de campus 
Monterrey. Estos apoyos son para estudiantes que presentan una alta necesidad 
económica.

navigate_next

Todos los apoyos que otorgan los Grupos Estudiantiles son complementarios. Existen 
los siguientes tipos de apoyos:
Estos apoyos están disponibles para estudiantes activos del Tec de Monterrey campus 
Monterrey.

  • Apoyos de colegiatura
  • Apoyos de Meal Plan
  • Apoyos de EdenRed
  • Apoyos de Vivienda (AEBMAC, AESMAC, SEIIS, SELEM)

NOTA: Todos los apoyos económicos están sujetos a disponibilidad de convocatoria

  • Ser estudiante inscrito en el Tec de Monterrey, Campus Monterrey durante el periodo de convocatoria.
  • Ser representado o representada por el Grupo Estudiantil que otorga la beca.
  • El estudiante debe permanecer en Campus Monterrey durante el periodo del apoyo.
  • Acreditar un rendimiento académico con un promedio igual o superior a 85 sobre 100 en el último semestre realizado y en el acumulado de los semestres realizados.
  • El estudiante deberá tener un expediente limpio de Deshonestidad Académica o condicionamiento de la beca por parte del Campus.
  • Tener entre 50% y 90% de beca-crédito que otorga el Tecnológico de Monterrey al inicio del período académico en que aplica al apoyo.
  • No contar con otra beca de algún otro Grupo Estudiantil durante el periodo de otorgamiento. 

NOTA: En caso de contar con Beca de Talento Académico, Beca de Talento Estudiantil, Beca de Emprendimiento, o Beca por parte de una Fundación externa o Empresa, hay que dirigirse con el Depto de Becas para que pueda determinar la necesidad económica.

  • Se abrirá un periodo de convocatoria en el cual los Grupos Estudiantiles autorizados podrán dar a conocer los apoyos que estarán disponibles para la comunidad estudiantil.
  • El Consejo de Responsabilidad Social Estudiantil invitará a los Grupos Estudiantiles a lanzar la convocatoria para apoyos a sus estudiantes / comunidad inmediata. A partir de este momento iniciará el proceso de aplicación de las y los estudiantes interesados, cuya información será evaluada por las autoridades correspondientes y resultará en la aprobación o negación del apoyo económico.
  • El apoyo podrá ser otorgado en los periodos académicos “Agosto- Diciembre” y “Febrero-Junio”. Cada periodo tendrá su proceso de solicitud y se mantendrán por separado.
  • Los apoyos complementarios serán otorgados en el siguiente periodo académico en el cual se realizó la convocatoria.
  • Dudas favor de comunicarse al siguiente contacto:
  • MEd. y MIP. Julio Adrián Morales Villarreal
  • Líder de Iniciativas de Impacto Social
  • Grupos Estudiantiles, campus Monterrey
  • Julioa.morales@tec.mx
