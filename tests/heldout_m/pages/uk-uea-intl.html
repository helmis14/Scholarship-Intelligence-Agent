UEA is a truly global university.

We’re proud to have over 3,500 international students, from over 100 countries around the world.

We are a friendly, welcoming campus surrounded by beautiful English countryside. We are the Number 1 choice for many international students. Not only do we have very high academic standards, with scholarships that recognise student excellence, we are also a safe, supportive and enjoyable place to study with brilliant graduate prospects. We also guarantee accommodation for International Students (subject to terms and conditions).

We know that your university experience will be quite different to that of students who already live in the UK, so we do everything we can to help you settle in and enjoy your university experience.

You can meet our Global team to help answer your questions, or meet them in your country at one of our many events. If you are visiting the UK, then visit us in Norwich to see our beautiful University for yourself, and have the opportunity to meet with academic staff and current students. 

You can also talk to our staff and current students on Unibuddy.
