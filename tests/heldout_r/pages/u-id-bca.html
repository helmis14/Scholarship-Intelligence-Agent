Bakti BCA Scholarship

Supporting outstanding students with strong passion for nation building

Every Champion is Unique, The Champion is You!

Beasiswa Bakti BCA provides assistance to undergraduate students pursuing S1 programs at universities. Bakti BCA Scholarship recipients receive financial support to aid their education, participate in development programs, and become part of the Bakti BCA Scholarship community, known as "Bakti Champions."

The Journey of Bakti Champions

Get a sneak peek at the exciting journey of #BaktiChampions! From the selection process, development programs, to the grand finale event with BCA's Board of Directors and renowned speakers.

BCA consistently supports the vision of sustainable Indonesia, particularly in enhancing the quality of education and the competitiveness of human resources. Since 1999, BCA has provided opportunities for outstanding Indonesian students who possess the passion to build the nation and become future national leaders.

Benefits of the Bakti BCA Scholarship

Bakti Champions will receive educational funding and one year of personal development opportunities through various Bakti Champions programs

Scholarship Categories

Bakti BCA Scholarship is open to high-achieving university students in the following categories:

Financial Assistance
For promising university students who require financial support.
Academic Achievement
For university students with academic achievements.
Non-Academic Achievement
For university students with non-academic achievements at a minimum provincial level within the past three years, such as in sports, arts, culture, and other fields

General and Specific Criteria

General Criteria

  • Indonesian citizen and unmarried.
  • Aged 21 or under as of December 31, 2026.
  • An active undergraduate (S1, non-vocational) student at a public university, currently in the 3rd semester at the time of application.
  • Not currently receiving any other scholarship.
  • Fully committed to participating in the entire development program for one year.
  • Meets the specific criteria of at least one Bakti BCA Scholarship category.
  • Demonstrates social awareness and commitment to learning, actively participates in organizations or committees, is not currently working full-time, and is not bound by a service agreement with any other institution.

Program Period and Selection Stages

Benefit Period of the Bakti BCA Scholarship

The scholarship benefit period is valid for January - December 2024 (1 year), for the 4th & 5th Semester students.

Stage Period
Online Registration August 3, 2026 - September 30, 2026
Announcement of Applicants Passing the Administrative Selection October 13, 2026
Online Assessment October 14–18, 2026
Announcement of Online Assessment Results October 26, 2026
Stage II Assessment & Interview October 27 – November 1, 2026
Scholarship Awardee Announcement November 25, 2026

Terms & Conditions

  • BCA’s decision regarding scholarship recipients is final and cannot be contested.
  • BCA reserves the right to disqualify candidates if any violations are found during the application process.

Only candidates who pass the administrative selection stage will receive an email invitation to participate in the Assessment & Interview stages.

Boost Your Skills with Bakti BCA

Scholarship recipients will receive training through Transformational Workshops, Company Visits, the Bakti Champions Movement, the National Summit, and various other engaging activities. These programs are designed to benefit not only the recipients, but also their communities, society, and the environment.

Bakti Champions will have the opportunity to further develop skills relevant to the professional world, including problem-solving, teamwork, character development, and career coaching. Outstanding Bakti Champions will also have the opportunity to participate in the National Summit, where they will gain valuable insights from BCA’s Board of Directors and distinguished speakers.

Bakti Champions Movement

As part of the Bakti Champions program, scholarship recipients take part in the Bakti Champions Movement initiative. This program encourages them to develop their skills while creating a meaningful impact on their communities.

Based on the projectbased learning approach, the program provides a platform for Bakti Champions to apply BCA’s values and develop essential competencies needed in the professional world. Through this program, BCA hopes that Bakti Champions will grow into agents of change.
