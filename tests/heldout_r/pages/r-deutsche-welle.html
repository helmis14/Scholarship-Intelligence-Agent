Tuition Fees, Financing and Scholarships

The tuition fees for the postgraduate, four-semester Master's Program will cost a total of 6,000 EUR. There is a limited number of scholarships available.

The tuition fees for the postgraduate, four-semester Master's Program will cost 6,000 EUR. Students can either pay the total sum before admission or pay monthly installments of 250 EUR. Additionally, students have to pay a semester fee to the University of Applied Sciences Bonn-Rhein-Sieg. This fee includes administrative costs, studentship fee as well as a ticket for use of public transport in the area's traffic network. The fee is subject to change. For more information, please visit their homepage.

There are different ways of financing your studies:

Scholarship

The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) offers a limited number of scholarships to up to 10 highly qualified applicants from developing countries. The scholarship covers the cost of living and accommodation. The tuition fees and the inbound flight to Bonn will also be covered. A committee will decide which applicants are to receive a scholarship after the application deadline has elapsed. Scholarship recipients will write their Master’s thesis and complete the degree in their home country. The current call for applications and necessary application documents for the scholarship for an EPOS study program can be found on the DAAD's website.

Self-finance

In addition to the abovementioned scholarship, there is the possibility to finance your own living costs (approx. 800 EUR per month), the semester fee and the tuition fees of 250 EUR per month over the course of all four semesters (6,000 EUR in total).


You will find a list of countries eligible for funding, information about the costs of living in Germany and other relevant documents in our FAQ section.
