Directorate National Council for Higher Education (NCHE)




						

					
				
			

		
		
		
	



	
		
		
		
		
		
			


				

				

					

					

						

						

							





































	

	
The Directorate is responsible for promoting a coordinated higher education system, ensuring quality higher education and enhancing students’ access to higher education.



WHAT WE DO



    
	
  • Coordinate the development of higher education to attract a knowledge-based society;
    • 
	
  • Enhance the relevance and responsiveness of higher education to national development goals;
    • 
	
  • Accredit programmes of higher education institutions;
    • 
	
  • Audit higher education institutions;
    • 
	
  • Provide advice on efficient and equitable funding for public higher education;
    • 
	
  • Oversee the implementation of funding framework for Public Higher Education Institutions.
