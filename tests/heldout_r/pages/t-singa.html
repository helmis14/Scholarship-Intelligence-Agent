Scholarships

With an A*STAR Scholarship, you’ll be at the forefront of science, supported every step of the way. Gain hands-on research experience, industry exposure, and mentorship from leading researchers who will help you shape tomorrow’s innovations. Tap into a vibrant ecosystem of talent, and benefit from developmental programmes that expand your network and sharpen both your technical and professional skills.

Undergraduate

National Science Scholarship (BS-PhD)

The National Science Scholarship (BS-PhD) is the only through-train scholarship in Singapore which provides for your undergraduate studies to PhD training. 

Application Deadline: 1 March

All are welcome to apply. Non-Singaporeans must obtain Singapore Permanent Resident status before starting their studies.

National Science Scholarship (BS-PhD)

Graduate

National Science Scholarship (PhD)

The National Science Scholarship (PhD) is awarded to outstanding individuals who wish to pursue a rewarding career in research. 

Application Deadline: 14 April and 1 October

All are welcome to apply. Non-Singaporeans must obtain Singapore Permanent Resident status before starting their studies.

National Science Scholarship (PhD)

A*STAR Graduate Scholarship 

The A*STAR Graduate Scholarship funds up to four years of PhD or EngD studies at a Singapore university, with four tracks to choose from —
• STEM • Semicon • Quantum • Computing

Application Deadline: 15 February and 10 August

All are welcome to apply. Non-Singaporeans who are not Singapore Permanent Residents will have a 3-year service commitment in Singapore-based companies.

A*STAR Graduate Scholarship 

Post Doctoral

A*STAR International Fellowship 

With the A*STAR International Fellowship, you’ll build a global network, gain overseas exposure, or commercialise your breakthroughs — through our Research and Innovation tracks.

Application Deadline: 14 April and 1 October

All are welcome to apply. Non-Singaporeans are required to have obtained Singapore citizenship prior to the commencement of the fellowship.
