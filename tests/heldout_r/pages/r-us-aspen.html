    Socrates Germany and Ukraine 2026 Seminar

    War raises some of the most difficult moral questions societies face. At a time marked by renewed geopolitical tensions and ongoing armed conflicts, a central dilemma emerges: When is the use of force justified – and what responsibilities do those involved carry once conflict begins? As technological advances – from autonomous systems to cyber capabilities – reshape warfare, these questions become even more complex, blurring established boundaries and challenging existing norms. At the same time, they reach beyond the battlefield, raising broader questions of responsibility: how should defense spending be prioritized, what ethical limits should guide research and development, and what role should the defense industry play in shaping security policy?

Fellowships

Where Personal Transformation Drives Action

AGLN Fellowship programs are the gateways to a lifelong journey of values-based leadership.

Modeled after our flagship Henry Crown Fellowship, each program selects 20-22 exceptional leaders annually to embark on a profound experience that moves them from “success to significance”. Through thought-provoking seminars, deep dialogue with diverse peers, and a commitment to act on our greatest challenges, Fellows discover new dimensions of their leadership potential while building connections that last a lifetime.

MORE ON FELLOWSHIP PROGRAMS

The Fellowship Experience

Nominations & Selection Process

Our rigorous nomination and selection process identifies leaders at a critical inflection point — accomplished individuals who have demonstrated significant success and are now ready to apply their unique talents, influence, resources, and networks toward addressing society’s most pressing challenges. 

Each Fellowship class forms a carefully curated “mosaic” of 20-22 diverse individuals whose varied experiences will challenge and inspire one another. This intentional diversity becomes the catalyst for transformation, as Fellows encounter perspectives that expand their worldview and possibilities for collaborative impact.

MORE ON BECOMING A FELLOW
Seminars

At the heart of our Fellowships is a transformational seminar arc that guides Fellows through foundational questions of values, leadership, and a good society. For over 75 years, the Aspen Institute has refined its distinctive text-based dialogue approach — a proven methodology for deep leadership transformation. Through carefully selected readings from classical and contemporary thinkers, skilled moderation, and the unique interplay of diverse perspectives, Fellows engage in conversations that challenge assumptions and expand their vision. 

Core seminars include:*

  • The Challenge of Leadership: Fellows explore diverse leadership models through the lens of Gandhi, King, and contemporary leaders, examining their own leadership choices and impact.
  • The Aspen Executive Seminar: Through classical and modern thinkers from Plato to Carson, Fellows construct their vision of a ‘good society’ while wrestling with fundamental questions of governance, economics, and social progress.
  • Leading in a Global Context: Engaging with voices like Roy, Tutu, and Shafik, Fellows confront the complexities of leadership in an interconnected world.
  • The Promise of Leadership: Fellows reflect deeply on their journey from success to significance, integrating lessons learned and charting their path toward lasting impact.

Within these trusted spaces, accomplished leaders gain the rare opportunity to step back from daily demands, examine their core values, and reimagine how they might use their leadership to address humanity’s greatest challenges. Our expertly trained Aspen Institute moderators create a space where Fellows can explore profound questions with both intellectual rigor and emotional authenticity.

*This seminar arc is tailored to each Fellowship’s context and needs based on the impact they seek to achieve. 

Commitment to Impact

Fellows embark on a leadership impact journey that begins with a fundamental question: Where should I direct my unique gifts to address what the world needs?

Through the Fellowship experience, leaders discover the intersection of their mission (what drives them), their capacity (their unique talents, resources, and influence), and the urgency (what the world needs). 

This discovery process leads Fellows to take meaningful action. Some Fellows launch new social impact ventures, some transform existing organizations, others influence policy or systems change or leverage their platforms to shift narratives, while others mobilize resources or build movements. 

As Fellows progress through their leadership journey, their approach to impact often evolves. The AGLN provides ongoing support through convening opportunities, peer learning, access to resources, and a global community that amplifies individual efforts through collective action.

This commitment to impact reflects our belief that leadership development is not a destination but a continuous journey of growth, adaptation, and ever-expanding circles of influence.
