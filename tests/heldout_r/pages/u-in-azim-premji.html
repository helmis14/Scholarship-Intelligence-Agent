Need-based Scholarships

We are committed to providing high-quality education with need-based financial aid for economically disadvantaged students

Details

At Azim Premji University, we are committed to ensuring that financial constraints do not stand in the way of a student’s education. We offer need-based scholarships each year to a limited number of students. This support can cover tuition and accommodation expenses, and in exceptional cases, food expenses as well. 

Scholarships are awarded after a thorough review of all submitted documents, in accordance with the University’s Financial Aid Policy.  As the number of applicants often exceeds the available aid, we may not be able to support all eligible candidates. 

All scholarship decisions are made at the sole discretion of the University. If any documentation is found to be incorrect or falsified, the University reserves the right to withdraw the offer of admission. 

Students with an annual family income of: 

  • Less than INR 8 lakh (for PG programmes), or 
  • Less than INR 10 lakh (for UG programmes), 
    are eligible to apply for different levels of scholarships. 

If you meet this criterion, please indicate your interest in the application form. 

Note: Requesting a scholarship does not guarantee financial support. Scholarships are awarded solely at the discretion of the University. 

We encourage you to appear for the first round of admission tests, as the scholarship process involves multiple steps, including the submission and verification of financial documents. Starting early ensures timely review and completion of all formalities. 

  1. Indicate interest: 
    While filling out the application form, select the option to be considered for a scholarship.
  2. Submission of documents: 
    If you are shortlisted for admission, you will be required to complete a detailed scholarship form and submit all supporting documents within the specified timeframe.
  3. Income details: 
    You must provide income details of all earning family members, including salary, pension, rent, agriculture, business, investments, commissions, etc.
  4. No late submissions: 
    Late or incomplete submissions will not be considered. Please ensure all documents are ready in advance.
  5. Review and assessment: 
    The University’s Scholarship Committee will review the application and may contact you for clarifications or additional information.
  6. Verification process: 
    A third-party agency will independently verify all financial details and documents submitted. Any discrepancies may lead to withdrawal of admission at any stage.
  7. Final decision: 
    All decisions regarding financial assistance are at the discretion of the University and will be final and binding. 

(Applicable for UG and PG programmes) 

  1. Father’s and mother’s bank statements (and other contributing members, if any) with last 3 years’ transactions. 
  2. Valid income certificate (if annual family income is less than INR 2.5 lakh) issued by a competent statutory authority. 
  3. Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 3 years (if income of any family member exceeds INR 2.5 lakh). 
  4. Audited Profit & Loss statements for the last 3 years (for self-employed members). 
  5. Other supporting documents such as salary slips, Form 16, medical documents, siblings’ fee receipts, etc. 

After verification, the University may request additional documentation on a case-to-case basis. 
We also conduct independent background verification to ensure scholarships are granted to deserving candidates. 
Any falsification of documents will result in withdrawal of admission. 

In certain postgraduate programmes, students with relevant work experience after graduation may apply for a tuition fee waiver, as detailed below. 

Executive MBA (Development Management) 

You may apply for a 60% tuition fee waiver if: 

  • You are currently employed in grassroots organisations, NGOs, or philanthropic institutions, and 
  • Your annual family income is INR 15 lakh or lower. 

The waiver is subject to proof of employment, verification of income, and assessment by the University regarding the nature of the organisation. 
All decisions of the University on financial assistance are final and discretionary. 

Other Postgraduate Programmes 

You may apply for a 50% tuition fee waiver if: 

  • You have more than 3 years of work experience after graduation, and 
  • Your annual family income is less than INR 15 lakh. 

The waiver is subject to proof of employment and verification of income. 

  1. Personal bank statements with last 1 year’s transactions. 
  2. Work experience certificate(s) indicating required experience for the relevant programme. 
  3. For income above INR 2.5 lakh, ITRs for the last 3 years.
