3 Advanced positions Achieved by Saudi Researchers in the Hamdan—ALECSO Award for Distinguished Educational Research at the level of the Arab World

05/01/2023
Page Content


Saudi Arabia won three advanced positions in the Hamdan-ALECSO Award for Distinguished Educational Research in the Arab World at the 25th session of the Hamdan Bin Rashid Award for Distinguished Academic Performance.
The winners include Dr. Ali bin Hassan Najmi from King Abdulaziz University in Jeddah Governorate and Dr. Mohammed bin Ali Al-Khraizi, Head of the Planning and Development Department at the Education Department in Al-Qunfudhah Governorate, along with Ms. Maryam Bint Yahiya Atif, the educational supervisor at the School Health Affairs Department at the General Administration of Education in Jazan.
This achievement is added to the previous nine positions achieved by the sons and daughters of the Kingdom last week in the categories of "distinguished student," "distinguished teacher," and "distinguished school and school administration." This brings the total number of advanced positions in the award to 12. ​
