About NORPART

NORPART is a flexible policy instrument for institutional collaboration with a long-term perspective. The overall goal of the program is to enhance the quality of Norwegian higher education and partner institutions in the Global South through academic cooperation and student mobility.

NORPART was established in 2016 and has been developed based on experiences from other schemes supporting institutional collaboration and mobility in higher education.

The Norwegian government has proposed to end support for the NORPART-programme in the 2025 National Budget. This means that only around seven projects will receive funding in the planned allocation in October, based on the NOK 35 million that was allocated to the programme for 2024.


Ongoing projects will receive funding in accordance with their agreements.

Results of the call for applications 2024

The deadline for the call for proposals for 2024 was 3. June. By the deadline, 191 applications were received. The Directorate for Higher Education and Skills allocates approximately 35 million NOK to 7 projects.

How the grant is administered

The NORPART programme is funded by the Norwegian Ministry of Education and Research and the Norwegian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and is administered by The Directorate for Higher Education and Skills (HK-dir).

Purpose

The purpose of NORPART is to contribute towards enhancing the quality of higher education in Norway and selected partner countries, through academic cooperation and mutual student mobility.

Desired impact

  • Enhanced quality of higher education in Norway and in the partner countries
  • Qualified candidates with knowledge and skills relevant for the employment market, who can contribute to solving local and global challenges.

Objectives 

To reach overall purpose and desired impact of the NORPART, the programme shall fulfil four objectives:

  • Strengthened partnerships for education and research between partner countries and Norway.
  • Increased quality and internationalisation of academic programmes at participating institutions.
  • Increased mobility of students from the partner countries to Norway, including mobility in connection with work placements.
  • Increased mobility of students from Norway to partner countries including mobility in connection with work placements.
Target groups
  • Higher education institutions in Norway and the partner countries.
  • Master’s and PhD students in Norway and in the partner countries.

See the NORPART programme document (PDF) for more information on the background and framework of the programme.

Who can apply?

  • The applicant must be an accredited Norwegian higher education institution
  • The application must include at least one partner that is an accredited higher education institution in one of the NORPART partner countries

Project story

How partnerships and student mobility help with reaching the SDG for sustainable water supply

Water and sanitation are crucial to human health, but in many parts of the world, access to these basic necessities remains a challenge. Sub-Saharan Africa is particularly affected. The ALL4WASH project aims to address this challenge by bringing together academic institutions across Africa and Europe to confront the local and global challenges of water supply and sanitation through cooperation.
