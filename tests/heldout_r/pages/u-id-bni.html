BNI OFFICER DEVELOPMENT PROGRAM (ODP) 2022

2022-05-11 09:00

Whether you are a fresh graduate or someone seeking a new challenge, BNI provides you with the opportunity to nurture your career in Officer Development Program.


As our bank continues to grow, we need more talented employees to become our future leaders. We need fresh, creative, skillful, and adventurous individuals who will lead and manage the bank’s transformation to achieve its mission in the future.


To be well prepared as our future leaders, you will be required to undergo a one-year comprehensive bankers training (you will be evaluated in the end of training).


1. ODP General Banking 2022

Entrance Requirements :

  • S1 /S2 from reputable universities with relevant field of studies with the minimum GPA of:
    - S1: 3.00 or equivalent
    - S2: 3.25 or equivalent
  • Maximum age to apply:
    - S1: 26 years old
    - S2: 28 years old
  • Proficient in English and submit your score in the application (TOEFL /IELTS), we only accept scores provided by official /authorized institutions and valid
  • The current status of applicant is not married and willing to not get married during the training program (1 year)

 

2. ODP Wholesale Banking (Global Analyst Program)

We are calling out the best talents to be BNI future leaders in wholesale banking! As one of the most reputable banks in Indonesia, we acquire new talents to join us and be Global Corporate Bankers in Wholesale Banking! We will help you kick off your career in the financial world with highly experienced bankers concentrated in Wholesale Banking.

With this opportunity, you will receive:

  • Placement as a Senior Officer at Corporate Segment/ Treasury / International Banking
  • Overseas Placement
  • Global Postgraduate Program


Entrance Requirements:

  • S1 /S2 from reputable local/international graduates from the relevant fields of studies with the minimum GPA of:
    - S1: 3.00 or equivalent
    - S2: 3.25 or equivalent
  • Maximum age to apply:
    - S1: 26 years old
    - S2: 28 years old
  • Fluent in English for both written and spoken with minimum score: TOEFL IBT 75 / IELTS 6/ TOEIC 800


3. ODP RM Commercial 2022

Entrance Requirements :

  • S1 /S2 from reputable universities with relevant field of studies with the minimum GPA of:
    - S1: 3.00 or equivalent
    - S2: 3.25 or equivalent
  • Maximum age to apply:
    - S1: 26 years old
    - S2: 28 years old
  • Proficient in English and submit your score in the application (TOEFL /IELTS), we only accept scores provided by official /authorized institutions and valid
  • The current status of applicant is not married and willing to not get married during the training program (1 year)

Specific for ODP RM Commercial :

S2 from reputable universities with relevant field of studies, or S1 with experience as Small Medium Enterprise (SME) Relationship Manager for 1 - 2 years.

 

4. ODP IT 2022

Entrance Requirements :

  • S1 /S2 from reputable universities with relevant field of studies with the minimum GPA of:
    - S1: 3.00 or equivalent
    - S2: 3.25 or equivalent
  • Maximum age to apply:
    - S1: 26 years old
    - S2: 28 years old
  • Proficient in English and submit your score in the application (TOEFL /IELTS), we only accept scores provided by official /authorized institutions and valid
  • The current status of applicant is not married and willing to not get married during the training program (1 year)

Specific for ODP IT :

  • Mastering the programming language that suits the company needs
  • Having skills in design and tools/ system development
  • Has experience in developing IT tools and systems in banking or other non-banking financial industries
