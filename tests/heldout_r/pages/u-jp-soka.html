Scholarships for International Graduate School Students

  1. HOME
  2. Graduate School of Science and Engineering
  3. Scholarships for International Graduate School Students

From the 2025 academic year, the graduate school scholarship system for international students was made a major change.       

Soka University Graduate School Academic Fees Exemption Scholarship for International Students (tuition waiver program), the Soka University Makiguchi Memorial Educational Foundation Scholarship for International Students (Master's Program only), and the International Student Teaching Assistant and Research Assistant Program were combined into one scholarship, the "Soka University Makiguchi Memorial Educational Foundation Scholarship for International Students".

Soka University Graduate School Scholarship for International Students

This scholarship is awarded to students who have passed the specific entrance examinations for which scholarship selection is conducted, and who have achieved the upper scores in the entrance examinations.

1. Application Eligibility for this Scholarship

Those who meet all of the following two requirements:

  1. Applicants who meet the qualifications for admission to the Graduate School of Science and Engineering as International Students.
  2. Applicants who apply for the specific entrance examinations for which this scholarship selection is conducted.

2. Number of Recipients and Scholarship Amount

Program Maximum Number of Recipients Amount to be paid Period of Scholarship Award
Master's degree 9 600,000 yen per year For two years
Doctoral degree 5 500,000 yen per year For three years
  • Each academic year, nine students from the Master's Program and five students from the Doctoral Program will be selected through scholarship selections conducted at the time of specific entrance examinations.
  • This scholarship is provided within the standard term of study, for two years for the Master's program and three years for the Doctoral program.
  • After enrollment in the graduate school, the scholarship will be paid in two installments, one for the spring semester and the other for the fall semester, after completing the prescribed procedures (the scholarship cannot be applied to the fees paid at the time of the enrollment procedures).

3. Entrance Examination conducting Scholarship Selection

  • The entrance examination for which the Graduate School of Science and Engineering conducts scholarship selection is the"Special Ccreening for International Students (For April Entry)".
  • Scholarship selection will also be conducted for the Special Screening for international students (For September Entry) only in the case that the number of students selected for spring admission does not meet all the quotas. 
  • Please note that the scholarship selection process is not conducted for the internal selection examinations (spring/fall), the General Selection Examinations and Special Screening for working adults and returnee students, so applicants cannot apply for the scholarship above.

4. Application and Selection Process

  • This scholarship will be awarded to the applicants with the highest grades who have passed the specific entrance examination for which this scholarship selection is conducted.

  • If you wish to apply for scholarship selection, please select whether or not you wish to be considered for scholarship selection when applying for the applicable entrance examination, and submit the documents required for scholarship selection through the web application page.

Scholarship for those who enrolled until AY2024

Master's Degree Program

Soka University runs a scholarship system for international (foreign national) students enrolled in the school in order to minimize their financial strain.
International graduate students accepted into the graduate programs can apply for:

  • Soka University Academic Fee Exemption Scholarship for International Students
  • Soka University Makiguchi Memorial Education Fund Scholarship for International Students
  • Teaching Assistantship opportunities.

*Scholarships are subject to change without advance notice.
*Please refer to the Admission Guideline for more information.

Doctoral Degree Program

Soka University runs a scholarship system for international (foreign national) students enrolled in the school in order to minimize their financial strain.
International graduate students accepted into the graduate programs can apply for:

  • Soka University Academic Fee Exemption Scholarship for International Students
  • Research Assistantship opportunities.

*Scholarships are subject to change without advance notice.
*Please refer to the Admission Guideline for more information.
