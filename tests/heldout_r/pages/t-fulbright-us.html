The Fulbright Program and Pulitzer Prizes

The Pulitzer Prizes have had a lasting influence on journalism, literature, music, and drama, as they have evolved and grown to encompass 22 different categories within these fields. Ninety-three Fulbright alumni have won a total of 98 Pulitzer Prizes since their inception in 1917, with four Fulbrighters receiving more than one, including three-time winner Edward Albee. The below list of Pulitzer Prize-winning Fulbright alumni features individual winners, but Fulbrighters have also been part of newsrooms and organizations that have won group Pulitzer Prizes. The alumni listed here are excellent examples of the Fulbright Program’s commitment to fostering opportunities and excellence in all fields of study and endeavor. We are delighted to recognize the contributions these globally-minded U.S. Fulbright alumni have made to their fields and to arts and letters around the world, and we look forward to seeing more Fulbrighters join the ranks of Pulitzer Prize winners in the years ahead.
