Where Your Research Journey Begins

At A*STAR, you’ll join a vibrant community of innovators advancing science, developing cutting-edge technologies, and improving lives.

Our people are our greatest asset. Driven by curiosity and passion, they push boundaries, spark discoveries, and turn ideas into impact. Through the A*STAR Graduate Academy, you’ll have the opportunity to study at top universities locally and abroad, explore bold ideas, and shape the future of science, Singapore, and the world.​​
