scholarships

You’ve earned it. Because capability deserves recognition.

At University of York Mumbai, strong academic performance is recognised and supported. We offer scholarships, and funding options to help with tuition fees. Available to eligible students on a merit and need-based basis, this support allows you to concentrate on your education and build your future with confidence.

York Academic Excellence Scholarship

This scholarship is available to both undergraduate and postgraduate applicants. Below are the key details you need to know.

  • Funding scope: Available across all programmes at York, whether you are pursuing a three-year undergraduate degree or a one-year postgraduate programme
  • Academic year: 2027/28 (commencing 2027)
  • Eligibility: Subject to course-specific requirements, academic performance, and personal interview
Apply now
Scholarship value

Undergraduate or postgraduate students who meet the merit criteria may be awarded up to 25% per year towards tuition fees.

Note: This scholarship continues through the duration of your course, provided you meet the qualifying academic criteria.

Scholarship Assessment

The scholarship is merit-based and awarded following a holistic assessment of your application. This includes:

  • Academic performance aligned with programme entry requirements, demonstrating outstanding academic achievement
  • Strength of your overall profile
  • Performance in the personal interview
Sustaining Your Scholarship
  • The scholarship is awarded as a tuition fee reduction and is applied at the start of each academic year of your programme.
  • To continue receiving the scholarship, you must maintain a minimum academic score of 70% each year.
Additional Information

Scholarship awards are subject to final review and verification. University of York Mumbai reserves the right to determine eligibility, revise award amounts, or update criteria, as applicable.

Frequently asked questions

Is every student eligible for a scholarship?

Yes, all students are eligible for scholarships.

Are need-based scholarships available?

In addition to merit-based scholarships, merit-cum-need-based scholarships may be available to eligible students whose annual household income is below ₹12 lakh. Applicants will be required to submit supporting documents, including proof of annual household income. Scholarships are subject to availability and are awarded at the discretion of the Admissions Committee, based on the applicant’s interview performance and academic records.

Will the scholarship continue throughout my programme?

Scholarships are reviewed annually. To be considered for continuation, you are required to maintain a minimum academic score of 70% each year.

How is the scholarship amount applied?

The awarded amount is applied as a tuition fee reduction and is adjusted proportionately across each semester of the academic year.

When will I know if I have been awarded a scholarship?

Scholarship outcomes are communicated as a part of your conditional offer letter.

What factors are considered during scholarship assessment?

Your academic performance, overall profile, and performance in the personal interview are taken into account as part of a holistic evaluation.
