Entrepreneurship helps women create reliable income sources, strengthen household stability, and build greater financial independence.
Participants learn practical business skills, including business planning, pricing, financial literacy, record keeping, and customer development.
The program supports parents, particularly women, who want to start or grow small businesses that support their families.
When women have access to income opportunities, they can better support household needs and create more stability for their children.
By providing practical business training and mentorship, the program helps women build sustainable livelihoods that strengthen their families and communities.

African Leadership is an initiative of Fuel A Fire, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in the United States. All donations are processed by Fuel A Fire and directly support African Leadership programs. Our Tax ID is EIN: 83-0425133.

We follow the "friends-of" model to collect tax-deductible donations in support of African Leadership programs. Donations can be made securely online, via wire transfer, or by check. Tax receipts are automatically issued after each successful contribution.

100% of donations go directly to community programs, including education, teacher support, fish farming projects, skills training, and community development initiatives across Africa.

Administrative and operational costs are covered separately, helping maximize the impact of donor contributions.

For more information, please email us at info@africanleadership.org

March 2026: New classroom resources distributed

Students received new exercise books, reading materials, pencils, and classroom learning resources at Khayelitsha School. The supplies will support daily lessons across multiple grade levels and help ensure students have the tools they need throughout the school year.

Teachers reported that access to updated learning materials has improved classroom participation and helped students complete assignments more effectively. The distribution was made possible through donor support and community partnerships.

Photos from the distribution day showed students receiving materials and participating in classroom activities as the new school term began.
