SCHOLARSHIPS

In response to the increasing interest in Modern Greek Studies abroad, the University of Athens will be offering scholarships for this year of 400,00 euros each. These scholarships will be awarded to the most qualified applicants, following the approval of the Selection Committee of the University of Athens.
