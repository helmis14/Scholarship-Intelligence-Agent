ASCENT SUBSIDY (Frequently Asked Questions)


01. Who is the sponsor of the subsidy, & when will applications open?
The subsidy is sponsored by the World Bank through a US$200 million grant implemented by REA, and the 2026 application window will open on 22 December 2025.

02. Who is eligible under this component?
All households and businesses in rural areas requiring a standard electricity connection must be located within 30 metres of an existing low-voltage pole.

03. When will the list of areas be known?
The list of beneficiary areas will be published on 22 December 2025, on REA and ZESCO social & digital media platforms.

04. What is the targeted number of beneficiaries?
The target number of beneficiaries is 100,000 for the year 2026 alone.

05. What is the connection fee?
All eligible beneficiaries are required to pay K300 ONLY, a 94% reduction from K4,846.
