Clarendon Scholarships

Full funding for graduate students

An Oxford University funding programme open to new graduate students with no restrictions on nationality, ordinary residence or field of study. Extends to taught programmes as well as research degrees. All applicants to whom places are offered are considered automatically. As part of the admissions process, the Law Faculty will decide which applicants to nominate to the University for a Clarendon Scholarship. 

Applicants must submit their graduate programme applications to the University of Oxford by the January application deadline.

Eligibility:Graduate Students
Special conditions:n/a
Value:Course fees
Maintenance costs
Duration:Full fee liability period
College:All
Application:No separate application
Decisions timelineClarendon Timeline

Queries: If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to contact us.
