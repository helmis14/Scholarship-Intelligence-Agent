Program Cost & Scholarships

The estimated full cost of the IMPH for 2025-2026 is roughly 33,840 ILS, equivalent to approximately $9,000 USD, *the cost of the program is subject to change depending on currency fluctuations.
Thanks to generous supporters of the Hebrew University-Hadassah Braun School of Public Health and Community Medicine’s IMPH Program, scholarships are available for selected students from low-income countries. These scholarships cover virtually all costs related to participation in the Program including tuition, housing, basic living expenses, medical insurance, social/cultural activities, administration fees, and textbooks.
Applicants are invited to contact us at ​irena.zaghmout@mail.huji.ac.il for information and inquiries regarding the International Master of Public Health (IMPH) program.
