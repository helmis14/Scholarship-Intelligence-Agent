Universities of the Russian Federation

Higher education in Russia is an investment in the future. You will be able to gain quality knowledge and lay the foundation for a successful career.

The country has centuries-old traditions of education. The first higher education institution in Russia, the Slavic Greek Latin Academy, was founded in 1687, and Moscow State University was opened by Mikhail Lomonosov in 1755. Since then, many higher education institutions have appeared in Russia. More than 40 Russian and Soviet scientists have received the Nobel Prize.

Today, more than 750 universities in 89 regions of the country are ready to welcome international students. You have a unique opportunity to come to Russia and personally connect with Russian academic traditions. Russian diplomas and academic degrees are recognized worldwide.

Preparatory faculty

A preparatory faculty helps students learn Russian to the level needed to follow lectures and study confidently at university, master essential subjects for their future degree, and adapt to a new academic environment.

Preparatory faculty profiles

Depending on their future field of study, international applicants take core and additional subjects under the programme approved by the university.

1
Natural sciences

Russian language, mathematics, chemistry, and physics.

2
Engineering and technology

Russian language, mathematics, physics, and computer science.

3
Humanities

Russian language, history, social studies, and literature.

4
Economics

Russian language, history, social studies, and mathematics.

5
Medicine and biology

Russian language, chemistry, biology, and physics.

Tuition

Preparatory faculty programmes may be available on either a tuition-paying or a tuition-free basis.

Tuition-paying

Each university sets its own tuition fee. Contact the university and meet the admission requirements for your chosen programme before applying.

Tuition-free

Tuition-free study is available through the Government of the Russian Federation quota or to applicants who are winners or prize-winners of an academic competition.

Important: A preparatory faculty programme is an additional general education programme. During this period, international students are not eligible for student benefits such as reduced public transport fares, discounts, or other support measures.

Dual degree programs

1. What is a dual degree program?

A dual degree program is a joint educational program delivered by two or more partner universities.

Students complete part of the program at a Russian university and part at a foreign or Russian partner university.

After successfully completing their studies, they receive a diploma from each university.

2. How the program works

  • The universities jointly determine the courses, learning modules, practical training and learning outcome requirements.
  • Applicants take part in a selection process under the rules of the partner universities.
  • Universities recognize study periods and results in accordance with the program.
  • After completing the studies and passing the final assessment, each university issues its own diploma.

3. Benefits for international students

1
A unified learning pathway

Partner universities coordinate courses and learning modules. Study results and periods are credited within the joint program.

2
Study in different academic environments

Students experience the teaching approaches, faculty and university environments of different countries.

3
Language practice and intercultural communication

Studying in an international environment helps students improve their foreign-language communication skills. Some programs also provide additional language training.

4
Two universities — one shared environment

Students gain access to the resources and infrastructure of both partner universities: libraries, digital resources, teaching and research spaces, and cultural and sports facilities.

5
More opportunities after graduation

Diplomas from two universities can broaden opportunities for further study and employment in different countries.
