ABOUT US

The Department of Civil Engineering, BUET, the largest department in the University, offers the very best civil engineering education in the country. It offers B. Sc. Engineering, M. Engineering, M. Sc. Engineering and Doctor of Philosophy degrees to its graduates. On an average, approximately 800 undergraduate students and 400 graduate students study civil engineering at this department.
