Our Scholarships

Championing access and representation for the world’s most impactful young leaders through One Young World scholarships.

Breadcrumb

What is a One Young World Scholarship?

One Young World scholarships open doors for extraordinary young changemakers to join the One Young World Summit and global Community, fully funded. These fully funded leadership scholarships cover travel, accommodation, and meals so your ability to make an impact is never limited by financial constraints.

Our Scholars

Our scholars demonstrate proven leadership ability, creating change in their communities. Many of our scholars are changing the world at the grassroots level, some are top-performing industry professionals, while others are paving the way politically in their countries.

Who funds One Young World scholarship programmes?

We partner with civil society organisations, governments, businesses, universities, and philanthropies to make these fully funded leadership scholarships possible — ensuring the brightest minds have the platform they deserve.

FAQs

If you have questions about the application process, take a look at our scholarship FAQs

One Young World’s annual Summit convenes the brightest young talent from every country and sector. Each year, we run a number of One Young World scholarships and grants for young leaders, making it possible for those shaping the future to attend the Summit fully funded.

Our scholarship programmes means that the best and brightest young leaders from around the world are able to attend our Summit fully funded, from beginning to end including travel, catering and accommodation, regardless of their financial circumstances. 

Most scholarships cover the costs of:

  • Access to the One Young World Summit.
  • Hotel accommodation on a shared basis for the duration of the Summit.
  • Catering which includes breakfast, lunch, and dinner for the duration of the Summit.
  • The cost of travel to and from the Summit location.

Please remember to thoroughly review your acceptance form for full details of what is included in your scholarship place.

Each scholarship programme has its own set of eligibility criteria. Please check these before applying.
