Architecture scholarships

November 28, 2023

You’ve got big plans for your future. Scholarships and grants can help you reach them. Explore undergraduate, graduate, and ARE scholarships and grants to support tuition (up to $20K), student debt, licensure, conferences, and research.

Grants and scholarships

Architects Foundation scholarships

Every year, the Architects Foundation awards thousands of dollars in architecture scholarships to high school students; to architecture students in NAAB-accredited undergraduate and graduate degree programs; to Architect Registration Exam (ARE) candidates who are working toward becoming licensed architects; and to licensed architects pursuing educational enrichment opportunities.

Opportunities include: 

  • Next Generation Scholarship | Up to $20,000
  • Jason Pettigrew Memorial ARE Scholarship | $1,910
  • Student Loan Relief Grant | $5,000
  • Richard Morris Hunt Prize | $20,000
  • Payette Sho-Ping Chin Memorial Academic Scholarship | $10,000 

 

View scholarships

AIAS scholarships

Higher education is expensive. Visit AIAS’s scholarship and fellowship page to connect with opportunities to help offset the cost. Scholarships include national, international, and regional scholarships as well as ARE scholarships, fellowships, and other opportunities

ACSA scholarships

ACSA publishes a list of scholarships and career resources for underrepresented students to help aspiring architecture students of color find networks and opportunities that help them to achieve their goals. There’s also a curated list of resources.
