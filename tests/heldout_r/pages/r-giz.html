Trainee programmes

Our trainee programmes in Germany and overseas offer an exciting environment to test your skills as well as opportunities to work all over the world.

 

  • German Development Cooperation (DC) Trainee Programme
  • Financial Management Trainee Programme (international locations)
  • Public Relations (PR) Traineeship

Kick-start your career with exciting work from day one – in the Development Cooperation Trainee Programme, the Financial Management Trainee Programme (international locations) or the Public Relations Traineeship. Whether you’re working for us in Germany or overseas, we offer an environment where you can test and honeyour skills. 

German Development Cooperation (DC) Trainee Programme

Implemented by GIZ, the DC Trainee Programme is an initiative of the German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ) for the advancement of young talent. 

It will give you insight into the complex coordination and management work integral to development cooperation. After completing the programme, you'll be ready to take on challenging technical and management roles with German and international development cooperation organisations.

Over the course of the 17-month programme you will get a hands-on introduction to many different aspects of German and multilateral development cooperation (DC). Your training starts in Germany with a one-month onboarding phase to prepare you for working abroad. 

This is followed by a foreign assignment ranging between 1 and 11 months. You will spend eight months working on a technical cooperation project in one of our partner countries and a further three months in a partner country or at the head office of another German or international DC organisation. The programme ends with a five-month assignment with the German Federal Ministry for Economic Cooperation and Development (BMZ) in Bonn or Berlin.

The prerequisite is a completed Master's degree or a comparable academic qualification. You must have completed your studies by the start of the DC trainee programme, not at the time of application. The programme is aimed at young professionals, has no age limit and is open to applicants of all nationalities. Fluent to business fluent written and spoken German and English are required for successful participation in the programme.

You should also have some experience in international cooperation, e.g. as part of an internship. Experience in a developing country and specialised knowledge of international development cooperation would be an advantage.

You will find detailed job profiles in the respective job advertisements.

You'll receive a monthly salary of EUR 2,600.

Positions for the DC trainee programme are usually advertised on our job board in September of each year. Outside of the application period, you will not find any job advertisements there. Applications can only be submitted online via the corresponding function on the job board; unfortunately, unsolicited applications cannot be considered. Multiple applications for up to three trainee positions are generally possible. Please note, however, that your professional qualifications should meet the requirements described in the job advertisements.

To apply, fill out the electronic application form and upload your cover letter, CV and any other documents you already have (e.g. certificates and references). Your cover letter and CV must be written in German. Once you have successfully submitted your application, you will receive an automatic confirmation of receipt.

The start date for the DC trainee programme is 1 July of the following year. After the application deadline, we will conduct a preliminary selection process. Only after this preliminary selection, which we will complete by December, will we contact all applicants. The selection days begin in December and end in March of the recruitment year, depending on the position.

Financial Management Trainee Programme (international locations)

Have you completed your degree? Are you looking to launch a career in business administration? 

We have the ideal programme for you. Build on your foundational knowledge with in-depth insight into the work of international financial management experts and the business administration responsibilities of heads of finance and administration at GIZ’s country offices. A trainee programme with us will enable you to supplement your theoretical knowledge with hands-on experience. 
 
The 18-month programme covers a number of assignments and areas of expertise. You will be assigned to several divisions in Germany and around the globe. 

Overview of programme stations 

Following three weeks of GIZ onboarding in Germany, you will be assigned to several different units in either Bonn or Eschborn. These assignments can last from two to eight weeks and will give you a comprehensive overview of GIZ's areas of work. You will rotate through the departments Finance, Procurement and Contracting, International Services, one of our regional departments, Legal Affairs and Insurance, Internal Audit and Compliance. 

The core of the programme is a 12-month assignment working on a project abroad. Equipped with the foundational knowledge gained in Germany, you will complete a full business administration cycle in one of our projects worldwide. While abroad, you will also work under supervision on a project specifically assigned to you. 

To help you become familiar with the financial management procedures and processes as well as the various interfaces the job involves, you will also spend around eight weeks job shadowing at the country office during your 12-month foreign assignment.

  • A Master’s degree in business administration, economics or a comparable field
  • Initial professional experience in finance, financial control, procurement or administration
  • Experience abroad through an internship is desirable; GIZ internship experience is an advantage
  • Excellent organisational and communication skills, teamwork skills, advisory skills, flexibility, dedication and intercultural sensitivity
  • Proficiency in relevant software (MS Office, especially Word and Excel) as well as knowledge of SAP
  • Business fluency in German and English; French language skills would be an advantage
  • Willingness to work in one of GIZ’s partner countries during the trainee programme and on successful completion of the programme 

Financial Management Trainee Programme (international) positions are usually advertised each May. Successful applicants are admitted into the trainee programme on 1 October of the same year. 

You can find current vacancies, on our job portal. Please submit your application in German. You can apply using the electronic application form. Please attach a CV showing relevant experience and a cover letter . You are welcome to attach further documents, but these are not required for shortlisting. You will receive an automated reply confirming receipt of your application.

You will be paid in line with GIZ’s collective bargaining agreement. During your time abroad, you will also receive a foreign service premium. 

During your trainee programme, you will receive ongoing support 

  • An introductory event
  • Get-togethers for trainees (regular social event or monthly meeting)
  • A final workshop on job shadowing in Germany
  • Staff assessment and development talks
  • A workshop during the foreign assignment
  • An end-of-traineeship workshop

On successful completion of the programme, we expect our trainees to be willing to work in one of GIZ’s partner countries.

We endeavour to recruit successful trainee programme alumni as financial managers in one of our partner countries. 

Public Relations (PR) Traineeship

Do you enjoy communications work? Are you passionate about international cooperation? Our Public Relations (PR) Traineeship is the ideal way to combine both. Over the course this 24-month traineeship, you will receive thorough training and be equipped for the communications roles of tomorrow, while exploring all areas of corporate communications.

The Corporate Communications Unit is responsible for delivering compelling communications about the company’s work, success stories, principles and values, both within the organisation and externally. During the traineeship, you will support the unit’s various sections. You will produce press releases and texts for online media, organise internal and external events and develop communication strategies. You will learn about the issues involved in ensuring a consistent corporate image, moderate social intranet communities and advise communicators around the globe. We will assign you a mentor, who will support you throughout your training.

Certified training and external insights

External training will enable you to acquire the skills needed for a PR advisory position. You will attend preparatory seminars run by an accredited examination body for the PR sector, allowing you to complete your training with a recognised industry qualification. GIZ will cover the cost of the course and examination fees. Job shadowing at a media company will give you further insight into the industry. GIZ’s press team will help you to choose a suitable media outlet. 

The majority of the training will take place in Bonn and Eschborn (near Frankfurt am Main), Germany. You will also gain insight into GIZ’s work on the ground in a partner country. 

You must have completed an undergraduate degree in a humanities, social science or business studies field. A Master’s degree is not essential, but would be an advantage. 

You are curious and passionate about trends in the communications sector. You have initial professional experience in communications – whether working for an agency, a company or a media outlet. 

You are interested in international cooperation and are ideally already familiar with some of the issues in the field. 

You have an aptitude for language, enjoy writing and are eager to immerse yourself in new topics. You should be fluent in German and have excellent English language skills; additional languages would be an advantage. 

Applications must be submitted through our job portal. We regularly advertise traineeships on the portal. The next vacancies are expected to be put out for tender at the end of 2027. In a first step, submit a CV and cover letter, along with samples of your work, via the online job portal. We regret that we cannot accept unsolicited applications. Applicants who have applied previously are welcome to reapply . It can make sense for candidates who have undergone personal and professional development to reapply.

Suitable applicants will be invited to a group selection event. This will involve interviews and tasks to test your aptitude and skills. You will receive feedback soon after the one-day event. 

The remuneration for trainees is currently EUR 2,600 per month gross. Travel expenses are covered and you may receive an accommodation allowance. In addition, you will receive a public ÖPNV-transportation subsidy

As part of the PR Traineeship, you will rotate through various sections every three months, including Communications Strategy, Internal Communications, Communications Advisory Services and Media and Public Relations. You will also learn more about local communications in Berlin and Brussels. 

The rotation sequence may vary depending on your experience. You will typically start your traineeship in Eschborn or Bonn. You will also spend three months on a work experience placement in a GIZ partner country. The company will take personal preferences into account for this element of the traineeship. Your mentor will advise you in selecting a suitable country of assignment.

 Corporate Communications sees the traineeship as an investment in the unit’s future. Accordingly, it is in the company’s interest to offer PR Traineeship alumni permanent positions. We will discuss the details and opportunities with you at the appropriate time.
