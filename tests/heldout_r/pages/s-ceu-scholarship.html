Open Society Scholarship

The Open Society Scholarship provides need-based financial aid to talented at-risk students from underprivileged family backgrounds who are deeply committed to social change. This opportunity is open to candidates applying for their first master’s degree. 

Eligible applicants’ monthly income must not exceed 1,030.49 EUR and family assets must be fully depleted. 

The scholarship provides a full tuition award, along with a monthly stipend and/or housing benefits, valued at €8,000 per academic year. This funding is designed to cover a portion of students' living expenses during their studies in Vienna (September to June). 

About the Scholarship

The Open Society Scholarship is for highly motivated students who are deeply committed to social change. We highly value candidates with a history of long-term volunteer work and civic engagement.

How to apply

Please note that applications for this scholarship will be open only to those candidates who applied in Round 1 (by October 15, 2026, deadline) for an eligible MA program and passed the first stage of the admission process.

The following programs are not eligible: Master of Science in Environmental Sciences, Policy and Management (MESPOM); Master of Arts in European Women's and Gender History (MATILDA); Master of Arts in History in the Public Sphere; Master of Arts in International Relations – New York City Track (1 Year); Master of Arts in Public Policy (Mundus MAPP); Master of Science in Business Data Analytics and AI (part-time); Master of Science in Finance, Data and AI (part-time).

Steps to be considered for the Open Society Scholarship

  1. Submit your MA application by October 15, 2026
    You must apply for an eligible MA degree program in Round 1 of the admissions process.

  2. Complete the Open Society Scholarship Application Form
    You will receive access to this application form via email once you pass the first stage of the admissions process.

    Your application will include: 

    • Personal Statement
    • References
    • Signed Declaration Statement about your personal income 
  3. Interview if shortlisted
    Shortlisted applicants will be invited to an interview.

Other Scholarships

In addition, we have the following donor funded scholarships to CEU students. At this point it is not possible to apply for these scholarships.  

In case of availability, students will be informed of the awards by Admissions or the Financial Aid Office generally as part of the acceptance offer.  

  • Dr. Elemer Hantos Scholarship:  Awarded to students from Central Europe or Latin America studying in select programs who would otherwise not have the resources to study at CEU. Undergraduate and graduate students in select programs in Economics and Business, Network Science, Legal Studies, International Relations, Political Science, and Public Policy are eligible. 
  • George Soros Leadership Fund Scholarship: Awarded to students from exceptional circumstances who embody open society ideals. 
  • Hiflylabs CEU Business Analytics Scholarship:  Covers tuition expenses for one student attending CEU’s 1 year Business Analytics MS program. 
  • Peter Hangartner Fellowship:  Awarded to the Economics MA degree student with the highest-grade point average in their class during their first year. 
  • Peter A. Nadosy Scholarship:  This scholarship is awarded annually to a Hungarian student entering a CEU MA program who demonstrates exceptional merit and financial need. 

  • Postgraduate Opportunity Scholarship: The Postgraduate Opportunity Scholarship is made possible through an endowed fund contributed by anonymous donors, with the belief that education is essential to liberty and democracy and should be accessible to any student who has the ability and commitment, regardless of financial resources. Up to nine awards will be available annually.  
    The Postgraduate Opportunity Scholarship is open to students pursuing a Master’s at CEU and who are citizens of any of the 30 eligible countries, primarily located within Central & Eastern Europe and the former Soviet Union. To be considered for this scholarship, applicants need to: 

    • Fulfill all of the application requirements of their chosen degree program. 
    • Hold citizenship from an eligible country (full list below*). 

    Beyond the formal criteria, it was the donors’ wish that any students who benefit from the scholarship fund will remember this support, and at some time in their life, help a young person in a similar way. 
    Awardees accepted for a two-year Master’s program are automatically eligible to receive continued scholarship funding, provided the previous year is completed in good standing. 

    *Eligible countries include: Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Estonia, Georgia, Hungary, Kazakhstan, Kosovo, Kyrgyzstan, Latvia, Lithuania, Macedonia, Moldova, Mongolia, Montenegro, Poland, Romania, Russian Federation, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Ukraine, and Uzbekistan.
