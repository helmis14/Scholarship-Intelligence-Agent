SAWISA

Placing Women at the Centre of Science, Technology and Innovation

The Department of Science, Technology and Innovation (DSTI) calls for applications for the 2026 South African Women in Science Awards.  The awards recognise and reward exceptional women scientists and researchers, and profile them as role models for younger women.

The awards are made to Distinguished Women Researchers and Distinguished Young Women Researchers in the categories of the Humanities and Social Sciences, the Natural (Life and Physical) and Engineering Sciences, Public Engagement with Science, and any field aligned with this year's special category, "Innovation for Impact: Women Leading Change".

Applications should be sent to sawisa@dsti.gov.za

The deadline for applications is 31 May 2026.  No late applications will be accepted.
