Building Human Capital

Indofood recognizes the importance of education in developing Indonesia’s human capital. We provide opportunities for formal and non-formal education through research grants and learning facilities.

Indofood Riset Nugraha ("IRN")
IRN is an Indofood grant awarded to undergraduates who are researching food security or food-related subjects.
Read More...
Indofood Rumah Pintar (“Rumpin”)
The Agribusiness Group has a community development program, where RUMPINs or nonformal education facilities are established in the plantation areas across Sumatra and Kalimantan.
Read More...
Vocational Program
The World Bank and International Monetary Fund (IMF) has predicted that Indonesia’s Gross Domestic Product (GDP) will be ranked fifth highest in the world by 2024. As such, the Indonesian government is bolstering competitiveness by revitalizing the Vocational Education System in Nawacita II to develop more skilled workers.
