Scholarships for international students

Swap financial stress for study success with a scholarship at Federation.

International students
Scholarships for international studentsChinese language students

We're passionate about your success at Federation, and about our global community across campuses. That's why we offer a range of scholarships just for international students.

Our scholarships help you achieve your Australian study dreams while contributing meaningfully to your field and giving back to your community.

Apply through StudyLink

You'll be automatically assessed for these seven scholarships when you submit an application to study at Federation. 
$10,000
The Paid Placement Support Guarantee provides up to $10,000 for students doing placement.
15%
The Achiever Scholarship is a 15% tuition fee discount.
50% off
The Accommodation Bursary is for students living on campus.
50% off
The VC Award Scholarship is a 50% tuition fee discount.
25% off
The Merit Scholarship is a 25% tuition fee discount.
20% off
The Partner Scholarship is for employability.life students transferring to a Federation campus.
50% off
The Vice-Chancellor's Award for VCE international students is a 50% tuition fee discount.

For other scholarships, you'll need to follow the application process (and you can apply for as many as you're eligible for).
