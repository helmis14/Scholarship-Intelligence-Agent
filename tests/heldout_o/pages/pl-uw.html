International students who study in Poland may apply for a number of scholarships. Most of them are delivered by the Polish National Agency for Academic Exchange (NAWA). Moreover, there is also a possibility of obtaining a scholarship from the University of Warsaw. Below you can find a list of scholarships for international students.
