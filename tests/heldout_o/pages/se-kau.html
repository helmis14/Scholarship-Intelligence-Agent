Scholarships

Karlstad University offers several scholarship opportunities to support high-achieving students who are required to pay tuition fees. Whether you are applying for a master’s programme or exploring other financial options, our scholarships and partner-funded programmes can help make your studies in Sweden possible.

Photo credit: Maria Obed

Explore Your Options

Karlstad University and its partners offer a range of scholarships for international students. Each scholarship has specific eligibility requirements, funding levels, and application procedures.

Applicants may apply for all scholarships for which they are eligible; however, only one scholarship can be awarded per student.

Scholarships exclusive to Karlstad University

Karlstad University offers several scholarships that are exclusive to our university.

The Karlstad University Global Scholarship and the Karlstad University EH International Programme Scholarship are awarded directly by Karlstad University. In addition, Karlstad University works in close partnership with local companies in the Värmland region to offer scholarships exclusively for our students. These scholarships are available only at Karlstad University and are designed to support outstanding international students by reducing tuition fees.

Karlstad University Global Scholarship

Provides a 50% tuition-fee reduction for outstanding students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland admitted to an eligible master’s programme at Karlstad University. Number awarded per year: 4–8 scholarships

→ Read more about the Karlstad University Global Scholarship

Karlstad University EH International Programme Scholarship

Offers a 50% tuition-fee reduction for excellent students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland admitted to an eligible STEM programme (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics) at bachelor’s or master’s level. Number awarded per year: 1 scholarship

→ Read more about the EH International Programme Scholarship

Erna Gruppen International Programme Scholarship

Offers a tuition-fee reduction of 100 000 SEK for outstanding students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland admitted to an eligible bachelor’s or master’s programme. The scholarship is awarded to students within the media and business fields, reflecting Erna Gruppen’s leading role in local media, distribution and logistics, and property management in Sweden. Number awarded per year: 1 scholarship

→ Read more about the Erna Gruppen International Programme Scholarship

Infrakraft International Programme Scholarship

Offers a tuition-fee reduction of 50 000 SEK for outstanding students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland admitted to an eligible bachelor’s or master’s programme. The scholarship is awarded to students within engineering and sustainability-oriented business fields, reflecting Infrakraft’s role as a Swedish infrastructure company delivering integrated solutions for sustainable transport networks. Number awarded per year: 1 scholarship

→ Read more about the Infrakraft International Programme Scholarship

Löfbergs International Programme Scholarship

Offers a tuition-fee reduction of 100 000 SEK for outstanding students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland admitted to an eligible bachelor’s or master’s programme. The scholarship is primarily intended for students from Colombia, reflecting Löfbergs’ strong ties to this coffee-producing country. Number awarded per year: 1 scholarship

→ Read more about the Löfbergs International Programme Scholarship

Uddeholms International Programme Scholarship

Offers a tuition-fee reduction of 100 000 SEK for outstanding students from outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland admitted to an eligible bachelor’s or master’s programme. The scholarship is awarded to students within the STEM fields (science, technology, engineering and mathematics), reflecting Uddeholms’ expertise as a leading manufacturer of tool steel for industrial applications. Number awarded per year: 1 scholarship

→ Read more about the Uddeholms International Programme Scholarship

Saab Dynamics International Programme Scholarship 

This scholarship is made possible through the generous support of Saab Dynamics. Saab Dynamics is a world-leading developer and manufacturer of advanced defense systems, built on high-tech innovation, systems integration, and customer-tailored solutions, with a shared mission: to help keep people and societies safe. The Saab Dynamics International Programme Scholarship provides a tuition-fee reduction of 100 000 SEK for outstanding students from countries outside the EU/EEA and Switzerland who are admitted to an eligible bachelor’s or master’s programme at Karlstad University.

→ Read more about the Saab Dynamics International Programme Scholarship

Funding Your Studies

Financing your education is an important part of planning for your studies in Sweden. Scholarships are one option, but they are often competitive and may not cover all costs. To ensure a well-prepared and sustainable financial plan, we encourage you to explore several additional avenues of support.

Many students combine different types of funding, such as scholarships, grants, personal savings, or education loans. Early financial planning can help you focus on your studies and make the most of your time at Karlstad University.

Other Financing Options

In addition to scholarships offered by Karlstad University and the Swedish Institute, you may find suitable opportunities through external organisations and national funding programmes. Many countries provide scholarships or study grants for students who pursue higher education abroad, while banks and government agencies may offer education loans with student-friendly terms.
