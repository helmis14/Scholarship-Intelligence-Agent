Tuition fees and scholarships

A legislation concerning tuition fees came into effect 1.1.2016 in Finland. According to the legislation, Universities and Universities of Applied Sciences are obliged to charge tuition fees for those students who are not citizens of countries of the European Union (EU) or the European Economic Area (EEA) and starting their degree studies 1 August 2017 and onwards. The legislation concerns degree programmes taught in English and leading to a bachelor's or master's degree.

There are NO tuition fees for Finnish or other EU/EEA-citizens.

On this page, you will find information on tuition fees and scholarship policy:

  • Students who have started their studies after 1 August 2025
  • Students who have started their studies between 1 August 2023 - 31 July 2025
  • Students who have started their studies before 1 August 2023
  • Students who have been admitted as a degree student from a separate application

Read also on:

  • Who is required to pay the tuition fee

Who is required to pay the tuition fee?

The fees will concern non-EU/EEA degree students who start their studies in English-taught Bachelor's and Master's degree programmes in August 2017 or after that.

The tuition fee does not apply to persons who are citizens of EU countries, in the EEA, or persons who are treated as EU citizens by agreement by the EU, or the family members of such persons.

Students who apply for a degree programme in which the studies starts in 1 August 2017 or later are required to pay tuition fees for that programme. However, if you have started the studies already earlier (e.g. in 2016), you may complete the studies (graduate) in the programme without having to pay tuition fees. The tuition fee does not apply to a student who is studying in a degree programme taught in Finnish or in Swedish.

New UK degree students who arrive in Finland after the 31st of December 2020 are liable to full tuition fee and residence permit requirements like other non-EU/EEA students.

To prove your citizenship or residency status, please attach a copy of your valid passport or national ID card where your citizenship is indicated, and/or a copy of your valid residence permit in Finland, into the online application form.

Read more about the obligation to pay the tuition fees (Studyinfo.fi)

Exempting documents

There were changes to tuition fee exemptions due to amendments to the Universities of Applied Sciences Act, which entered into force on 1 December 2024. The tuition fee exemption depends on the residence permit type and the residence permit history in Finland. 

The fees will not concern non-EU/EEA students, who have:

  • Passport or identity card to indicate the citizenship of EU/EEA/Switzerland
  • EU Blue Card in Finland
  • EU Family Member's Residence Card in Finland. You must hold the residence permit yourself, i.e. being a family member of a person who has a residence permit in an EU country does not grant you a tuition fee exemption.
  • EU residence permit for third-country citizens with long-term residence permit card in Finland (Type P-EU)
  • Permanent residence permit card in Finland, Type P permit
  • Brexit residence permit card, Type SEU = Right of residence under the withdrawal agreement, or P SEU = Right of permanent residence under the withdrawal agreement
  • a residence permit based on temporary protection in Finland
  • Continuous residence permit card in Finland (Type A permit) issued for purposes other than studies may exempt from paying tuition fees. However, the student is required to pay tuition fees if:
    • their first residence permit in Finland was granted for studying, they have to pay tuition fees, even in case they have later obtained a continuous residence permit (type A) on other grounds OR
    • they have a continuous residence permit (type A) based on family ties in Finland, and their family member originally came to Finland on a residence permit granted for studying, they are required to pay tuition fees.
    • If the continuous residence permit (type A) is based on family ties in Finland, and the family member is a Finnish citizen or is staying in Finland with a permanent residence permit, the student will be exempted from tuition fees.
    • Please notice that Jamk UAS is obligated to verify the basis of the first residence permit from Finnish Immigration Service.

The above-mentioned list concerns:

  • all new students starting their studies after 1 December 2024 AND
  • students who have begun their studies before 1 December 2024 and who have not had an exempting document before 1 December 2024.

Receiving the exempting document during the studies

Jamk UAS follows the Universities of Applied Sciences Act that entered into force on 1 December 2024. Notice, if the student receives one of the documents mentioned above at any point of the application period or later during studies, the student must submit the document to Jamk Student Services immediately. The student will be exempted from paying the tuition fees starting from the next semester at Jamk as following:

1) If you have begun your studies before 1 December 2024 and you are already exempted from paying the tuition fee: 

In order to be exempted from paying the tuition fee, the residence permit card (or any of the above cards/passport) must have been renewed and presented to the Student Services by the beginning of the semester (1.1. or 1.8.). 

Please note that based on the new law, student's obligation to pay the tuition fee will be reassessed if there is a gap between the original and renewed residence permits, and if the new residence permit is issued on different grounds than the original residence permit.

2) If you have begun your studies before 1 December 2024 and have not been exempted from paying the tuition fee before 1 December 2024:

In order to be exempted from paying the tuition fee, see the list above (Exempting documents).

The exempting document must have been granted and presented to the Student Services before the beginning of the academic semester (1.1. or 1.8.). 

3) If you have begun your studies after 1. December 2024 and have not been exempted from paying the tuition fee during admission:

In order to be exempted from paying the tuition fee, see the list above (Exempting documents).

The exempting document must have been granted and presented to the Student Services before the beginning of the academic semester (1.1. or 1.8.)

 

The tuition fee exemption is tied to the time that the exempting document is valid. If the validity of the exempting document expires and is not renewed, the full tuition fee liability will start from the academic semester following the expiration of the exempting document.

The student is responsible to inform Jamk UAS about any changes concerning the residence permit status.

If you are unsure about your situation regarding the exemptions, please contact Jamk Student Services: studentservices@jamk.fi.

Terms and conditions

By paying the tuition fee, the student commits to the rules for tuition fee and scholarship system of Jamk. The tuition fee entitles the student to enrol as an attending student in order to study and receive a degree and to use the general support services available for degree students at Jamk. Tuition fee is not paid for semesters when the student has enrolled for non-attendance.

If a student does not pay the required tuition fee by the given deadline one will lose their study entitlement. The decision shall be notified to the student and to the Finnish immigration service. If the student interrupts their studies in the middle of the academic semester, only the tuition fee for the next academic semester can be refunded.

Only tuition fee paying students are eligible to receive scholarships. If student has been exempted for paying tuition fees, scholarship can't be granted. Jamk is obligated by Finnish Tax Law to report all granted scholarships, with the minimum amount of 1000€ per year, to tax authority.

Withdrawal of study right

Jamk University of Applied Sciences will withdraw the study right:

  • if the applicant/student does not pay the tuition fee by the given deadline
  • if the applicant/student does not enroll for the academic year

The decision shall be notified to a student and to the Finnish immigration service.

Tuition fee refund

Student who will start their studies from 1 August 2026 onwards & Continuing students who have started their studies before 31 July 2025

Jamk can refund the tuition fee (payment) in the case a student’s residence permit application is declined or they interrupt the residence permit application process, consequently, a student is not able to enter Finland. In this case, Jamk needs an official decision from the Finnish immigration service of the declined residence permit decision before the tuition fee is paid back for the student.

If a student who has paid the tuition fee will not receive a residence permit to Finland, or they interrupt the residence permit application process, the tuition fee can be refunded for the student only starting from the next academic semester from providing the declined residence permit decision or a document stating that the residence permit application process has been cancelled.

  • For example: student receives a negative residence permit decision during Autumn Semester; the refund needs to be applied before December 31st by submitting the negative decision to Jamk Student Services. Only the following Spring Semester tuition fee will be refunded to the student.

Students who have started their studies between 1 August 2025 and 31 July 2026

Tuition fee refund is related to checking the original documents on campus. If the student fails to submit copies of the documents or do not present the original school documents personally by 30 September 2025 or has given false or insufficient information concerning the certificates, the study place will be cancelled and tuition fee payment refunded for the ongoing academic year.

Additional information about refunds

Note, in a case a student has already paid the tuition fee for the entire academic year, but graduates by 31 December the fee (for spring semester) is returned only if requested by the student.

If a student changes the enrollment during enrollment period from attending to non-attending, the tuition fee will be refunded by request.

The tuition fee is refunded using the same method of payment the student used when they made the payment. For example: if the method of payment has been a bank transfer, the refund must be made to the same bank account.

Refunds are processed without handling fees. Please note, that your bank may charge service fees.

Master's degrees

When to pay the tuition fee

The first academic year's tuition fee must be paid by the deadline of accepting the offered study place. If you have been accepted from waiting list, please follow the given personal schedule.

After the first academic year the tuition fee for the following whole academic year is due by 31st of July. It is also possible to pay the tuition fee one semester at a time. Scholarships: Bachelor's degrees

New scholarship programme is starting from 1 August 2025. The scholarships for tuition fee paying bachelor's degree students are granted based on Finnish language skills and can only be granted for an attending student. 

Jamk does not offer scholarship for the first year students.

After the first academic year (two semesters as an attending student) at the earliest the student can apply for a first scholarship. The requirement for the first scholarship is Finnish language skills level A2 (CEFR Basic level). The first scholarship is 4000 €.

After the second academic year (four semesters as an attending student) at the earliest the student can apply for the second scholarship. The requirement for the second scholarship is Finnish language skills level B1 (CEFR, Intermediate level). The second scholarship is 6000 €.

Only National Certificates of Language Proficiency (YKI) is accepted to prove the level of Finnish language skills.

Please note that scholarship is not a discount from the tuition fee. Tuition fee must be paid fully by the given deadline and scholarship application will be processed separately. You must apply for the scholarship before you have graduated.

Language level skills

The National Certificates of Language Proficiency (YKI) test consists of four subtests: 

  • speaking
  • writing
  • listening comprehension
  • reading comprehension. 

For the scholarship it is required to have one of the following combinations at the required level (A2 or B1):

  1. speaking and writing OR
  2. listening comprehension and writing OR
  3. reading comprehension and speaking.

Read more from Language centre's Elmo-intra page

How to apply for the scholarship

Step-by-step instructions for applying:

  1. Take the YKI-test and make sure that you have completed YKI-test successfully.
  2. Wait for your YKI certificate (NOTE! It might take two months)
  3. Apply for the scholarship by using the "Application for scholarship - Bachelor's degrees" form and attach the digital YKI-certificate. (Application form will open in August 2026)

Application can be submitted around the year. Scholarship decisions are made in October, February, March and April. Delays may occur during peak times and during holidays. If the scholarship application is approved the scholarship is paid within one month of the decision. Student will receive the decision to their personal email.

Scholarships: Master's degree

New scholarship programme is starting from 1 August 2025.

A scholarship for tuition fee paying master's degree students can be granted if the student graduates within the degree programme's recommended period of study. The scholarship is 4000 €.

The recommended period of study is

  • 2 years for 60 ECTS credits degree programmes
  • 3 years for 90 ECTS credits degree programmes

The student can apply for the scholarship at the end of the studies. It is not possible to apply for the scholarship after graduating.

How to apply for the scholarship

Step-by-step instructions for applying:

  1. Make sure that you have completed all the required studies (courses and thesis) and their grades have been marked in Peppi and you will graduate within the degree programme's recommended period of study.
  2. Submit the degree certificate application.
  3. Apply for the scholarship by using the "Application for scholarship - Master's degrees" form. 

The aim is to process the application for scholarship within approximately 1-2 weeks of the student's graduation. Delays may occur during peak times and during holidays. If the scholarship application is approved the scholarship is paid within one month of the decision. Student will receive the decision by their personal email.

Tuition fees and scholarships after the first year

In the following academic years the 40 % scholarship can be received:

  • if a student earns at least 60 credits per academic year in the bachelor’s degrees (30 credits per semester)
  • if a student earns at least 30 credits per academic year in the master's degrees

If the student has attended only for one semester, the minimum required amount of studies for the scholarship is:

  • 30 credits in bachelor's degrees
  • 15 credits in the master's degrees

The scholarship can be received only for the standard study period and only for the semesters one has enrolled for attendance. Therefore, the fee must be paid for all semesters and academic years for which the student enrolls as attending student, including any extension period.

How the credits are counted

The credits are counted cumulatively in the bachelor's and master's degrees. In bachelor's degrees a student must have 60 credits after the first year, 120 after the second and 180 after the third academic year OR at least 60 credits during the previous academic year. For example, a second year student will receive a scholarship if he has studied 80 credits during the first year and 40 credits during the second year (= 120 ECTS credits after the 2nd year).

When to pay the tuition fee

After the first academic year the tuition fee for the following whole academic year is due by 31st of July. It is also possible to pay the tuition fee one semester at a time. In this case, the tuition fee for the following autumn semester must be paid by 31 July and the following spring semester by 30 November.

  • On the 15th of August, the student's completed credits during the previous academic year will be checked.
  • If a student has completed 60 credits (30 credits in Master's degrees) or more, one is granted a scholarship and she/he does not have to pay the rest of the tuition fee*.
  • If a student has completed less than required, one needs to pay the rest of the tuition fee by the 31st of August.

Transfer students from another Finnish UAS

When a transfer student accepts a study place via the transfer process, the study right ends in the present university and starts at Jamk in the beginning of the next academic term.

The starting date of the study right in the previous University of Applied Sciences where the student is transferring from determines the applied tuition fee and scholarship regulations at Jamk.

The credits for the scholarship are counted from the student’s transcript of records of the previous UAS. The scholarship can be received only for the standard study period and only for the semesters one has enrolled for attendance. Therefore, the fee must be paid for all semesters and academic years for which the student enrolls as attending student, including any possible extension period.

Frequently asked questions

I have been admitted to a degree programme at Jamk UAS. Is it possible to defer my admission to next year, as I have difficulties paying the tuition fee by the deadline?

Deferring admission is not possible. Postponing the start of your studies is possible only by enrolling as an absent student, and as a first-year student, according to the Universities of Applied Sciences Act (L 932/2014, Section 29) that is possible only for statutory reasons. Your absence must be verified with official documents submitted to Student Services during the enrolment period. If the documentation is insufficient or not submitted on time, you cannot enroll as non-attending. In that case, you must enroll as an attending and pay the tuition fee.

For more information: Enrolment | Jamk University of Applied Sciences 

The residence permit process is taking longer than expected and I may not be able to arrive in Jyväskylä by the beginning of the academic year. Can I start my studies online while waiting for the decision?

Yes, you can and should begin your studies online while waiting for the residence permit decision. Even though you haven’t been able to arrive to Finland, you are considered as an attending student and therefore required to pay the tuition fee. Your study entitlement period has started and continues to run, regardless of your arrival in Finland. 

Please carefully follow the instructions provided in your Welcome Letter attached to your acceptance email. The possibilities for online studies vary between degree programmes, so please check the detailed information in your acceptance email. 

If I have accepted the study place but I haven’t attended classes or earned any credits, can I receive a refund for the tuition fee or defer it to the next academic year?

The tuition fee is based on attendance, not on the credits you have completed. A tuition fee refund is possible if you do not receive a residence permit for Finland or if you withdraw your residence permit application. In these cases, the tuition fee can be refunded starting from the next academic semester following either the negative residence permit decision, or an official document confirming that the residence permit application has been withdrawn. 

The refund applies only to the next semester, and the refund request must be submitted by 31 July or 31 December of the ongoing year. To apply, send the negative decision or cancellation document to Student Services. Refund requests submitted after the deadline will not be processed. Whether or not you study remotely while waiting for the residence permit decision, the refund policy remains the same. 

The tuition fee cannot be deferred to the next academic year under any circumstances. Once you have enrolled as an attending student, you are required to pay the tuition fee and are expected to start your studies either online or on campus. 

At Jamk UAS all the students are treated equally, and the fee and scholarship rules and regulations apply to all students alike.  

Is it possible to pay the tuition fee in instalments?

The tuition fee for the first academic year must be paid in one instalment. 
After the first study year, you may pay your tuition fee one semester at a time. The payment deadline for the following autumn semester is 31 July, and for the following spring semester 30 November. 

What happens if I don’t pay the tuition fee by the given deadline?

If you do not pay the tuition fee by the deadline, you will lose your right to study at Jamk UAS. If you wish to begin your studies in the next academic year, you may apply for the restoration of your study right. Please note that if you have lost your study right and you apply for a regranted study entitlement, the tuition fee may be different than the tuition fee of your admission year. If the study break is a semester or more, the current tuition fee rules and regulations will always apply at the time of restoration. For more information on restoring your study right, please contact the Student Services. 

Jamk UAS reserves the right to make changes.
