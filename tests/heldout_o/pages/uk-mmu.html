International scholarships from Manchester Met

Find out how you can get help with the cost of studying with our international scholarships.

January 2027 scholarships

We are committed to recognising high-achieving students and have a comprehensive range of scholarships  available. 

The scholarships below apply to January 2027 and September 2027 entry. Learn more about these scholarships, as well as their terms and conditions. 

January 2027 scholarships

  • Postgraduate (January 2027 entry - international applicants only)

    Postgraduate (January 2027 entry - international applicants only)

    Aimed at: 

    Postgraduate students from outside the UK, who will pay full overseas tuition fees. 

    Eligibility: 

    You must have been accepted onto a taught masters degree course, starting in January 2027. 

    See terms and conditions below for full eligibility criteria. 

    Value: 

    • £5,000 tuition fee scholarship in the first year of study for most eligible postgraduate taught courses.
    • £3,000 tuition fee scholarship in the first year of study for selected programmes.

    The scholarship value applicable to your course will be confirmed at the offer stage. You do not need to apply separately.

    How to apply: 

    We will assess your eligibility when you apply to study at Manchester Metropolitan. You do not need to apply separately. View our terms and conditions  for the January 2027 postgraduate scholarship. 

  • Business School International Postgraduate Scholarship (January 2027 entry - international applicants only)

    Business School International Postgraduate Scholarship (January 2027 entry - international applicants only)

    Aimed at: 

    Postgraduate students from outside the UK who will pay full overseas tuition fees. 

    Eligibility: 

    You must have been accepted onto a taught masters degree course, delivered by the Business School, starting in January 2027. See terms and conditions below for full eligibility criteria. 

    Value: 

    £5,000 in the first year of study.  

    How to apply: 

    We will assess your eligibility when you apply to study at Manchester Metropolitan University. You do not need to apply separately. View our terms and conditions  for the January 2027 Business School International Postgraduate Scholarship.

September 2027 scholarships

  • Undergraduate (September 2027 entry - international applicants only)

    Undergraduate (September 2027 entry - international applicants only)

    Aimed at:

    Undergraduate students from outside the UK, who will pay full overseas tuition fees.

    Eligibility:

    You must have been accepted onto a taught bachelors degree course, starting in September 2027. You cannot combine this scholarship with any other financial support from Manchester Metropolitan. 

    See terms and conditions below for full eligibility criteria. 

    Value:

    £2,000 for each year of your degree. 

    How to apply:

    We will assess your eligibility when you apply to study at Manchester Metropolitan. You do not need to apply separately. View our terms and conditions  for the September 2027 undergraduate scholarship. 

  • Business School International Postgraduate Scholarship (September 2027 entry - international applicants only)

    Business School International Postgraduate Scholarship (September 2027 entry - international applicants only)

    Aimed at: 

    Postgraduate students from outside the UK who will pay full overseas tuition fees. 

    Eligibility: 

    You must have been accepted onto a taught masters degree course, delivered by the Business School, starting in September 2027. See terms and conditions below for full eligibility criteria. 

    Value: 

    £5,000 in the first year of study.  

    How to apply: 

    We will assess your eligibility when you apply to study at Manchester Metropolitan University. You do not need to apply separately. View our terms and conditions  for the September 2027 Business School International Postgraduate Scholarship.
