🎓 Call for Applications

Academic Year 2025/2026

Full Undergraduate Scholarships under Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at the University of Rwanda

The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at the University of Rwanda is pleased to launch the call for application for Mastercard Foundation Scholarships at the University of Rwanda for the academic year 2025/2026. This scholarship opportunity is open to African students of all gender and regions with limited access to opportunities.
The scholarship is one of the approaches for achieving Young Africa Works Strategy which aims to enable 30 million young people across Africa to access dignified and fulfilling work by 2030, with a focus on young women, refugees and displaced persons, and people with disabilities. The applicants to this scholarship will have applied for admission into first year's UR academic programs for the academic year 2025/2026 which are also eligible for the scholarship by Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at UR (*See details in the attached call for scholarship application*).

Apply Now

Interested applicants are encouraged to apply online using the following link:

https://mcfsprecruitment.ur.ac.rw/ 🚀
