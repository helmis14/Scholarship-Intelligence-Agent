    Student Mobility Incoming

    If you want to break out of your comfort zone, discover a foreign country, meet new people, get to know different teaching methods and acquire new skills, you have come to the right place. Become an exchange student at the University of Innsbruck and study at our Alpine-urban campus in a highly engaging and modern academic environment while living at the heart of the mountains and making the most of the stunning surrounding nature!

    Mobil­ity pro­grammes

    Study­ing at UIBK

    Organ­is­ing your stay in Inns­bruck

    The information provided on this website is mostly destined for exchange students. If you are an international student and would like to complete an entire degree in Innsbruck, please have a look at the website of our admission department.

    Tip: Some of the websites mentioned on this website or its sub-pages are only available in German. We recommend that you download a browser such as Google Chrome with an automatic translation function (check the language settings). This will allow you to read German websites in English or other languages. Even if the translation is not perfect, it grants you access to up-to-date information.

    If you are interested in scholarships for international students, please visit the  grant database of OeAD (=Austria's Agency for Education and Internationalisation).

    The IWW Office (Internationale Wirtschaftswissenschaften = International Economic and Business Studies) is responsible for incoming students in business-related subject areas. Please find adequate information on the  IWW website.
