Who will need to pay tuition fee?

The tuition fees concern students who are not citizens of EU/EEA countries and who have started their studies in English-taught Bachelor's and Master's degree programmes in August 2017 or after that. However, students can be exempted from the tuition fee e.g. on the basis of their residence permits.

Please note! There has been changes to tuition fee exemptions due to changes to the Universities of Applied Sciences Act, taking place from 1 December 2024. More information about the changes in legislation at studyinfo.fi

From 1 December 2024 onwards in order to be exempted from the tuition fee at Metropolia, an applicant must deliver a copy of one of the following documents:

  • Passport or identity card to indicate the citizenship of EU/EEA/Switzerland *)
  • EU Blue Card in Finland
  • Continuous residence permit card in Finland (Type A permit) issued for purposes other than studies may exempt from paying tuition fees. However, they are required to pay tuition fees if:
    • their first residence permit in Finland was granted for studying, they have to pay tuition fees, even in case they have later obtained a continuous residence permit (type A) on other grounds.
    • they have a continuous residence permit (type A) based on family ties in Finland, and their family member originally came to Finland on a residence permit granted for studying, they are required to pay tuition fees.
  • EU Family Member's Residence Card in Finland
  • EU residence permit for third-country citizens with long-term residence permit card in Finland (Type P-EU)
  • Permanent residence permit card in Finland, Type P permit
  • Brexit residence permit card, Type SEU = Right of residence under the withdrawal agreement, or P SEU = Right of permanent residence under the withdrawal agreement
  • a residence permit based on temporary protection

*) Citizens of Finland are not required to provide proof of their citizenship as it will be verified from the population register.

The document exempting from the tuition fee must be valid on 1 August for studies starting in autumn term and on 1 January for studies starting in spring term. The document giving the exemption from the tuition fee must be uploaded to the online application at Studyinfo.fi. A photo taken with a mobile phone or camera is acceptable.

More information on tuition fees for Finnish higher education institutions in Studyinfo

Early Bird discount

Metropolia's Early Bird terms have been updated on 17 June 2026. The new Early Bird discount applies to those whose right to study begins on 1 August 2026 or after.

Metropolia offers an Early Bird discount to applicants accepted

  • in the Joint Application
  • to top-up programmes in the Separate Application for International Degree Programmes
  • to SAT/ACT test and GMAT/GRE test based student selection in the Separate Application for International Degree Programmes

The Early Bird discount is offered on the tuition fee of the first study year´s second academic term for applicants who accept their study places no later than 14 days after the announcement of the student selection by 3 pm (local time in Finland). The applicant must pay the tuition fee within the deadline announced by the university of applied sciences. The Early Bird discount is 1.000 € for study rights starting in autumn term 2025 or after.

The discount does not apply to the following selection methods:

  • Application on the Basis of Finnish Higher Education Studies
  • Application on the Basis of Metropolia’s Open UAS Studies
  • Transfer Application
  • Separate Application for International Degree Programmes, when the offered study place is in a programme based on Pathway studies or commissioned education studies

Payment instructions for students starting their studies

These instructions apply to new students liable to pay tuition fees, who have been admitted to Metropolia UAS through one of the following application procedures:

  • Spring Joint Application I
  • Autumn Joint Application
  • Separate Application for International Degree Programmes
  • Application on the Basis of Metropolia’s Open UAS Studies
  • Application on the Basis of Finnish Higher Education Studies
  • Transfer application

Those, who are required to pay the tuition fee, must pay the tuition fee by the deadline. The deadline of the payment of the tuition fee is two weeks (14 days) from the date on which the student accepts the study place.

Please note: If you do not pay the tuition fee or deliver a document exempting you from the tuition fee by the given deadline, you will lose your study entitlement.

If you are later able to pay the tuition fee or receive the document exempting you from the tuition fee, you may apply for the restoration of the study entitlement online in Metropolia Web payment service. Statutory application fee EUR 50.

The tuition fee is to be paid using the Flywire service portal. No invoice will be sent.

Flywire is an easy-to-use payment solution that provides international students with a streamlined payment experience, saving time and money.

Admission services will send you the link to Flywire service for the payment after the student selection has been published.

Please note:

  • Metropolia UAS does not accept other payment methods, for example cash payments.
  • The tuition fees does not include possible bank service or Flywire´s charges. Ask your bank or Flywire about the charges.

Studies start in autumn term

Students whose study entitlement begins in the autumn term must pay the annual tuition fee for the whole academic year, in one instalment. The tuition fee must be paid as a single payment. Academic year is 1 August - 31 July.

Studies start in spring term

Students whose study entitlement begins in the spring term (1 January - 31 July) are required to pay the tuition fee for the spring term. The tuition fee for the next academic term must be paid in May when registering for the academic year. For more information, please visit on Metropolia´s Student’s Guide.

Early Bird discount

The Early Bird discount is applied to the student’s second term of their first study year. New students who are eligible for the Early Bird discount and whose study entitlement begin:

  1. in the autumn term must pay the full academic year tuition fee at once and deduct the Early Bird discount from the total amount of the tuition fee.
  2. in the spring term must pay the spring term tuition fee in full, and the discount is deducted from the tuition fee for the following academic term (autumn term).

If you have any questions about tuition fees, please contact admissions [at] metropolia.fi (admissions[at]metropolia[dot]fi)
