A fully-funded 5-year program that begins with lab rotations and develops into an individually tailored curriculum and thesis guided by world-class faculty.

Research Internship

Gain firsthand experience of research and student life at OIST through a 4-6 month internship program designed for undergraduate and master's students interested in pursuing a PhD.

Application Deadline: October 15, 2026

World-class Faculty

Work alongside internationally renowned faculty members in a department-free environment that fosters cross-disciplinary innovation. With expertise spanning diverse fields, our faculty members are dedicated mentors who guide and inspire the next generation of scientists.

Cutting-edge Research Facilities and Equipment

Access state-of-the-art, shared facilities and instrumentation across disciplines, empowering you to advance your research from day one. From high-performance computing to advanced imaging and fabrication labs, OIST provides the tools to turn bold ideas into discoveries.

Internationally Competitive PhD Student Support

Benefit from comprehensive financial packages, well-being services, on-campus housing and research support for all students. OIST is committed to providing the resources and environment you need to excel in your studies and research.

KEY RESOURCES

  • Research Support
  • Student and Financial Support
  • Reasonable Accommodation

Life at OIST

Live and study on a subtropical campus surrounded by ocean and forest, with a rich array of club activities, vibrant student life, and local culture just steps away. Okinawa’s rich natural beauty and cultural heritage provide a unique backdrop for personal growth. At OIST, you will join a welcoming international community where diverse perspectives and relationships flourish.

Meet with the Admissions Team

For prospective students visiting OIST campus, we offer individualized consultations for prospective students on the admissions and application process for the OIST PhD Program and Research Internship Program. 

These consultations are intended for Bachelor’s and Master’s students who are interested in applying for the OIST PhD or Research Internship programs and wish to learn more.
