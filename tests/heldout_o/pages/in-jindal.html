Providing Vocational training to Women
Set up in 2003 near Chikabidarkallu Village especially to encourage income generation ability of the rural under privileged women.
 
Establishing and running hospitals
SJF has established many hospitals and healthcare related schemes to address the need of proper and scientific healthcare among people.
