International scholarships

You do not need to apply for an international scholarship.

(Please note, this page is not applicable for UK students)

When you apply for an eligible course and receive an offer, we automatically check whether you qualify for an international scholarship (or discount). If you are eligible, we will include the scholarship amount in your offer letter.

How it works

  • Apply for your course as normal.

  • We assess you automatically after you receive an offer.

  • If you qualify, the scholarship is shown in your offer letter.

  • The award is taken off your first-year tuition fee.

To be considered, your course application must be complete, including your personal statement and supporting documents.

Scholarship amount

  • Scholarships range from £500 to £5,000 off your first-year tuition fees.
  • The amount depends on your course.
  • Awards are made at the University’s discretion.

Courses not covered

International scholarships are not available for:

  • Short courses or exchange/study abroad programmes
  • Franchised programmes
  • Pre-Masters programmes
  • International Foundation programmes
  • Pre-sessional English programmes

Questions?

Email our International Office: international@uel.ac.uk

Other scholarships accepted by UEL

We also welcome students funded by external schemes, including:

  • Chevening Scholarships (apply through Chevening).
  • Commonwealth Scholarships Commission (apply through the Commission).

If you are unsure whether we accept a scholarship, email international@uel.ac.uk.

International scholarship FAQs

Do I need to apply separately for an international scholarship?

No. You only need to apply for your course. Once you receive an offer, we automatically assess your eligibility and confirm any award in your offer letter.

When will I find out if I have a scholarship?

After we make you an offer, and you have submitted a complete course application. If you qualify, the amount will appear in your offer letter.

How much scholarship funding could I get?

Awards range from £500 to £5,000 off your first-year tuition fee. The exact amount depends on your course.

Are all UEL courses eligible for scholarships?

Scholarships are not available for short courses, exchange/study abroad programmes, franchised programmes, Pre-Masters, International Foundation, or pre-sessional English courses.

I have funding from Chevening or another provider. Can I use it at UEL?

Yes, UEL accepts a range of external scholarships, including Chevening and Commonwealth schemes. You apply through those organisations, not through UEL. Email international@uel.ac.uk if you want us to confirm a specific award.
