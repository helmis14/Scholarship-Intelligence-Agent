Scholarships

For unabridged information on scholarships go to Scholarship regulations of the Prague University of Economics and Business.

Scholarship for Semester Abroad

1. Erasmus and Switzerland

See the complete information here.

2. Non-Erasmus (Overseas)

Students going to North and Latin America, Asia, Australia or New Zealand receive a grant from VSE Scholarship Fund. The Scholarship will be paid to student’s CZECH bank account. The bank account must be uploaded in Student’s InSiS profile in the section Bank accounts with “for trip abroad benefit pay-out” purpose.

  • The amount of the grant is 10,000 CZK per month (rounded up to half months).

 

Scholarship for Internship

Please note, a student has to apply for the scholarship before the internship begins.

1. Erasmus

After fulfilling the requirements, students receive financial support for the internship (with a minimum length of 60 days).

The amount of grant varies based on the country of the internship. The maximum period supported can be 4 months (120 days). In the case of paid internships, financial support is not provided.

Countries are divided into 3 groups based on the cost of living, and therefore the grant differs accordingly:

Group Host country Grant/month
1 Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Norway, Sweden 810 €
2 Cyprus, Estonia, Latvia, Malta, Portugal, Greece, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain 750 €
3 Bulgaria, Croatia, Hungary, Lithuania, Poland, Romania, Serbia, North Macedonia, Turkey 690 €

2. Non-Erasmus Internship  (Switzerland, Overseas)

Students who plan to undergo their internship outside the EU may apply for a grant from VŠE’s Stipend fund. The grant is a fixed amount and amounts 15,000 CZK (approx. 600 EUR). The Scholarship will be paid to the student’s CZECH bank account. The bank account must be uploaded in Student’s InSiS profile in the section Bank accounts with “for a trip abroad benefit pay-out” purpose.

Merit scholarship

The scholarship will be granted to those students of the Faculty of Business Administration who submit an application through the InSIS Contact Centre and fulfill the following conditions:

  • they are students of the 3rd or 4th semester at the Faculty of Business Administration
  • they have earned at least 59 ECTS during the last two semesters
  • their total average score for study is at least 1.3 and better
Information for the Academic Year 2026/2027

The scholarship will be granted to those students of the Faculty of Business Administration who submit an application by October 15, 2025, 12:00 pm through the InSIS Contact Centre and fulfill the following conditions:

• are students of at least the 3rd and at most the 4th semester of the master’s study program,

• achieved a GPA of up to and including 1.3 in the academic year 2025/26,

• have received at least 59 European credits (ECTS) in the academic year 2025/26.

The Dean decides on granting a merit scholarship based on a proposal from the Faculty Scholarship Commission.

The request must be submitted by October 15, 2026, at 12:00 pm through the InSIS Contact Centre.
