Our initiatives

Community is at the heart of Fundação Estudar. We identify and support Brazilian talent who find here a lifelong environment of belonging and exchange.

Líderes Estudar Fellowship Program

We offer scholarships to study in Brazil or abroad, as well as lifelong support for the personal and professional development of our Estudar Fellows.

Prep Program

Criado por Wolff Klabin, Joaquim Ribeiro, Thiago Picolo, Samuel Elia e Álvaro Piquet, o Prep Program oferece orientação personalizada para brasileiros no ensino médio que sonham fazer graduação no exterior.
