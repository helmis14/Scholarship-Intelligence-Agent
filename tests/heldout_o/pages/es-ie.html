    EXPLORE Your Options

    Use the filters below to explore the financial aid options available. Begin by selecting your academic unit — Undergraduate or Master’s — and then apply additional filters to identify scholarships and awards that match your program of interest, nationality, or country of residence. If you already know the name of a specific scholarship or award, you can enter it directly in the “Search for a financial aid” box.​

    Built to Transform People. And Through Them, the World.

    Our purpose is to foster positive change. Rooted in a belief in human potential, we exist to shape extraordinary individuals who go on to transform their communities, their industries, and the world through education, research, and innovation. Extraordinary, to us, means going beyond the ordinary: a step further in thinking, in action, and in impact.

    Extraordinary doesn't just happen. It's earned through effort, resilience, and courage. It breaks barriers, values knowledge, art, and creativity, and sees the full power of humanity. It takes action.

    To tackle the challenges of today, and drive the progress of tomorrow. That is what we ask of ourselves, and what we build together.
