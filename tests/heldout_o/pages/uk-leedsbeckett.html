International Study Centre scholarship

We offer a partial tuition fee scholarship to support your pathway programme at the Leeds Beckett University International Study Centre.

  • Scholarship availability and value may vary by region and are awarded on a first-come, first-served basis. 
  • To be considered, you must hold an offer to study at the Leeds Beckett University International Study Centre.
  • This scholarship is not available to students sponsored by a government, organisation, or company. 
  • For more information, please contact your agent representative or one of our Student Enrolment Advisors.

Progression scholarship awarded by the university

Progression scholarships will apply to LBU ISC students progressing to join Leeds Beckett University undergraduate study in September 2026 or September 2027. Students will automatically receive a £3000 per year scholarship award.

Please note: The scholarship awards are for self-funded students only. The awards are automatically awarded on degree enrolment and do not require any additional application or grade achievement during the ISC programme.

  • Be assessed as having an 'Overseas or EU' residency status
  • Be assessed to pay the overseas fee
  • Be studying the relevant course full time
  • Be actively enrolled on an eligible course
  • Be a self-funded student, who is paying the full course fee for that year (those students who have their tuition fees paid by a third-party sponsor, employer or government agency will not be eligible)
  • Studying that level of the course for the first time (i.e. not repeating any level during that year)

You will not be eligible for the scholarship if any of the following apply to you;

  • Courses or schemes where particular rates or payment arrangements exist (such as those relating to Franchise / Overseas / Partner Based delivery or any other special arrangement)
  • Part time students
  • Any repeat year
  • Any year where the student is out on placement
  • Students on Distance Learning courses
  • Those studying PGDip or PGCert programmes
  • Those studying LLM Legal Practice course
  • Those studying Doctorate of Business Administration (DBA), Doctor of Education (EdD), or Research By Published Works.

If you are in receipt of the Scholarship you may be eligible to receive another scholarship or bursary offered by the university, subject to the terms and conditions of those other schemes. Leeds Beckett University reserves the right to vary or withdraw the scholarship at its sole discretion.
