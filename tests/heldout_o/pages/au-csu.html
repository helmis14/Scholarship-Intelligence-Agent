Scholarships

Scholarships

Get a helping hand with a Charles Sturt scholarship designed to support students just like you.

Our support for international students goes beyond the classroom.

Studying in a new country is a big step and we know a little bit of financial support can go a long way.

Each scholarship is different and they aren't all about academic performance.  See the list of all scholarship opportunities for international students.

Scholarships for international students

International students that study at Charles Sturt University can apply for government-funded, externally funded and university-specific scholarships at bachelor, master and PhD level.

International Student Success Merit Scholarship

Save 30% on your tuition fees from your second session until graduation.

International Student Success Scholarship

Enjoy a 15% tuition fee reduction from your second session until graduation.

Government-funded scholarships

Explore scholarships available from the Australian Government for international students who want to study in Australia.

Australian Awards Scholarships (AAS)
Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP)

Vice-Chancellor International Excellence Scholarship

Achieve a 50% tuition fee reduction for the duration of your course.

Regional Accommodation Bursary for International Students

Get $1,000 AUD to help you move to regional Australia as an international student.

International Joint Cooperation Program Scholarship

For Joint Cooperation Program students, get a 25% tuition fee reduction for further studies at Charles Sturt.

Outbound study abroad and exchange scholarships and grants

If you are a student undertaking a Charles Sturt Global study experience outside of Australia, you may be eligible for a financial helping hand.

  • Internal scholarship opportunities
  • External scholarship opportunities
  • Vice-Chancellor Travel Grants $400 (short term)
  • Vice-Chancellor Travel Grants $1450 (exchange)
  • New Colombo Plan - Provided through the Australian Government.
  • Department of Industry
  • The Government of Brunei Darussalam Scholarship - Provided by the Government of Brunei Darussalam to support undergraduate or postgraduate study.
  • Think Swiss Research Scholarships - A scholarship for research internships.
  • Laurel-Trinidad Conservation and Research Scholarship - Participate in the African Lion Conservation Program.
  • Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarships (QECS) - Fully-funded Master’s scholarships to Commonwealth citizens to study in Fiji, Ghana, Mauritius, Papua New Guinea, Samoa, South Africa, Sri Lanka, and Vanuatu.
