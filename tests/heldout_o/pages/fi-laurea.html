Applying to Laurea

Tuition fees and scholarships

All information regarding Laurea Tuition fees and scholarships are available on this page.

  1. Home
  2. Education
  3. How to apply to Laurea
  4. Tuition Fees and Scholarships

Who has to pay a tuition fee?

At Laurea University of Applied Sciences tuition fees are charged from students whose citizenship is outside of EU/EEA/Switzerland and are registered as present in a Bachelor or Master degree programme taught in English.

However exemptions apply and therefore, prospective non-EU/EEA students intending to study in the degree programmes offered in English are advised to check their status below under “Exemptions of tuition fees”.

The Laurea degree programmes taught in Finnish language do not have tuition fees.

Tuition fees at Laurea

  • Bachelor’s degree programmes tuition fees are EUR 9500 per academic year (4750 EUR per semester). The normal duration of a Bachelor’s degree programme is 3,5 academic years.
  • Master’s degree programmes tuition fees are EUR 11 000 per academic year. (5500 EUR per semester). The normal duration of a Master’s degree programme is 1,5 -2,5 academic years.

For Laurea students who were given admission before autumn 2024 and therefore the study entitlement at Laurea started before 1st Jan 2025, the tuition fee remains as EUR 8000 per academic year(4000 EUR per semester) for Bachelor’s degree programmes and EUR 10 000 per academic year(5000 EUR per semester) for Master’s degree programmes.

Payment schedule

The tuition fee is charged when the student is registered as present for the academic year/semester. The academic year runs from 1st August to July 31st and is divided into the autumn semester (1st August – 31st December) and spring semester (1st January – 31st July).

Laurea itself may decide on the size of this fee and the arrangements for its collection.

We reserve the right to make changes.

Payment terms

The Student Agreement (pdf) describes the responsibilities of Laurea and the tuition fee paying Student. The student confirms the acceptance of the agreement by paying the tuition fee. After payment, the agreement is valid from the date of the student accepting the study place offered.

All non-EU/EEA area applicants who have not provided a document for exemption and who have been selected for admission will receive in an email notification of admission and instructions for paying the Laurea tuition fee.

The admission of a tuition fee liable student is conditional until the student’s eligibility to apply has been checked by Laurea Admissions, the student has confirmed their study place and paid the tuition fee for the first academic year.

The tuition fee is charged when the student is registered as present for the academic semester. The academic year is divided into two semesters. The autumn semester is 1st August -31st December and the spring semester is 1st January – 31st July.

First year students and transfer students must pay the whole year tuition fee by the deadline for accepting the study place. In subsequent years of study, the student can pay the tuition fee in semester-wise parts. Half of the tuition fee must be paid before the beginning of the autumn semester. Payment for the autumn semester must be completed by 31st July. The other half must be paid before the beginning of the spring semester. Payment for the spring semester must be completed before 31st December.

Students who have paid the tuition fee can be registered as present for the academic year. Students must register themselves as present or absent each academic year in compliance with the regulations set by Laurea. For tuition fee paying students the registration as present for the academic year is valid after payment of the tuition fee for the said academic year has been made unless a valid document for exemption is provided. Failure to register for the academic year results in loss of study entitlement at Laurea. In circumstances that you lose your study entitlement at Laurea, you have possibility to apply for restoring study entitlement (50 EUR processing fee).

We reserve the right to make changes.

Please notice, the tuition fee covers only tuition for the degree programme in question in Laurea UAS. It does not include Health Insurance costs or costs needed for the residence permit application process. For the residence permit, student is obliged to have sufficient funds to cover living in Finland as required by the Finnish Immigration Service. These funds are not included in the tuition fee payment.

The tuition fee does not cover other living costs or travelling costs either which admitted students need to cover themselves. Laurea UAS does not have its own student dormitories or other housing for students. Students are responsible for acquiring accommodation for the studies.

Payment instructions

The Laurea tuition fee payment is completed with Flywire payment links below. You can complete payment for the whole academic year or just the semester with a variety of payment methods available in your country. Please note that first year students must pay the whole first academic year. From second year and onwards, students can pay the tuition fee per semester.

  1. Begin your tuition fee payment by selecting your degree programme Flywire payment link in the list below.
  • Business Management, Bachelor’s degree
  • Business Information Technology, Bachelor’s degree
  • Safety, Security and Risk Management, Bachelor’s degree
  • Hospitality Management & Service Design, Bachelor’s degree
  • Nursing, Bachelor’s degree
  • Social Services, Bachelor’s degree
  • Service Innovation and Design, Master’s Degree
  • Leading Transformational Change, Master’s Degree
  • Global Health and Crisis Management, Master’s Degree
  • Technology Law, Master’s Degree
  • Development and Management of Cosmetics Expertise, Master’s Degree

2. Enter the country your payment will come from. Enter the amount you will pay.

3. Select the preferred payment method available for your country.

4. Provide the payer’s information.

5. Provide the student’s information.

6. Review and confirm the payment information. Check the progamme of study is correct.

7. Complete the payment to Flywire with the selected payment method.

If you need assistance with completing the tuition fee payment via Flywire, please go to the Flywire payment link and select the Help link found in the top right corner. Flywire offers assistance via Live Chat and telephone.

If you have other questions about tuition fees, please contact Laurea Admissions (admissions@laurea.fi).

Tuition Fee Exemptions

Please note: Changes to tuition fee exemptions took effect on 1 December 2024 due to amendments to the Universities of Applied Sciences Act. More information is available at studyinfo.fi.

Who does not have to pay tuition fees?

Students from outside EU/EEA/Switzerland do not need to pay tuition fees if they have one the following documents.

Accepted documents

You are exempt from tuition fees if you have:

  • Passport or official identity card to indicate the citizenship of EU/EEA country or Switzerland
  • EU Blue Card in Finland
  • Continuous residence permit card in Finland (Type A permit) issued for purposes other than studies may exempt from paying tuition fees. However, you are required to pay tuition fees if:
    • Your first residence permit in Finland was granted for studying, even if you later receive a type A residence permit for another reason.
    • You have a type A residence permit based on family ties, but your family member originally came to Finland with a residence permit for studies.
  • EU Family Member’s Residence Card in Finland
  • EU residence permit for third-country citizens with long-term residence permit card in Finland (Type P-EU)
  • Permanent residence permit card in Finland, Type P permit
  • Brexit residence permit card
    • SEU – Right of residence under the Withdrawal Agreement
    • P SEU – Right of permanent residence under the Withdrawal Agreement
  • a residence permit based on temporary protection

Validity of your document

Your exemption document must be valid on the following dates:

  • 1 August for studies starting in the autumn semester
  • 1 January for studies starting in the spring semester

If the document expires before these dates, it cannot be used for tuition fee exemption for the semester in question.

Please note:

  • Pending residence permit applications do not qualify for exemption.
  • Make sure you extend your residence permit well in advance.

Your document validity will be checked every academic year.

If your document expires before 31 July and you do not pay the tuition fee by 31 July, and you do not register as absent, your study right at Laurea will end on 31 July. If your study right ends due to unpaid tuition fee, you may apply for restoration of study entitlement (processing fee 50 €).

If your situation changes

If you have been admitted as a new student and your situation changes so that you are no longer required to pay tuition fees, please inform Laurea Admissions. Upload your tuition fee exemption document to your application in Studyinfo.fi Submit your document as soon as possible after receiving the admission notification, preferably before confirming your study place. Please note that admission is conditional until Admissions Services has verified all required documents.

If your situation changes later during your studies, please inform Laurea Admissions. The change will come into effect starting from the next semester.

Scholarships

You have the right to apply for a scholarship intended for students paying yearly tuition fee. Please note the scholarships mentioned below do not apply to students of tailor-made programmes.

The scholarships are provided to students applying for them provided that

  • you have completed at least 60 credits of studies that count towards your Programme in the previous academic year. The academic year runs from 1 August to 31 July. Note that in international separate application, the academic year starts from the start date of the studies and ends 12 months from start date of studies. You may apply for the scholarship based on previous year studies once per academic year.
    • Apply here
  • you have successfully completed a YKI Finnish language skills test at basic level (A2) or middle level (B2). The scholarships provided on the basis of YKI tests can each be paid only once during studies.
    • Apply here

The scholarship amount can be up to 15% of the annual tuition fee.

We reserve the right to make changes.
