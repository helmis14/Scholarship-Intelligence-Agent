Becoming an International Student at TU Graz

TU Graz offers you a wide range of English language Bachelor's, Master's and Doctoral programmes along with many opportunities for further specialisation. The university spans three campus locations with state-of-the-art laboratories and excellent infrastructure for both studying and simply spending time with fellow students. Enjoy your student life to the fullest in this great university town and soak up the incomparable atmosphere of the city!

Become part of an international study and research community with around 13,000 students from over 100 nations and gain unique experiences that will help to shape your future.

Start your studies successfully!

Your first semester in Graz starts off with the (International) Welcome Days in September. There, we will tell you all about studying at TU Graz, the services and offers available to you, benefits, etc. The aim of this event is to welcome you to campus and give you the opportunity to get to know your fellow students and teachers. Please also stop by the International House to get to know the international community at TU Graz.
