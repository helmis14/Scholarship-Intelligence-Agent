Presentation

The International MBA at emlyon business school is a leading Master of Business Administration, ranked #41 worldwide and #4 MBA in France (Global MBA Financial Times ranking 2026). Taught 100% in English and recognised by the French State (Master's Degree, RNCP level 7, n°38348), this MBA program is designed for professionals with at least 3 years of work experience who want to accelerate their career, switch industries, develop their own business, or strengthen their international leadership. 

The program is available in two flexible formats. The Full-Time MBA is an intensive 11-month experience delivered entirely on campus in Lyon. It is ideal for professionals seeking rapid career progression through an immersive learning environment, a diverse international cohort, specialised global seminars, and a 7-month consultancy project aligned with their career goals. 

The Part-Time MBA is completed over 2 years, combining online learning with on-campus residencies, allowing participants to continue working while studying. This flexible format enables professionals to apply new knowledge directly in their organisation, expand their global business perspective, and build an international network through enriching study trips—without taking a career break. 

The International MBA confers the Master's Degree and enables the validation of the DipViGrM – Diploma in Advanced Management of International Business professional certification (RNCP level 7, n°38348), awarded by emlyon business school and registered with the RNCP on 1 September 2023.

Program content

Core courses 

Core courses cover essential areas like strategy, finance, marketing, operations management, human resources and leadership. Admission

1 - Start your online application

Begin your application through our Online Application Portal, accessible from our website.

You will need to provide details about your academic and professional background, as well as your motivations for joining the MBA.

You will also be required to upload your supporting documents.

2 - Take a digital test

This consists of a series of tests that include professional case studies related to the program you are applying for and the innovative mindset of emlyon business school. You will also need to complete cognitive tests and a video exercise that will allow us to get to know you and your project better.

The digital tests require a computer and a stable Wi-Fi connection.

3 - Admission committee

Once you have completed your online application and digital test, the Admission Committee will review your application.

At this stage, four possible outcomes are:

  • Direct admission
  • Conditional admission, subject to meeting additional requirements
  • Invitation to an in-person or online interview
  • Rejection 

  • University degree (minimum Bachelor degree or equivalent)
  • At least 3 years of professional experience
  • GMAT, GRE, or TAGE MAGE score (You can send your scores directly using our codes: GMAT/MBW-FX-70 and GRE/0761)
  • English proficiency test score (TOEIC, TOEFL, IELTS, Cambridge CPE, or PTE)
  • 2 professional recommendation letters, which can be provided after your application

Please note:

You can apply even if you have not yet taken the GMAT/GRE/TAGE MAGE test. A GMAT/GRE/TAGE MAGE test waiver is possible, but only under specific conditions.

The program is eligible to VAE (Validation of Acquired Experience).

FAQ

 

Frenquently asked questions

An International MBA is a Master of Business Administration focused on global business management, combining core subjects (strategy, finance, marketing, operations) with international exposure, cross-cultural management, and multinational strategy. It prepares professionals for leadership roles in global organisations. Unlike a traditional MBA, it places stronger emphasis on international markets and cultural intelligence. The emlyon International MBA is ranked #41 worldwide (FT 2026) and is available full-time (11 months) or part-time online (2 years).

France is home to several globally ranked MBA programs holding triple accreditation (AACSB, AMBA, EQUIS). emlyon's International MBA ranks #4 in France and #41 worldwide according to the Financial Times MBA Ranking 2026 - placing it consistently in the global Top MBA programs for international students and french students , with strong career outcomes (100% employment within 6 months) and cohort diversity. 

To apply for the International MBA at emlyon business school, follow a three-step admission process: 

Start Your Online Application 

Apply through emlyon’s Online Application Portal. Submit details about your academic background, professional experience, career goals, and upload required supporting documents. 

Complete the Digital Tests 

Candidates must take online assessments, including professional case studies, cognitive tests, and a video exercise. A computer and stable internet connection are required. 

Admission Committee Review 

After submission, the committee may grant direct admission, conditional admission, invite you to an interview, or issue a rejection.  

Admission Requirements 

  • Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) 
  • Minimum 3 years of professional experience 
  • GMAT, GRE, or TAGE MAGE score (waivers possible under conditions) 
  • English proficiency test score 
  • Two professional recommendation letters 

This structured application process ensures candidates meet emlyon MBA program standards. 

The duration of emlyon business school’s International MBA (IMBA) varies by format, offering flexible program lengths (full time and part time MBA programs) 

The full-time International MBA is 100% on campus and can be completed in 11 months or 16 months. The 11-month option is an accelerated track for experienced professionals, while the 16-month option allows additional time for electives, internships, or career transitions. Both formats cover the same core curriculum and academic credit requirements. 

The part-time International MBA is a 2-year blended program, combining online learning with on-campus residential sessions. This format is designed for working professionals who want to earn an MBA without interrupting their careers. 

Overall, emlyon’s IMBA offers flexible program lengths while maintaining rigorous academic standards and global exposure. 

The tuition fees for the International MBA at emlyon business school for the 2027/2028 academic year are €52,000 for the full-time format and €47,000 for the part-time format, plus a €120 application fee. Tuition includes administration fees and lifelong membership in the emlyon alumni network. If a student extends their studies beyond the official program length, an additional €500 administration fee per year applies. 


Financial Aid and Scholarships 

emlyon offers International MBA Scholarships through a guaranteed scholarship support fund. Eligible candidates who meet specific criteria are automatically awarded financial aid. 

The total cost of MBA programs vary depending on living expenses and personal circumstances. Tuition pricing is subject to change for future academic years.

  • Higher earning potential: Many graduates see increased salary prospects and faster career progression.
