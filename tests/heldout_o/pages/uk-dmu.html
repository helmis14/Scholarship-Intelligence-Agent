International Scholarships

Information on each of our scholarships can be found below. 

 

Global Access Scholarship

We are proud to present the Global Access Scholarship for 2026/27 entry. Eligible prospective international students joining a full-time undergraduate or postgraduate degree at De Montfort University will be automatically awarded a Global Access Scholarship on meeting set conditions

Europe Access Scholarship (2027/28)

Eligible EU students can get reduced tuition fees at DMU: £11,950 a year for undergraduate study or £12,950 for postgraduate study from 2027.

Empowering Excellence Scholarship

Apply for DMU’s £8,500 Empowering Excellence Scholarship for eligible international undergraduate and postgraduate students starting in February 2027.

EU Access Scholarship

Eligible EU students can get reduced tuition fees at DMU: £12,000 a year for undergraduate study or £13,000 for postgraduate study from 2026.

Leicester Postgraduate Partnership

As part of an exciting new partnership between Leicester’s two universities, De Montfort University and University of Leicester, we are proud to offer graduates a 20% fee discount on a selection of postgraduate taught courses.

  1. Postgraduate Alumni Scholarship

    Our dedicated Postgraduate Alumni Scholarship provides De Montfort University Leicester (DMU) graduates with a tuition fee discount of 20 per cent.

    Learn more about the Postgraduate Alumni Scholarship
