Give to Goldsmiths

Transforming lives at Goldsmiths

Goldsmiths thrives thanks to our community – staff, alumni and friends like you – who come together to support students from all backgrounds to pursue an education and achieve their academic goals.

Yet, many students still face financial hardship, struggling to cover basic living expenses. Your support can ensure that no student faces hunger or homelessness.

For students from low-income families or those who are the first in their family to attend university, your contribution towards a scholarship or award could open the door to a brighter future.

Your support is vital to Goldsmiths students.

Goldsmiths is a beacon of academic excellence

Established in 1891, Goldsmiths is renowned globally for our contributions to the arts, humanities, social sciences, business, entrepreneurship, law and computing.

Goldsmiths is changing the world through:

Impactful research
Our groundbreaking research, like the Turner Prize-nominated ‘Precarious Trajectories’ by Goldsmiths Forensic Architecture, exposed human rights abuses and was used in evidence in international courts of law.
Leadership in arts education and culture
Through innovative programmes we foster talent and creativity. With 7 Turner Prize winners, 51 nominees, and numerous winners and nominees for BAFTAs, Mercury Prize, Booker Prize, Pulitzer Prize, Grammys and BRIT Awards, Goldsmiths alumni and academics continue to shape the cultural landscape.
Commitment to Equity and Justice
As the first London institution to develop a Civic University Agreement, we actively engage with our local community in Lewisham, addressing issues of poverty and supporting initiatives like Alchemy, which supports young people ‘at risk’ of exclusion from education through music.
