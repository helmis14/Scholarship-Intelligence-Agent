International student scholarships

ACU offers a range of scholarships specifically for international students. Browse our most popular scholarships below – you could be eligible for one or more to fund your studies.

 

ACU International Student Accommodation Scholarship

The ACU International Student Accommodation Scholarship supports international students with the cost of living in ACU Student Accommodation. It provides a 20% remission of rental fees in ACU Student Accommodation properties for a 12-month period.

View scholarship

ACU Executive Dean's International Scholarship

This premier merit-based scholarship offers up to 20% off annual tuition* for academically excellent international students.

Learn more

ACU Health Sciences Towards Brisbane 2032 Scholarship

This scholarship provides commencing international students with 15% off annual tuition fees at ACU’s Brisbane campus to support those enrolling in courses aligned with the sport and health workforce needs of the Brisbane 2032 Games.

Learn more

ACU Flourishing Lives International Scholarship

This scholarship guarantees a 20% tuition fee reduction* for the entire course duration for eligible students from the Pacific Islands, and Timor Leste.

Learn more

ACU Early Accept International Grant

The ACU Early Accept International Grant provides a tuition fee waiver for eligible commencing international students commencing their studies at ACU.

Learn more

ACU Global Horizons International Scholarship

This new scholarship guarantees up to 25% tuition fee reduction* for eligible international students commencing selected programs, creating greater access to ACU’s world-class learning opportunities.

Learn more

External scholarships

We are committed to your success. While we offer a range of scholarships, we also encourage you to explore other funding options. External scholarships can provide the financial support you need to thrive, along with valuable networking connections.

Learn more
