International Tuition Fees and Scholarships

Study at MTU

Tuition Fees

Programme Fee
Non-EU Undergraduate €13500 per year
Non-EU Postgraduate: MSc Data Science and Analytics, MSc Cybersecurity, MSc Artificial Intelligence €15000 per year
Non-EU Postgraduate: All other programmes €13500 per year
Non-EU Postgraduate: MA Art Therapy* €13500 per 2 year programme

*MTU Scholarships do not apply to the MA Art Therapy

Students On Programmes Of More Than 1 Year's Duration

The tuition fee (or tuition fee net of any scholarships that are granted) that applies in the first year of entry applies for all years of the programme, unless otherwise stated. This does not apply to students who are granted a deferral or those with a break of one year or more in their studies.

 

Non-EU International Student Scholarships

Scholarships details for Academic Year 2026/2027

Programme Scholarship Fee After Scholarship
Non-EU Undergraduate €2500 €11000
Non-EU Postgraduate: MSc Data Science and Analytics, MSc Cybersecurity, MSc Artificial Intelligence €3000 €12000
Non-EU Postgraduate: All other programmes €2500 €11000

Please be aware that MTU does not currently offer full scholarships. 

 

How to apply for an MTU Scholarship

  • Applications must be received on or before the dates specified. (31st May 2026 for September 2026 entry)
  • Candidates will be considered automatically for a scholarship once they have been approved for a programme at MTU.
  • Candidates must be self-funded and classified as non-EU for tuition fee purposes.
  • Successful candidates will be notified if they are awarded a scholarship on their Offer Letter.
  • Successful candidates will be required to accept their offer and pay the required deposit of €1,000 within four weeks of receiving the offer to secure their place on their chosen course and to secure the awarded scholarship.
  • The purpose of the scholarship awarded is to reduce the published non-EU tuition fee by the amount of the scholarship award. The scholarship amount is not to be disbursed directly to the successful candidate.
  • Scholarships are applicable for each year of study

 

Other International Student Scholarships

The Government of Ireland International Scholarships are awarded to high-calibre students from non-EEA countries to study in Ireland for a period of one year.
Please direct all queries to goi-ies@hea.ie 

 

The Victory Scholar Program is awarded to US Student-Athletes who play Golf or Basketball and gives them the opportunity to continue to play the sport they love, gain a Masters and contribute to communities throughout the Island of Ireland.
Please direct all queries to info@sportchangeslife.com 


Please note that scholarships do not apply to all programmes.
