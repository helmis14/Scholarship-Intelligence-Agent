Announcing the 2026 Apple Scholars in AIML

The Apple Scholars in AIML PhD fellowship program supports emerging leaders in academic research through funding as they pursue their PhDs, mentorship with Apple researchers, and internship opportunities. Scholars are nominated by their universities and are selected for the program based on their innovative research, leadership, and commitment to advancing their fields.

Now in its seventh year, the fellowship program has supported more than 120 emerging leaders in computer science and engineering at the graduate and postgraduate levels. Scholars who participated in Apple internships have co-authored more than 60 publications, resulting in acceptances at top venues around the world, including the following Spotlight papers in 2025:
