Tuition Fees and Scholarships

Turku University of Applied Sciences will charge a tuition fee in the Bachelor’s and Master’s degree programmes offered in English from students who are not citizens of countries of the European Union (EU), European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland.

The annual tuition fee for the degree programmes starting in 2026 or later:

The annual tuition fee in Turku UAS is 12 000 EUR per academic year (1 August – 31 July) for students who are not citizens of countries of the European Union (EU), European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland in the Bachelor’s and Master’s degree programmes offered in English starting in January 2026 or later. For the degree programmes that started before January 2026, the previously valid tuition fee will apply until the end of the studies.

The annual tuition fee for the degree programmes starting in 2020-2025:

The annual tuition fee in Turku UAS is 11 500 EUR per academic year (1 August – 31 July) for students who are not citizens of countries of the European Union (EU), European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland in Bachelor’s or Master’s degree programmes offered in English starting in 2020-2025.

Tuition fee does not include student housing or other living or study related costs.

The tuition fee does not concern those non-EU/EEA students who

  • study in degree programmes taught in Finnish;
  • have started their studies before autumn 2017;
  • have been accepted as transfer students in Turku UAS, started their studies in another UAS before 1 August 2017 and have not been required to pay tuition fee earlier.

Early bird discount

Early bird discount 1000 EUR for the tuition fee of the first academic year is applied for the degree programmes starting in January 2026 or later if you have been accepted in joint application, separate application or rolling admission. We offer you the early bird discount if you

  • accept the offer of admission within 14 days from receiving the acceptance email from Studyinfo; AND
  • pay the tuition fee for the first academic year within 30 days through Flywire from receiving the acceptance email.

Detailed instructions for tuition fee payment and early bird discount are given in the payment instructions sent from admissions@turkuamk.fi.

Please note that early bird discount is not offered in the following applications:

  • Transfer application
  • Renewed right to study
  • Separate application through Open UAS
  • Separate application for top-up degree programmes

Tuition fee payment

Tuition fee payment is considered a registration as an attending student for the academic year. The payment instructions are sent to accepted applicants by email from admissions@turkuamk.fi and continuing students by email from studentoffice@turkuamk.fi.

Turku UAS has partnered with Flywire to accept payments from international students worldwide. Flywire provides a secure global payment network that simplifies paying the fees from countries around the world. Payment options can include bank transfers, credit cards, e-wallets and more, typically in your home currency for most countries. To learn more, please visit Flywire.

Exemption from paying tuition fees

You are exempted from paying tuition fees if you have one of the documents below and provide a copy of it:

  • Passport or identity card to indicate the citizenship of EU/EEA/Switzerland*
  • Permanent residence permit card in Finland, Type P permit or
  • EU residence permit for third-country citizens with long-term residence card in Finland (Type P-EU) or
  • EU Blue Card in Finland or
  • EU Family Member’s Residence Card in Finland
  • Residence permit card on the basis of temporary protection in Finland
  • Brexit residence permit card, Type SEU-sopimuksen 50 artikla = Right of residence under the withdrawal agreement, or P SEU-sopimuksen 50 artikla = Right of permanent residence under the withdrawal agreement
  • Continuous residence permit card in Finland, Type A permit issued for purposes other than studying. However, you are required to pay tuition fees in the following cases:
    • If your first residence permit in Finland was granted for studying, you are required to pay tuition fees, even if you later obtained a continuous residence permit (type A) on other grounds.
    • If you have a continuous residence permit (type A) based on family ties, and your family member originally came to Finland on a residence permit granted for studying, you are also required to pay tuition fees.

*Citizens of Finland are not required to provide proof of their citizenship as it will be verified from the population register.

The decision whether you are required to pay fees or not is made based only on the Finnish Immigration Service’s residence permit decision, which is checked from the copy of your residence permit card attached to your application documents. The document must be valid on 1 August to be exempted from tuition fee on autumn semester and 1 January to be exempted from tuition fee on spring semester. The document is verified.

Notice, if you receive one of the documents mentioned above at any point of the application period or later during your studies, please provide us the document immediately. You will be exempted from paying the tuition fees starting from the next semester at Turku UAS.

Tuition fee refund policy

The tuition fee refund policy is determined in the degree regulations of Turku UAS:

  • If the student is issued a negative decision on a residence permit, the paid tuition fee is refunded. In addition, the paid tuition fee is refunded if the student is unable to start the residence permit process due to restrictions caused by official activities. Turku UAS withholds EUR 300 from the refund to cover administrative costs. 
  • If the student cannot start the studies in the first semester due to a delay in receiving the residence permit decision for the student or his/her child under 10 years of age, the paid tuition fee is refunded in connection with the payment of the tuition fee of the second semester or academic year. A proof of delay must be delivered by the final deadline to arrive for starting the studies defined by Turku UAS (the deadline is stated in the admission letter).

Please note that the tuition fee is not refunded if the student pays the tuition fee but

  • applies for and receives a cancellation of the positive residence permit decision for studies; or
  • otherwise decides on his/her own initiative to resign from Turku UAS, e.g. due to health condition or life circumstances.

Please contact us by email if you have any questions about the tuition fee refund policy: admissions@turkuamk.fi.

Scholarship programme

This scholarship programme applies to students whose right to study has started in 2021 or later.

Turku UAS grants scholarships on the basis of the national certificate of language proficiency in the Finnish language, i.e. the YKI certificate. Degree students who are required to pay tuition fees can apply for a refund for the tuition fee based on the national certificate of language proficiency in the Finnish language, i.e. the YKI certificate. Read more about the national certificate of language proficiency.

The amount of the scholarship based on the YKI certificate in the Finnish language is EUR 4 000 when the student has paid EUR 11 000 – 12 0000 tuition fee for the previous academic year and EUR 2 000 when the student has paid EUR 5000 – 6000 tuition fee for the previous academic year.

The following requirements for scholarships are minimum requirements. The student must have an active study right to apply for the scholarship. You can read the more detailed instructions on the scholarships from our intranet (requires logging in with Turku UAS user ID) or ask from the Student Office.

Requirement for the 1st scholarship:

  • accepted YKI 2 certificate at the basic level, the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages level A2
  • at least two components of language skills must meet the requirements of level 2, and the other components at least of level 1.


Requirement for the 2nd scholarship:

  • accepted YKI 3 certificate at the intermediate level, the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages level B1
  • at least two components of the language skills must meet the requirements of level 3


Requirement for the 3rd scholarship:

  • accepted YKI 4 certificate at the intermediate level, the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages level B2
  • at least two components of language skills must meet the requirements of level 4, and the other components at least of level 3


Requirement for the 4th scholarship:

  • accepted YKI 4 certificate at the intermediate level, the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages level B2
  • the target time of completing the certificate is over three years.

If the requirements for the scholarship are met, the scholarship will be refunded to the student in connection with the next tuition fee payment. If the student applies for the scholarship for the last academic year of the studies, the scholarship will be paid to the student’s bank account after the studies have ended.
