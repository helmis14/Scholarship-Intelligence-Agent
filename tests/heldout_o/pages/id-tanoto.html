Discover Our Programmes

Education

We invest in access to quality education and early childhood development by improving teacher quality, foundational literacy and numeracy, and educational management, driving sustainable, systemic change in learning environments that empower communities.

Healthcare

We catalyze funding and support medical research to address critical health challenges across the South-East Asia region, leveraging integrated healthcare ecosystems to foster innovation, enhance local research talent, and create long-term impact in public health.

Leadership Development

We focus on developing the next generation of leaders by building essential skills, fostering leadership qualities, and empowering individuals to drive sustainable development and positive change within their communities.

ECED

SIGAP

Strengthening Indonesia’s Early Generation by Accelerating Potential (SIGAP) is an initiative focused on preparing Indonesia’s future generations by ensuring that all children are developmentally on track and school-ready.

Find out more
ECED

HOPE

The Harnessing Opportunity through Parenting and Education (HOPE) program focuses on improving early childhood development in rural China.

Find out more
Basic Education

PINTAR

The Promoting Improvement to Innovate, Teach, and Reach (PINTAR) program aims to improve the quality of basic education in Indonesia, supporting the country’s goal of becoming one of the top five most improved countries for International Student Assessment (PISA) student performance rankings by 2030.

Find out more
Medical Research

Tanoto Foundation Professorships

Tanoto Foundation partners with SingHealth Duke-NUS Academic Medical Centre to establish Professorships and Research Initiatives in the areas of Diabetes, Medical Oncology and Cardiovascular Medicine.

Find out more

Research Initiatives

Research Initiatives Since 2009, Tanoto Foundation has contributed catalytic funding to support scalable healthcare and medical research initiatives.

Public Health

Stunting

Tanoto Foundation supports Indonesia’s efforts to reduce stunting through the SIGAP program, aligning with the government’s ambitious targets. At the national level, we assist in implementing key stunting prevention initiatives, while at the sub-national level, we work with provincial and district governments to drive program convergence and behavior change communication.

Find out more
Scholarships

TELADAN

Teaching Leadership, Advancing the Nation known as TELADAN – which means “role model” in Indonesian – is a leadership development program designed to equip and empower the next generation of Indonesian leaders.

Find out more
Scholarships

BEACON

Be Empowered and Active to Contribute to the Nation (BEACON), is a scholarship and leadership development program designed to prepare the next generation of leaders to make meaningful contributions to their communities and beyond in Singapore.

Find out more
Scholarships

Belt & Road

The Belt & Road Talent Development (BRTD) Program, established by Tanoto Foundation in support of China’s Belt & Road Initiative (BRI), designed to groom student talents into global leaders.

Find out more
Fellowship

Tanoto Foundation Fellowship Program

The Tanoto Foundation Fellowship Program is an exclusive, year-long opportunity for young leaders to immerse themselves in Indonesia’s education ecosystem and contribute to meaningful change.

Find out more
GCB

Government Capacity Building

In Government Capacity Building (GCB) program, Tanoto Foundation works in partnership with key ministries and agencies, including the National Institute of Public Administration (LAN), to improve the effectiveness of policymaking, enhancing the competencies of government leaders, and reinforcing institutional capacity. Through these efforts, GCB aims to advance human capital development as a foundation for Indonesia’s long-term growth.
