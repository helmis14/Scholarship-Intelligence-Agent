International Undergraduate Scholarships

We want all our students to achieve their full potential. With our undergraduate scholarship schemes, you could receive support to kick-start your BCU journey.

Undergraduate Scholarships 2026/27

BCU is offering a guaranteed scholarship of £5,000 to all self-funding international undergraduate students joining BCU on a full-time degree, starting in September 2026 or January 2027.

The scholarship will be applied as a tuition fee discount of £3,000 in year one and £2,000 in year two. 

There are no extra forms to fill in – just make your application to study at BCU and leave the rest to us.  

To be eligible for one of these scholarships, you will need to: 

  1. Be confirmed as a self-funded international fee payer 
  2. Successfully secure a place on a full-time undergraduate degree course starting in the 2026/27 academic year.
  3. Enrol at BCU and begin your studies in the 2026/27 academic year. 

Frequently asked questions:

The scholarship is available for full-time undergraduate degree courses, including the Graduate Entry LLB Law.

The following courses are excluded:  

  • Royal Birmingham Conservatoire Acting and Music courses (see details of our Music scholarships for 2026)
  • Sub-degree courses, such as foundation year entry, foundation degrees (FdA/FdSc) and HNC/HND
  • Courses delivered by BCU partners (BA Conductive Education) 
  • Fast-track/accelerated degrees
  • Part-time course
  • Professional Practice/CPD courses
  • Short courses (credit-bearing and non-credit-bearing)
  • Apprenticeships
  • Study abroad or exchange programmes
  • Pre-sessional English courses

We also offer international scholarships for postgraduate students if you are applying for a postgraduate course.

This scholarship is available for those joining Birmingham City University in September 2026 or January 2027. 

There is no need to apply. You will automatically receive the scholarship – applied as a tuition fee discount – if you enrol on an eligible course.

If you apply for, and receive an offer for, an eligible course, you will see details of the scholarship in your offer letter. If you go on to enrol with us, the discount will be applied to your tuition fee invoice after enrolment. Please note that you will still need to pay 50% of the full tuition fee prior to receiving your CAS

You can only receive one BCU scholarship. If you are eligible for more than one BCU scholarship, we will automatically award you the larger scholarship.
