Scholarships

Are you an international student planning to study at Fontys? You may be eligible for a scholarship!

Scholarships help talented students by providing financial support for their studies. Below, you can find information about different scholarships, eligibility criteria, and how to apply.

You might also be eligible for other scholarships not provided by Fontys. These can be found on Grantfinder.nl.

Already a Fontys student or staff member?

Please find scholarships opportunities following the links below.

  • Information for Fontys students (Study abroad)

Erasmus+ Programme and Scholarships

Fontys UAS participates in the Erasmus+ programme. Erasmus+ (2021-2027) is a European funding programme, providing grants for a wide range of activities.

NL Scholarship

The NL scholarship provides selected non-EEA students a partial scholarship for the first year of their studies at Fontys.
