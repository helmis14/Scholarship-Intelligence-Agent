University of Gävle Scholarship Opportunities for Prospective Fee-paying International Students

The University of Gävle offers scholarships to high performing international students who are required to pay tuition fees.

The scholarship offers a reduction of 75 % of the tuition fee. The application for the Spring semester of 2027 will be open until 1 November, 2026.

Scholarship for African Students in International Social Work

This is a scholarship for you who seek to work in the field of International Social Work, Development Studies, Eco-social Work and Green Social Work and to contribute to the development of your country and region.

The scholarship, initiated and funded by Iyabo Theresa Ogionwo Memorial Foundation, covers the entire tuition fee, for studies at Bachelor of Science in Social Work – Specialization International Social Work.

For the time being we have no information about future calls. Those of you who have applied to start your Bachelor of Science in Social Work in August 2026 are recommended to apply for the University of Gävle Scholarship for International Students. Please see more information at the top of this page.
