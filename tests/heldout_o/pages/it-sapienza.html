Scholarships for Meritorious Students from Italian and International Universities

Wanted the best - Scholarships 2026-2027 

A competition based on qualifications called "Wanted the best" is being held for the award of 85 two-year scholarships of € 3,000.00 per year (gross of the statutory withholdings paid by the student and net of those paid by the University) to students who have obtained a first degree in other Italian universities than Sapienza, with a grade between 105/110 and 110/110 cum laude, and who enrol in a Master's degree programme at Sapienza in the academic year 2026-2027.

  • Deadline: October 30, 2026;
  • Eligibility requirements are in article 2;
  • How to apply is in article 3.
