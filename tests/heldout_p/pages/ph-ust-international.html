Scholarships

SANTO TOMAS

Academic Scholarship

This scholarship, named after the Patron Saint of the University, is given to students with excellent academic performance.

SAN LORENZO RUIZ

Student Assistance Scholarship

This scholarship is given to students who are in need of financial assistance and are willing to render assistantship hours (20 to 30 hours per week) in the University.

SANTO DOMINGO DE GUZMAN

Scholarship for the Gifted

This scholarship, named after the Founder of the Order of the Preachers, is given to the students who excel in arts, music and sports.

SAN MARTIN DE PORRES

Equity Scholarship

This scholarship, named after the Dominican Patron Saint of the poor, is extended to Deserving students in need of financial assistance.
