ATTENTION: Please refer to the Immigration Updates Page for the latest information which may impact international students, scholars, faculty, and staff.

ATTENTION: ISSS has built a standalone D/S Rule Page to address the Final Rule Ending D/S and Restricting Academic Mobility.  Please see this page for the latest information about the new rules for F and J-visa holders.  NOTE: D/S RULE IS CURRENTLY PAUSED.

OFFICE HOURS – Monday – Friday, 8 am – 5 pm CDT

DROP-IN ADVISING HOURS – Monday – Thursday, 11 am – 3 pm. NO APPOINTMENT NEEDED

General advising hours are Monday through Thursday, 11 am – 3 pm via Zoom or In-Person.  Please note, drop-in advising is meant for shorter inquiries of 5 – 10 minutes. No appointment is needed for Drop-in Advising.  NOTE: No Drop-in Advising on Fridays.

Have a complicated question? Need a longer appointment? Book an appointment with ISSS.

For all other questions, email international@ua.edu or call 205-348-5402.  Please be sure to provide your full name and CWID (if known) with your inquiry.
