Investing in a Luther education means investing in the rest of your life.

The deep knowledge and practical experience that you’ll acquire here will help you enter your future profession, and the lasting friendships and rich co-curricular programs that are an inevitable part of the Luther experience will make for an unforgettable undergraduate journey.

Luther strives to make this journey affordable for all students through scholarships, work-study opportunities, loans and grants.

Regardless of where you are in your education, Luther’s Financial Aid Office provides a wide variety of resources and information. At Luther, we make it a point to be transparent in our tuition and other costs.
