About UNN

To seek Truth, teach Truth and preserve Truth.

The University of Nigeria, Nsukka (UNN), founded by Dr. Nnamdi Azikiwe and formally opened on 7 October 1960, is Nigeria’s first full-fledged indigenous and autonomous university. Modeled after the American educational system, UNN is also the first land-grant university in Africa.

Call for Abstracts: Faculty of Education 2027 Hybrid International Conference

October 16, 2026

 

The Faculty of Education, University of Nigeria, Nsukka, invites members of the university community including academics, researchers, and students to submit abstracts for the forthcoming 2027 Annual International Hybrid Conference of the Faculty of Education. Abstracts should not exceed 250 words and must be submitted in Microsoft Word format. Submissions should include the author’s full name, institutional affiliation, email address, and WhatsApp number. Please send abstracts as attachments to the Local Organizing Committee (LOC) not later than Monday, March 15, 2027 at: facultyofeducation.conference@unn.edu.ng.

THEME: Repositioning Education for a Changing World: Resilience, Wellbeing, Inclusion, and Human Capital Development
Opening Ceremony: Wednesday, March 17, 2027
Technical Session: Thursday, March 18, 2027
Further details about the conference can be found in the attached flyer.
Prof. Justina Ifeoma Ofuebe
Chairperson, LOC
