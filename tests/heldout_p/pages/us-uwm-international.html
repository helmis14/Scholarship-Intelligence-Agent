International Students

Your Compass to UWM

Explore the list of resources for prospective and current international students below.

International Student Resources

Navigate the road to success as a UWM international student. Here is everything you need to know, from applying to UWM to what life is like once you land in Milwaukee. 

Fees & Scholarships

Access info about tuition, living expenses, insurance and other fees associated with being an international student. Plus, review available scholarship and funding opportunities.

Immigration Information & Resources

Learn how to maintain your immigration status, options for working on campus, health insurance and other important information. Students also can meet with an immigration advisor. 

International Admissions Overview

Review application requirements for bachelor’s, master’s and PhD students and learn how to apply to UWM.

International Student Life

Get information about housing on and off campus, grocery store options, and how to set up a U.S. bank account.

International Student Services

Center for International Education staff are ready to help with visa services, immigration status and any other questions you may have. 

New International Students

Admitted to UWM and ready for next steps? Explore a thorough checklist of everything you need to do before and after you arrive on campus, from what to pack to how to register for classes. 

Academic Resources

As a top-ranked research university, UWM has a wide range of resources available to help you make the most of your academic journey. 

Academic Calendar

Access the UWM academic calendar to see important dates, such as the start and end of each semester, holiday breaks, final exams and graduation. 

Academic Catalog

Explore the complete list of UWM academic programs, certificates and degrees. You’ll also find faculty rosters and course descriptions. 

English Language Academy

Refine your English language skills with the English for Academic Purposes (EAP) program. These courses also count for credit toward your degree. 

Schedule of Classes

Search the comprehensive schedule of classes and plan your semester. See when and where specific courses are being offered and any needed prerequisites. 

STEM-Designated Degrees

UWM offers a robust list of STEM-designated degrees, which are a requirement for 24-month OPT extension stays for F-1 status students. 

Tutoring & Supplemental Instruction

From small group and drop-in tutoring to course-specific supplemental instruction, undergraduate students are encouraged to take advantage of the many tutoring options UWM has available. 

Writing Center

Undergraduate and graduate students can meet one-on-one with a trained tutor for free writing help in any subject. Sessions are offered in person and online. 

Helpful UWM Resources

Make sure your path to a UWM degree goes smoothly when you access additional campus resources and guidance. 

Adding or Dropping a Class

Sometimes a student needs to add, drop or change a class in a specific semester. Use this resource to make sure you’re following the correct steps to do so. 

Billing & Payments

View your tuition and fees bill, see current tuition figures and important due dates, make payments and review additional information about UWM’s billing and financial aid process.

Dean of Students

Students are encouraged to reach out to the Dean of Students office for help resolving campus conflicts, accessing emergency resources and managing their academic future at UWM.

Graduate School

The graduate student experience at UWM consists of cutting-edge research and high-quality labs combined with personal mentorship from world-class faculty. 

Off-campus Housing

Learn how to find off-campus housing and roommates, how to navigate the rental process including signing a lease and receive support through the Off-Campus Resource Center.

On-campus Housing

International students who live in on-campus housing get the convenience of being close to their classes and having a built-in community. Learn more about on-campus housing and what to expect.

Student Health & Wellness Center

Access free and low-cost services, such as physicals, immunizations and lab tests. Licensed counselors provide one-on-one and group counseling.

Virtual Tour

Get to know UWM’s campus from the convenience of home with the UWM virtual tour. See inside popular campus hubs, including the UWM Student Union and all four residence halls.
