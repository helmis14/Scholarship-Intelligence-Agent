International Students

In this section
  • Undergraduate International Students
  • Graduate International Students
  • Private (Alternative) Loan

For more than 55 years, LMU has been honored to welcome international students within our campus community. International students at LMU today represent more than 90 countries, enriching LMU’s cultural diversity and broadening LMU’s global perspective.

If you are an international student, you can learn more about Applying for Aid at LMU today by reading the information included in this section and elsewhere within our site.

  • Undergraduate International Students - All first-year applicants are automatically considered for academic scholarships.

  • Graduate International Students - Diverse and advanced Graduate degree programs are offered to international students in every college of the University. If you hold a bachelor's degree or the international equivalent, visit our Graduate Division website dedicated to prospective international graduate students.

  • Alternative/Private Student Loan - Are you an international student looking for some extra help paying for school and is not a U.S. citizen? Check out the Alternative/Private Student Loan section to see how to get a private loan so you can enjoy attending one of the best universities in California.   

  • The Rowe Fund - For 75 years, the Rowe Fund program of the Organization of American States (OAS) has granted financial aid to thousands of international students to help them complete their studies in the United States.  The Rowe Fund grants interest-free loans of up to $15,000 dollars to Latin American and Caribbean students, who have been admitted or are currently enrolled in their last two years of undergraduate, graduate, or post-graduate program at universities in the United States.  

International Community at LMU

  • Visit the International Admission website for more information about applying for admission to LMU.
  • Visit LMU’s Office of International Students and Scholars (OISS) for general information about attending LMU as an international student.

And, if you would like to contact us directly to ask questions or for general assistance, please email us at any time.
