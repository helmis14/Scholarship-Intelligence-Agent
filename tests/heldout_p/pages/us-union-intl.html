International Programs

2027 Spring Term Application Decisions

Decisions letters for 2027 spring term programs will be emailed on Friday, October 16. Accepted students will have until October 27 to commit to a program. Click here for more information.

Our International Programs Office is a hub for learning about Union's diverse study abroad opportunities. Throughout the year, we offer information sessions on how to plan and prepare for overseas studies. Your major adviser and other professors can also offer advice about studying abroad, and students share their abroad experiences at regular meetings and lunchtime talks. We also provide information and resources for international exchange students whom we welcome into our community.
