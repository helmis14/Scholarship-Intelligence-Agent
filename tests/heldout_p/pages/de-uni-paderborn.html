Shap­ing your stud­ies

A degree programme is more than just the acquisition of knowledge. It is a journey on which you can shape your own educational path according to your own ideas. No one is left to their own devices. Our wide range of support services are on hand to help you make the most of your studies.

 

Study start

When do the preliminary courses take place? Which courses do I have to take? What contact points and support services are there? Here you will find answers for a successful start to your studies.

Learn more

Study or­gan­isa­tion

Student Services provides information on re-registration, changing degree programmes/subjects, taking a leave of absence, exmatriculation or cancelling a degree programme and provides forms/applications for various purposes relating to the organisation of studies.

Learn more

Dates & dead­lines

We help you never miss a deadline again. Regardless of whether it's an application deadline for a change of subject, an overview of the lecture periods or the registration and cancellation deadlines for courses and examinations.

Learn more

"PAUL" cam­pus man­age­ment sys­tem

Students can use the "PAUL" campus management system to register/deregister for modules, courses and examinations, view their achievements, submit applications and apply for another subject area or a Master's degree programme.

PAUL

Ex­am­in­a­tions

Examination Management (PM) provides support with all examination-related questions relating to the degree programme. It provides information on examination deadlines and dates or on changes to the examination regulations. Admissions, registrations and withdrawals from examinations are processed and transcripts of records and certificates are issued.

Audit management

Ex­am­in­a­tion reg­u­la­tions, con­tact points

Search for your degree programme on the page Study programmes A-Z. On the right-hand side of the screen you will find the examination regulations and the module catalogue. At the bottom of the programme page you will find "Contact" or "The right contact point for every request". Here you can find the right contact for your enquiry.

Study programme A - Z

Re­cog­ni­tion of achieve­ments

If you want to change your degree programme/subject area or come to us from another university, you can have your previous achievements recognised at Paderborn University. This saves time and opens up new opportunities. Find out about the guidelines and procedures to organise your study time efficiently.

Recognition

Stays abroad

Stays abroad not only broaden and deepen professional, cultural and linguistic knowledge - they offer new perspectives and points of view and are an unforgettable personal experience! Internships or studying abroad are possible. In any case, it is important to inform yourself in good time!

International Office

Mas­ter's pro­gramme

In addition to Bachelor's degree programmes, we also offer a variety of interdisciplinary Master's degree programmes. Regardless of whether you have already completed your Bachelor's degree with us or come from another university.

  • Master's programme
  • Transition to the Master's programme

Study­ing with a dis­ab­il­ity

We support you with your health impairments, chronic illnesses or disabilities so that you can participate in your studies in a self-determined way. Find out about compensation for disadvantages, take part in various events, meet like-minded people or get advice from the Service Centre.

Learn more

Study­ing in the fam­ily without uni­ver­sity ex­per­i­ence

Are you the first person in your family to want to study? We can help you on your way: from tips for starting your studies to information on financing and counselling services.

Learn more

Learn­ing

Our university sees itself as a place where every student is catered for. We place particular emphasis on individualised learning and offer a wide range of learning spaces and resources. Because different types of learners need different approaches. Here you can discover and develop your own way of learning, be it in specialised learning centres, in our library, in the Centre for Language Teaching or in many other places and support options.

In addition to information about accommodation near the campus and various funding options, we help you find the right pathways. Prac­tic­al ex­per­i­ence & ca­reer entry

We actively encourage and support you in gaining valuable practical experience during your studies and help you on your way into the professional world. There are a variety of options for you, such as finding internships, exploring opportunities for part-time jobs during your studies, counselling and participating in career orientation events, preparing for a possible business start-up or planning your path to a doctorate.

Coun­selling & sup­port ser­vices

Whether you have questions about organising your studies, personal challenges or career prospects - our wide range of advisory services will accompany you on your journey and leave no question unanswered. The General Students' Committee(AStA) also offers further counselling services.

 

Central Student Counselling Service

We support prospective students in choosing a degree programme and in making their study decision. We also advise students in all phases of their studies, especially in phases of reorientation.

Learn more

International Office

We provide information about the possibilities of a stay abroad during your studies and provide information on application deadlines and procedures, internships and scholarships. We also support international students in the application process.

Learn more

Academic advisors

We provide advice on all subject-related questions and help with credit transfers. You can find the relevant contacts under "Contact" or "The right point of contact for every concern" in the study programme.

Study programme A - Z

PLAZ - Professional School of Education

We provide advice on all aspects of teacher training programmes. In addition to information on planning your studies and examinations, we also provide support during the practical phases of the teacher training programme and during the transition to the preparatory service.

Learn more

Study doubts & dropping out

We offer support in reflecting on the difficulties, determining the current situation and reorientation.

Learn more

Service centre for studying with disabilities

We advise and support students with disabilities or chronic illnesses in obtaining equal opportunities in their studies by helping them, for example, to apply for compensation for disadvantages or the needs-based implementation of stays abroad and internships.

Learn more

FamilyServiceOffice

We provide advice and support for questions and concerns regarding the compatibility of studying and caring for relatives or children.

Learn more

Psychosocial counselling

We offer counselling and support for study-related and personal problems.

Learn more
