    BINUS INTERNATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP

    The BINUS International Scholarship offers an exciting opportunity for outstanding students to pursue an international undergraduate degree with financial support of up to IDR 127.5 million. Through this scholarship, students can graduate with two degrees: a Sarjana (Indonesian degree) and an international Bachelor’s degree from BINUS partner universities worldwide.
