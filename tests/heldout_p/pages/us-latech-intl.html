We are here to support you!

Moving across the globe for your education is a big decision. We are here to make your transition as seamless as possible.

If you are interested in pursuing an undergraduate degree program at Louisiana Tech University, please refer to the checklist below. All undergraduate admission requirements needed to make an application for admission are outlined below.

Please email all required documents to international@latech.edu. Even if an official copy is sent through the testing institution, please email a PDF to our office.

Admission Requirements:

Freshman:
  1. Complete the application form online
  2.  2.5 GPA - converted to US grading scale by our admissions coordinator
  3. English Proficiency - You must have one of the following:
    1. High school curriculum taught in English
    2. SAT - EBRW score of 500+
    3. English Accuplacer - score of 250+
    4. English Proficiency Test: TOEFL iBT (4.5, 79 pre-2026), IELTS (6.5), Duolingo (100), PTE (50)*

      *please note that PTE is not currently accepted for Bulldog Scholarship
  4. English Placement - You must have one of the following:
    1. 3.0 GPA* - converted from your high school transcripts into the US grading scale by our admissions coordinator
    2. SAT - EBRW score of 500+
    3. English Accuplacer - score of 250+
    4. English Proficiency Test: TOEFL iBT (4.5, 79 pre-2026), IELTS (6.5), Duolingo (100), PTE (50)**

      *This will place you in the lowest available level for English classes. You may be asked to take a placement test in order to place higher. 
      **Please note that PTE is not currently accepted for the Bulldog Scholarship
  5. Math Placement - You must have one of the following:
    1. 3.0 GPA* - converted from your high school transcripts into the US grading scale by our admissions coordinator
    2. SAT - Math score of 510+
    3. Math Accuplacer - score of 250+

      *This will place you in the lowest available level for math classes. You may be asked to take a placement test in order to place higher.
Transfer:
  1. Complete the application form online
  2.  2.25 GPA - converted to US grading scale by our admissions coordinator
    1. If you have less than 24 credit hours at a post-secondary institution, you must also submit high school transcripts. 
    2. You must submit transcripts from each and every post-secondary institution you have attended. 
  3. English Proficiency - You must have one of the following:
    1. Passing grade in a university-level English class in the US
    2. SAT - EBRW score of 500+
    3. English Accuplacer - score of 250+
    4. English Proficiency Test: TOEFL iBT (4.5, 79 pre-2026), IELTS (6.5), Duolingo (100), PTE (50)*

      *please note that PTE is not currently accepted for Bulldog Scholarship
  4. English Placement - You must have one of the following:
    1. Passing grade in university-level English
    2. SAT - EBRW score of 500+
    3. English Accuplacer - score of 250+
    4. English Proficiency Test: TOEFL iBT (4.5, 79 pre-2026), IELTS (6.5), Duolingo (100), PTE (50)**

      *This will place you in the lowest available level for English classes. You may be asked to take a placement test in order to place higher. 
      **Please note that PTE is not currently accepted for the Bulldog Scholarship
  5. Math Placement - You must have one of the following:
    1. Passing grade in university-level math
    2. SAT - Math score of 510+
    3. Math Accuplacer - score of 250+

      *This will place you in the lowest available level for math classes. You may be asked to take a placement test in order to place higher.

Immigration Checklist:

The following items are required in order to receive your Initial I-20 form. This form is used to apply for your F-1 Student Visa. Upon your acceptance, you will receive your I-20 and instructions on how to apply for the F-1 Student Visa. For more details on the visa application process, please click here. **Please note** that we do not advise applicants to schedule a visa interview appointment prior to receiving their I-20. We cannot expedite the issuance of anyone's I-20 due to an approaching interview date.

Bank Statement or Scholarship Letter: Proof of available yearly funds to cover each academic year of study (Fall, Winter, and Spring) in the form of an official bank statement of account, government scholarship letter, or athletic scholarship letter (accounts must be accessible at any time). Sending Your Application Documents


Most admission documents can be sent as PDF attachments via email (international@latech.edu). You also have the option to send your documents by express or standard mailing options.

The OIA uses University Express Mail Service (UEMS) to ensure your express mailing needs are met. To create your account with UEMS, please click here.

Bulldog Out-of-State Scholarship

The Bulldog Scholarship is an out-of-state tuition fee waiver that will subtract approximately 50% of your total tuition costs. If eligible for this scholarship, you will be considered an in-state student for tuition purposes.

International Freshmen Requirements:
  • 2.5 GPA on a US scale (as assessed by our admissions coordinator)
  • Admission Test (you need only one):
    TOEFL 4.5 (79 pre-2026)
    IELTS 6.5
    Duolingo 105
  • PTE 58
    SAT 1130
    or ACT 23

You must meet only one of the English and only one of the Math requirements to be eligible.

English:

  • TOEFL 4.5 (79 pre-2026)
  • IELTS band 6.5
  • Duolingo English Test 105
  • PTE 58
  • SAT English subscore 500
  • ACT 23
  • English Accuplacer 250
  • or cumulative high school GPA of 3.0 as evaluated by our admissions coordinator (as of Fall 2024 only)

Math:

  • SAT math subscore 510
  • Math Accuplacer 250
  • or cumulative high school GPA of 3.0 as evaluated by our admissions coordinator

Note: Check your application portal to accept the scholarship. You must then also sign and upload the terms and conditions agreement. This document will be available in your portal. 

*You must maintain an overall quarterly GPA of 2.0 in order to keep the Bulldog Scholarship.

Transfer/Currently Enrolled International Students

Requirements:

  • Completed 12 hours of credit from Louisiana Tech University or an accredited school
  • Earned a cumulative GPA of 2.75
  • Have no need for remedial course work 

*You must maintain an overall quarterly GPA of 2.0 in order to keep the Bulldog Scholarship. 

Graduate Students

International graduate students are not eligible for the Bulldog Scholarship. However, they may apply for graduate assistantships, which will also waive out-of-state tuition and fees.
