ACADEMIC SCHOLARSHIPS

All full-time freshmen applicants are automatically considered for merit scholarships when they apply for admission. Each student is eligible for one academic/merit scholarship.

Curious what you might receive?
Use our Merit Scholarship Calculator to get an estimate in less than a minute—no commitment, no pressure.

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

 

PRESIDENTIAL SCHOLARSHIP
  • For full-time students with a strong academic record (90+ GPA), honors/AP coursework, and community involvement.
  • SAT/ACT optional, but 1160+ SAT or 25+ ACT if submitted.
THE FRANCISCAN SCHOLARSHIP
  • For full-time students with solid academics (87+ GPA) and a strong record of involvement.
  • SAT/ACT optional, but 1120+ SAT or 24+ ACT if submitted.

THE BISHOPS SCHOLARSHIP 

Awards $3,000 annually to full-time students who gain admission to Siena from the following high schools within the Roman Catholic Diocese of Albany: Bishop Maginn High School, Catholic Central High School, Notre Dame-Bishop Gibbons Middle and High School, Saratoga Central Catholic High School, The Academy of the Holy Names, Christian Brothers Academy and La Salle Institute.

THE SIENA GRANT

Based on FAFSA and academic performance. Award amounts vary based on financial need.

ENDOWED SCHOLARSHIPS

Siena offers over 330 donor-funded scholarships. To be considered, email aid@siena.edu (include your résumé if you’d like us to match you with the best fit!).

TRANSFER STUDENT SCHOLARSHIPS

Siena recognizes your hard work. We offer merit scholarships based on GPA:

  • Phi Theta Scholarship – For members of the Phi Theta Kappa Honor Society
  • Franciscan Transfer Scholarship – 3.2 GPA and higher
  • St. Claire Scholarship – GPA of 3.0 to 3.19
  • Mission Scholarship – GPA below 3.0

Each student is eligible for one transfer merit award. Need-based aid and endowed scholarships are also available for FAFSA filers.

GRADUATE STUDENTS

Affordability: While Siena does not offer scholarships for graduate programs, we pride ourselves on competitive tuition rates. Most graduate students offset costs through:
  • Federal and state financial aid
  • Employer tuition benefits
  • Course waivers (where applicable)
  • Grants for educators (such as New York State funding)

Learn more on the graduate financial aid page.

Best Value. Proven Return.

Siena is consistently ranked a Best Value Institution—but what does that mean?

It means:

  • You’ll earn your degree on time.
  • You’ll graduate with experience that matters—like internships and research.
  • You’ll launch your career quickly. Ranked #1 in NY for job placement three years in a row.
