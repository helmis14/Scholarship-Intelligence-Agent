Steps to Becoming an Online Student

STEP 1

Find Your Program

LSU Online provides access to nationally ranked programs from the LSU family of Institutions (LSU, LSUA, LSUE, LSUS, LSU HSC-NOLA) with no geographical limits. Visit the programs page to find the degree or certificate that's right for you.

Find Your Program
STEP 2

Ready to Apply

Once you have selected your program, it's time to apply! The programs we support are perfectly designed to allow students to earn the same degree as they would on campus in a convenient and easily accessible format.

Apply Now
STEP 3

Consider Cost & Financial Aid

Understanding what financial aid options may be available to you is an important part of the decision-making process. Explore financial aid and scholarship services to learn more.

Tuition Benefits
Advance your career by taking advantage of your company's tuition benefits. Reach out to our partnerships team to learn how your employer can help with your education expenses and what you can do to share your educational goals with them.

FINANCIAL AID AND SCHOLARSHIP SERVICES
STEP 4

Get the Support You Need

LSU Online is committed to your success even before you apply. Our Enrollment Coaches are available to help you with the application process, answer questions about admission requirements, and offer guidance in choosing the right program for your education and career goals. Need one-on-one support?

The LSU family of institutions also offers a range of support services to help empower you to realize your goals. From tutoring to career services, we have you covered! LSU Family of Institutions Support Services.
