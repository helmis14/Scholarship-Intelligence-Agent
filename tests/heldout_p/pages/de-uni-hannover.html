Financial Support for International Students

Financial Support for International Students

The International Office awards emergency grants (Notfall-Beihilfen) and degree completion grants (Studienabschlussbeihilfen) to international students from non-EU countries who are currently enrolled at Leibniz University Hannover.  

Emergency grants (Notfall-Beihilfe)

International students who are temporarily in an acute financial emergency through no fault of their own can apply for emergency aid. Applicants must be enrolled at Leibniz University Hannover and not be on leave of absence. As a rule, this allowance can only be granted once during the period of study. Exceptions are possible in cases of hardship, but must be justified separately. There is no legal entitlement to this financial support.

Examples of emergencies: replacement after theft, water or fire damage, job loss through no fault of your own, illness or accidents, etc.

The following are not considered emergencies: long-term tuition fees, exam periods, final thesis, accumulation of debt over a past period, lack of funds per se, late payment due to insufficient planning of your study project, court costs, fines, penalties, insignificant expenses.

The amount of the emergency grant depends on the type of emergency and the available funds.

Personal counselling is recommended before applying for emergency grant. Please direct requests for counselling to: incoming-student@uni-hannover.de

Degree completion grants (Studienabschlussbeihilfe)

Degree completion grants (Studienabschlussbeihilfe) are intended to support international students at Leibniz University Hannover during their degree completion phase.

Personal counselling is recommended before applying for a degree completion grant. Please direct requests for counselling to:  incoming-student@uni-hannover.de

  • How much is a grant?

    The Awards Committee decides on the amount and duration of the grant for each applicant individually. This amount is based on the available funds, the number of applications and the applicants' level of need. The grant is paid in monthly instalments and only while the student is writing their thesis. The grant is not a full scholarship and can only cover part of your monthly expenses.

  • What are the application requirements?

    The degree completion grant is only awarded to foreign students (non-EU, residence permit §16.1) of Leibniz University Hannover. Applicants must be demonstrably registered for the final examination or submit a subsequent registration for the final examination within one month of the application deadline. Only complete applications will be accepted. Please check whether you have submitted all the required documents.

  • Application deadlines

    The degree completion grant is awarded four times a year. The application periods for the grant are the same each year:

    1–31 January

    1–31 March

    1–31 July

    1–31 October

  • Selection interview

    If your application is accepted, you will promptly receive an invitation to a personal interview with the Awards Committee. The interview lasts 20 to 30 minutes, during which you can explain your situation again in person. A few days after the interview, you will receive a decision on the amount and duration of your degree completion grant.

Necessary Documents

Please submit copies of the following documents as available in a single PDF file:

  • Passport and residence permit (EAT)
  • Registration of your thesis (only for degree completion grant)
  • Certificate of Enrolment
  • Transcript of Marks issued by the Examination Office
  • Bank statements of all accounts of the last 3 months (incl. Paypal, credit card, etc.)
  • Statement of your blocked account or copy of decleration of commitment
  • Proof of income or employment contract
  • Rental Agreement
  • Scholarship certificates
  • Any document that can explain your situation
