Applying to Lehigh as an International Undergraduate Student

You are considered an international student if you are a non-U.S. citizen, regardless of where you attend high school. However, if you are an eligible non-citizen for Federal Aid purposes (such as a permanent resident or refugee), we will consider you a domestic student for both admissions and financial aid purposes.

U.S. citizens living abroad should apply as domestic students. Visit the Office of Financial Aid website to learn more about the financial aid application process for domestic students.

We currently accept the Common Application and Coalition Application with Scoir—follow the procedures on the Common Application or Coalition website to get started. To see all of our requirements, forms and deadlines, please visit our Admissions Requirements page.

Additional Admission & Financial Aid Application Requirements for Non-U.S. Citizens

All non-US citizens who cannot pay the full cost of attendance, must submit the CSS Profile through the College Board. Note: If the $25 CSS Profile fee presents a significant financial burden, you may submit the International Student Application for Financial Aid (ISAFA) instead; however, Lehigh has limited financial aid for international students. Therefore, the CSS Profile is strongly preferred as there are a limited number of spaces available for those submitting the ISAFA each year. The ISAFA can also be uploaded through our Document Upload Center once you've submitted your application and established your applicant portal. Please allow up to 5 business days for our staff to review your documents and mark this requirement as satisfied on your financial aid checklist.

View Admissions Requirements

CSS Profile Deadlines:

  • Early Decision I: November 1
  • Early Decision II or Regular Decision: January 15

Important Notes:

  • Financial aid offers made at admission are final and will not be increased during your time at Lehigh, except to reflect rises in cost of attendance.
  • Once enrolled, Lehigh cannot adjust aid due to changes in your home country's currency value.
  • If your family cannot contribute more than $10,000 per year and the application fee is a hardship, you may request a fee waiver using code: INTLwaiver2027 in the Common Application or Coalition Application with Scoir.
  • Tuition, room, and board can total over $98,900 USD per year. If your family cannot fully cover this amount for all four years, your application must include the CSS Profile.
  • You do not need to submit a bank statement or certificate at the time of application; this will be requested if you are admitted and decide to enroll.

Curriculum

Applicants must be completing or have completed the equivalent of 12 years of education (primary and secondary) or the program of study that leaves them eligible for admission to university-level study in the educational system in which they are enrolled. For example, students who are completing or who have only completed O level or GCSE/IGCSE study are not eligible to apply and should do so when they are completing the final year of A level study, or the equivalent.

Testing

All testing requirements needed to apply to Lehigh can be found on the Admissions Requirements page. To ensure full consideration of your latest test results, please submit them to Lehigh before the application deadline. Exam results submitted after the application deadline may not be received in time to be considered when applications are reviewed by the Admissions Committee. Please plan accordingly so that you register and complete the exams with sufficient time for the testing agency to score your exam, for you to request the testing agency submit those results to us and for us to process the results before reviewing your application. Official score reports should be sent to Lehigh by the testing agency. Emailed, faxed and photocopied scores are not official and will not be accepted.

English Proficiency Requirement

Lehigh requires all applicants to demonstrate proficiency in English. Results from an English proficiency assessment must be submitted unless the applicant’s first language is English, or the last two full years (not including current year of study) of completed formal instruction have been in English.

The TOEFL and IELTS are preferred, however TOEFL IBT Home Edition, and Duolingo English Test will be considered if those exams are not available to the student.

Scores must be submitted directly from the testing/assessment agency and the most competitive candidates score above a 90 on the TOEFL, 7.0 on the IELTS or 120 on Duolingo. SAT, SAT Subject Test and ACT results cannot be used to meet the English proficiency requirement.

English Proficiency Score Guidance

Scores that generally indicate strong English proficiency and readiness for academic success at Lehigh

  • TOEFL iBT (tests taken before Jan. 21, 2026): 95+
  • TOEFL iBT (starting Jan. 21, 2026): 5.5+
  • IELTS: 7.5+
  • Duolingo English Test: 120+

Scores that may suggest a need for additional English-language support

  • TOEFL iBT (tests taken before Jan. 21, 2026): 86–94
  • TOEFL iBT (starting Jan. 21, 2026): 4.0–5.0
  • IELTS: 6.5–7.0
  • Duolingo English Test: 110–119

Scores below these ranges raise considerable concern around readiness for academic success at Lehigh

  • TOEFL iBT (tests taken before Jan. 21, 2026): 85 or below
  • TOEFL iBT (starting Jan. 21, 2026): 4.0 or below
  • IELTS: 6.0 or below
  • Duolingo English Test: 109 or below

Frequently Asked Questions for Non-US citizens

The English testing requirement (TOEFL, IELTS, or Duolingo) is one main difference. In addition, those who will need financial aid during any of their intended years of enrollment at Lehigh are required to submit the CSS Profile, which must be submitted by your application deadline. For instance, if applying Early Decision 1, then the forms are due November 1, and if applying Regular Decision or Early Decision 2, then the forms are due by January 15.

All non-U.S. citizens who will need financial aid during any of their years enrolled at Lehigh are required to submit the CSS Profile through the College Board by the appropriate admissions application deadline. If paying the $25 fee to complete the CSS Profile would present a significant financial hardship, you can submit the International Student Application for Financial Aid (ISAFA).

Lehigh has limited international financial aid available and therefore practices a need-aware review of applications from non-US citizens. We strongly prefer the CSS Profile as there are a limited number of spaces available for those submitting the ISAFA each year. The ISAFA can be uploaded through your admissions checklist once you've submitted your application and established your applicant portal. Please allow up to 5 business days for our staff to review your documents and mark this requirement as satisfied.

An application will not be complete without these forms if your family cannot cover the full cost of tuition, room and board for all intended years of undergraduate enrollment.

The CSS Profile or ISAFA must also be submitted by your application deadline. For instance, if applying Early Decision I, then the forms are due November 1, and if applying Regular Decision or Early Decision II, then the forms are due by January 15.

**Note: U.S. citizens abroad can apply for aid using the FAFSA and CSS profile by deadlines indicated on the Financial Aid website.

Non-U.S. citizens who are not planning to apply for financial aid during any of their intended years of undergraduate enrollment do not need to submit the CSS Profile. Please note that Lehigh’s tuition, room & board, fees, etc. can cost upwards of $93,400 USD per year. We do not have a minimum family contribution as financial aid packages range from full tuition, room and board to a few thousand dollars. Merit scholarships range from $5,000 to full tuition and there is no separate application to be considered for merit.

Please note that if admitted to Lehigh, there will not be additional funding awarded beyond the initial financial aid offer outside of adjustments made based on increases in cost of attendance. We are unable to provide additional financial aid due to changes in the value of your home country’s currency during your enrollment at Lehigh.

Lehigh University provides need‐based financial assistance to a limited number of admitted non-US citizens who have applied for financial aid and who have demonstrated financial need. It is our goal to meet 100% of the demonstrated need (not including travel and personal expenses) for as many admitted students as possible with the limited funds available. Admissions decisions for non-US citizens are, however, need-aware. This means that international citizens will be evaluated for admission with consideration of the ability of students, guardians or sponsors to pay educational costs alongside our holistic admissions process. Some candidates who apply for financial aid and do not demonstrate need may be admitted without a financial aid offer. Other candidates whom we would like to admit may be declined because we have exhausted our financial aid budget.

Cultural Clubs and Organizations

During the 2024–2025 academic year, Lehigh hosted 967 international students representing 87 countries, along with 158 international scholars from 41 countries. Of the international student population, 615 were graduate students and 352 were undergraduates.

For a more detailed breakdown, please refer to the Global Data Report for the 2024–2025 academic year.

Non-U.S. citizens who qualify for federal student aid are known as "eligible noncitizens”. If you fall into one of the categories listed on the Federal Student Aid website and have the specified documentation listed, please notify LUinternational@lehigh.edu. Students are considered in the same category as domestic candidates for admission and financial aid consideration and should apply using the FAFSA and CSS Profile. Note the “eligible non-citizen” status only applies if you have been formally granted Refugee/Asylee/Parolee/or Permanent Residency status. Having applied for one of these statuses alone does not qualify. Please review the instructions on Lehigh’s Financial Aid website.

Please select the citizenship status option on the Common Application or Coalition Application with Scoir that applies to you at the time you submit your application. If your status changes after you submit your application, please email LUinternational@lehigh.edu with a copy of your green card or I-94 attached and we will update your application to reflect your new status and provide instructions on applying for financial aid.

If you are a non-US Citizen, are unable to contribute more than $10,000 each year toward your college costs AND paying the application fee would be a financial hardship for your family, you can request an application fee waiver in the Common Application or Coalition Application with Scoir. Use fee waiver code: INTLwaiver2027. Please note that Lehigh has limited international financial aid available and therefore practices a need-aware review of applications from non-US citizens. The number of non-U.S. citizen applicants that can be admitted each year who require financial assistance is very limited.
