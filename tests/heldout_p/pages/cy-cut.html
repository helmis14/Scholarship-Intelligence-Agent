International Students

Foreign nationals can apply for a position, if they:

  1. Are graduates of Secondary School, recognized and approved by the according authorities of their country.
  2. Have a good knowledge of the language in which the program of their choice is taught. It should be noted that so far all degree programs (Bachelor) are offered in Greek language.
  3. Have a citizenship other than Cypriot or Greek.

Admission criteria

Applicants can apply based on their international exams results or can take part in exams, written and/or oral,  that are organized by the University.

The University may organize exams in a foreign country. The tests are organized in Cypriot embassies or accredited exam centers of the foreign country. Please keep in mind that currently, for practical reasons, the University assesses applications based only on the results of international exams.

Please see the entrance courses and grades for the Bachelor programs according to international examinations (for GCE's or International & European Baccalaureate).

International Students Guide

Coming to study in another country is an exciting experience and you will definitely have a lot of questions. This webpage (click here) is addressed to all students coming to Cyprus from abroad, in order to study at the Cyprus University of Technology. It has been compiled with you in mind and we hope that the information it contains will answer some of your questions and help you plan for your studies in Cyprus and especially in Limassol, as well as smoothly adjust to Cyprus University of Technology life and the Cypriot culture.
