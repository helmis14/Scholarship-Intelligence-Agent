Norstip Scholarship Scheme

The Norwegian Directorate for Higher Education and Skills (HK-dr) established a Norwegian scholarship scheme, NORSTIP.

UPDATE: NORSTIP has been discontinued, and we are no longer accepting scholarship applications.

NORSTIP is designed to promote economic development and welfare in Norway's partner countries. By enabling student mobility to Norwegian universities and colleges, including the Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU), NORSTIP aims to educate future leaders who will drive positive change in their home countries.

NMBU has received a limited number of NORSTIP scholarships for 2 year master programs

A NORSTIP scholarship covers tuition fees and the recipient will receive a moderate scholarship, equivalent to the monthly loan and grant that Norwegian students receive from the Norwegian State Educational Loan Fund.

  • Who Can Apply for a NORSTIP Scholarship?

    NMBU offers the NORSTIP scholarship exclusively to employees or students (current and former graduates) from selected partner institutions. To qualify for the NORSTIP scholarship, applicants must fulfill the following criteria:

    • Be an employee or student (current or former graduate) at one of NMBU's partner institutions involved in ongoing collaborations with NMBU (for example, NORHED and NORPART)


    Aspire to gain admission to one of the two-year master’s programs at NMBU and fulfill the admission prerequisites.

    • Be aged between 20 and 30. Women will be prioritized.
    • Not possess a master’s degree prior to this scholarship.
    • Be a citizen and resident of the country of the selected partner institution.

  • What the Scholarship Covers

    • Full tuition fee
    • Scholarship of 13,297NOK per month (the amount is equivalent to the basis support guaranteed to Norwegian students through Lånekassen)
    • Start-up scholarship - a one-time payment of 15,000NOK
    • Travel costs, including visa-related costs
    • Fieldwork travel expenses as part of the degree

  • Autumn 2025 Admission - Deadline for applications: 1 December 2024

    For the Autumn 2025 admission, NMBU has approximately 21 NORSTIP scholarships on offer and valid from Autumn 2025 to June 2027. It is a highly competitve process to gain admission to the programs and secure a scholarship. Applicants must have an excellent academic track record and a proven proficiency in English to be considered for a NORSTIP scholarship.

    Application Process

    The application deadline is December 1st, 2024 through SøknadsWeb in the link below. To be considered for the NORSTIP scholarship, applicants must be nominated to apply for admission to a master program (you can list up to 3 options). Only applicants who are nominated will be considered for a NORSTIP scholarship.

    Nomination Procedures
    Apply now


    If your application for admission and a NORSTIP scholarship are successful, you will be notified through an admission and scholarship award letter.

    Please be advised that the scholarship will be disbursed only after the student arrives at NMBU. More detailed information will be provided to successful applicants.

    Enquiries can be sent to norstip@nmbu.no.

  • Scholarship Conditions

    • Fulfill the academic qualifications for admission to a master program with NMBU and continually fulfill the terms set by NMBU to maintain student status.
    • Possess and maintain a valid residence permit from The Norwegian Directorate of Immigration (UDI).
    • Arrive on campus at NMBU in Norway within the deadline stated on the admission letter and start the studies in accordance with the master program’s education plan.
    • Complete studies within the 2 years allocated to the master program.

    As the scholarship is a capacity building initiative, scholarhip holders are expected to return home after graduation.

    Note: The scholarship does not include costs, including, but not limited to:
    • Family members/ children in home country or in Norway
    • Travel- and accident insurance for travel to or from Norway
    • Costs related to sending extra luggage to or from Norway
