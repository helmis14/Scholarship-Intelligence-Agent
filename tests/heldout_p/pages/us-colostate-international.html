International Student Scholarships

Undergraduate Scholarships

Colorado State University is excited to offer automatic consideration scholarships to incoming undergraduate international students. Scholarships for first-year students range from $2,000 to $12,000 per year. Students receiving our largest awards have a combination of outstanding grades (GPA), challenging coursework, and demonstrate strong engagement in extracurricular involvement.

$2,000 – $12,000

Scholarship consideration for admitted, undergraduate international students.

No additional application necessary.
Scholarships are renewable for up to four years (must main good academic standing).
Students will learn of their admission decision AND scholarship award amount within 2-4 weeks after submitting an application.

Get Started!

Apply by these dates for the best opportunities:
December 15: Highest scholarship consideration
March 15: Regular consideration
