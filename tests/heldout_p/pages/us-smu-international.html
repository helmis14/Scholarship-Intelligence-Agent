Academic-based scholarships

For incoming, first-year students who have demonstrated a remarkable level of academic achievement. 

  • Award amount: Full tuition and fees, plus one study abroad experience. On-campus students also receive a housing scholarship.
  • Selection: There are a number of qualifications, including a minimum of 20 high school academic units in a challenging curriculum. 
  • Maintaining eligibility: Must maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of 3.300 or greater and full-time enrollment (12 or more credit hours).
  • All SMU merit and need-based awards, including National Merit, cannot exceed "tuition and fees".

Learn more about the program here.

For incoming, first-year students who have demonstrated extraordinary leadership in their high schools, as well as academic achievement.

  • Award amount: Students receive about 75% of tuition and fees, plus one study abroad experience during May term of sophomore year. Scholarship is awarded for eight semesters or four years or graduation — whichever occurs first.
  • Selection: Submission of the Hunt Essay and YouTube statement, along with demonstrated extraordinary leadership and high academic performance.
  • Maintaining eligibility: Must maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of 3.000 or greater and full-time enrollment (12 or more credit hours).
  • All SMU merit and need-based awards, including National Merit, cannot exceed "tuition and fees".

Learn more about the program here.

  • Award Amount: Up to full tuition, does not include fees.
  • Selection: Must have been a President’s Scholar finalist in your incoming academic year.
  • Maintaining eligibility: Maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of 3.000 or greater and full-time enrollment (12 or more credit hours).
  • All SMU merit and need-based awards, including National Merit, cannot exceed "tuition and fees".

For select students who graduate from North Dallas High School.

  • Award amount: Full tuition and general student fee, including a housing and dining scholarship (up to Double Occupancy rate and Mustang Max $350 dining Plan).
  • Selection: Students are expected to be in the top 10% of their graduating class, earn a grade average of at least 90 (on a 100 point scale), take at least two Advanced Placement (AP) courses and participate in extracurricular activities. Finalists will be contacted for an interview by the selection committee.
  • Full-time enrollment (12 or more credit hours) is required.
  • All SMU merit and need-based awards, including National Merit, cannot exceed "tuition and fees".

Learn more about the North Dallas High School Robert H. Dedman SMU scholarship here or by visiting the high school’s counseling office.

For select local students who graduate from a high school located in Dallas County.

  • Award amount: The Mustang Scholarship combines with other aid to ensure tuition, fees, and campus housing are covered for selected students. The program is designed to remove financial barriers and help students fully engage in the SMU experience. Mustang Scholars join the Turner Scholars cohort and take part in a four-year experience centered on mentoring, community engagement, leadership development, and personal growth.
  • Eligibility: Students may be considered if they:
    • Graduate from a public, private or charter high school in Dallas County
    • Are admitted to SMU for the fall semester after high school graduation
    • Demonstrate financial need, including Pell eligibility through the FAFSA or equivalent eligibility through the TASFA
  • Selection: Students who apply for admission to SMU are automatically considered for merit-based scholarships, including the Mustang Scholars Program. Selection is based on a holistic review of academic achievement, leadership, involvement, and financial need.
  • All SMU merit and need-based awards, including National Merit, cannot exceed "tuition and fees".

For students who have been named a National Merit or Achievement recipient.

  • Award amount: Up to $5,000 annually for eight terms or graduation — whichever occurs first.
  • President and Hunt Scholar recipients will receive up to $2,000 annually, not to exceed the cost of attendance.
  • Requirements:  Designate SMU as your 1st choice school and meet the submission deadline set by National Merit Corporation. The deadline is usually early June.
  • Maintaining eligibility: Must maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of 3.000 or greater and full-time enrollment (12 or more credit hours).
  • All SMU merit and need-based awards, including National Merit, cannot exceed "tuition and fees".

Transfer academic-based scholarships

For students transferring from Collin College, Dallas College, Grayson College, Navarro College, North Central Texas College, Tarrant County College and Trinity Valley Community College and completing the admission application by April 1 (fall term entrants only).

  • Award amount: Full tuition and general student fee for up to five terms. 
  • Selection: This award is based on demonstrated leadership, community service and submitted materials including essay, letters of recommendation and application information through the Office of Transfer Admission. 
  • Maintaining eligibility: Must maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of 3.000 or greater and full-time enrollment.

For active Phi Theta Kappa members who are transferring to SMU. 

  • Award amount: $2,000
  • Selection: This award is based on demonstrated leadership, community service and submitted materials including essay, letters of recommendation and application information. 
  • Maintaining eligibility: Must maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of 3.000 or greater and full-time enrollment.

For Dallas College students who are transferring to SMU. 

  • Award amount: Full tuition and general student fee for five terms. 
  • Selection: Must complete at least 50 transferable credit hours with a minimum cumulative 3.50 transferable GPA.
  • Maintaining eligibility: Maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of 3.000 or greater and full-time enrollment (12 credit hours).

Departmental scholarships

For incoming, first-year students with a primary major in Dedman College of Humanities and Sciences who qualify for the University Honors Program (UHP). 

  • Scholars receive scholarship support for eight consecutive fall and spring terms.
  • Maintaining eligibility: Must maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of 3.3 or greater and full-time enrollment with primary major in Dedman College and membership in the University Honors Program.

Learn more about the Dedman College Scholars program here.

For students who have indicated primary academic interest in certain STEM disciplines.

  • Award amount: $10,000 per year on average.
  • Maintaining eligibility: Students must maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of a 3.000 or greater and full-time enrollment for eligibility in addition to declaring a
    STEM major.

For the top pre-engineering students in every admitted class who are expected to make a difference in the world. Must list an Engineering discipline as a primary academic interest and apply for admission by the January 15 Regular Decision/Early Decision II deadline.

  • Award amount: Typically $8,000 to $20,000 over a four-year period.
  • Selection: This award is based on academic work and demonstration of community impact, along with an interest in using engineering to tackle society’s most audacious problems. 
  • Full-time enrollment (12 credit hours) is required.

For the top engineering students in every admitted class who show outstanding academic and leadership abilities. Must list an Engineering discipline as a primary academic interest and apply for admission by the January 15 Regular Decision/Early Decision II deadline. 

  • Award amount: $2,500 per semester for eight semesters on average.
  • Selection: This award is based on class rank and demonstration of exceptional academic work, along with service to the student’s school and community. 

For incoming, first-year students who are top business students. Applicants must have listed a business major as their first academic interest on the SMU application for admission.

  • Maintaining eligibility: Must maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of 3.000 or greater and full-time enrollment with primary major in Edwin L. Cox School of Business. 

Learn more about the Edwin L. Cox School of Business BBA Scholarships here. 

For incoming, first-year and transfer students who have been admitted to Meadows School of the Arts and are majoring in Art, Dance, Film & Media Arts (B.F.A.), Music, or Theatre. 

  • Award amount: Varies by discipline.
  • Selection: Based on audition performance or quality of portfolio, as well as departmental need for the specific discipline/instrument.

Learn more about Meadows School of the Arts Artistic Merit Scholarships here.

For incoming, first-year students who have demonstrated high achievement in both the arts and academics and have indicated an interest in a Meadows School of the Arts major on their SMU application or submitted a Meadows application.

  • Selection: Based on SMU admissions application information. Artistic merit is also considered based on audition performance or quality of portfolio.

United Methodist Church scholarships

For dependent undergraduate children or spouses of ordained ministers of the United Methodist Church who are engaged in active pastoral work and dependent children of retired or deceased United Methodist ministers.

  • Award amount: up to $3,500 per term for eight terms or four years or graduation — whichever occurs first for undergraduate students.
  • Maintaining eligibility: Maintain a cumulative SMU GPA of 2.000 or greater and full-time enrollment (12 or more credit hours).
  • Award amount applied towards tuition only. Not applicable to general student fees or any other University charges. For additional information, please visit Minister's Family Tuition Award.

For students who can identify themselves as active United Methodist Church (UMC) members.

  • Award amount: Varies each year.
  • Selection: Based on UMC activities and financial need.

These awards are usually not determined until after the beginning of the fall semester.

 

Additional scholarships

Incoming, first-year full-time students from Texas who receive federal Pell Grant funds, an SMU merit scholarship and recognized as an academically exceptional students. 

  • Selection: Based on receiving the federal Pell Grant and awarded the Founders’, Second Century, Distinguished or Provost academic scholarship from SMU.

Learn more about Access SMU here.

For students involved in Air Force ROTC or Army ROTC.

  • Award amount: Award amounts vary. Students who receive a full-tuition scholarship through Air Force ROTC or Army ROTC are eligible for a housing and dining scholarships, upon housing availability. Please note, double-occupancy housing and standard food plan scholarships will be offered only to ROTC students who are living on-campus and receive a full tuition ROTC scholarship. Off-campus housing expenses will not be covered.
  • Selection: This award is offered through a partnership with host universities — the University of North Texas for Air Force ROTC and the University of Texas at Arlington for Army ROTC. Incoming, first-year students should notify the Student Financial Services Office of an anticipated ROTC Scholarship by contacting Abby Garcia at abbygarcia@smu.edu. 
  • Maintaining eligibility: Must initiate the process/funds request via goarmy.com and with your respective recruiter each semester. 

Students receiving full tuition and fees ROTC scholarships are not eligible to receive additional SMU merit aid. General information about ROTC Programs and scholarships is available online here. Refer to the above UNT and UTA websites for program specifics.

Students must provide SMU with the approved ROTC Scholarship letter from their host university to SMU’s Student Financial Services. Send the letter to Lisa Kelley at lgkelley@smu.edu. 

 

For students involved in SMU Athletics. Those interested should contact the SMU Athletics Compliance Office, at athleticcompliance@smu.edu.
