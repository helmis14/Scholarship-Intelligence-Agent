  • Scholarship

    Xi’an Jiaotong University provides a number of scholarship opportunities to support international students. We are one of the designated universities to receive and educate international students under China Scholarship Council (CSC). Other scholarship opportunities including MOFCOM Scholarship, International Chinese Language Teachers Scholarship, XJTU Siyuan International Student Scholarship, etc. will also support outstanding international students studying at XJTU.
