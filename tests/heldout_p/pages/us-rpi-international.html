International Applicants

RPI considers any student who is not a U.S. Citizen or Permanent Resident to be an international applicant (regardless of where the student currently lives and/or attends school). 

RPI accepts international students into all of our programs with the exception of the Physician-Scientist (B.S./M.D.) Program. 

There are limited merit scholarship opportunities available for undergraduate international students, up to $20,000 per year. All non-U.S. citizens/permanent residents must be prepared to assume the full cost of attendance for the duration of their studies.

International Applicant Requirements

It’s important for the exact name that appears on your passport to be used on your application, recommendations, and test scores. This will eliminate any confusion in the processing of your application.

In addition to the regular application requirements, RPI currently stresses the following requirements for its undergraduate international applicants: 

First-Year Applicants

Evidence of financial support to cover educational and living expenses is required for students who will study on a non-immigrant (F-1 or J-1) visa.

There are limited merit scholarship opportunities available for undergraduate international students, up to $20,000 per year. All non-U.S. citizens/permanent residents must be prepared to assume the full cost of attendance for the duration of their studies.

You must submit the International Student Financial Statement (ISFS) form if you are accepted and enroll, and we must see documentation reflecting at least one year's worth of liquid funding (approximately $80,000 USD). The ISFS and all supporting financial documents should be uploaded to your application portal once you submit your enrollment deposit.

An English language proficiency examination is required for all non-native English speakers. Applicants must demonstrate English language proficiency by submitting one of the following exams:

  • Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL): RPI expects a minimum TOEFL score of at least 88 iBT (or a 4.5 on the new 6.0 scale). The TOEFL iBT Home Edition is accepted.
  • International English Language Testing System (IELTS): RPI expects an overall band score of at least 6.5.
  • Duolingo English Test (DET): RPI expects a minimum score of 115
  • Pearson’s Test of English (PTE): a PTE score of at least 59 is expected.

Please plan on taking the exam no later than December of your senior year. Make sure that the name you use for test registration is identical to the way your name is listed on your passport and in your admissions application (if this is not the case, please contact our office at adm_updates@rpi.edu).

RPI requires that official scores be sent directly from the testing agency; self-reported scores will not be accepted. Our TOEFL Institutional Code is 2757.

Applicants who have completed three or more years of secondary education in the U.S. may request an exemption once they have submitted their application by completing the form found on their application status page. Students MUST use this form to request an exemption; email requests will not be accepted.

Exemptions

Applicants may be exempt from the English proficiency examination requirement if they meet one of the following criteria: 

Academic Qualifications 

  • SAT: Evidence-Based Reading and Writing (EBRW) score of 640 or higher 
  • ACT: English score of 22 or higher • IB: English A Higher Level (HL) score of 6 or higher 
  • IGCSE: English or English First Language with a grade of B or higher 
  • A/AS Levels: English or English First Language with a grade of B or higher 
  • Cambridge English B2 First: Score of 185 or higher 
  • Cambridge English C1 Advanced: Score of 185 or higher

U.S. Education 

  • Applicants who have completed three or more years of secondary education in the U.S. may request an exemption once they have submitted their application by completing the form found on their application status page. Students MUST use this form to request an exemption; email requests will not be accepted. 

English-Medium Education Countries 

Applicants who have successfully completed a high school diploma or equivalent from one of the following countries are exempt: 

Africa (Anglophone): Botswana, Burundi, Cameroon, Gambia, Ghana, Kenya, Lesotho, Liberia, Malawi, Mauritius, Namibia, Nigeria, Rwanda, Sierra Leone, South Africa, St. Helena, Swaziland, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe 

Caribbean and Americas (Anglophone): American Samoa, Anguilla, Antigua, Bahamas, Barbados, Belize, Bermuda, British Virgin Islands, Canada, Cayman Islands, Dominica, Falkland Islands/Malvinas, Grenada, Guyana, Jamaica, Montserrat, St. Kitts & Nevis, St. Lucia, St. Vincent & the Grenadines, Trinidad & Tobago, Turks and Caicos, U.S. Virgin Islands 

Other Countries: Australia (including Christmas Island), Cook Islands, Fiji, Gibraltar, Irish Republic, Micronesia, New Zealand, Palau, United Kingdom (England, Scotland, Northern Ireland, Wales).

Official copies of all academic transcripts from secondary schools, with English translations. 

Applicants from China may submit junior high school transcripts (grades 7-9) along with their A-level coursework for admission consideration.

Applicants completing secondary school in Ontario, Canada may submit their Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD) for admission consideration.

Please only submit the required documents. It is not necessary to submit certificates, copies of awards, or diplomas. Academic awards and honors - as well as extracurricular, volunteer, and work activities - may be listed on the application. 

RPI encourages all international applicants to participate in an interview through one of the following programs as part of their application: InitialView; The Duolingo English Test (DET); OR Vericant. While not required for admission to RPI, it allows applicants to describe their educational experiences and showcase their personality. It is also an opportunity for applicants to further demonstrate their English language proficiency. Please note that there is a fee for these services that will allow you to send your interview to a number of U.S. colleges/universities. Please contact InitialView, The Duolingo English Test (DET), or Vericant directly to schedule your interview.

Frequently asked questions regarding applying as an international student

DACA & DREAMer Students

Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA) & DREAMer students should follow the application instructions for an international applicant. Detailed instructions may be found above. At RPI, our definitions of domestic versus international, as far as the application process goes, are aligned with the federal government’s definitions of eligibility for federal financial aid (versus residency, for example). So, without U.S. citizenship or a green card, a student would be considered an international applicant versus a domestic applicant.   

How should DACA & DREAMer students indicate citizenship status on the application?

For the question, "Select your citizenship status," please choose  "U.S Resident".
