Scholarships

Scholarships for bachelor's and master's students

  • There are no scholarships available for students on full bachelor's and master's degree programmes.
  • The University of Oslo does not offer any scholarships.

Scholarships for students on exchange programmes

The grants and scholarships which are available through short-term mobility programmes such as Erasmus+ and Nordplus/Nordlys, are usually awarded and paid by the student's home institution. Incoming exchange students must contact their home university about the scholarship.

See admission for exchange students

Other scholarships

You may be eligible for scholarships offered by public or private institutions in your home country. Unfortunately, the University of Oslo cannot help you with information about this.

Please see:

  • Scholarships on studyinnorway.no
  • Norwegian State Educational Loan Fund (applicable for residents in Norway)
