Full Degree Students

Scholarship for Outstanding New International Students

    • Candidate Selection: Outstanding new international students selected annually by the Office of International Admissions and Management

    • See: http://international.hufs.ac.kr > Announcements for more information

    • Selection Period: New international students’ final registration period

    • Scholarship Amount: Amount and period may vary (entrance fee - full tuition / 1 semester ~ 8 semesters)

    • Selection Criteria: Determined annually

GPA-based Scholarship

Candidate Selection 

All students who were enrolled (and attained a GPA) for the semester before scholarship selection are eligible. As all aforementioned students are eligible automatically, there are no individual requirements.

*Detailed criteria may differ according to major (e.g., limitations regarding the amount ofobtained major class credits, etc).

Payment Period: Reduced tuition is reflected on the tuition bill in February and August 

Scholarship Amount: 

    • President’s Scholarship: 100% tuition

    • Dean’s Scholarship: 40% tuition

    • Head of Department Scholarship: 30% tuition

Selection Criteria: 14+ credits / Higher than 3.5 GPA for the previous semester

GPA-based International Students Scholarship 

Candidate Selection

    1. International students who are in their 2nd - 7th semester.

    2. Recipients of full-scholarships such as the 6.25 UN Korean War Descendent Scholarship, Korean Government Scholarship, Overseas Korean Foundation Scholarship, King Sejong Foundation Scholarship (etc.) are not eligible to apply.

    3. Recipients of GPA-based scholarship are not eligible to apply.

Application Period 

    • 1st Semester: End of March 

    • 2nd Semester: Early October

Apply through the Office of International Admissions & Management

Scholarship Amount

    •  “우” Scholarship: 700,000 won 

    • “수” Scholarship: 1,000,000 won

Selection Criteria

    • 12+ credits

    • higher than 3.5 GPA for the previous semester

Topik Scholarship

Candidate Selection

    1. International students who are in their 2nd semester-7th semester.

    2. International students who have received TOPIK 5 or 6 certificates.

    3. Recipients of full-scholarships such as the 6.25 UN Korean War Descendant Scholarship, Korean Government Scholarship, Overseas Korean Foundation Scholarship, King Sejong Foundation Scholarship (etc.) are not eligible to apply.

    4. TOPIK scholarship for the same level can be obtained only once.

Application Period

    • 1st semester: Late March

    • 2nd semester: Early October

Apply through the Office of International Admissions & Management

Scholarship Amount

    • TOPIK level 5 : 300,000 won

    • TOPIK level 6: 500,000 won

Selection Criteria: 12+ credits

Family Scholarship

Candidate Selection

Two or more siblings enrolled at Seoul or Global Campus at the same time

* Applicable to only undergraduate students at HUFS

Application Period

    • 1st Semester: mid-March

    • 2nd Semester: mid-September 

Scholarship Amount: 1,000,000 won (total sum for all siblings, can be paid maximum two times during enrollment period)

Selection Criteria

    • 12+ credits

    • higher than 2.00 GPA for the previous semester

Organization Activities Scholarship (Student Organization)

Candidate Selection

Students involved in independent student councils, campus media organizations, and other student bodies

* E.g. Student council, department student council, club associations, FBS, GSA, CSO, The Argus, school magazine editorial committee, etc.

Application Period: During the semester, submit documents to relevant organization

Scholarship Amount: Amount varies

Selection Criteria

    • 12 credits during previous semester

    • 2.0 or higher GPA

    • Each student organization has their own regulations—requirements vary.

 7+1 Study Abroad Scholarship

Candidate Selection

Enrolled students who have been selected for the study abroad program by the Office of International Affairs (OIA)

Application Period: Please contact the OIAMS for more information

Phone: 02) 2173-2070

Website: http://oia.hufs.ac.kr

Scholarship Amount: Full tuition at HUFS (scholarship recipient is responsible for paying tuition at the foreign institute)

Selection Criteria: Higher than 3.0 GPA (could be higher depending on the foreign institute)
