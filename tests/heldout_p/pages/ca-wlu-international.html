    Tuition and Scholarships

    We want you to be informed about the costs to study at Laurier by giving you an overview of educational expenses. You can use this information to build your budget, then explore options, such as scholarships and bursaries, to help make your studies more affordable.

    International Tuition and Student Fees

    The first-year international undergraduate tuition values and student fees listed below are for the 2026/27 academic year and are subject to increase for the 2027/28 academic year. All tuition fees are in Canadian dollars.

    International Tuition Guarantee

    Tuition rates are set each year for new, incoming international students. Once admitted, tuition rates for continuing international students will increase by no more than 5% between years of study.*

    *Students that change their undergraduate program during their studies will be subject to the tuition fees for their new program, which may exceed a 5% increase.

    Scholarships for International Students

    Scholarships reward you for all the hard work you've done to prepare for university, and are a great way to help fund your studies in Canada. At Laurier, we have two scholarships awarded specifically to our international students.

    Renewable Entrance Scholarships 

    All applicants are automatically assessed for a renewable entrance scholarship. This scholarship is awarded based on your final high school admission average and no application is required. 

    The entrance scholarship is valued at $5,000 to $6,000 CAD and is renewable at these same amounts in your upper years as an in-course scholarship. See how this renewable scholarship can add up over your years at Laurier:

    *This scholarship is the President’s Gold Scholarship. Students who receive the President’s Gold Scholarship in their first year but do not meet the requirements to renew the scholarship in their upper years will be eligible for the $5,000 CAD in-course scholarship. 

    Inspiring Lives Scholarship

    Inspiring Lives Scholarships range in value from $500 to $5,000 CAD and are awarded each year to incoming international undergraduate students who inspire lives of leadership and purpose in their communities and through extracurricular activities. This scholarship is not based on your admission average and requires a separate application. 

    Explore More Scholarship Options

    Be sure to look for scholarship options available to all students applying to Laurier. Here is a snapshot of some of our scholarships, but you can find a complete list on our Scholarships, Awards and Bursaries page.

    Starting at Laurier

    As an incoming student, entrance scholarships provide funding for your first year.

    • Entrance scholarship: You are automatically assessed for an entrance scholarship when you apply to Laurier and are offered based on your admission average.
    • The President's Gold entrance scholarship is awarded when you are admitted to Laurier and is worth $6,000 CAD per year for international students. This scholarship is renewable in your upper years for four years (or five years for approved double degree programs) when you meet renewal requirements.

    Competitive and Upper Year Scholarships

    Beyond your first year, here are some scholarship options to help you continue funding your studies: 

    • Every year, you can apply for competitive scholarships, awards and bursaries by completing an online student profile about yourself. Scholarships can range in value from $250 to $5,000 CAD.
    • In your upper years, you will automatically be considered for in-course scholarships based on the grade point average (GPA) of your Laurier courses from the previous academic year, ranging between $5,000 to $6,000 CAD annually.

    Financial Support

    In addition to awards and scholarship, here are some financial resources and support that you can explore. 

    Part-Time Employment

    You are eligible to apply to on-campus student positions. In addition, here are some other employment opportunities and resources to consider: 

    • The International Student Work Experience Program (ISWEP) provides a limited number of international students with part-time opportunities. 
    • Consult with the Career Centre to explore and apply for part-time positions off campus. 

    Financial Literacy Support

    Get confident in managing money through Laurier's Dollars and Sense program. You can also meet with our financial literacy specialist to get help with creating a personalized budget. 

    Next Steps

    Apply to Laurier

    Ready to apply? Follow our application checklist to stay on track with our application process and when you're ready, you can apply using the Ontario Universities' Application Centre (OUAC). If you're applying to Laurier to start in the winter term, use our winter application. 

    Accept Your Offer

    Accept your offer through OUAC. After accepting your offer, you are required to pay a tuition deposit. We recommend paying your tuition deposit within two weeks of accepting your offer.

    Once your tuition deposit has been processed, we will initiate the provincial attestation letter (PAL) request on your behalf.

    Payment of Fees

    Already accepted your offer? Make sure you pay your tuition and fees so they are received and applied to your LORIS account by the due date to avoid late fees. The due date for each term typically happens the first week of the start of term.
