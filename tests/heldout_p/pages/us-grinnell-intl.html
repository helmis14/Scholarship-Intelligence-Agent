Scholarships and Fellowships for International Students

  • Share This: Share Scholarships and Fellowships for International Students on FacebookShare Scholarships and Fellowships for International Students on LinkedInShare Scholarships and Fellowships for International Students on X

Many scholarships and fellowships require U.S. citizenship or residency. Below are several resources to assist Grinnell’s international students who seek merit-based funding for advancing their educations. 

Fischlowitz International Student Travel Fellowship

Grinnell’s international students should know about the Fischlowitz International Student Travel Fellowship, which provides $5,000 for an international student to explore the USA each summer. (Applying for the Fischlowitz Fellowship is excellent practice for applying for the Watson Fellowship during the senior year.)​​​​​​​
