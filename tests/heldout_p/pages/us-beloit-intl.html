This is Beloit

Pay no more than $30,000 

All-In Pricing Guarantee

Beloit College is all-in on providing a transformative education at a world-class value. We guarantee that accepted students who apply for admission and financial aid by the regular decision deadline will not be asked to pay more than $30,000 in direct costs for the 2027-28 academic year. Some students may pay significantly less based on financial need.

All families can move forward with confidence and clarity in the application process thanks to Beloit’s commitment to providing value and transparency.
