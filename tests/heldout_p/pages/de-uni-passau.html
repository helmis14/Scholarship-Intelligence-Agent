The International Office

The International Office

Our Responsi­bilities Head Administrative Officer Team Student assistants Address & Office Hours

The team of the International Office is at your disposal for all questions concerning studying abroad as well as the study stay of foreign students in Passau.

What we do:

  • General information on studying abroad
  • Arranging scholarships for Germans who want to study abroad
  • Nurture relations with partner institutions abroad
  • Support the initiation of new partnerships and programmes
  • Supervise visiting students and scholarship holders from all over the world

At the International Office you can obtain various information brochures on topics related to studying abroad as well as the DAAD Scholarship Guide, which is published annually in May.

We also provide up-to-date information on our Facebook page.

 

Enquiries by e-mail

If you have any questions, please contact only one of the staff members listed here at a time. Reaching out to multiple staff members will not speed up the process. We are looking forward to helping you as soon as possible.
