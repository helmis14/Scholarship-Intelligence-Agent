An Excellent Education and a Place to Call Home

As a top-tier research university, the University of Dayton offers top programs in arts and sciences, business administration, education and health sciences and engineering.

There is a wonderful international presence on our campus as students from 46 countries call themselves Dayton Flyers. Our close-knit, safe, residential community values connectedness and one-on-one access to faculty.

Academic Programs

The University of Dayton provides challenging academics and opportunities to gain experience in your field of study. UD offers more than 70 undergraduate academic programs, 50 master's degree programs and 12 doctoral programs.

Tuition and Scholarships

Ranked as a top-tier national university by U.S. News & World Report, the University of Dayton provides a superior education, an excellent value and a welcoming place to call home. Learn about tuition, cost and scholarships for international students.

Services and Support

Transitioning to the U.S. from your home country can be both exciting and challenging. International Student and Scholar Services helps make the process easy for you and your family.
