Erasmus and NTNU grants

Detaljer Skriv ut
(Videresendt fra Exchange scholarships administered by NTNU)

Most students who go on exchange abroad as part of their studies receive one of the grants administered by NTNU: the NTNU or Erasmus+ grant.

This page in Norwegian

See more about exchange funding or the topic page about exchange abroad.

Questions about grants? See Frequently asked questions about exchange.

When you apply for exchange or a practical stay abroad, you will automatically be considered for an Erasmus+ or NTNU grant. The grant is awarded in addition to support from Lånekassen.

Grants

Erasmus+

Grant amounts may be subject to change depending on NTNUs national funding allocation.

The duration of all stays within the Erasmus+ program must be between 2 and 12 months (60-365 days). You may receive funding for up to 12 months on bachelor level, and up to 12 months on master level.

Erasmus+ base funding

Please note that the rates for students travelling during the Autumn semester 2026 and Spring semester 2027 has changed. A maximum funding duration of 3 months per semester has been implemented (does not apply to traineeships)

Base amount monthly rates

Land 2025/2026 2026/2027
Austria, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Iceland, Italy, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Sweden €530 €460 (up to 1380€ per semester)
Other countries in the Erasmus programme €470 €410 (up to 1230€ per semester)

Erasmus+ traineeships

In most cases, you can receive an Erasmus+ grant for a traineeship (internship, practical training, or fieldwork) lasting at least 60 days. The grant is awarded in addition to any remuneration received from the host organisation.

In addition to the monthly base rates described above, an additional 150€ is awarded for going abroad on an internship. The maximum funding duration for traineeships is 12 months on bachelor level, and 12 months on master level.

You may also receive grants for traineeship stays within 12 months after you have graduated (bachelor or master) from NTNU as a so called Recent Graduate. To qualify for the Recent Graduate grant you must contact Unit for International Relations before you have graduated.

Erasmus+ travel grant

The travel grant is paid out together with the rest of the Erasmus grant. In Mobility Online, you will need to submit information on on your travel to and from your host institution. The travel grant depends on the (air) distance from NTNU to your host institution and your method of transportation. You will receive the travel grant once per stay.

You will receive a higher travel grant if you use a mean of transportation with low emission for the majority of your travel to and from your host institution (in air distance). Means of transportation using low emissions is defined by the European comission to be, but is not limited to: train, carpooling or biking. Transportation using a plane, boat (with motor), motorcycle, or travelling alone in a car is not defined to be a method of transportation using low emissions.

The following rates for the academic year 2026/2027 apply:

Air distance from NTNU to institution abroad Travel grant Green travel grant

You will receive an additional 4 days of grants when you travel green. You do not need to travel green all 4 days, and you do not need to travel for 4 days as long as the majority of your trip to and from your host institution is environmentally friendly (in air distance).

Other rates for the academic year 2025/2026 apply. You can receive up to 6 additional days when you travel using mean of transportation with low emissions.

To receive the green travel grant and the four additional days of grant, you must submit documentation that states that the majority of your travel to and from your stay is environmentally friendly, for example with receipts for each leg of your journey. If you do not have a receipt, you may submit pictures or find other creative ways to document your journey.

Erasmus+ top-up grant for underrepresented groups

The Erasmus programme aims to provide equal opportunities for all. This is why additional an additional top-up is offered to students from groups that are less likely to go on exchange. This scholarship is part of the EU's efforts to promote inclusion and diversity in Erasmus+.

The additional grant is €250 per month, and comes in addition to the regular Erasmus base amount and travel grant. You apply for the additional grant in the Application Portal after you have received an offer of an exchange. You must sign a confirmation that you belong to the category you are applying for. You can only receive the additional grant once per stay, even if you may belong to several categories at the same time. The top-up grant is also awarded for a maximum of 3 months per semester (does not apply to internships).

Students with children or other care responsibilities
Students with children under 18 years of age can receive an additional scholarship regardless of whether the child joins your stay. The top-up is given once, regardless of the number of children or people you care for.

Students with care responsibilities for younger siblings, older family members and/or family members with serious illnesses (including drug addiction) can receive an additional scholarship.

Students with permanent disabilities or chronic illnesses
Students who fall under the the UN definition of people with disabilities can receive the top-up grant. This definition is broad, and refers to the fact that the disability and/or chronic illness must be long-term.

The UN definition: "People with disabilities include those who have long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory impairments which, in the face of various barriers, may prevent them from participating fully and effectively in society on an equal basis with others".

Students with refugee status
Students with refugee status may receive the top-up grant

Students belonging to indigenous populations and national minorities
All students who have ties to indigenous populations (e.g. Sami) or national minorities (Jews, Kvens, Romani/Taters, Roma and Forest Finns).

The affiliation can be linked to language and cultural roots, in that the student either:

  • Uses the relevant language as a mother tongue, or has (had) a parent or grandparent/great-grandparent with the relevant language as a mother tongue.
  • Is, or has been, registered in the Sami electoral register, or has (had) a parent in the Sami electoral register. 

NTNU Grant

The NTNU grant is awarded to students who are going abroad for a period of between 1 and 12 months, who do not qualify for the Erasmus grant or a scholarship through SEMP (Switzerland). The NTNU grant is also awarded to students who travel to institutions where NTNU does not have a formal exchange agreement (so-called freemovers). You must meet common requirements for all types of scholarships (see below). The rate for NTNU scholarships varies from semester to semester, but will normally be approximately NOK 2,500 as a one-time payment.

Payment of the grant

Requirements for the grants

To receive the grant from NTNU you must have arrived at your host institution. In addition:

  • Your stay must be pre-approved by your department/faculty at NTNU

  • All the required steps necessary steps under the category "During the mobility - Grants" must be completed

For the Erasmus grant, you also need to:

  • Fill out and sign your Learning Agreement. The Learning Agreement for studies is created digitally in Mobility Online. The Learning Agreement for traineeships template can be downloaded from Mobility Online and will need to be filled out together with your department and host institution.

  • Sign your Grant agreement and uploaded it in Mobility Online. Your Grant agreement will be sent to your student email once the above steps has been completed.

The deadlines for submitting and finsishing the relevant steps for the grants is

  • December 1st for students departing in the autumn semester

  • June 1st for students departing in the autumn semester

If the steps above are not completed within the the given deadlines, we will not be able to award you a grant.

Payment

The grants on this page is usually paid out at the start of the semester.

  • Erasmus students on regular study exchange will receive the grant in two payments, 80% at the start of the stay, and the rest after the stay.

  • The NTNU grant is paid once at the start of the stay

  • Students on traineeship or internships will receive 70% at the start of the stay, and the rest after the stay.

Grants from NTNU is usually paid out twice per month on the payments dates for Bonus salary (fees) on this page NTNUs Payment schedule. The payment is made by the Service center at NTNU

NB! It may take up to 6 weeks from the time when you upload your Grant agreement to when the Erasmus payment has been made to your account. For the NTNU grant, it should take no more than 4 weeks from you uploading your confirmation of arrival.

Reduction or withholding of scholarship

  • The scholarship amount is awarded subject to NTNU being allocated sufficient funds from the EU and/or Norwegian authorities.

  • The amount may be reduced if you receive a scholarship from other EU programs (does not apply to support from the Norwegian Loan Fund).

  • The scholarship will be withdrawn if you apply for leave of absence from NTNU for the period you are abroad.

  • If you cancel your study stay, or end earlier than planned, you will have to repay part of the scholarship.

  • Once you have been paid an exchange scholarship from NTNU, you undertake to fill in mandatory information in Mobility Online after the stay has been completed. If this is not done, you risk receiving a claim for repayment.

Requirements for the grant

  • You must be a student in a bachelor's or master's program at NTNU, and have completed at least 60 credits before departure.

  • The stay abroad must be pre-approved as part of the degree at NTNU. You apply for pre-approval to your faculty.

  • You can only be awarded one scholarship per stay.

  • If you are going to extend your stay, you must apply again within the ordinary deadlines in order to receive an Erasmus grant.

  • You must submit an experience report from your stay in Mobility Online.

Reservations regarding changes

Changes to the scholarship rates on this page may occur. For Erasmus scholarships, the rates given by the EU and HK-dir are applicable at all times.
