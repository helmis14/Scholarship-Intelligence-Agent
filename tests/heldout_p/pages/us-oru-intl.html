Cost of Education Information

Students who are not green card holders or citizens of the United States are considered international students. Oral Roberts University is proud to serve students from more than 170+ nations, and we’re ready to help you get a financial plan in place so that you can join our diverse campus community. Find important information about ORU’s cost of education below.

 

Undergraduate Cost of Education

 

Direct Costs Per Semester Per Year
Tuition $18,125 $36,250
Housing* $2,455 $4,910
Food* $3,325 $6,650
General Fee $660 $1,320
Technology Fee $250 $500
Academic Fee $180 $360
International Student Fee $200 $400
Total Direct Costs $25,195 $50,390

  • *The financial aid budgeted amount for Housing and Food is based on the cost of a meal plan and a standard double room. Your actual cost may change depending on the room type you select.
  • The International Student Fee helps to cover exclusive services provided by the university to international students. These include: special events, such as new international student orientation and non-residential tax filing; immigration services from the university’s DSOs, including CPT and OPT applications; and the purchase and upkeep of software for immigration services.
  • The general fee listed above covers matriculation and general student activities such as health services, campus security, campus publications, intramurals, library services, athletic events, and others.
  • U.S. Department of Homeland Security requires that evidence of adequate financial support for educational expenses for a full year must be submitted to ORU before the University can issue a Form I-20.
  • If you are married and your spouse and/or children will accompany you to the U.S., please note that additional funds will be required. Support for your dependents must amount to $6,000 for a spouse and $4,000 for each child.

Modular Courses

Please note: If you plan to enroll in modular courses, this will result in lesser budgetary allowances for living expenses. The per diem for modular students’ lodging/meals is $122 per day, with an additional $500 travel budget per trip to Tulsa. If you plan to enroll in online courses, there will be no allowance of housing/food/travel allowances in your financial aid package.

Leveling Courses

Some programs may require you to complete leveling courses. For example, if you plan on enrolling in the MBA program but do not have a bachelor’s degree in business, you may have to complete leveling courses such as “Principles of Financial Accounting” before continuing on to core MBA courses.
