Financial aid for international students

Most types of financial aid are not available for students with the following Visa types: F-1 or F-2 Student, G Series, H Series, J-1 or J-2 Exchange Visitor. International students may, however, be eligible for scholarships, private loans, and short-term loans. Below are some helpful resources to review.

Undergraduate scholarships

For information on institutional and prestigious scholarships, please visit Undergraduate and Prestigious Scholarships.

Outside scholarships

For a list of available scholarship opportunities, please visit Outside Scholarships for international students.

Private loans

The University of California maintains information on Private Loans. Please refer to the lender list provided specifically for international students.

Short-term loan

For information on short-term and emergency loans, please visit our Emergency and Short-Term Loan Programs.

Cost of attendance

The UC Davis cost of attendance is an estimated figure used to determine financial aid eligibility. Review the cost of attendance.

UC Davis affordability

UC Davis costs less than comparable private universities for the same world-class education. Compare costs.

Campus resources

For more information about the services for international students on the UC Davis campus, please contact Services for International Students and Scholars.
