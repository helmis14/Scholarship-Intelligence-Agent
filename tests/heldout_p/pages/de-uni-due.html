Welcome to the International Office

The International Office is the central service office for international contacts of the University of Duisburg-Essen (UDE). We offer support and guidance for international students, post-graduates and other applicants who are interested in our courses  of studies. We especially advise them on matters of application and enrolment, and we are in charge of all matters concerning the admission of international students. The International Office also counsels UDE students who are interested in studying abroad or who are looking for international scholarships.
We are happy to help foreign scholars and the inviting UDE institutes to deal with bureaucratic obstacles and everyday problems. We offer counselling and support to individual UDE professors, research groups and departments by taking care of collaborations between universities, by establishing contacts with partner universities and with international projects.

Go abroad ambassadors

UDE’s go abroad ambassadors support students on their way towards a stay abroad. All of our go abroad ambassadors have studied or completed a placement abroad themselves and are happy to support their fellow students with help and advice.

Take a look at the go abroad ambassador page or follow them on Instagram  and/or Facebook.
