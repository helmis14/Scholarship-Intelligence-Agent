Center for International Education scholarships

At NAU, we’re committed to supporting international students like you. That’s why our Center for International Education offers tuition waivers—including up to $11,000 per year toward NAU tuition and fees through the International Excellence Award (IEA) —for eligible students upon admission. Explore scholarship opportunities below!

The International Excellence Award (IEA) is NAU’s premier merit-based tuition-reduction award for high-achieving international students. Designed for first-time, degree-seeking F-1 undergraduate and graduate students admitted directly into a university program on the Flagstaff Mountain campus, this scholarship provides up to $11,000 per year toward NAU tuition and fees. No separate application is required, and the award is renewable for the duration of your degree if you maintain full-time enrollment and a 3.0 GPA or higher. Award amounts vary.

Eligibility requirements
  • First-time, full-time F-1 international students pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree at NAU’s Flagstaff campus.
  • Admitted and enrolled in classes for the upcoming semester.
  • Minimum 3.0 GPA at the time of admission.
  • Submission of required financial documentation for I-20 issuance.
Students not eligible to apply include those:
  • Sponsored by a government or other organization.
  • Receiving assistantships, full athletic scholarships, or other tuition waivers.
  • Enrolled in a dual-degree, including community college bridge programs and the Program in Intensive English (PIE).
  • Participating in the Programa de Beneficios de Pago (PROBEP) tuition benefit program.
Application
  • No separate application is required—students who meet the criteria are automatically considered upon submitting a complete international admission application.
  • Awarded on a first-come, first-served basis.
Fund disbursement
  • Disbursed as an out-of-state tuition waiver only.
  • Covers up to the award amount, not exceeding out-of-state tuition charges for the semester.
  • Cannot be applied to mandatory or special class fees, housing, meal plans, or other non-tuition expenses.
Renewal criteria
  • Renewable each academic year with a minimum 3.0 GPA and continued full-time enrollment.
  • Must meet all mandatory attendance requirements.

Additional NAU scholarships

From departmental scholarships to graduate assistantships, NAU offers opportunities to help make your education more affordable.

Several colleges and departments at Northern Arizona University offer scholarships to qualified students pursuing a particular field of study. Explore scholarship opportunities based on your academic program. Contact your academic department directly for additional information.

Graduate students at NAU may be eligible for graduate assistantships (GAs), which provide valuable academic experience along with financial support. GA positions include a tuition waiver that covers all or part of a student’s tuition.

To learn more about eligibility, requirements, and the types of assistantships available, visit the Office of Graduate and Professional Studies website. You can explore current openings at the bottom of that page or by contacting the department directly.
