Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship for October 2027 admission

Since 2021, the Discovery Program for Global Learners has been selected for the Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship (Special Selection) (“MEXT Scholarship”).

The Discovery Program for Global Learners will select several applicants who are accepted through the October 2027 admissions process, meet the eligibility requirements, have outstanding academic records, and achieve excellent screening results.
In principle, the MEXT Scholarship is intended for students from ASEAN and African countries, but this may not apply in all circumstances.

Scholarship Coverage
●Application fee
●One-time admission fee
●Tuition fees
●Monthly living allowance (117,000 yen per month)
●Airfare to and from Japan (only at the beginning and the end of the 4-year undergraduate program)
*Applicants who are already residing in Japan will not be provided with airfare.

Selection Process
The MEXT Scholarship recipients will be selected from the top successful applicants, based on their performance in the admission process.

Notification of Selection Results
The selected applicants will be notified of their results by e-mail after the announcement of their admissions results.
If selected applicants have not replied by the deadline specified in the e-mail, the selected applicants will be considered to have declined the offer.

For more information, please see the page.

 

Discovery Scholarship（including tuition reductions）

For all international students in the Discovery Program for Global Learners, tuition reductions will be provided to bring their tuition to the same level as Japanese students. In addition, only students with particularly outstanding academic performance will be eligible for planned full tuition waivers or scholarships.

JASSO Scholarships

JASSO (Japan Student Services Organization) offers scholarships for students who are residing outside of Japan at the time of admission to a Japanese university. The university applies for this scholarship on behalf of enrolling students. Successful applicants receive a monthly stipend of 48,000 JPY for the first six months after arrival. 

Other scholarships

There are other scholarships that international students studying in Japan can apply for before/after acceptance to the Discovery Program. Some of the Discovery students are granted full-scholarship that offer full-tuition and stipends. We highly encourage Discovery applicants to look for scholarship opportunities on their own.
