Fairfield University welcomes applications from international students. We value the diverse perspectives your experience will bring to your classrooms and the greater University community. Join students from more than 50 countries.

During your academic journey at Fairfield, you will have plenty of time between lectures and lab sessions to explore the landscape, visit iconic landmarks, and experience a brand-new country with brand-new customs and activities.

With the shoreline and beach just one mile from campus and New York City only a 60-minute train ride away, the opportunities for adventure are endless.
