Global Scholarships

Whether you’re starting your studies or continuing your journey in Australia, these scholarships recognise ambition and reward academic potential for international students worldwide.

  • Motivational Scholarship 
    • Value: Up to 25% off
    • Study level: Undergraduate, Postgraduate
    • Faculty: All faculties (except BMIHMS)
    • Enrolment status: New student
    • Deadline: Rolling per intake
    • Scholarship duration: Entire course
    View scholarship
  • Family Scholarship
    • Value: 35% off
    • Study level: Undergraduate, Postgraduate
    • Faculty: All faculties (except where the family member is enrolling in the Bachelor of Nursing)
    • Enrolment status: New international applicants
    • Intake: 2026 and 2027 offers, plus 2028 offers where the student is packaging with an approved English pathway provider in 2027
    • Scholarship duration: Entire course
    • Eligibility: New international applicants who are an immediate family member of a current student, future student or recent graduate of Torrens University Australia.
    View scholarship
  • PEPEA Scholarship 
    • Value: 50% off
    • Study level: Undergraduate, Postgraduate
    • Faculty: All faculties
    • Enrolment status: New student
    • Deadline: Rolling per intake
    • Scholarship duration: Entire course
    View scholarship

    • Study level: Undergraduate, Postgraduate
    • Faculty: All faculties
    • Enrolment status: New student
    • Deadline: Rolling per intake
    • Scholarship duration: Entire course

  • Kings College Malaysia 
    • Value: 45% off
    • Study level: Undergraduate
    • Faculty: Business
    • Enrolment status: New student
    • Deadline: Rolling per intake
    • Scholarship duration: Entire course
    View scholarship
  • Pearl Pathway
    • Value: 30% off
    • Study level: Undergraduate, Postgraduate
    • Faculty: All faculties
    • Enrolment status: New student
    • Deadline: Rolling per intake
    • Scholarship duration: Entire course

BMIHMS Scholarships

Start your hospitality journey with confidence. Our BMIHMS scholarships support passionate students aiming to excel in global hotel and event management careers.

  • Three Pillars Blue Mountains International Hotel Management School Hospitality Scholarship
    • Value: 10%–50% off
    • Study level: Undergraduate, Postgraduate
    • Faculty: BMIHMS
    • Enrolment status: New student
    • Deadline: Rolling per intake
    • Scholarship duration: Entire course
    View scholarship

Scholarships for offshore online students

Torrens University Australia’s Offshore Online Scholarship makes studying from abroad more accessible for international students. It offers up to 30 % off tuition fees for undergraduate and postgraduate courses across all faculties, for new students studying online and residing outside Australia.
