Your nature is to seek an excellent education. Our nature is to make it accessible.

Iowa State University offers merit scholarships to both first-year and transfer international students. These scholarships are awarded based on academic excellence and a demonstrated record of accomplishments, offering valuable financial support as students pursue their educational goals at Iowa State.

First-year international students

Merit scholarships are available for a limited number of high-achieving first-year international students. Selection is highly competitive and based on a holistic review of SAT/ACT scores and high school grades.

Scholarship amounts

ScholarshipTotal valueAnnual award
Award of AchievementUp to $40,000$5,000–$10,000
Award of DistinctionUp to $16,000$2,000–$4,000

Funding is limited to a maximum of 8 semesters or until degree completion, whichever comes first.

Application deadlines

TermPriority deadlineFinal deadline
FallFeb. 15May 1
SpringNov. 15Dec. 15

Eligibility

  • Offered admission to Iowa State University as an international first-year student.
  • Assessed international tuition rates.
  • Accept your offer of admission by May 1 (fall) or December 15 (spring)
  • Submit the OneApp application:
    • Complete the General Application first.
    • Then, under Opportunities > Recommended, select International Student Admissions Scholarship Application for your term.

Renewal requirements

  • Maintain a minimum 2.5 cumulative GPA.
  • Enroll in at least 12 credits per semester (excluding summer).
  • Remain classified as an international student.
  • Apply funds only toward undergraduate tuition and fees.

Apply for an International Merit Scholarship

To be considered for an International Merit Scholarship, students must complete the OneApp scholarship application.

  • OneApp scholarship portal

International transfer students

Merit scholarships are awarded to transfer students with strong academic records and exceptional achievements in areas such as math, sciences, arts, leadership, community service, innovation, or entrepreneurship.

Scholarship amounts

ScholarshipTotal valueAnnual award
Award of AchievementUp to $20,000$5,000–$8,000
Award of DistinctionUp to $10,000$2,000–$4,000

Funding is available for up to 5 semesters (2.5 years) or until degree completion, whichever comes first

Application deadlines

TermPriority deadlineFinal deadline
FallMar. 1Jun. 30
SpringNov. 15Dec. 15

Eligibility 

  • Offered admission to Iowa State University as an international transfer student.
  • Assessed international tuition rates.
  • Cumulative GPA equivalent to 3.0 out of 4.0 (B average).
  • Accept your offer of admission by May 1 (fall) or December 15 (spring)
  • Submit the OneApp application:
    • Complete the General Application first.
    • Then, under Opportunities > Recommended, select International Student Admissions Scholarship Application for your term.

Renewal requirements

  • Maintain a minimum 2.5 cumulative GPA.
  • Enroll in at least 12 credits per semester (excluding summer).
  • Remain classified as an international student.
  • Apply funds only toward undergraduate tuition and fees.
