Student-Athlete Required Forms

KNOX COLLEGE STUDENT-ATHLETE REQUIRED FORMS
Required annual NCAA Forms are distributed through Front Rush. Student-athletes will receive directions via email when forms are available for each academic year. All student-athletes are required to complete the NCAA paperwork every year and are not permitted to practice or compete until forms are complete.
 
 Know what you sign:
  • 2020-21 Student-Athlete Statement 
  • 2020-21 Drug Testing Consent
  • 2020-21 Student-Athlete Authorization/Consent for Disclosure of Protected Health Information for NCAA-Related Research Purposes
  • 2020-21 General Amateurism and Eligibility Form for International Student-Athletes (Required for first-year/new international student-athletes)
 
All student-athletes must also be medically cleared to participate by the Knox College Athletic Training Staff. Visit the Athlete Physicals and Required Forms page for further details. Contact Scott Sunderland, Head Athletic Trainer, at 309-341-7378 or ssunderl@knox.edu regarding required medical forms.
 
 
For additional resources and information, visit www.ncaa.org. Please contact Lexie Vernon, Knox College Athletics Compliance Administrator with any questions at 309-341-7552 or akvernon@knox.edu.
