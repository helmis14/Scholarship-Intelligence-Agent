SA Scholarships


UTEP International Education Fee Scholarship

  • Awarded to degree-seeking UTEP students going on an approved study abroad program
  • Student must be earning academic credit as part of the program
  • Up to $800 (short-term) or $1000 (semester) awards
  • Paid for by UTEP student International Education Fee – this is your investment; use it!

Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship

  • Applicant must:
    • Be an undergraduate student & Pell Grant recipient
  • Program: Must be at least 3 weeks abroad
  • Term Available: Summer, Semester, Academic Year
  • Award Amount: Up to $5,000
    • $8,000 if studying a critical language
  • Deadline: March & October

https://www.gilmanscholarship.org/

Gilman-McCain Scholarship

  • Applicant must be:
    • US Citizen
    • An undergraduate student in good standing
    • Child dependents of active duty service members
    • Recipient of any type of federal financial aid
  • Program: Must be at least 3 weeks abroad
  • Term Available: Summer, Semester and Academic Year
  • Award Amount: $5,000
  • Deadline: March, October
    • Special deadlines for academic year 2019-2020: November, December & January

https://www.gilmanscholarship.org/program/gilman-mccain-scholarships/

Freeman-Asia

  • Applicant must:
    • Be a U.S Citizen or Permanent Resident
    • Demonstrate financial need
    • Be enrolled as an undergraduate student
    • Have a minimum 2.8 GPA
  • Program: Must be in East or Southeast Asia
  • Term Available: Summer, Semester, Academic Year
  • Award Amount:
    • Summer Award: up to $3,000
    • Semester/Quarter Award: up to $5,000
    • Academic Year Award: up to $7,000
  • Deadline: April & October

https://www.iie.org/en/Programs/Freeman-ASIA

The Rainbow Scholarship

  • Applicant must:
    • Be a US citizen, Permanent Resident or DACA student
    • Be a member of the LGBTQ+ community.
  • Program: Must be at least 4 weeks abroad
  • Term Available: Summer, Semester & Academic Year
  • Award Amount: up to $10,000
  • Deadline: January & September

Fund for Education Abroad

  • Applicant must:
    • Be U.S citizen or Permanent Resident
    • Be enrolled as an undergraduate student
  • Program: Must be at least 4 weeks abroad
  • Term Available: Summer, Semester, Academic Year
  • Award Amount: range from $1,250 - $10,000
  • Deadline: January & September

https://fundforeducationabroad.org/

Phi Kappa Phi

  • Applicant:
    • Does not have to be a member of UTEP’s Phi Kappa Phi chapter
    • Must be enrolled as an undergraduate student
    • Have 3.75 GPA or higher
  • Term Available: all study abroad terms
  • Award Amount: $1000
  • Deadline: March

https://www.phikappaphi.org/grants-awards/study-abroad

BUTEX

  • Applicant must:
    • Have already been accepted to the overseas school
  • Program: Must be for a minimum of one semester at a BUTEX-affiliated university in the UK
  • Term Available: Semester, Academic Year
  • Award Amount: £500
  • Deadline: June

https://www.butex.ac.uk/scholarships/

Study Abroad in Japan: Bridging Scholarships

  • Applicants must:
    • Be a US citizen
    • Be enrolled as an undergraduate student
  • Program:
    • Must be for at least 3 months abroad
    • Must be for academic credit
    • Must take place in Japan
  • Term Available: Semester & Academic Year
  • Award Amount: $2,500 - $4,000
  • Deadline: October

https://www.aatj.org/studyabroad/japan-bridging-scholarships

Boren Awards

  • Applicant must:
    • Be a US citizen
  • Program:
    • Must have a serious language component
    • Must be outside of Western Europe, Canada, Australia, or New Zealand
    • Must be at least 12 weeks abroad
      • Preferred academic year-long program (25+ weeks)
      • Summer awards limited to STEM majors only
    • Preferred list of languages and countries can be found on the scholarship’s website
  • Term Available: Summer (STEM majors only), Semester, & Academic Year
  • Award Amount:
    • 25-52 weeks: up to $20,000
    • 12-24 weeks: up to $10,000
    • 8-12 weeks: up to $8,000 (STEM majors only)
  • Deadline: February

https://www.borenawards.org/

The Go Overseas Volunteer Abroad Scholarship.

  • Award Amount: $500 dollars
  • Deadline for this scholarship is twice a year

https://www.gooverseas.com/scholarships

Tortuga Study Abroad Scholarship

  • Applicant must:
    • Be a U.S citizen, Permanent Resident , or currently studying in the US on a student visa
    • Be a full-time undergraduate student
  • Term Available: Winter, Summer & Semester
  • Award Amount: $1,000 & One travel backpack
  • Deadline: May & December

https://www.tortugabackpacks.com/pages/study-abroad-scholarship

Explore the World Travel Scholarship

  • Applicant must:
    • Be U.S Citizen or Permanent Resident
    • Be 18-30 years old
    • Demonstrate financial need
  • Reside in one of 14 identified metropolitan areas (including Austin or Houston, TX)
  • Program: Travel outside USA for at least 7 days
  • Award Amount: $2,000
  • Deadline: April

https://www.hiusa.org/programs/travel-scholarships/explore-the-world

Scholar Trips

  • Applicant must:
    • Be a legal U.S resident
  • Award Amount:
    • 10 second-prizewinners will receive a $1,000 scholarship.
    • 25 third-prizewinners will receive a $500 scholarship.
  • Deadline: January

http://www.scholartrips.org/

Corinne Jeannine Schillings Foundation

  • Applicant must:
    • Be an undergraduate student in college
    • Have a silver or gold award in Girl Scouts
  • Program: Must be at least 6 weeks abroad
  • Term Available: Summer, Semester & Academic Year
  • Deadline: May & December

https://cjsfoundation.org/

Good Colleges Study Abroad Award

  • Applicant must: Open to all students
  • Terms Available: Summer, Semester
  • Award Amount: $2,000
  • Deadline: November

https://goodcolleges.online/study-abroad-award/

Owner Direct Vacation Rentals Study Away Scholarship

  • Applicant must:
    • Be US or Canadian
    • Be a full-time student
    • Be over the age of 18 years old
  • Term Available: Summer & Semester
  • Award Amount: $500
  • Deadline: August

https://www.ownerdirect.com/documents/scholarship

  • AIFS Generation Study Abroad Scholarships
  • Diversity Scholarships
  • Need-Based Scholarships
  • Additional AIFS Scholarships & Grants

API (Academic Programs Internationals)

  • Applicant must:
    • Already be accepted into an API program
  • Award Amount:
    • Summer Award: up to $1,000
    • Semester/Academic Year Award: up to $2,000
  • Deadline: April, October & December

https://apiabroad.com/study-abroad/#financial-info-faq

CEA (Cultural Experiences Abroad)

  • The scholarships listed in the CEA website have different requirements in order to qualify.
  • The top applicants to CEA Diversity, Academic Merit, Global Career, and Global Education Scholarships receive these competitive awards of an additional $1,000 for semester programs, and an extra $250-$500 for summer programs.

https://www.ceastudyabroad.com/financial-options/cea-scholarships

CIEE (The Council on International Educational Exchange)

  • The scholarships and grants listed in the CIEE website have different requirements in order to qualify.
  • Deadlines vary according to the type of scholarship
  • All UTEP students would receive the Minority Serving Institutions grant of:
    • Summer: $500
    • Semester: $2,000

https://www.ciee.org/go-abroad/college-study-abroad/scholarships-grants

ISA (International Studies Abroad)

  • The scholarships listed in the ISA website have different requirements in order to qualify.
  • Award Amount:
    • Summer Award: up to $1,000
    • Semester/Academic Year Award: up to $2,000
  • Deadline: February, April, & October

https://studiesabroad.com/admissions/document/scholarships

Sol Education Abroad

  • The scholarships listed in the Sol Education Abroad website have different requirements in order to qualify.
  • Need-Based Scholarships
  • Additional Sol Education Scholarships & Grants

https://www.soleducation.com/study-abroad-scholarship

Spanish Studies Abroad

  • The scholarships listed in the Spanish Studies Abroad website have different requirements in order to qualify.

https://www.spanishstudies.org/scholarships.html

Rotary Award availability varies by chapter. Check with your local Rotary for scholarship and grants.

  • Scholarships last from one to four years and can include an entire degree program
  • District grants can be used to sponsor secondary school, undergraduate, or graduate students studying any subject, either locally or abroad.
