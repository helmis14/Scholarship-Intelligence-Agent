Grants

There are websites (most in Danish) that have searchable databases of grants that you can search for free. It is worth using these, rather than the ones, where you have to pay.

If you are an international student, we also recommend that you apply for grants from your home country.

The following sites have information about grants

  • Legatnet
  • Studerende online
  • Study Abroad Funding
  • Den Franske Ambassade (grants for studies in France)
  • On Legatbogen you can get advice concerning application for grants.
  • The Denmark-America Foundation & the Fulbright Commission offer grants for Danes going to the United States to study

ITU Students have previously received grants from the following foundations

  • Lemvigh-Müller Foundation
  • Ib Henriksen
  • Knud Højgaard
  • Vilhelm Pedersen
  • William Demant Fonden
  • Dansk Tennis Fond 
  • Marie og M.B Richters Fond

  • Stibo

  • Augustinus Fonden
  • KV Fonden
  • Ulla og Carsten Mouriers Legat Til Unge Talenter På Vej
  • From local municipalities
  • From former places of Upper Secondary Education 

You can apply to the Travel Pool for funds to cover travel expenses.
