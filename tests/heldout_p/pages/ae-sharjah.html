National UAE Student's Opportunity

Your Gateway to Scholarships, Internships, Exchanges & Graduate Studies

The Student Opportunities Portal provides a comprehensive list of programs that support academic development, international exposure, research advancement, and career readiness. Students are encouraged to explore the opportunities below and apply to the programs that best match their goals.

1. Scholarships & Academic Funding

  • Tabadol – UAE National Scholarships

A government-funded scholarship program supporting UAE nationals in pursuing undergraduate and postgraduate studies.
🔗 https://www.mohesr.gov.ae/en/EServices/ServiceCard/pages/Scholarships.aspx

  • TDRA Betha Scholarship

Supports outstanding Emirati students in ICT-related fields for undergraduate and postgraduate studies in top institutions.
🔗 https://tdra.gov.ae/en/ictfund/education/scholarships-betha

  • Sharjah Citizens' Scholarship for Graduate Studies

Scholarships offered to eligible Sharjah citizens pursuing advanced degrees.
🔗 https://old.sharjah.ac.ae/en/Admissions/fees-scholar/Pages/shi_reg.aspx

  • SEWA Scholarship

Financial aid program offered by SEWA to support Emirati students in their higher education journey.
🔗 https://scholarship.sewa.gov.ae/Home/Apply

2. Exchange & International Mobility Programs

  • Tabadol Student Exchange Program

National-level student mobility initiative enabling UAE students to study for one semester in partner institutions.
🔗 Same as Tabadel link above

  • University of Sharjah – International Student Exchange Program

Study abroad opportunity for one semester or a summer term with partner universities around the world.
🔗 https://www.sharjah.ac.ae/Services/International-Student-Exchange-Program

3. Internships & Training

  • CASTO Internship Opportunities (University of Sharjah)

Professional internship listings across various industries for UoS students.
🔗https://www.sharjah.ac.ae/en/Services/Departments/CASTO/iop

  • IAESTE – International Engineering Internships

Global technical internships in engineering, science, and technology fields.
🔗 https://www.sharjah.ac.ae/Services/Departments/CASTO/IAESTE-Outgoing-Interns

4. Graduate Studies & Research Opportunities

  • University of Sharjah – Graduate Studies Portal

Applications and forms for master’s and PhD programs across all colleges.
🔗 https://www.sharjah.ac.ae/Academics/Graduate-Studies/Applications-and-Forms

  • Funded PhD Positions at the University of Central Florida (UCF)

Fully funded research positions for international PhD applicants in engineering and computer science.
🔗 https://grad.cecs.ucf.edu/prospective-students/research-positions/

  • Fulbright Scholarships for Non-U.S. Citizens

Fully funded master’s/PhD scholarships and research grants for international students seeking to study in the United States.
🔗 https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/apply

  • DAAD – German Academic Exchange Service
  • Scholarships for master's and PhD studies in Germany for international students.
    🔗 https://ed.sidinitiative.org/daad-scholarships-2025-2026-eligibility-requirements-and-application-guidelines/
