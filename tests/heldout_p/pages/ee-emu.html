    Scholarships for Students

    An active student today could be your valuable colleague or business partner tomorrow. The Estonian University of Life Sciences has two funds established to support the collaboration between the university and businesses in the education of our students, offering scholarships for this purpose.

    Explore opportunities to support future talents on the funds' pages.

    Joosep Toots Foundation

    The Joosep Toots Foundation coordinates the majority of scholarship-related activities across the fields of the Estonian University of Life Sciences, with the exception of forestry and wood processing

    Artur Nilson Foundation

    The Artur Nilson Foundation coordinates scholarship-related activities in the fields of forestry and wood processing.
