Required Steps

At Furman, we treat each applicant as an individual.  To that end, we’ve tailored three different application processes to meet each student’s particular needs.

Early Decision I and II
If Furman is your first choice and you are confident that you will attend if admitted, apply Early Decision.  Furman’s Early Decision is binding, meaning that if you are admitted, you agree to withdraw your applications from all other institutions and commit to enroll at Furman.

Early Action
Early Action allows you to apply and learn about your admission decision early.  With Early Action, you’re still free to investigate other schools.

Regular Decision
If you want to keep your options open and need the most time to complete admission applications, apply Regular Decision.

All students may complete and submit the Common Application or the Coalition Application online.

Once you have applied for admission, you will receive an email with instructions to log in to your Furman status page.  This page can be used to monitor the status of your application, upload documents, and complete your Furman Financial Information Form form.

Requested via your application, your school counselor will be prompted to complete their portion of these forms online via the Common Application. Please note that your school report must be submitted in English.

Your high school must submit an official copy of your transcript, which are mark sheets or certificates from all secondary or high schools attended. (Furman requires transcripts for a minimum of three full years of secondary level coursework). Please note that your school transcript must be submitted in English. Unofficial transcripts from students will be not be accepted.

Furman reserves the right to request a transcript evaluation from an external credential evaluator, at the applicant’s expense, if the authentication of a transcript is deemed necessary. Upon request from the international admissions office, students should have a course-by-course evaluation of transcript(s) completed by a NACES accredited credential evaluation service. Once the evaluation is complete, the agency will forward all required documents to Furman.

Minimum scores required:

  • TOEFL (0-120): 100. School Code: 5222
  • TOEFL (1-6): 5. School Code: 5222
  • IELTS: 7.0 (Overall band score)
  • Cambridge English Exam Advanced (CAE): 180 (Overall score)
  • Duolingo English Test: 120 (Furman is unable to provide fee waivers)
  • International Baccalaureate (IB)
    • Completion of IB Diploma
    • Higher Level English Grade of 5 or Better
  • English Composition Courses
    • Grade of C or 2.0 or higher in two college-level English composition courses from a U.S. college/university.
  • SAT Evidence-Based Reading and Writing (ACT English Score Equivalent)
    • Minimum score of 550.
  • Completing Four Full Years of Secondary School in the U.S.
    • Must have completed a minimum of 3 full years of English courses at the time of review (excluding English as a Second Language Courses).
  • Letter on letterhead with official signature confirming English is the primary language of instruction at your school or email from school official sent from a school-affiliated email account confirming English is the primary language of instruction at your school.

Optional Steps

The Admission Committee believes that your potential for success cannot be determined solely by standardized test scores. Therefore, the SAT Reasoning Test or the ACT is optional of all undergraduate applicants.

If you plan to take the test, lease visit either www.sat.org or www.act.org to register. When submitting your test scores with multiple test results, the Admission Committee will select the scores that offer you the greatest advantage in the admission review process. Furman’s school code for the SAT is 5222, and the code for ACT is 3858. If you do not wish for us to consider your scores, please indicate so on the Common Application.

In order to provide an in-person interview opportunity for international applicants to Furman, we have partnered with Vericant and Initial View. They will conduct video interviews and provide short writing samples with our non-native English speaking applicants. These third-parties do not evaluate candidates but, instead, post the interviews online for our admissions team to review. Although the interview is not mandatory, we highly recommend you participate in an Initial View/ Vericant interview, as it will give you an excellent opportunity to showcase your language skills and professionalism to our admissions team.

We are always looking for ways to connect with interested applicants and are happy to arrange a virtual meeting with a member of our staff; however, we are unable to do so between January 1 and March 15, as most of our application review takes place during this time. For students who have been admitted, we are most happy to arrange a virtual meeting to answer questions and talk about the student experience! Thank you for your understanding.

The #YouAreWelcomeHere Scholarship at Furman University will recognize two international students who have a vision for enhancing intercultural understanding. This scholarship will cover up to a minimum of 50% tuition and is renewable annually with satisfactory academic progress. The scholarship is open to students of all academic majors and fields of study.

Eligibility Criteria:

  1. Apply to Furman University as an International student with an F-1 student visa.
  2. Demonstrate interest and personal initiative in activities involving intercultural learning and exchange.

Application Process:

  1. Apply for admission to Furman University using the Common Application or the Coalition Application online.
  2. Complete the #YouAreWelcomeHere Scholarship Application through your Furman online status page by February 1st.
  3. Scholarship recipients will be notified by March 15th.
  4. Confirm acceptance of scholarship and enroll at Furman by May 1st.

Important Dates:

Application Deadline: February 1st

Scholarship Notification: By March 15th

Enrollment Deposit Deadline: May 1st
