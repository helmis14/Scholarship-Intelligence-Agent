Why should you study at Hofstra University as an international student?

Hofstra has a long tradition of welcoming international students (see what countries are represented at Hofstra) who enjoy the benefits of American higher education in a selective and private campus community that values diversity and promotes cross-cultural understanding.

Here are some highlights:

  • Location: Located in New York, 25 miles from Manhattan
  • Programs: 160 undergraduate and over 175 graduate program options with more than 100 dual-degree program options
  • Scholarships: Partial merit scholarships (SAT and ACT exams are not required for undergraduate programs.)
    *Partial scholarships are available for graduate students. These awards are limited, competitive, based on merit and awarded at the time of admission by graduate program faculty.
  • Job placement rate: Above 93%. We’re ranked in the top 9 percent of colleges and universities nationwide for midcareer salary of all undergraduate alumni. Our graduates are world changers!
  • Diversity: Hofstra has earned the highest ratings for diversity and inclusiveness.

Explore our site and learn why Hofstra is a special place to live and study, and the right choice for you!

How To

Apply to Hofstra

To help you as you apply for admission to Hofstra University, we’ve provided an easy checklist for you to follow as you gather your forms and materials.

Apply for Undergraduate Admission Apply for Graduate Admission

Get Ready

Once you’ve submitted your application, your next step will be applying for a student visa and financing your study.

Obtaining an I-20

An I-20 form is an official government document that certifies a person is eligible to be an F-1 nonimmigrant student at Hofstra.
Learn About the I-20

Scholarship Opportunities

Merit scholarships from Hofstra and scholarship opportunities from external organizations can help fund your studies.
Learn About Scholarships

Obtaining a Student Visa

Once you are accepted into Hofstra University and submit the necessary documents for your I-20, your application is sent to International Enrollment.
Learn About Visas

Learn More

Check out translated brochures and watch the video Q&A to learn more about International Student Life at Hofstra.

Resources

  • I-20 Application Form [PDF]
    Please note that the I-20 application can be completed and submitted at any stage of the application process (even prior to receiving a decision).
  • F-1 Visa Transfer Form [PDF]
    (required if transferring from a U.S. high school, college, or university)
  • Affidavit of Support (if financial documents include an account that is not in the student’s name)
  • Dependent Information Form (if any dependents will accompany you to the U.S.)
  • Sponsor’s Promise of Free Room and Board Form (if a family member or sponsor will be providing you with free off-campus accommodations for the duration of your education program)

Please email InternationalAdmission@hofstra.edu with any questions about required documents or the admission process.

All documents can be scanned and emailed to International Admission.

*Remember that you will need to bring original copies of your financial supporting documents to your visa interview.
