About King Saud University Initiative

The initiative aims to attract talented and distinguished graduate students from Saudi Arabia and around the world to study at King Saud University (KSU), Riyadh. It has been approved by KSU Board of Directors, and comes as a strategic step towards attracting exceptional academic and research competencies within the framework of Saudi Vision 2030, which targets to develop higher education and enhance scientific research as essential components to support sustainable development. This initiative also comes at a time when demand for advanced scientific and research competencies is increasing, and the urgent need for developing effective strategies to attract brilliant minds from all over the world.

More information
Scope of the Initiative

The initiative aims to attract highly talented and distinguished students, locally and globally, who have exceptional academic and research achievements, to pursue a Ph.D. degree in selected programs offered by KSU. This initiative comes in line with KSU transformation to be a world-class university.

Objectives of the Initiative

The general objective of the initiative is to attract top students from inside KSA, and from around the world, to join KSU community strengthening the quality of PhD students, in a culturally diverse environment, with the following specific objectives:

  • To facilitate local and international students' contributions to research productivity in alignment with KSU’s priorities
  • To advance academic development and innovation at the university level.
  • To cultivate a dynamic research environment that fosters excellence and interdisciplinary collaboration within the university and with international universities

Eligibility Criteria

To apply, the initiative has a set of general requirements, set by the Deanship of Graduate Studies, the initiative owner, to ensure high academic standards. Further specific requirements might be required based on the selected programs, which are set by the department/program.

General Requirements

  • Master's degree from an accredited institution
  • English proficiency: Only official TOEFL, IELTS, and STEP scores will be accepted. KSU requires a minimum TOEFL score of 61 on the IBT (Internet Based Test) or 6 on the IELTS (International English Language Testing System) or 85 on STEP. The KSU admissions code for IELTS (147080) and for TOEFL (7498). A waiver from this requirement is possible if the applicant received a Master’s degree from an accredited institution in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, or New Zealand.
  • Two letters of recommendation
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV), with an evidence of a recent publication or a patent if available.
  • A statement of purpose.
  • The GRE general test is preferable, but not required, unless specified by the selected program.
  • A personal interview may be required (virtual or in-person).

Study Subjects and Application Deadlines

The initiative gives opportunities for exceptionally talented students to purse their PhD in a setting of academic and research environment. KSU has a variety of PhD programs in the following fields of study:

  • Computer Sciences
  • Engineering
  • Basic and Applied Sciences
  • Health and Medical Sciences

All PhD programs have the following components:

  • Courses: In this component, the student is expected to complete a set of credit hours that consists of core courses and elective courses.
  • Dissertation: In this component, the student is required to carry out an independent research in the specialized area of study. This component is typically equivalent to a minimum of 24 credit hours.
  • Written & Oral Qualifying Examination: The student must successfully pass a written and oral qualifying examination on a set of selected areas before being allowed to progress further in the program.
  • Research Proposal: The student must develop a research proposal in his area of specialization in order to conduct his dissertation project.
  • Dissertation Examination: The student must complete his dissertation project and defend it successfully in a dissertation oral examination (Dissertation Defense) to earn his degree.

Application Deadlines:

Full and complete applications via the application portal will be accepted starting from Monday March 24th, 2025 till May 15th, 2025 (This phase has been closed for now, next phase will be announced in the Fall of 2025). Decisions by the Initiative Steering Committee for the initial nominations will be made shortly, and nominated applicants for the next phase (residents of KSA and from abroad) will be communicated with, to start uploading all required official documents via Study in Saudi portal. Failure to do so will result in rejection to continue for the next phase of nominations. Notified applicants will be able to start as early the Winter semester (January 2026) if possible, pending completing all required documentations and legal forms.

Scholarship Benefits

All eligible full-time students will be granted the following benefits under the Initiative, which provides support throughout their PhD studies (for 3 years). These benefits include:

  • Full tuition support
  • Monthly living allowance [a total of 54,000 ($ 14,400) annually]
  • On-campus Housing
  • Comprehensive health coverage at the university hospitals
  • Relocation support for students from outside Riyadh
  • Part-time work opportunities might be available
  • Eligibility to work in KSA for up to one year after graduation

FAQs

Applications will be received via the application portal for the initiative. Go to Apply online for more info and registration.

The initiative is endorsed by KSU Board of Directors to attract outstanding and talented graduate students in selected PhD programs at different phases. It has its own set of specified programs, rules, requirements, and benefits that differ from current and previous admission cycles.

The initiative in its first phase is only for pursuing a PhD degree.

Only selected PhD programs, in alignment with university research priorities, are offered at this stage. Other PhD programs might be offered in the next cycle.

Students, who are not citizens or legal residents of KSA, need to apply for Educational visa via Study in Saudi portal. Further information will be sent to accepted students on how to process their visa.

First, there are general requirements as set by the initiative (Eligibility Criteria Section). Furthermore, there are specific requirements, indicated by each program and set by the academic department, that are available within the e-portal after going through registration process within the portal.

The only accepted English tests are IELTS, TOEFL, and STEP, with the minimum specified scores set for this initiative as described in the general requirements. A waiver from this requirement is only possible if the applicant received a Master’s degree from an accredited institution in the following countries: the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, and New Zealand.

Scholarships are awarded through a competitive process across all programs, and meeting the minimum requirements doesn’t guarantee awarding the scholarship. Best candidates will get notifications based on program’s assessment and the initiative steering committee decisions.

The initiative is for new applications with a set of general and specific requirements, and scholarships will be awarded in a highly competitive process for best candidates.

Scholarships are only awarded for full-time students, and not intended for people who are currently employed and hold full/part-time job.

After the initial selection process, applicants may be asked to attend an online or in-person interview as part of the assessment.
