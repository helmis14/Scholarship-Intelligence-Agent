Undergraduate Scholarships

Attending university does require a financial commitment. But over a lifetime, it's one of the best investments you can make. 

UNI's tuition is below the national average for both public and private institutions and is consistently ranked among the country's "best buys" in higher education. However, the scholarships below are available to give additional support to international students to ensure that UNI is even more affordable.  

Students must follow the directions to apply (for awards that are not automatic) and can earn more than one scholarship.

Academic Excellence Scholarships

Students are automatically considered for the following scholarships upon admission to UNI. Scholarships are renewable for up to four years with continuous full-time enrollment and good academic standing. 

International Scholars Award: $2,000-$5,000 / year

International students applying to UNI directly from high school or transferring to UNI are automatically considered for the International Scholars Award of $2,000-$5,000 annually. Award is based on academic achievement of 3.0 or higher (SAT or ACT scores can also be considered). No UNI Scholarship Application is needed. 

Qualifications: 

  • 3.0 GPA or 1020 or higher (SAT)
  • Completed application file
  • Spring 2027 & Fall 2027 Intake
Transfer Scholar Award: $1,000 for one year

International students applying to UNI as a transfer student are automatically considered for this $1,000 scholarship. Awards are based on academic achievement (GPA only) and are non-renewable. No UNI Scholarship Application is needed. 

Qualifications: 

  • Transfer GPA of 3.25+ 

Additional Scholarships

The following scholarships require that you apply through the UNI Scholarship Application. The application opens July 1 and the priority deadline is February 1. Students must be admitted in order to complete the scholarship application. Most scholarships are renewable for up to four years with continuous full-time enrollment and good academic standing. 

International Excel Award: $1,000 - $4,000 per year, for up to four years

All new incoming degree-seeking students, including freshmen and transfer, with strong academic backgrounds may qualify for this scholarship with awards ranging from $1,000 - $4,000 per year. Admitted students can apply through the UNI Scholarship Application or this separate online application. 

 

Qualifications:

  • Proof of English proficiency
  • Examples of leadership and service
  • Completed application file
  • UNI Scholarship Application submission 
Presidential Scholar Award: $4,000 per year, for up to four years

Awarded each year by the University Honors Program to high school seniors with a history of outstanding academic performance. Recipients must participate in the Presidential Scholar Program. 

 

Qualifications:

  • 3.80+ GPA and 29+ ACT Composite (SAT 1340+)  or 3.95+ GPA if admitted by test-optional review
  • Completed application file
  • UNI Scholarship Application submission 

For more information, visit the Presidential Scholars webpage. 

Iowa Partner State Award: $4,000 per year, for up to four years
  • All new incoming degree-seeking students, including freshmen and transfer, from the Iowa Economic Development Authority (IEDA) Partner State Program regions are automatically considered UNI's Partner State Award of $4,000 annually.  Degree-seeking students from the Partner State Program will also be automatically considered for the Scholars Award of $5,000 annually.  Totaling a $9,000 annual scholarship for Partner State Program students.
  • Students from partner states enrolling in the Culture and Intensive English Program (CIEP) program are eligible to receive a 50% tuition discount.  More details on CIEP website.
  • The IEDA international partnerships include the following partner states:
    • Yamanashi Prefecture, Japan
    • Yucatan State, Mexico
    • Hebei Province, China
    • Taiwan
    • Cherkasy Oblast, Ukraine
    • Veneto Region, Italy
    • Kosovo
    • Maharashtra, India
