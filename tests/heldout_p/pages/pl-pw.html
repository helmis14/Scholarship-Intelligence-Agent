International Students enrollment

Information about the Faculty of Electrical Engineering offer of studies for International Students»

General information

Application and admission process for all international students to Warsaw University of Technology is coordinated by the International Students Office (ISO) of Warsaw University of Technology.

For more information on application and admission, please visit this website: students.pw.edu.pl/index.php/How-to-Apply 

Polish citizens applying to studies in English should register at pw.edu.pl/engpw/Admissions.

Deadlines

Admission is organized twice a year:

  • for Winter semester (studies starting in October) – by default, admission runs from April to July.
  • and Summer semester (studies starting in February) – by default, admission runs from November to December.

The list of offered programmes varies in each intake. Check when the programme you are interested in is available here: https://www.students.pw.edu.pl/Studies-Offer 

For more information about the current status of the application and pending deadlines, please visit this website: students.pw.edu.pl/index.php/Resources/Application-Glossary/Deadlines 

Scholarships

Warsaw University of Technology offers scholarships during studies for students with the highest GPA.

If you are looking for entry scholarships, please visit this website for more information on the scholarship programmes of the Polish National Agency for Academic Exchange (NAWA): students.pw.edu.pl/Resources/Scholarships.

For EU candidates, we recommend Poland, my first choice scholarship programme (second-cycle studies).

For non-EU candidates, we recommend Ignacy Łukasiewicz or Stefan Banach scholarship programmes (second-cycle studies).

More information

If you want to know more details about a given programme in English or have other questions to the Faculty, you can contact the Faculty coordinator of studies in English. 

For more information on the application process, please visit www.students.pw.edu.pl website.

 

For changes of the content of this page please contact: doc. dr inż. Tomasz Winek (tomasz.winek@pw.edu.pl).
