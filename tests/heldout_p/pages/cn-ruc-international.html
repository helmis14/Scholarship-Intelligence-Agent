    Specialization Track

    Currently, the IMBA program offers the specialization career track of “Financial Management”. To be qualified for the IMBA career track, students are required to complete 4 electives of 8 credits along many other electives we offer. An official RMBS Career Track Certificate will be awarded upon completion of qualifying electives and graduation.


    2

    Financing Your MBA

    RMBS offers students with many merit-based scholarships, high GMAT score award, financial aid, and exchange subsidy. Over 85% of our international students are offered some type of scholarship or financial aid.


    3

    Extracurricular Activities

    Various student clubs and cross-cultural activities enhance interaction between international and local students, and provide a more enriched extra-curricular life at RMBS.


    4

    Team Action Project

    The Team Action Project (TAP) is a distinctive managerial consulting program where students combine knowledge with practice applied to real-world projects.


    5

    International Exchanges

    Globally, RMBS has established over 50 MBA exchange and cooperation partnerships with leading business schools, allowing students to go overseas for semester-long exchange or study tours.


  • 6

    MBA Practical Ability Promotion Program

    The MBA Practical Ability Promotion (PAP) program matches students with senior managers to help students develop their networks and expose them to real business practices. Guest lecturers and CEOs are invited to the classroom to directly interact with MBA students. IMBA classes and company visits are often merged together to create the optimal learning environment for our students.


    7

    Business Competitions

    At RMBS, business competitions are held throughout the school year to offer students opportunities to develop innovative ideas and teamwork skills. Furthermore, through the network of our partners, our students can take part in many international competition opportunities such as the GNAM Investment Competition, Yale Case Competition, MBAT Competition, CEISM Competition and Globalization Insight Competition.


    8

    International Immersion & Global Opportunities

    Since 2013, our students have been participating in “Global Network Week” activities offered by member universities of the Global Network for Advanced Management (GNAM). Students participate in a weeklong study tour in leading business schools around the world, during which they interact with industry leaders, government officials, and faculty and students from other business schools.

    As another benefit of being a member of the GNAM, RMBS' MBA students can also enjoy GNAM’s global online courses (SNOCs) without needing to leave Beijing or the School.

    In recent years, we have established several partnerships with leading business schools around the world to provide our IMBA students with various dual and double degree opportunities.

    The Yale MAM program, as an example, is one of the most popular concurrent degree programs. Students may apply for the Yale MAM concurrent degree after their first semester in the IMBA program. Successful candidates will study for two master's degrees at the same time and gain insight into both western and eastern management approaches.
