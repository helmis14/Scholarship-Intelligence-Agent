Why IPB University?

IPB University as Southeast Asia’s global leader for Agro-maritime and Bioscience, seeking applications for the Rector IPB Scholarship. This Scholarship is for prospective international students enrolling in undergraduate and graduate degrees and non-degree (exchange).

The Goals:

  • Promotion – strengthening IPB’s global position
  • Creating new networks
  • Maintaining established collaborations
  • Attracting more international students
  • Promoting Indonesian language and culture

Scholarship Coverage

✅ Full Coverage

  1. Tuition and fees waiver (2000 USD/year)
  2. Living cost
  3. Health insurance
  4. Accommodation
  5. International flights

✅ Partial Scholarship

e.g: tuition and fees waiver only (2000 USD/year)

Important Dates

  • Admission Period: February- June
  • Exam I: May
  • Exam II: June
  • Application Period for Scholarship: April 1st – June 30th
  • Announcement: July 1st (Tentative)

Eligibility

  1. International students coming from abroad or those who are already in the country (priority country: Southeast Asia, Asia, Pacific, Africa, Middle East, South-Middle American and Kariba andeast Europe)
  2. Recommended by IPB’s counterparts (not mandatory)
  3. Recommended by the government (e.g. embassy, ministry, research institutions) (not mandatory)
  4. Excellent academic records
  5. Strong interest in IPB university and Indonesia

How to Apply?

Please fill out the form: https://ipb.university/rector-scholarship-form

FAQ

When can I apply for the Rector's Scholarship?

The Rector’s Scholarship application is open from April 1st to June 30th each year.

Who is eligible to apply for the scholarship?

Applicants must:

  • For undergraduate and graduate student
  • Have submitted their application to IPB University through the official admission portal
  • Pass the admission selection process and receive a Letter of Acceptance (LoA) from IPB University
  • Prepare and submit all required documents during the scholarship application period
  1. International students coming from abroad or those who are already in the country (priority country: Southeast Asia, Asia, Pacific, Africa, Middle East, South-Middle American and Kariba andeast Europe)
  2. Recommended by IPB’s counterparts (not mandatory)
  3. Recommended by the government (e.g. embassy, ministry, research institutions) (not mandatory)
  4. Excellent academic records
  5. Strong interest in IPB university and Indonesia

Where should I apply for admission to be considered for the scholarship?

  • Undergraduate Applicants: Apply via the International Class Admission Portal
  • Graduate Applicants: Apply through the Graduate Admission Portal

Can I apply for the scholarship before receiving the LoA (Letter of Acceptance)?

No. You can only apply for the Rector’s Scholarship after you have received the Letter of Acceptance (LoA) from IPB University.

What documents are required for the Rector's Scholarship application?

You must submit the following documents:

  1. Letter of Acceptance (LoA) from IPB University
  2. Certificate of Completion or Diploma
    • High school certificate for undergraduate applicants
    • Bachelor’s/Master’s certificate for graduate applicants
  3. Academic Transcript

How do I submit the scholarship application?

Fill out the application form: https://global.ipb.ac.id/rector-scholarship-forms/ 

What happend after I submit the scholarship application?

Your documents will be reviewed by the scholarship selection team. Selected applicants will be notified by email.

Is the application fee still required if I'm applying for the scholarship?

Yes. The application fee must be paid during admission, even if you intend to apply for the Rector’s Scholarship.

Where can I get more help or information?

  • Visit the IPB Admission Website
  • Email: dpi_ipb@apps.ipb.ac.id for scholarship-related inquiries
  • For graduate program information: https://pasca.ipb.ac.id/en/pendidikan/
