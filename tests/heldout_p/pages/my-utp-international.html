 Scholarship & Bursary

​Yayasan UTP is raising funds for four types of scholarships to provide opportunities while catering for the needs of various deserving students in realising their dreams and their greatest goals.


Yayasan UTP Prestigious Scholarship (Postgraduate)

Recognised as elite and the most lucrative, this scholarship is awarded to the 'creme de la creme' amongst highly intellectual students who have achieved excellent results in academics and demonstrate outstanding characteristics in leadership and civic consciousness through extra-curricular activities and community service, among others.

This prestigious scholarship is aimed at rewarding students for their outstanding achievements and motivating them to further excel in their lives.

Application criteria:

  • Malaysian
  • Not a holder of any scholarship award
  • Obtained a minimum CGPA of 3.25 in Bachelor's Degree & CGPA 3.25 (coursework/mix-mode) or at least one (1) published article in a peer-reviewed journal (research mode) for Master's Degree
  • Demonstrate a continuing involvement in extra-curricular activities (i.e. team or individual sports; voluntary service; affiliation with a recognised association or society) as well as special achievements in academics, social work or similar contributions
  • Possess strong appreciation and appetite for current affairs (i.e. general knowledge of Malaysia's constitutional and legal framework, economic levers, government initiatives and nation-building philosophies)
Please note that the scholarship application for this programme is temporarily unavailable.


Yayasan UTP Scholarship (Undergraduate)

This scholarship is awarded to deserving students who have achieved excellent results in their academics and demonstrate commendable characteristics and civic consciousness through extra-curricular activities and community service, among others.

Application Criteria:

  • Malaysian
  • Minimum 8A in SPM and CEFR (B1)
  • Household income of RM4,000 or below
  • Active in extra-curricular activities

Applications must be submitted via the i) PETRONAS Education Sponsorship portal and ii) YUTP Full Undergraduate Scholarship application form​. Only shortlisted candidates will be called for interview. 


Yayasan UTP Education Grant

The Yayasan UTP Education Grant is a financial assistance programme to finance the tuition fees of outstanding students from low to middle-income families in recognition of their academic achievements.

Duration of sponsorship: 5 years

Award: Full tuition fees / Partial tuition fees

Application criteria:

  • Malaysian citizen;
  • Current active UTP student;
  • Not a holder of any other scholarship award;
  • 17 – 30 years old;
  • Monthly household income of not more than RM10,000, and;
  • Obtained a minimum of 8A- in SPM (including Grade A- in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and English) or;
  • O-Level (minimum Grade B in Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and English) or;
  • Matriculation/Foundation – minimum CGPA 3.50 or;
  • STPM – minimum CGPA 3.50 (at least Grade A- in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry) or;
  • A-Level – at least Grade B (equivalent GPA 3.70) in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry or;
  • International Baccalaureate - minimum point 33 (at least Point 5 in Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry) or;
  • Diploma – minimum CGPA 3.50 (at least Grade B in Mathematics at SPM)

Assessment:

  • Cognify Cognitive Assessment (CCA)
  • Adept-15 Behavioral Assessment (ABA)
  • Views Motivation Assessment (VMA)
  • Verbal Communication Assessment (VCA)

Compulsory documents to be uploaded:

  • Copy of applicant's Identification Card
  • Latest academic qualification results
  • Parents' salary or income proof (You may use this ​income declaration form if needed)

Opening date: January, May & September​​

Deadline: 22 September 2026

Apply through: UCampus​​


Bursary (Student Support Fund)

The Student Support Fund provides one-off financial assistance to students in need to ensure financial pressures and unmet needs do not affect their academic performance.

Application Criteria:

  • Malaysian
  • Has completed at least one (1) semester at UTP
  • Open to PTPTN or self-sponsored students ONLY
  • Net household income of RM6,000 and below
  • Good disciplinary record
  • Obtained a minimum GPA of 2.75 for the immediate past semester

Each student is entitled to one financial assistance award for the entire study period in UTP. Application for this fund must be made through UTP Centre for Student Experience. Kindly contact Ms Sakinah (nursakinah.salim@utp.edu.my) for assistance.
