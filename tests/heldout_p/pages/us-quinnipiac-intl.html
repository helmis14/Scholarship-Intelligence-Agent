      First Year Online Appointments

      Current high school students are welcome to meet individually with an admissions counselor who can answer any questions you might have about Quinnipiac University and the application process. All appointments will be held virtually and require access to internet. Transfer students - please visit our Transfer visit page to schedule an online appointment.

      To reserve an appointment, click on a date on the calendar and select a time. You will select the format of your appointment on the registration form.

      The appointment options include:

      • Interview - formally meet with an admissions counselor for a Q&A about your high school experiences and college aspirations

      • Meeting - connect with an admissions counselor regarding questions about the admissions process or Quinnipiac University
