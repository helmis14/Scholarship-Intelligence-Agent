Montana State University offers millions of dollars in merit scholarships and waivers to new students with excellent academic records and outstanding promise for student success. Explore some of our opportunities below!

Fall 2027 scholarship information will be available later this fall. Scholarship awards may change each year and are only applicable for the specified year of enrollment. Please check back soon for more information for fall 2027!

Test Optional

Test scores are no longer required for admission or scholarship consideration at Montana State University for the foreseeable future. Please note that other academic information, including a self-reported cumulative GPA, is still required. Students can still submit ACT/SAT scores for math and writing placement purposes.
