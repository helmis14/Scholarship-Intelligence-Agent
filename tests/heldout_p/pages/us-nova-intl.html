Office of International Students and Scholars

Important Update: Court Action on Duration of Status (D/S) Rule:
On Monday, September 14, 2026, a federal judge in the U.S. District Court for the District of Massachusetts (Presidents' Alliance et al. v. DHS) has issued an injunction, temporarily blocking DHS from implementing this rule for F and J visa holders. As a result, the rule ending D/S will NOT take effect on September 15th as originally scheduled, and the current D/S framework remains in place for now.

What this means is that:

  • You and your dependents may continue to be admitted and to remain in the U.S. for Duration of Status, as long as you continue to comply with the terms of your program.
  • You do not currently need to apply for an Extension of Stay under the fixed-admission-period system the new rule would have created
  • If you are an F-1 student, you are not subject to the rule's new limits on changing your educational objective, transferring institutions, or pursuing additional programs of study – these restrictions also do not go into effect while the preliminary injunction is in place.
  • No action is required on your part at this time.
Please note that this is a preliminary court order, not a final resolution. The administration may appeal, and the underlying litigation is ongoing. The rule may still take effect later, depending on how the case proceeds. We will continue to monitor developments closely and update you as soon as anything changes.

 


On July 17, 2026, DHS published a final rule establishing a fixed time period of admission and an extension of stay procedure for F nonimmigrant academic students and J exchange visitors. This rule is effective on September 15, 2026, and will impact several aspects of the F-visa and J-visa program regulations. For detailed information regarding the DHS Final Rule, we encourage you to visit Study in the States, the official online resource managed by the U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) and the Student and Exchange Visitor Program (SEVP) by clicking HERE. Accordingly, updates to our website content are in progress.

 

Where Global Community Meets Excellence

 

Nova Southeastern University is home to over 900 international students from more than 100 countries, creating a diverse and vibrant campus community. With comprehensive educational opportunities, cultural and athletic events, and continuously improving facilities, NSU offers a top-tier experience, preparing students for success in a competitive world.

The Office of International Students and Scholars (OISS) provides immigration assistance and acts as a liaison with U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS). Our goal is to offer excellent service and ensure a remarkable overall experience through support services and events like international student orientation, International Education Week, and networking events.

Mission Statement 

With unfailing courtesy and respect, the Office of International Students and Scholars staff strives to exceed the expectations of our F and J visa holders by providing quality immigration-related services, guidance, and counseling.

F-1 and J-1 Student Advising

NSU’s international student advisers support you with academic questions, course planning, permits, and visas. Whether you're a current or prospective student, we’re here to help make your transition to NSU and South Florida smooth and seamless.

Advising Hours - Phone or In-person:

  • Monday and Wednesday: 9:00 a.m. - 12:00 p.m.
  • Tuesday and Thursday: 1:00 p.m. - 4:00 p.m.
