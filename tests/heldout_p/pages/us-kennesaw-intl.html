About KSU Education Abroad Scholarships


Scholarships are awarded based on eligibility criteria, academic merit, and financial need. Availability and award amounts may vary each cycle.

Kennesaw State University offers a variety of scholarships to support students participating in education abroad programs. When you apply, you are automatically considered for multiple scholarships through a single application.

Tips for a Strong Application

  • Start early and plan ahead for deadlines
  • Provide thoughtful, specific application responses
  • Demonstrate how your selected international experience aligns with your academic and personal goals
  • Do not use AI to generate content as your answer will not be unique
  • Application Process
  • Global Education Scholarships
  • Other KSU Scholarships
  • External Scholarships

Application Process

How It Works

  • Submit one application to be considered for all eligible KSU Education Abroad scholarships in ScholarshipUniverse
  • Applications are reviewed after each cycle closes
  • Awards vary based on eligibility and available funding 
  • Selected applicants are usually notified one to two months after the cycle closes

Application Cycles

  • Cycle 1: September 21st to November 15th
  • Cycle 2: Mid-December to Early February

Exact dates are announced each fall semester.

Application Requirements

Applicants may be asked to:

  • Respond to short essay prompts
  • Describe academic, personal, and professional goals
  • Explain financial need and how funding will support participation

Global Education Scholarships

Below are scholarships administered by Education Abroad. Eligibility requirements vary by award.

  • General Education Abroad Scholarships

    • Global Learning Financial Support
      • Award: $500
      • Open to students participating in education abroad programs

    • Global Education Staff Scholarship
      • Award: $500
      • Minimum 2.75 GPA
      • Full-time undergraduate student
      • Demonstrated financial need
      • Preference for first-time travelers

  • Program-Specific Scholarships

    • Fortezza Fund Education Abroad Scholarship
      • Award: $1,000
      • For students participating in the Montepulciano Summer Consortium program
      • Demonstrated financial need
    • Tom and Jane Keene Montepulciano Travel Scholarship
      • Award: $1,000 (two awards available)
      • Must be enrolled in the Journey Honors College
      • Participating in a Montepulciano program
      • Demonstrated financial need

  • College or Major-Specific Scholarships

    • Clark Family Education Abroad Scholarship
      • Award: $1,000
      • Must be a Coles College of Business undergraduate student
      • Preference for students from single-parent households

    • ENPLAS International Education Scholarship
      • Award: $1,000
      • Minimum 3.0 GPA
      • College of Science and Mathematics students
      • Undergraduate or graduate
      • Demonstrated financial need

    • Brian Olcott LaRaia Travelling Scholarship
      • Award: $1,200
      • Minimum 3.0 GPA
      • Humanities and Social Sciences students
      • First-time education abroad participants
      • Demonstrated financial need

    • William W. Davidson International Archaeology Scholarship
      • Award: $1,000
      • Anthropology majors with archaeology focus
      • Participation in an international archaeological field experience
      • Includes post-program reporting and presentation
      • Demonstrated financial need

    • Hughes Family Study Abroad Scholarship
      • Award: $5,000 (Africa programs)
      • $3,000 (other locations)
      • Coles College of Business students
      • Preference for programs in Africa
      • Minimum 3.0 GPA

    • Shelby R. Wilkes & Jettie M. Burnette Global Fellows (MBA)
      • Award: $1,500
      • MBA students
      • Minimum 3.5 GPA

    • True Family Education Abroad Scholarship
      • Award: $500
      • Undergraduate or graduate students
      • Preference for Business or Arts students

    • Dr. Marietta Monaghan Endowed Travel Scholarship  
      • More details coming soon!
      • Full-time undergraduate student majoring in Architecture in their third, fourth, or fifth year 
      • Active in Student Organization(s): AIAS, NOMAS, Alpha Rho Chi, CACM MAC
      • Minimum 2.5 GPA

    • Rizzuto Family Endowed Travel Scholarship
      • More details coming soon!
      • Full-time undergraduate student majoring in Architecture 
      • Junior or Senior status 
      • Minimum 2.5 GPA

  • Regional or Program-Focused Scholarships

    • Christina and Thomas Emerson Scholarship
      • Covers program fee plus airfare (excludes tuition/fees)
      • Summer programs outside Western Europe
      • Must include a community engagement component
      • Minimum 3.0 GPA
      • Demonstrated financial need

    • Takada Scholarship for International Education
      • Award: $1,000 (two awards available)
      • Summer programs outside Western Europe
      • Minimum 3.0 GPA
      • Demonstrated financial need

  • Special Populations and Student Type Scholarships

    • Michele Miller International Student Scholarship
      • Award: $1,000 (two awards available)
      • International students with valid visa
      • Minimum 3.0 GPA
      • Renewable up to four years
