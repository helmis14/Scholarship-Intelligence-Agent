We want you at GU!

Our international student advisors are happy to help you navigate the path to studying at Gonzaga. Remember, when planning to study abroad, you should consider both tuition and living expenses.

Because Gonzaga is a private institution, international students pay the same tuition as domestic students. Tuition and fees vary by level of education and by program, so please review estimates for each program.

Scholarships

To help support your studies, Gonzaga offers a wide range of scholarships that international students may be eligible for. Additionally, many international students qualify for on-campus employment. If you have any questions, please contact Theresa Howlett at: howlett@gonzaga.edu.
