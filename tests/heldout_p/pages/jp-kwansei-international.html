For Graduate Students

(1) KGU Graduate School Scholarship for International Students
▪️Tentative Selection Pending University Entrance
Eligible applicants: Those who hold "student" residence status or are recognized as international students by the applicable graduate school
Amount awarded: An amount equivalent to 50% of annual tuition fees

* The screening for this scholarship is based on the entrance examination results, etc. Those selected to obtain this scholarship will receive notification of the scholarship award before they enroll at KGU. With the awarding of the scholarship already determined in advance of enrollment, prospective scholarship awardees can enroll at KGU without any financial concerns.

▪️Selection after University Entrance
Eligible applicants: Those who hold "student" residence status or are recognized as international students by the applicable graduate school
Amount awarded: An amount equivalent to 35% of annual tuition fees
(2) KGU Tuition Reduction for Privacy Funded International Students
Under this program, KGU offers 30% reduction of tuition fees (as of this year) for privately-funded international students studying at a regular (degree-seeking) graduate course and hold "student" residence status.

* The reduction does not apply to those who have attended KGU beyond the minimum years required to complete a course of study.
* The amount of tuition reduction for the next academic year and beyond has not been decided.
* Undergraduate students are not eligible.
