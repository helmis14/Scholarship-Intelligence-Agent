International Graduate School of Neuroscience

The International Graduate School of Neuroscience (IGSN) offers an English-language PhD program covering all aspects of neuroscience, from the molecular level to higher cognitive functions. The IGSN was founded in October 2001 as an initiative of the Northrhine-Westphalian government to provide a forum for PhD candidates with outstanding academic track records. The IGSN offers an international network of renowned scientists, world-leading supervisors, and an excellent scientific environment.

Read more...

The IGSN provides PhD candidates with a comprehensive, multidisciplinary research training program in basic, cognitive, clinical, and computational neuroscience. A key goal of the IGSN is to give PhD candidates the opportunity to conduct high-level research in a supportive educational environment that equips them to work in a multidisciplinary manner in the neuroscience field. An international PhD in neuroscience, combined with additional training in management and complementary skills, aims to produce a new generation of young neuroscientists capable of competing at the highest professional and international levels.
