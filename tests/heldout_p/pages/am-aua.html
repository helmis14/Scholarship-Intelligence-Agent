    The American University of Armenia is a leading institution in the country with a goal to educate the best and brightest students and equip them with a world class education to become forward thinking and innovative citizens of Armenia. AUA’s need-blind admission policy ensures this reality for students, encouraging students from all over Armenia to apply and become a part of the AUA community.

    Your contribution towards the scholarship program, whether through the establishment of an Endowed Named Scholarship or an Annual Named Scholarship, will help AUA invest in talented students, allowing them to carry forward towards their path to obtain an excellent education, and jumpstart their careers in a multitude of fields.

    WAYS TO ESTABLISH NAMED SCHOLARSHIPS

    $50,000+

    Minimum investment of $50,000 may be pledged over a 5 year period

    Endowed Named Scholarship

    An Endowed Named Scholarship creates a lasting legacy by providing scholarship support in perpetuity. The principal is invested through AUA's endowment, which is professionally managed by the University of California (UC). Only the investment earnings are awarded as scholarships, preserving the principal while generating sustainable support for generations of AUA students.

    Endowed scholarships may be established in honor or memory of a loved one, creating a meaningful and enduring tribute. A minimum commitment of $50,000, payable over a period of up to five years, is required to establish an Endowed Named Scholarship. The full benefit to the student will be realized once the endowment principal reaches $50,000.

    Annual Named Scholarship

    Full support — $6,000  |  Partial support — $3,000

    The annual cost of attendance at AUA is approximately $11,000 per student. However, AUA subsidizes tuition for Armenian citizens, who pay between $6,110 and $7,460, depending on their field of study. Today, approximately half of AUA students depend on need-based aid from the university.

    An Annual Named Scholarship may be established with a minimum gift of $6,000 to fully fund one student's tuition need or $3,000 to provide partial tuition assistance for an academic year.

    Most donors choose multi-year pledges so their scholar is supported from first year through graduation.

    Donors may choose to designate their scholarship to support students pursuing a specific field of study, aligning their philanthropy with the academic discipline or profession they are most passionate about.
