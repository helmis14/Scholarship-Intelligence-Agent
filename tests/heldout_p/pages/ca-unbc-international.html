Awards and Financial Aid helps students obtain the funds necessary to finance their UNBC education and to provide educational budgeting services. Funds may be obtained from a variety of sources, including government and bank loans, private, corporate, and public scholarships and bursaries, and UNBC awards.

 

Main Awards Competition 2027-2028 Academic Year

The main awards competition for the 2027-2028 academic year is now open!

To apply, students must be admitted to the University and have received a user ID and PIN number for their myUNBC student account to access the online award applications.

The deadline to submit applications for the main awards competition is April 1.

Refer to our undergraduate awards or graduate awards webpage for more information.

Recommended award applications

Grade 12 or new undergraduate students:

  • Early Entrance Awards (new undergraduates entering directly from high school): No application required. Apply for early admission by January 15.
  • Leadership Awards (new undergraduates): Apply for early admission. Submit application via your myUNBC Student Account. Deadline January 15.
  • Entrance Awards: Deadline April 1
  • General Awards: Deadline April 1

New graduate or doctoral students:

  • Master's Doctoral Level Awards: Deadline Apr 1st
  • General Awards: Deadline Apr 1st

Continuing undergraduate students and graduate or doctoral award students:

  • UNBC students already in-course (i.e., students continuing in their undergraduate, graduate or doctoral programs) are able to apply using the myUNBC Student Account once the competition opens. Deadline April 1.

Other prestigious award information for graduate applicants / students

For graduate students or applicants (master's and PhD) review the Graduate Entrance Research Scholarship (GERS) information and British Columbia Graduate Scholarships Program (BCGSP) information on the graduate awards webpage. You may also want to check out the Office of Research and Innovation internal and external award competitions and teaching assistantship career opportunities.
