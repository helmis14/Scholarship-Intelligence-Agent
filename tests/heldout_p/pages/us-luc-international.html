International Admissions

Application Process

1. Complete the online application.

Submit the Common Application or Loyola’s online application. There is no application fee. 

2. Send your high school transcripts.

Send us an official transcript from every high school/secondary school you’ve attended. If you are enrolled in your final year of high school, please send records for the previous three years of school completed. If necessary, you should provide a translated copy in English. International transcripts may be sent electronically by school officials to internationaladmission@luc.edu.  

3. Submit proof of English proficiency.

The application may require you to submit official TOEFL, IELTS, PTE, or Duolingo English Test scores as proof of proficiency. A minimum score of 4.5 (79)* is required for the TOEFL, 6.5 for IELTS, 53 for the PTE, and 110 for Duolingo.

*4.5 applies to tests taken after January 1, 2026 while a 79 applies to tests taken before that date

4. Ask for a recommendation letter.

A letter of recommendation may come from a teacher or school counselor who can speak to your ability to be successful at Loyola. Students using the Common Application will submit a letter of recommendation through that system. Students using the Loyola application may send recommendation letter requests within the application. Recommendation letters may also be sent via email to admissionforms@luc.edu. 

NOTE: Letters must always come directly from a teacher or counselor to be considered.

OPTIONAL: Submit your ACT or SAT scores.

Loyola is test-optional so students can decide if they would like to include ACT and/or SAT scores in their application. Loyola superscores results, meaning we review the highest subscore from each section over multiple exams. If you include test scores, we recommend sharing all results. Students may self-report test scores or send them to Loyola via the testing agencies. If accepted, you will need to have the testing agency send your official scores to Loyola by May 1.  

  • SAT Code: 1412  
  • ACT Code: 1064 

Optional: Tell us more about yourself with a resume and essay.

Complete the activities section of your chosen application or submit a resume after you've submitted the application. Share more about yourself through an essay or personal statement. 

Deadlines

Fall Semester Start

  • August 1: Application opens
  • December 1: Priority application deadline
  • May 1: Enrollment deposit deadline

Note: Loyola admits students after the December 1 priority deadline as long as space is available. If space is available in the first-year class, Loyola will continue to accept deposits after May 1.

Spring Semester Start

  • August 1: Application opens
  • November 1: Priority application deadline
  • January 1: Enrollment deposit deadline

Note: Loyola admits students after the November 1 priority deadline as long as space is available. If space is available in the first-year class, Loyola will continue to accept deposits after January 1.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for a student visa?

If you need a student visa, you will receive additional instructions after your admission decision. You will be invited to the International Student and Scholar Services Portal through your LUC email address to complete an I-20 Request. Be prepared to upload a copy of the ID page from your passport, supporting documents showing funds available for the total cost of the year of study, and an Affidavit of Support (if applicable). These items are not required to receive your admission decision. 

Are there exemptions for the English proficiency requirement?

We do not waive English proficiency based on country of origin or medium of instruction. However, if you are taking AP or IB classes we can review your transcripts to see if you can receive an English language proficiency requirement waiver. You are also welcome to submit the SAT or ACT as proof of English proficiency if you prefer. Please have your test scores sent directly to us from the testing website. 

How can I see if my application materials have arrived?

Visit your student portal to review your application checklist. Keep in mind that it does take time for materials to be processed and connected to your application file, so please be patient. If you are concerned that materials are missing or lost, please contact your admission counselor.   

Where can I view my admission decision?

Your admission decision will be available on your student portal, and we will email you once a decision is made. Admitted students will also receive a letter to their home address with the good news.  

Who will review my application?

Your admission counselor will review your application. They read every single page and are excited to learn more about you during the process. 

How can I apply for a scholarship?

You’re automatically considered for merit scholarships when you apply for admission. Initial merit scholarship information will be shared in your acceptance letter. Loyola will post additional scholarships that require separate applications on your portal in early January.  

Can I transfer credit from AP, CLEP, IB, or Cambridge programs?

Yes! It’s not required at the review of your application, but once you’re admitted, you should submit this information during the summer before your first semester. Learn more about Loyola’s transfer credit policy.  

Can I transfer credit from a college as a new first-year student?

Yes, incoming first-year students can receive credit for college courses completed during high school. Learn more about Loyola’s transfer credit policy.  

Can I transfer here from another university as an international student?

Yes! Please make sure to send copies of transcripts from all post-secondary institutions you’ve attended, and check out specific requirements for transfer students. 

How can I apply as a home-schooled student?

Home school students should follow all the steps described above. Your Secondary School Report should be completed by your primary instructor or supervisor of your school program, and describe the curriculum and methods used. A letter of recommendation should come from someone other than a family member. Transcripts with course titles and grades from secondary school are required. Official transcripts from any enrollment in traditional high school, community college or university level work must be provided.

Who do I contact for other questions?

Email us at internationaladmission@LUC.edu and we’ll get back to you as soon as we can.
