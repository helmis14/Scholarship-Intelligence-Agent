Proč už nepodporujeme prohlížeč Internet Explorer

Prohlížeč Internet Explorer již nepodporujeme a také důrazně nedoporučujeme, protože dlouhodobě funguje bez zásadních aktualizací, vylepšení a podpory. Obsahuje řadu závažných bezpečnostních rizik, které mohou způsobit únik citlivých informací a vašich osobních dat. Nehledě k tomu, že prohlížení a práce s webovými stránkami je v něm pomalejší až o 50 % v porovnání s ostatními prohlížeči.


Které prohlížeče tedy používat a kde si je můžete stáhnout?

Změna prohlížeče je jednoduchá, nezabere vám moc času a my vám s ní pomůžeme. Nachystali jsme pro vás seznam doporučených prohlížečů.

Vyberte si prohlížeč a klikněte na jeho název. Otevře se vám stránka, na které si prohlížeč můžete bezpečně nainstalovat.

TIP: Klidně si můžete nainstalovat všechny a vyzkoušet si, jak se vám s nimi pracuje.

A nebojte se, že změnou přijdete o všechny záložky, historii a uložená hesla. Můžete si je totiž jednoduše přenést. Postup pro dva nejpoužívanější prohlížeče přidáváme.

Merit-based Scholarship

Full-time students enrolled in degree study programmes taught in the English language can apply for the merit-based scholarship.

Conditions

The scholarship shall be granted to the student provided the following requirements are met:

  • The student has complied with all study obligations set out in the study plan in the semester for which they submitted the application
  • The student has obtained a minimum of 25 credits by the end of the examination period of the given semester
  • The student's total study average is in the range of 1.00–1.80

Documents

  • Application Form
  • 2 Letters of Recommendation issued by UCT lecturers
  • Transcript of Records

Steps

  1. Fill in and sign the Application form
  2. Ask two teachers to write a Letter of Recommendation for you
  3. Get the Transcript of Records
  4. Scan the documents, upload them to the online form, and submit your application

You can download the Transcript of Records from your SIS (Study Information System) on the Tab Summary of Examination Results, by clicking the tiny button on the right side "Show PDF," OR you can visit your faculty's Study Officer, who can issue the Transcript of Records for you.

ONLINE FORM: Submit the application

Miroslav Vlček Scholarship

The Miroslav Vlček Extraordinary Scholarship is a form of honoring the memory of professor Miroslav Vlček – the spiritual father of the idea and the coordinator of the Study in Prague consortium (2015–2019), which brought together seven Prague public universities and attracted hundreds of foreign students to study in Prague.

The Miroslav Vlček Extraordinary Scholarship aims to contribute to the higher internationalization of public universities in Prague by increasing the number of foreign students studying in foreign language study programs.

Conditions

  • Students who are not citizens of the Czech Republic may apply for a scholarship
  • The student must study an English-taught bachelor's, master's, or doctoral full-time degree program
  • Disciplinary proceedings may not be conducted against the student
  • In the year the scholarship is awarded, the student will not exceed 36 years of age
  • The application for the scholarship is submitted by the student on the prescribed application form 

Application Form

The application form contains the applicant's identification data, a description of his/her contribution to the internationalization of the study environment of UCT Prague, and the signature of at least one lecturer expressing his/her support of the application for the scholarship. The application can be submitted directly by the student or by his/her lecturer to the Department of International Relations.

Incomplete or incorrectly filled forms will be excluded from the selection process.

Application Deadline: 11. 11. 

Selection Process 

When selecting a scholarship awardee, his/her extraordinary contribution to the strengthening of the international reputation of UCT Prague and studies in Prague is evaluated. E.g. exceptional extracurricular activities in the form of project involvement, research, spreading the good name of UCT Prague and Czech higher education in the world, etc.

Consideration is also given to the quality of the application process and whether the student has already received a scholarship in previous years.

The awarding of the scholarship is decided by the Vice-Rector for International Relations. The Vice-Rector can also decide not to award the scholarship.

Announcement of the Result: by 30. 11. 

Nominated students will be informed about the results of the selection process by authorized staff.

The scholarship-winning student will be invited to the Scholarship Award Ceremony.

Examples of Contributions to the Strengthening of the International Reputation of UCT Prague and Studies in Prague

  • Participate in Exchange Programs: Engage in study abroad or exchange programs, representing the university and sharing positive experiences with peers in other countries.
  • Join International Student Organizations: Get involved in organizations that promote cultural exchange and collaboration, helping to build the university's international community.
  • Share Experiences on Social Media: Use platforms like Instagram or LinkedIn to highlight academic achievements, campus events, and personal growth while attending the university.
  • Engage in Research and Conferences: Participate in international conferences or research projects, showcasing the university's academic strengths and innovations.
  • Network with International Peers: Build connections with students from other universities, sharing insights about the university’s programs and opportunities.
  • Act as an Ambassador: Take on roles like student ambassador or peer mentor, providing prospective international students with information and support about the university.
  • Promote Cultural Understanding: Organize or participate in cultural events that celebrate diversity, fostering positive perceptions of the university among international audiences.
  • Write Articles or Blogs: Contribute to blogs or publications that highlight student life and experiences at the university, reaching a broader audience.
  • Collaborate on Global Projects: Engage in community service or international projects that align with the university's values, enhancing its reputation through impactful work.
  • Encourage Prospective Students: Share personal experiences with prospective students from other countries, encouraging them to consider the university for their education.
