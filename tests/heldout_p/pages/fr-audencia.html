Scholarships and funding for international students at Audencia

Financing solutions to help you make your project a reality.

Audencia Business School offers merit-based scholarships to international students, alongside access to French government funding programmes including the Eiffel Excellence Scholarship (up to €1,200/month for Master's students) and Campus France grants. Tuition fees range from €19,400 for a one-year MSc to €36,000 for the full two-year Master in Management. This page gives you a complete overview of all funding options available to help finance your studies at Audencia.

Audencia scholarships — what is available and how to apply

How to Finance Your Studies at Audencia

Audencia awards scholarships based on academic merit and, in some cases, financial need. Scholarships are assessed at the admissions stage, no separate application is required. The earlier you apply, the better your chances, as places and funding are allocated on a rolling basis.

Quality applications are given priority. The Early application and the Project-based scholarships for our International MBA program cannot be cumulated.

Merit-based scholarship

Awarded to candidates with an outstanding academic record. The scholarship reduces tuition fees by a percentage determined at the time of your admission offer. To be considered: submit your application as early as possible with your full academic transcripts and any evidence of academic distinction or awards.

Diversity and inclusion scholarship

Audencia is committed to welcoming students from all backgrounds. Specific scholarships may be available for students from underrepresented regions or backgrounds. Contact the International Admissions Office directly to discuss your situation: international@audencia.com

Early application discount

Candidates who confirm their place by a given deadline may benefit from a tuition reduction. Contact the admissions team for the current conditions: international@audencia.com

Audencia scholarships

Students receiving CROUS financial aid who are admitted to the Grande École or Bachelor in Management programs may qualify for need-based admission scholarships, subject to specific eligibility criteria for each program.

Within the Grande École program, students receiving CROUS financial aid at Level 7 are eligible for full tuition coverage for the entire program. Students at Levels 5 and 6 are eligible for 100% tuition coverage during their first year (Pre-Master's year).

For more information, visit the “Admission Scholarships” page for each program:

  • Grande École program (link coming soon)
  • Bachelor in Management program

During their studies, students may qualify for Audencia scholarships awarded on the basis of financial need. To apply, students must submit a scholarship application during their program, within the application periods specified by their respective programs.

Depending on the participating program's policies and the student's financial circumstances, these scholarships may cover between 10% and 50%, or even up to 60%, of the tuition fees for the academic year.

Envoléo Scholarship

The Pays de la Loire Regional Council provides financial support for international stays of at least three months, whether for an internship or further study abroad, as part of a degree program based in the Pays de la Loire region.

More information

Erasmus+ Study Grant

The Erasmus+ grant provides financial assistance to students wishing to complete part of their studies in one of the 28 European countries, Turkey, Iceland, Norway, North Macedonia, or Liechtenstein.

More information

Who is eligible to apply?

Students who hold official recognition of their status as a disabled worker (Reconnaissance de la Qualité de Travailleur Handicapé, or RQTH), or who have a pending RQTH application, provided they submit proof that their application has been filed with the Departmental Office for Persons with Disabilities (Maison Départementale des Personnes Handicapées, or MDPH).

What is the scholarship amount?

The scholarship can cover up to 10% of the tuition fees for the student's program during the current academic year.

The scholarship may be renewed each academic year by submitting a new application.

When can students apply?

There are two application periods each year: October and May.

Need-based scholarships, mobility grants, support for entrepreneurial projects, emergency financial assistance, and more: thanks to the generosity of its many donors and partners, the Audencia Foundation supports various student populations at Audencia, helping them continue their studies and bring their student projects to life.

French government scholarships — funding you may not know about

France has one of the most generous scholarship systems in the world for international students. These programmes are funded by the French government and are open to students enrolling at Audencia.

The External Scholarships & Funding list below is for information purposes only. All terms and conditions are at the discretion of the funding institution. Audencia has agreements with certain of these institutions but not all. Please contact us to find out.

The Eiffel Excellence Scholarship Programme is funded by the French Ministry
for Europe and Foreign Affairs. It is one of the most prestigious scholarships
available to international students in France.

What it covers:

- Monthly stipend of €1,200 at Master's level (from January 2026)
- International travel costs (outbound and return)
- National transport assistance
- Health insurance
- Housing search assistance
- Cultural activities

Who can apply:

- Non-French nationals only (dual nationality including French is not eligible)
- Maximum age 29 at Master's level
- Students who are not currently studying in France
- Priority fields include Economics & Management — Audencia's programmes are eligible

How it works:
The scholarship is not applied for directly by students. Audencia nominates
candidates on your behalf. You must first apply to Audencia and be offered a
place. The school then submits your Eiffel application to Campus France.

Key dates (2026 call):

- Application window: October 1 – January 8, 2026
- Results published: from March 30, 2026

To be considered, contact international@audencia.com as early as October with
your complete application dossier.

More information: campusfrance.org/en/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-program

Campus France manages a wide range of bilateral scholarships between France and
partner countries. Depending on your nationality, you may be eligible for
government-to-government grants that cover part or all of your tuition and
living costs.

How to check your eligibility:
Go to campusbourses.campusfrance.org and enter your country to see all
scholarships available to students from your country studying in France.

If you are fiscally domiciled in France (or your parents are), you may be
eligible for a CROUS social grant — the French national student support scheme.
CROUS grants are means-tested and can significantly reduce your tuition fees
at Audencia, as the school adjusts its fees according to your CROUS level.

More information: messervices.etudiant.gouv.fr

The Pays de la Loire region, where Nantes is located, offers mobility grants
and support for students choosing to study in the region. These are particularly
relevant for students doing exchange semesters or internships outside France
as part of their Audencia programme.

More information: paysdelaloire.fr (section Bourses & Aides)

Other ways to fund your studies at Audencia

Payment in instalments

Audencia offers the possibility to pay tuition fees in several instalments rather than in one lump sum. Contact the admissions team at the time of your application to discuss the available options: international@audencia.com

Student loans — bank partnerships

Audencia has partnerships with banking institutions that offer student loans at preferential rates for enrolled students. Details are provided during the
admissions process.

Work-study (apprentissage)

Some Audencia programmes are available in apprentissage (work-study) format, where your employer covers part or all of your tuition fees. This option is available for certain MSc and Mastère Spécialisé programmes. Check individual programme pages for eligibility. Note: the Master in Management (Grande Ecole) is not available in work-study format.

Scholarships from your home country

Many governments, foundations and companies offer scholarships specifically for students studying abroad. Before applying to Audencia, check:

  • Your national ministry of education or higher education
  • The French embassy in your country
  • Major companies and foundations in your country that support international study
  • Campus France's country-specific scholarship database (campusbourses.campusfrance.org)

Paid Work Opportunities with Companies

Apprenticeship programs allow you to finance your education while combining classroom learning with hands-on professional experience at a company.

Beyond their educational benefits, apprenticeships allow you to become an employee of a company and earn a salary throughout the duration of your contract, based on the statutory minimum wage, which may be negotiated under certain conditions.

Throughout the apprenticeship, your tuition fees are covered through the apprenticeship funding system.

For more information, visit the “Apprenticeship Program” page for your degree program.

Most programs at Audencia include a company internship period. These internships are generally paid.

How to maximise your chances of getting financial support — step by step

  1. Apply to Audencia as early as possible (admissions open from autumn)
    Early applicants are considered first for merit scholarships and Eiffel nominations.
  2. Check your Eiffel eligibility immediately
    If you are under 29, not currently studying in France, and your field is Economics & Management — contact international@audencia.com in October to be considered for Eiffel nomination.
  3. Check Campus France scholarships for your country
    Go to campusbourses.campusfrance.org and filter by your nationality.
  4. Ask about payment plans at the time of your offer
    Once you receive your admission offer, ask the admissions team about instalment plans and bank partnerships.
  5. Confirm your place early for any early application discount
    Ask the admissions team about the current early confirmation discount.
