International Student Admissions

With close to 1,000 international students from 103 nations, UND offers a variety of cultural, recreational and academic programs.

Located in Grand Forks, N.D., the University of North Dakota offers 250+ academic programs. Our University is the best school on the Northern Plains with world-recognized graduate and online programs.

We offer international students an experience like no other. You'll work with faculty who are experts in their field, gain experience in research and hands-on learning and you'll become part of the UND family.

Campus Community

On campus, the UND International Center is here to help. They can answer your questions on everything from academic support to student visas and American culture.

Paying for College

At UND, you'll find one of the best education values in the nation. It's our priority to help all students afford UND through financial aid, including scholarships and loans.

Tuition & Payment

Financial Aid

Although many scholarships at U.S. schools may seem reserved for U.S. citizens, some are available to international and Canadian students as well. Contact the One-Stop Student Services and search the Scholarship Central.
