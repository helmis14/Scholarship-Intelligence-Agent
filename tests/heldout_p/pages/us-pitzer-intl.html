International Fellowships

Finding Your Fellowship
  • International Fellowships
    • Watson Fellowship Application Instructions
  • National Fellowships

International fellowships provide funding to go abroad. An international fellowship can help you gain experience learning a language, conduct research in a foreign country, or take courses at some of the best universities in the world. And we're here to help you get one, whether you're browsing programs or actively applying.

Napier Fellowship

The Napier Fellowship is an intergenerational mentorship award offered by Pilgrims Place in Claremont. Select fellows will have the opportunity to receive one of two financial awards for up to $20,000. Awards will be given to graduating seniors who demonstrate outstanding leadership promise in the areas of social justice, peace, and environmental sustainability. Each of the five Claremont undergraduate colleges can nominate up to three students for the awards.
