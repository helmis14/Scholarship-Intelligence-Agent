Limited number of student fellowships are available to cover the  registration fee to attend this conference. Please send an email to imaps@ee.iitb.ac.in if you are interested in these fellowships.

Eligibility Criteria for Fellowships:
 

1.  Must be a student member of IEEE for at least 2 years

2.  Must be registered with a University/College for a degree

3.  Must have demonstrated interest in Microelectronics & VLSI (given seminars, undertaken projects, attended   short term courses on Microelectronics & VLSI, etc.)

4.  Since there are a limited number of fellowships available, all cases meeting the above criteria will be screened by  the organizing committee and a final decision will be communicated by October 31, 2005
 

The fellowship does not cover travel costs. The fellowship would only cover waiver of registration fee for the tutorials and the conference and free accommodation in IIT hostels
