International Student Admission
Explore admission requirements and support services for international students at Samford University. Join a welcoming, Christ-centered community committed to your academic and personal success.
 
Samford University welcomes students from around the world into a Christ-centered community, nationally ranked for academic excellence. This page has been curated to provide clarity to the admission and enrollment processes for international students applying for undergraduate admission to Samford.

Undergraduate Admission Checklist

  • Undergraduate admission application
  • Personal essay
  • Official transcripts (translated to English)
    • If your courses were not taught in English, you may also be required to provide a third-party evaluation. Samford currently accepts course evaluations through the following providers:
      • WES – World Education Services
      • TEC – The Evaluation Company
      • ECE – Education Credential Evaluators
    • Transfer applicants:
      • Samford requires official college transcripts to be sent directly from every postsecondary institution where the applicant was enrolled. We will accept transcripts electronically via E-script and Docufide.
      • Applicants who have earned 24 or more college credits are not required to submit a high school transcript.
      • Applicants with less than 24 college credits are required to submit a final high school transcript that documents secondary school completion date.
    • Academic recommendation
    • Official ACT/SAT scores*
    • $50 application fee
    • Official government-issued identification (passport, visa)
    • Identity verification
    • English proficiency test scores*
      • English proficiency tests required if your high school courses were not taught in English
        • Favorable admission will be offered to students with the following recommended scores:
          • TOEFL:
            • IBT: 90 or above
            • Computer: 232 or above
            • Paper: 575 or above
            *Samford’s test code for TOEFL is R1302
          • IELTS: 6.5 or above
          • Duolingo: 120 or above

Admitted Student Enrollment Checklist

Admitted undergraduate students ready to enroll at Samford can secure their space in the entering class by submitting their Decision Response Form and $300 enrollment deposit by the stated enrollment deadline.

The enrollment process includes, but is not limited to, the following checklist:

  • Residence Hall Application (for residential students)
  • Housing Contract (for residential students)
  • Registration for Bulldog Days New Student Orientation
  • Financial Affidavit and financial support documentation
  • Immunization Forms
  • Health Insurance
  • Registration for Samford Immigration Check-in Day
  • Pre-Orientation Survey
  • FERPA Agreement

VISA

SamfordGlobal maintains important new international student information for obtaining and maintaining a VISA to study at Samford University. Enrolled students may contact SamfordGlobal with VISA-related questions.

Life at Samford

SamfordGlobal

The SamfordGlobal Office promotes international education and intercultural understanding; enriching the academic and cultural environment of Samford University by facilitating the exchange of people and ideas and collaborating with faculty and staff to make international opportunities accessible for all Samford students.

Samford’s commitment to providing international programs to its students stems from the school’s mission to nurture persons in the development of intellect, creativity, faith and personhood.

Student Life

Student Life at Samford offers endless possibilities. Making the decision to enroll at Samford University means being immersed in a vibrant community that offers hundreds of student clubs and organizations.
