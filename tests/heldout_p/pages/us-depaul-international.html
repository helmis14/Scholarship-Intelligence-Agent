International Admission

A Home for Global Changemakers

DePaul University welcomes international students with personalized support and multilingual resources. Join peers from over 100 countries, earn scholarships and pursue prestigious STEM programs with support towards career growth. Experience a vibrant, purpose-driven community that empowers you to build a global career and make a positive impact.

How to Apply

Step 1: Application

Begin your application by following these simple steps:

  • Select the appropriate application type: freshman, transfer or graduate.
  • Gather required documents, such as transcripts, test scores and recommendations.
  • Submit your online application and pay the application fee, if applicable.

Step 2: Supporting Documents

To complete your application, please ensure the following documents are prepared and submitted:

  • Transcripts:
    Submit official U.S. transcripts or for international credential evaluation, a course-by-course evaluation from a university approved agency.
  • English Proficiency Tests:
    Provide TOEFL, IELTS, Duolingo or other accepted scores, if applicable.

Step 3: Deadlines

For details on deadlines according to your admission category, see the following section.

Undergraduate Application Deadlines:

  • Early Action: November 15
  • Regular Decision: February 1
  • Transfer: Rolling admissions

Graduate Deadlines:

  • Graduate application deadlines vary by program.
  • Explore specific deadlines for each program.
Types of Admission
Now that you’re all set to apply, let’s first determine which type of applicant you are and what steps you need to follow.

  • Freshman

    Are you ready to launch your college career directly after high school? If you are applying as a freshman, find detailed application instructions.

  • Transfer

    Already earned college credits? You can transfer your coursework and continue your studies at DePaul. If you’re a transfer student, find detailed application instructions.

    DePaul also offers formal transfer agreements with several international institutions, so you can start your degree at home and transfer to DePaul.

  • Graduate

    Ready to pursue a master's or PhD program? Learn more about the application process today.

  • Professional

    Ready to pursue a professional program? Learn more about our College of Law.

  • Visiting

    Want to study at DePaul for a year or less while earning your degree at home? Our visiting student program lets you do just that. Learn more about the application process.

  • Exchange Programs

    Students currently enrolled in a partner institution are qualified to take part in our exchange program. Learn more about eligibility and requirements.
