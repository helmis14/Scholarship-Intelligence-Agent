Title
Peterborough

Description

Defined by the flow of the historic Trent-Severn Waterway and shaped by iconic architecture, Trent’s 1,400-acre Peterborough, Ontario campus is a place where ideas move as freely as the Otonabee River that runs through it. Trent’s welcoming Peterborough Campus is a place of possibility, curiosity, and beauty.

Title
Durham Greater Toronto Area

Description

Located in Oshawa, Ontario, on the doorstep of Canada’s largest job market, in one of Canada’s fastest growing regions, Trent’s Durham Greater Toronto Area Campus is built for connection. Here, where education is shaped by real-world experiences and community engagement, with one of the best student-to-faculty ratios in Ontario.
