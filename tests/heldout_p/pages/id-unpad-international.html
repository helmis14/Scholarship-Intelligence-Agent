    Scholarships for International Students in Unpad

    At Universitas Padjadjaran, international students have the opportunity to be awarded with scholarships, which include:

    ​

    1. KNB Scholarship (Developing Countries Partnership Scholarship) 

    2. TIAS (The Indonesian AID Scholarship)

    3. UAS (Unpad ASEAN Scholarship)

     

    Through these scholarships, Unpad aims to contribute to regional and global development and to enhance cross-border cooperation with other countries through people-to-people connections.

    ​
