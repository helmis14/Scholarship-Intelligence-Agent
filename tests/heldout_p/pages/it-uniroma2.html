100 scholarships for refugees by Ministry of the Interior-CRUI

Call for applications 100 scholarships for students with international protection to enroll in Bachelor, Master degree and PhD programs without scholarship in Italian Public Universities - A.Y. 2023/24 

Call for 100 scholarships for holders of international protection financed by the Ministry of the Interior, in collaboration with CRUI and ANDISU.

The deadline to submit the application is September 4 th , 2023.
