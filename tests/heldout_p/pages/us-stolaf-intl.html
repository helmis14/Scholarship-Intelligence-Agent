A Globally Engaged Community

St. Olaf is committed to enrolling students from around the world. The college is on the top 20 among the nation’s baccalaureate colleges for the number of international students on campus, named by the Open Doors Report on International Educational Exchange.

Photo: International Night Celebration at the Lion’s Pause

We are in the top 20 in the country
In 2024-2025 St. Olaf ranked #14 baccalaureate college in the number of international students.

The college’s emphasis on languages, international and off-campus study, and a global perspective makes this an excellent place for international students to live and learn. International students are an important and growing part of the student body.

FINANCIAL AID

Degree-seeking students may apply for financial aid and scholarships. While the average award is approximately one-half of tuition, the range of financial aid awards is based upon family resources and can be up to the full comprehensive fee.

MAJORS AND PROGRAMS

St. Olaf fosters learning by challenging students academically and surrounding them with a supportive community that provides opportunities to engage and grow. Our curriculum fosters critical thinking, creative problem solving and communication skills. Our residential community builds connections that lead to lifelong friendships.

Campus Life
