Which best describes you?

Domestic student

You are an Australian domestic student if you are:

  • an Australian citizen;
  • an Australian permanent resident;
  • an Australian permanent humanitarian visa holder;
  • a New Zealand citizen.

International student

You are an international student if you are:

  • a temporary resident (visa status) of Australia;
  • a permanent resident (visa status) of New Zealand;
  • a resident or citizen of any other country, intending to study on a student visa.

How can I get a scholarship?

A Deakin scholarship can open doors to real-world possibilities. Most of our international scholarships do not require a separate application. When you apply to study with us, we automatically assess your eligibility based on your past academic record.

A separate application is required if you wish to be considered for the Deakin Vice-Chancellor's International Scholarship. Full details and application instructions are available on the dedicated page.

If your application to study at Deakin is successful and you have been awarded a scholarship, we will email you, usually within four weeks of the closing date. You can view your application status in your scholarship application portal account.

Australia Awards scholarships

The Australian Government’s Australia Awards Scholarships program supports high-achieving students from developing countries to undertake undergraduate or postgraduate study at leading Australian universities, including Deakin University. Scholarship benefits may include tuition fees, return airfares, health cover, an establishment allowance and a contribution towards living expenses.

Explore eligible countries and in-country contacts through the participating countries list, and browse Deakin’s international programs to find the right degree for you. 

For full scholarship details, eligibility requirements and application information, visit the Australian Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade website or contact a Deakin representative in your home country for personalised support with your application.
