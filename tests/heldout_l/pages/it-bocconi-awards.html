Funding - Bachelor of Science and Law Programs

Share

Undergraduate and Law Support Scheme a.y. 2026-27

 

Please find below the funding opportunities available for a.y. 2026-27.
These opportunities offer a different kind of support to families and, in most cases, take into consideration the economic and asset situation of students' families.
Please visit our dedicated webpage if you are an italian candidate. 

Bocconi Access to education Scholarships

Bocconi aims to support deserving students who are willing to invest in their future, even in challenging and potentially constraining economic family conditions.

These funding opportunities guarantee a wide coverage of the student's needs, depending on the circumstances:

  • 100% Full tuition waiver
  • 80% Partial tuition waiver
  • 60% Partial tuition waiver

 

Boost your Future Scholarships

Through these Scholarships, Bocconi supports students who, even with their family support, still require significant assistance in addition to their family's investment, that could enable them to pursue their studies without concerns:

  • 40% Partial tuition waiver
  • 20% Partial tuition waiver

 

Tuition waiver based on specific student features

Tuition waivers for students with specific features, regardless of the family's financial situation:

  • Women award
  • Tuition waiver for students with disabilities equal or greater than 66%
  • Partial tuition waiver for simultaneously enrolled siblings
  • Bocconi Sport Scholarship.

Below you can find a list of the benefits available to students enrolled in the a.y. 2026-27 to years subsequent to the first:

  • ISU Bocconi Scholarship application;
  • Excellence awards. They refer to full tuition exemptions offered by Bocconi to the most deserving students.
  • International mobility grant. Students who decide to undertake a mobility experience for study abroad will be considered for the assignment of the International mobility grant, which can be awarded thanks to a mix of resources between Erasmus+ funds, ministerial funds and Bocconi's own funds.
  • Tuition waiver for students with disabilities equal or greater than 66%

For renewal for a.y. 2026-27 of benefits already assigned to students enrolled to a.y. 2025-26, please refer to the section "Years prior to a.y. 2026-27".
 

Our University, in agreement with major Italian banks, offers its students the possibility to finance their education through student loans, at particularly favorable interest rates with the repayment provision only after entering the job market. 
 

  • Loans
  • Direct loans for US citizens
  • Funding by country

Access to this link in order to check the applications you already submitted.

Remember that for submitting a new application you must refer to the specific webpage on our website, related to the application of your interest.
