Obama Foundation Scholars

The Obama Foundation Scholars program empowers graduate student leaders who are already making a difference in their communities the opportunity to take their work to the next level. In partnership with the University of Chicago, the program provides students the tools, insights, and networks to accelerate their impact, identify innovative solutions to complex global problems, and promote change through values-based leadership.

About

The University of Chicago Scholars program launched in 2018 and has supported over 125 students in their final year of study in a Master’s program at one of three Schools at the University of Chicago: the Harris School of Public Policy; Crown Family School of Social Work, Policy, and Practice; and Booth School of Business. After graduating from the program, Scholars have been turning their bold visions and plans for their communities into reality. They have led city-wide youth initiatives, created vibrant community arts spaces, and spearheaded workforce development programs that foster economic equity.

Program Overview

The Obama Foundation offers a robust curriculum of leadership development, training, networking activities, and customized support throughout the academic year to help the Obama Scholars gain the real-world skills, tools, and experiences they need to expand the impact of their work after graduation. These experiences allow Scholars to gain insights from high-level leaders across sectors and from the wealth of knowledge across the network of Obama Foundation program participants while planning the way they’d like to reinvest after they graduate from the program.

With a particular focus on the neighborhoods near campus, the program also taps into the rich research ecosystems of the University of Chicago and the city of Chicago itself, providing opportunities to learn about place-based change.

Over the course of this one-year program, students develop a distinctly interdisciplinary approach to advancing social change, and leave the University and Scholars program committed to working to address complex social challenges through impactful careers across sectors and the world.

Key components

Led by The Obama Foundation

  • Convenings

    In-person gatherings in Chicago bring Scholars together during the program to build community, participate in skill-building workshops, and engage with local organizations advancing place-based change.

  • Workshops

    Leadership Development Workshops and Conversations designed to improve Scholars’ ability to tell the story of their work, reflect and refine their approach, and build sustained resilience. Through a variety of virtual session formats, Scholars also hear about innovative solutions with high-level leaders across sectors and engage with global changemakers from the Obama Leadership Network, the Obama Foundation’s  alumni community.

  • Action Plans

    Development and refinement of an action plan that draws on their experiences throughout the program, centered around their initiative of focus and future vision for their work.

  • Networking

    The Foundation facilitates direct connections between current Obama Scholars and prior cohorts, based on their issues of focus.

  • Coaching

    Tailored support including an executive coach and systems thinking and fundraising experts to aid Scholars in their action plan development, and their leadership journey.

Led by the University of Chicago

  • Community Impact Seminars

    Community Impact Seminars that draw from faculty across the University’s research and impact ecosystem, designed to help students better understand how engaged scholarship can bridge rigorous research with the practice of uplifting communities–from Chicago’s South Side to across the globe.

  • Quarterly Civic Treks

    Quarterly Civic Treks that take Scholars off-campus to learn from civic leaders and community activists who are confronting social issues that shape Chicago’s diverse neighborhoods and are of critical concern to communities around the world. Through these treks, Scholars develop sustained relationships with civic practitioners who are working on the South Side and access new opportunities to support local nonprofits.

  • Exclusive Opportunities

    Scholars have an exclusive opportunity each quarter to meet with, question, and learn from the Institute of Politics Pritzker Fellows, a diverse cohort of elected officials, diplomats, policymakers, activists, nonprofit leaders, and journalists.
