Bursar of Ampère Scholarship of Excellence

Published on
19/06/2023
Two international economics students have obtained the 2023/2024 Ampere fellowship.

Congratualations to Maddalena BRUSCAGIN (Università di Padova) and Marina PODGORNEAC (Academia de Studii Economice din Moldova) for obtaining the 2023/2024 Ampère Scholarship of Excellence. The Department is looking forward to welcoming you to Lyon and the master program in Advanced Economics.
