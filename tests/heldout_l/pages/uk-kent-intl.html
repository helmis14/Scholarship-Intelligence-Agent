International scholarships

Scholarships for international students

The University has a long tradition of welcoming students from all around the world and we are pleased to have funding opportunities available for students from a wide range of countries.

Kent International Scholarships January 2027

Our Kent International Scholarships are guaranteed tuition fee discounts to international applicants who meet the criteria listed. There's no need to apply - if you meet the eligibility criteria, you will receive notification of the award shortly after accepting an offer to study at Kent!

Postgraduate taught offering: Applicants must be domiciled in Bangladesh, China, India, Indonesia, Malaysia, Nepal, Nigeria, Pakistan, South Korea, Taiwan, Thailand, Türkiye, or Vietnam

Undergraduate offering: All markets

Kent International Scholarships | January 2027 

  • Kent Business School taught Master's - £3,000
  • All other taught Master's - £5,000
  • Undergraduate - up to £5,000

British Council GREAT Scholarship (January) - Ghana and Kenya

We are delighted to be offering two scholarships of £10,000 to students from Ghana and Kenya, applying for a one year taught master's programme starting in January 2027. Click below for more information!

Find out more!

September 2027

Kent International Scholarship for Undergraduates

International applicants who receive and accept an offer for a Kent undergraduate programme starting in September 2027, could receive the Kent International Scholarship (UG) of £5,000 (subject to the eligibility criteria). Further details on eligibility criteria and deadlines coming soon!


The scholarship will be applied as a tuition fee waiver.

  • £3,000 will be applied as a tuition fee waiver in the first stage of your degree
  • £2,000 will be applied as a tuition fee waiver when you progress to the second stage of your degree.   
  • If you are studying with us for just one year, you will receive the £3,000 tuition fee waiver only  

You do not need to make an application, the award will be added to your application once you have accepted your offer, although please allow a few weeks to receive your scholarship offer.
