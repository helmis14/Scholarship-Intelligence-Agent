We have a well-established reputation for academic leadership, research excellence and innovative teaching grounded in real-world issues.

When you choose Massey, you will learn practical skills with renowned lecturers and academics who bring an innovative lens to their subjects.

Scholarships and funding

You can apply for a scholarship or financial aid to make your studies more affordable. We offer scholarships from undergraduate funding to doctoral and PhD awards.

Explore everything you need to know about our fees and funding.

Visas and student insurance

You are responsible for managing your visa. Once you are enrolled in our courses, you are covered by the Studentsafe insurance policy.

Visas for international students

You will need a student visa to come and study in New Zealand. Learn how to apply for and renew your student visa, including when you need to make changes to it.

Insurance for international students

International students in New Zealand need medical and travel insurance. Find out about the cost of Studentsafe insurance, what it covers and how to make a claim.

Find an education agent

International students do not need an education agent to apply to study at Massey. However, an education agent may be able to help you with your application to study with us and the visa process.

Services for international students

Moving to a new country to study is a big step. Living in New Zealand may be very different to your home country. You will find a range of support services for international students studying on campus in New Zealand.
