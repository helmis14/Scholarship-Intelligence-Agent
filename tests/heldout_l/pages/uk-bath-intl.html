Taught postgraduate scholarships, bursaries, and other funding

Find out about the scholarships, bursaries, and other funding available to master's students to help fund your studies at Bath.

Master's scholarships

If you are applying for a master's course in the faculties of Science, Engineering & Design, or Humanities & Social Sciences, you may be eligible for a scholarship.

Bath Global Excellence Scholarship

This scholarship is worth £10,000 and is open to international students. Register your interest now for September 2027 entry.

Diamond Scholarship

This scholarship is open to applicants from the UK and is awarded for academic excellence. Register your interest now for September 2027 entry.

School of Management master's scholarships

If you are applying for a master's course in the School of Management, you may be eligible for one of these scholarships.

School of Management MSc funding and scholarships

We’re investing in scholarships to help talented students like you achieve your ambitions. Find out more, including eligibility.

MBA scholarships

There are many scholarships available to full-time and Executive part-time MBA students worth up to £15,000.

Funding for international students

If you're an international student, you can apply for one of our specialist international bursaries or scholarships.

Bath Merit Scholarships

These scholarship awards are made to overseas fee-paying offer-holders from selected countries for September 2027 entry.

International Sponsorship and Scholarship opportunities

Sponsorship and scholarship programmes available for prospective international students.

Sports scholarships

If you are talented at sports you could be eligible for a scholarship.

Sports scholarships

Find out about scholarships available for talented athletes to study and train at Bath.

Student loans and bursaries for master's study

Find out about loans and bursaries available to help fund your postgraduate studies.

Master’s Access Bursary

Bursaries available for eligible applicants from the UK starting in the academic year 2026-27

Funding and loans for US students studying in the UK

American students can apply for US government or private education loans to help towards funding their studies at the University of Bath.

Funding and loans for Canadian students studying in the UK

If you are a Canadian citizen or national you can choose to fund your studies in different ways.

Additional financial support

You may be eligible for postgraduate funding based on your personal circumstances.

Start your research career in mathematics with our Pathway to Research scheme

Gain advanced skills and experience needed to apply for competitive PhD programmes in the UK and overseas, and apply for a £10,000 bursary to support yourself.

Sanctuary Scholarship

If you are an asylum seeker living in the UK, you may be eligible for a scholarship to pay for your taught postgraduate fees and a bursary worth £20,780.

Students with caring responsibilities

If you are a young adult carer you can find information here on additional support and financial assistance.

Financial support for Refugees

If you are applying for University and have refugee, asylum seeker, HP or DLR status, there is information about financial and other support here.

Funding for current students, alumni, and University staff

If you're a current Bath student, alumni, or member of staff, you could claim a discount on a master's course at the University.

Alumni discount on postgraduate tuition fees

You may be eligible for a 10% reduction in postgraduate tuition fees if you're University of Bath Alumni.

Staff discount on postgraduate tuition fees

If you are a University of Bath employee, you may be eligible for a 75% discount off your postgraduate tuition fees.

Alternative funding

You can look for funding from sources other than those offered by the University.

Alternative funding options

Use The Alternative Guide to Postgraduate Funding to find out about funding options offered by external bodies.

Postgrad Solutions Study Bursaries

The team behind Postgrad.com is offering 5 bursaries, worth £2,000 each, to postgraduate students.
