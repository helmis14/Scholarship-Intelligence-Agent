Dean's Global Excellence Scholarship

To reward excellence, the Dean's Global Excellence Scholarship offers a tuition fee discount to talented undergraduate students within the faculties.

Funding
Full tuition fee discount for first year of study; £10,000 tuition fee discount for second and third year of study
Academic year
2026/27
Open to
International (including EU) students
Qualification level
Undergraduate
Number available
Six

Applications for 2026/27 are closed.

Our scholarships are awarded to international students to support them in achieving their goals at the University of York. 

All undergraduate students holding an offer at York will be automatically considered. There is no need to formally apply for the scholarship. Successful candidates will be informed from 15 May 2026 onwards.

Dean's Global Excellence Scholarship contact details

International Recruitment Team
+44 (0)1904 323534
international@york.ac.uk

Eligibility

Open to International (including EU) students only.

To be eligible for the Dean's Global Excellence Scholarship, you must
  • have a conditional or unconditional offer to study at the University of York by Wednesday 6 May 2026
  • be paying fees at the overseas fee rate (any fee assessments must be completed by Wednesday 6 May 2026) 
    intend to study a full-time undergraduate course.
You will not be eligible if
  • you hold a 100% tuition fee scholarship or are fully sponsored.
Students on the following pathways (either previously or currently) are not eligible for these scholarships
  • Hull York Medical School
  • International Pathway College
  • Distance and online learning courses
  • Part-time courses
  • Kaplan International College London
  • Partnership and exchange students (including visiting students)
How to apply

Application deadline: Tuesday 1 September 2026, 11.59pm BST

The application deadline has now passed. We will update this page with more current information once it is available.

There is no need to formally apply for the scholarship.

All undergraduate students holding an offer at York will be automatically considered. Successful candidates will be informed from 15 May onwards.

How we allocate

The Dean's of our three faculties will select the successful scholarship awardees.

Terms and conditions
When you receive your scholarship letter, you must
  • accept the scholarship offer formally by using the link provided within your offer email

This action must be completed by the deadline provided in your offer email. Otherwise, you will lose your scholarship, and it will be offered to a reserve candidate.

If you have been informed that you require a credibility interview, your scholarship will be conditional on the successful outcome of this interview. Should you be unsuccessful, the scholarship offer will be rescinded.

If you defer your place at the University of York, you cannot keep your scholarship.
