University scholarships

Find a scholarship to help fund your studies. Some are automatically awarded if you meet the criteria, while others require an application.

Scholarships are a type of financial aid that don't need to be repaid. They can help to cover tuition fees or living costs and might be awarded based on academic merit or skill, achievement or through a competitive application process.

Whether or not you can apply to some scholarships will depend on whether you'll pay UK or international tuition fees. This is your "fee status". Find out what your fee status is before applying.

Check your fee status

  • Sports scholarships for elite athletes

    Sports scholarships are available to high level athletes studying or applying to an undergraduate or postgraduate course at Southampton. You could receive up to £800 towards your sport alongside other benefits like a sports annual membership and bespoke training programmes.
