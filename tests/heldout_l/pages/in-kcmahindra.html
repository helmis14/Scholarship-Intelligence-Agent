Scholarships and Grants

Since its inception, the trust has promoted education mainly by way of scholarships and grants to deserving and needy students. Some of these were instituted way back in the mid 1950s while others were founded more recently, a proof of its continuous efforts to bring about social and economic development through a literate, enlightened and empowered populace.

 

To encourage and motivate students, K. C. Mahindra Education Trust has set up the following scholarships:

K C Mahindra Scholarship for Postgraduate Studies

We are excited to announce a new milestone for the K C Mahindra Scholarship!

 

Starting this year, the K C Mahindra Scholarship for Postgraduate Studies has been extended to students pursuing postgraduate studies at select premier institutions within India. This step reflects the Trust’s commitment to equitable access to opportunities. As India’s leading institutions increasingly offer world-class education and research, the expansion ensures that meritorious students are supported wherever they choose to pursue excellence.

 

K C Mahindra Scholarship for Postgraduate Studies Abroad was the Trust’s very first initiative. Designed as an interest-free loan scholarship, it has supported over 1,830 Indian students to pursue postgraduate education at leading global universities. These scholars have gone on to study at top-ranked institutions worldwide.

 

Each year, the Trust awards interest-free loan scholarships to a minimum of 50 students:

  • Up to ₹10 lakh for the top 3 K. C. Mahindra Fellows
  • Up to ₹5 lakh for other awardees

Eligibility:

  1. The candidate must be an Indian citizen.
  2. The candidate should possess a First-Class degree or an equivalent diploma of similar standard from a recognized University.
  3. The candidate should have enrolled in a post-graduate program commencing between:
    August 2026 and February 2027 for universities overseas
    June 2026 to September 2026 for universities in India
  4. Candidates studying in the FINAL YEAR of the degree or diploma courses are also eligible to apply, provided they produce before end of June their final year marksheet or provisional certificate from the institute stating that they have completed their degree/diploma course and have secured a first class.

Key Dates & Notes:

  1. Applications are closed for the academic year 2026.

  2. The deadline to apply for the scholarship was 30th April 2026, 6 pm.

Mahindra Search for Talent Scholarship

This scholarship was initiated in 1983. This scholarship has been ongoing in 37 educational institutions across the country with an objective to enthuse and reward excellence in academics. This scholarship is awarded to students obtaining the highest aggregate marks, based on the year end examination. Further, a student who receives the Mahindra Search for Talent scholarship more than once, is awarded the 'Honour scholarship' which includes a cash prize of Rs. 5000/- and a citation from the Trust.

K.C. Mahindra UWC Scholarship

This scholarship is offered to deserving students in the age group of 16 to 18 years. These students are given an opportunity to study at the United World Colleges. This scholarship aims to ensure that students benefit through the experience of international education, shared learning and community service which the Mahindra United World College is offering.

Mahindra All India Talent Scholarship (MAITS)

The K. C. Mahindra Education Trust established the Mahindra All India Talent Scholarship in 1995. This scholarship is awarded to students belonging to economically disadvantaged families, who wish to pursue a job-oriented Diploma course at a recognised Government Polytechnic in India. This Scholarship was established to encourage and support young students from rural and urban areas to pursue job-oriented Diplomas. This Trust awards Rs. 10,000/- per annum to 550 students annually, for a maximum period of 3 years. Till date, 13,490 students have been awarded the scholarship across India.

 

Eligibility Criteria:

  1. Candidate should have passed SSC/HSC or equivalent examinations in the 10th /12th standard with more than 60% marks.
  2. Candidate should have secured admission in the first year or direct admission into the second year (lateral admission) in a government or any other recognised Polytechnic Institute for diploma course.
  3. The candidate should belong to a family with low yearly income.

Mahindra Finance KCMET Scholarship (2022-23)

Announcing the Mahindra Finance KCMET Scholarship to support girls from disadvantaged backgrounds to continue their education.

 

Eligibility Criteria:

  • This scholarship is for underserved girls pan India
  • The girl must be studying between Class 1 to Post-Graduation
  • Annual family income of the girl from all sources should not be more than INR 4,00,000/-
