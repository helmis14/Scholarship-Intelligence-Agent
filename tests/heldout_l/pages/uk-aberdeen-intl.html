Aberdeen Global Scholarship - Postgraduate

Summary

The University of Aberdeen is delighted to offer new self-funded international on campus postgraduate Masters students starting in Academic Years 26/27 and 27/28 a £8,000 tuition fee waiver.

Description

Please note, this scholarship is only available for Aberdeen campus students.

The University of Aberdeen is keen to welcome talented students from all over the world. To help students fulfil their academic ambitions and ease the costs associated with studying abroad, the University is pleased to offer the Aberdeen Global Scholarship worth £8,000. The scholarship is available to all self-funded full-time on campus Masters students classified as international fee status and starting their studies in in Academic Years 26/27 and 27/28. The scholarship applies to both taught and research Masters. The scholarship is in the form of a partial tuition fee waiver; the £8,000 scholarship amount will be deducted from your tuition fees.

To be eligible students must be registering for a full-time on campus program and cannot be fully sponsored or in receipt of fully funded external scholarships or other University of Aberdeen scholarships, except for awards granted by the University of Aberdeen Development Trust. Please note that the scholarship does not apply to the DPLP, PGDE, or Dentistry.

We look forward to welcoming you to Aberdeen. If you have any queries about this scholarship, please get in touch via our online form.

View all our Postgraduate Taught degrees

View all our Postgraduate Research opportunities

Application Procedure

The Aberdeen Global Scholarship does not require an application. It is automatically applied to all eligible students and will be reflected on their CAS.

Deadline

Available until further notice.

Further Info

This scholarship aims to provide essential financial support to ease the transition into life in the North East of Scotland. By alleviating some of the costs associated with studying abroad, the Aberdeen Global Scholarship enables recipients to focus on their academic and personal growth as they establish their new home in our vibrant and welcoming community.

Living in Aberdeen, often referred to as the "Granite City", offers a unique blend of urban and coastal charm. It boasts a rich maritime history, vibrant cultural scene, and proximity to stunning natural landscapes, including beautiful beaches and the captivating Cairngorms. Residents enjoy a walkable city with a variety of shops, restaurants, and a bustling nightlife, alongside access to outdoor activities like hiking and water sports. The local economy provides diverse employment opportunities. Aberdeen also embraces a welcoming community spirit, making it an appealing place to call home.
