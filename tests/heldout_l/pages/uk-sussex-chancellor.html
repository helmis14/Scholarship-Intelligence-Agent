Chancellor's International Scholarships (2026)

A limited number of scholarships are available to 'excellent' undergraduate international applicants. These are highly competitive and only applicants with exceptional academic achievement and potential will be considered. If you have exceptional grades to date, you can apply for a scholarship after you have received an offer to study at the University of Sussex in 2026.

What you get

£5,000 in the first year of full-time study

Type of award

Tuition fee reduction

Eligibility

  • Who is eligible

    To be eligible for this scholarship, you must:

    • Be classified as ‘Overseas’ for fee purposes
    • Have received an offer of a place to study a full time Bachelors degree at Sussex in 2026 this can be conditional)
    • Be self-financing
    • Have excellent academic grades (see 'Qualifications advice' for indication of minimum grades required; this does not include all accepted qualifications and is instead intended as guidance)
  • Qualifications advice
    Qualification/Country Minimum grades required to apply for the scholarship

    A-Levels

    A*A*A

    BTEC Level 3 National Extended Diploma

    D*D*D

    Bangladesh

    2-3 years of Bachelor’s degree with an overall average of 80% or higher

    Canada

    Ontario secondary School Diploma with an overall average of 80% from 6 grade 12 4U or 4M courses

    China

    1-2 years of Higher Education (IFY or 2+2) with an overall average of 85% or higher

    Egypt

    General Secondary School Certificate + 2 years of Higher Education with a CGPA of 3.70 or 80%

    European Baccalaureate

    Overall average of 85%

    German Abitur

    Abitur 1.6

    Greece

    Apolytirion 19.2/20

    Hong Kong

    Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education (HKDSE) – 5*5*5

    India

    CBSE/CISCE Standard XII – 90% or higher

    International Baccalaureate

    36 points

    Kazakhstan

    The Nazarbayev Intellectual Schools Grade 12 Certificate with three Advanced Level subjects at A*A*A

    Nigeria

    1 year of university study with excellent grades

    Pakistan

    Bachelor (Pass)/Bachelor of Science (Pass) degree with an overall result at 90% in Division I

    Saudi Arabia

    2 years of Higher Education with an overall CGPA of 4.0/1st Class

    Spain

    Titulo de Bachillerato 8.5 overall average

    Turkiye

    Lise Diplomasi/Bitirme with at least 85% in the final year (4.5/5)

    USA

    High School Graduation Diploma with a GPA of 3.7 + 3 Advanced Placement Tests at 555
  • Who isn’t eligible

    You are not eligible for this scholarship if you:

    • Are applying for a Bachelors at a university that is not at the University of Sussex
    • Are a current Sussex student
    • Are progressing into the final year of an integrated Masters at Sussex

    You are not eligible for this scholarship if you are going to study any of the following:

    • Courses at Brighton and Sussex Medical School (BSMS)
    • Degrees in Social Work (if receiving an NHS bursary)
    • Courses which include Foundation Year
    • PGCE courses
    • School Direct teaching courses
    • Postgraduate diplomas

Deadline

30 April 2026 23:59

How to apply

Applications closed

Contact us

scholarships@sussex.ac.uk

You might also be interested in

  • Information for International Applicants 
  • How to apply for an undergraduate degree

Timetable

All applications will be considered after the closing date and applicants can expect a decision by the end of July.

Terms and conditions

This scholarship is awarded as a tuition fee in the first year only, no cash equivalent is available.

It is not possible to combine this scholarship with the Sussex Pakistan Scholarship or Sussex Turkey Scholarship. Applicants eligible for both will always be awarded the highest value scholarship. 

This scholarship is available for the 2026/27 academic year only, if a scholar defers or temporarily withdraws from their course, they will no longer be eligible to receive the scholarship.

We are aware AI has been used by some applicants to produce statements for their scholarship applications. Please be advised these applications are often unsuccessful. Any personal statement submitted needs to be written by you, to demonstrate your suitability for the scholarship.
