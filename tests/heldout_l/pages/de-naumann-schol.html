    • Europe
      Europe

      Through the connection of EU experts, civil society, and decision makers we seek to foster open dialogue and discuss liberal policy solutions to European challenges. Together with our liberal partners, we develop formats and campaigns to promote rule of law, free markets and human rights. Moreover, we coordinate EU co-funded projects in the field of democracy support, development cooperation and human rights protection.

Do you have good to very good grades or academic achievements, are you interested in politics, are you committed and have a liberal, cosmopolitan attitude? Then the Friedrich Naumann Foundation for Freedom is the right place for you and you can apply for a scholarship here. We support German and foreign students who study full-time at state or state-recognized universities and technical colleges.

  • Scholarships for German students

  • Scholarships for EU Students

  • Scholarships for foreign students from non-EU countries

  • Scholarships for Refugees

Unfortunately, we cannot support the following:

  • Second degrees
  • Part-time degrees
  • Distance learning courses that are not completed full-time
  • Advanced and supplementary degree courses
  • Bachelor programmes for foreign, non-EU students
  • Studies during their final phase (the minimum funding period is 2 semesters when starting the scholarship)
  • Any type of project (internships, language courses, travel subsidies, etc.)
