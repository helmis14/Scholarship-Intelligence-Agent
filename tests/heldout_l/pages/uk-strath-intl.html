Study in the largest Faculty of Engineering in Scotland

As the largest Faculty of Engineering in Scotland we have more than 800 international students from 100 different countries around the world studying with us here at Strathclyde. We have over 40 undergraduate courses and over 60 masters courses on offer within our 8 departments, providing a broad range of study options for our students. We believe in useful learning, that's why we have strong links with industry and our staff are leading experts in their fields. 

Watch our video to find out more about how a scholarship can assist you with your studies.

Available Scholarships - January 2027

We offer scholarships for our postgraduate taught courses starting in January 2027, and for postgraduate research students starting in the 2025/26 or 2026/27 academic years.

All self-funded, international (non-EU), fee-paying offer holders joining a full-time, on-campus course are eligible.

January 2027
Faculty of Engineering International Scholarship for Masters Study

We are delighted to offer our Faculty of Engineering International Scholarship for Masters Study for applicants to postgraduate taught degrees starting in January 2027. The scholarship award of £6,000 will be deducted from tuition fees.

View full eligibility details
Dean's International Excellence Awards

We also have a limited number of Dean’s International Excellence Awards for postgraduate taught applicants joining the Faculty of Engineering in January 2027. These scholarships are worth between £9,000 and £12,000 and will be offered to exceptional applicants at postgraduate taught level only. 

View full eligibility details

Postgraduate Research

Faculty of Engineering International Scholarship for Postgraduate Research Study

We are delighted to offer our Faculty of Engineering International Scholarship to postgraduate research students starting in the 2026/2027 academic year. If you start your PhD studies in 2026/2027 you'll receive a scholarship award equivalent to a 15% reduction of your fees for each year of study. This scholarship will be awarded automatically to eligible offer holders and no application is necessary.
