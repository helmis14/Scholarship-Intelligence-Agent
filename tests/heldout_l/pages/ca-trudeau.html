PhD Scholarships Program

The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation PhD scholarship supports students with an excellent academic record who are engaged in humanities and social sciences research that contributes to the advancement of Canada and society.

Benefits of the PhD Scholarship

The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation PhD Scholarship is designed for doctoral students who want to go beyond academia, grow through mentorship, and contribute to major societal issues through their research.

As a Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholar, you will receive:

Up to $50,000 per year for three years

To cover tuition and living expenses related to your doctoral studies

Up to $20,000 per year for research and travel

To support your research, networking activities, and travel related to your doctoral work.

Mentorship and experiential learning

Take part in meetings, masterclasses, seminars, and field experiences designed to broaden your perspectives, connect you with Mentors, Fellows and Scholars, and enrich your doctoral journey.

An engaged multidisciplinary community

Join a dynamic network of people working across disciplines and sectors, and benefit from exchanges that complement your academic experience.

A network of more than 500 alumni

Become part of a community of more than 500 Foundation alumni whose career paths reflect the value and reach of this exceptional recognition.

More Than Financial Support

The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation PhD Scholarship offers more than funding. It connects engaged doctoral students with a community of researchers, mentors and leaders working to advance ideas that matter for Canada and the world.

Key Criteria for
a Successful Application

Applications are assessed based on the candidate’s academic excellence, the clear alignment of their research project with the Foundation’s mission and four founding themes, the rigour of the research proposal, and their ability to share knowledge for the benefit of society.

Check my scholarship eligibility

Is This PhD Scholarship Right for You?

The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation PhD Scholarship is intended for doctoral students who stand out among their peers for their engagement, whose research is rooted in the humanities and social sciences and is aligned with one of the four themes of the Foundation, and that contributes to major issues facing Canada and the world.

Beyond academic excellence, the Foundation looks for candidates who can mobilize knowledge, engage actively in their communities, and contribute to meaningful conversations on today’s most pressing societal challenges.

Before applying, review the eligibility and selection criteria to make sure your profile aligns with the program.

View the Selection Criteria

Only applications that meet the program’s eligibility criteria will be assessed.
