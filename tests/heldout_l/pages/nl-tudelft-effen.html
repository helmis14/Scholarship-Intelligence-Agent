Scholarships

Come to TU Delft with a Scholarship!

At TU Delft, we embody the principal of creating impact for a better society and aim to attract the brightest, most talented, and highly motivated students worldwide to our MSc programmes. We offer 16 full scholarships for our regular MSc programmes from the Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence foundation and from the MSc Electrical Engineering Programme 2 partial Scholarships.

The scholarships enable students to study in the Netherlands, contribute to a diverse and innovative academic community, and grow into future-proof engineers with a strong commitment to sustainability.

Every year, TU Delft receives far more applications for its scholarships than it can fund, leaving many students disappointed. Carefully evaluate your MSc application, ensure you meet all eligibility criteria, and consider the financial costs of a two-year programme if you do not receive a scholarship. Scholarships are extremely limited and highly competitive, with only the strongest candidates selected. For transparency, we recommend checking the Tuition Fees & Finances page to avoid unexpected financial challenges later in the process.
 

Heads up!

Missing the scholarship deadline means your scholarship application won’t be reviewed.

Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship?

Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarships are financed by the legacy of Justus and Louise van Effen. Mr. van Effen studied at TU Delft in the 1940’s and strongly believed technological developments will contribute to solving societal issues. Awarded students will receive full tuition fees per year for a TU Delft MSc programme based on the Statutory or Institutional rate, according to the registered nationality, AND contribution for the living expenses.

2 per faculty

  • You are an excellent international applicant (conditionally) admitted to one of the 2-year regular TU Delft MSc programmes (not an International Joint Education Programme).
  • Students must have achieved excellent academic results in their previous study programme(s), which must be relevant to the Master's programme for which they are applying. As an indication, the student is amongst the top 10% of graduates in his/her previous study programme. Note that proof of ranking is not required – this information is purely to indicate the level of competitiveness.
  • You have submitted a complete MSc application before the scholarship deadline.

1 December 2026 (23:59 CET)

  • Complete your MSc application (check the admission requirements)
  • Upload the Scholarship Application Form
  • Upload one reference letter (if you have two references, upload as 1 pdf.)
  • Upload the English language certificate. (All applicants who wish to be considered for a scholarship are required to include their English test with their MSc application before the Scholarship deadline application).
    Are you exempt? If yes, the English language certificate is not needed. Check the requirement information here).
  • Full tuition fees for the first year for a TU Delft MSc programme (based on the statutory fee or institutional rate, according to the registered nationality).
  • Contribution for the living expenses.
  • To be eligible for the scholarship allowances in the second year you are required to have made sufficient study progress. At the end of the first academic year, you must have completed at least 48 ECTS out of 60.
  • Scholarship holders are not allowed to receive any other form of partial or full scholarship funding in addition to the Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship.
  • Working part-time is not recommended as it may affect your study. For more information, For more information FAQ.

Students who have been granted a scholarship will be informed by TU Delft via email by the end of March 2027.
Receiving the scholarship is conditional on the student fulfilling all admission criteria.

  • Financial extensions beyond the 2-year period are not supported by the scholarship.
  • The student must remain enrolled at TU Delft during the scholarship period
  • Full details will disclosed in the award letter.

Electrical Engineering Scholarship

The Microelectronics Scholarship is financed through the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science (EEMCS). The goal of the scholarship is to help financially challenged students with proven potential to study at the TU Delft. Awarded students will receive a partial scholarship towards the cost of their MSc studies at TU Delft.

Up to two faculty scholarships offered by the Microelectronics research department

  • You are a promising excellent international student
  • Admitted (conditionally) to the 2-year Regular TU Delft’s MSc programme in Electrical Engineering (track Microelectronics).
  • You have a cumulative grade point average (GPA) of 80 percent or higher of the scale maximum in the bachelor’s degree from an internationally renowned university outside The Netherlands.

1 December 2026 (23:59 CET)

  • Complete your MSc application (check the admission requirements)
  • Upload the Scholarship Application Form
  • Upload 1 reference letter (if you have two references, upload as 1 pdf.)
  • Upload the English language certificate. (All applicants who wish to be considered for a scholarship are required to include their English test with their MSc application before the Scholarship deadline application).
    Are you exempt? If yes, the English language certificate is not needed. Check the requirement information here).
  • Partial scholarship for up to two students
  • Partially covering tuition fees for a TU Delft MSc programme (based on the statutory or institutional tuition fee applicable to the student's nationality).
  • Extension of the scholarship to the second year is subject to the student maintaining an average grade of 8.0 (80%) during the first year.

Students who have been granted a scholarship will be informed by TU Delft via email by the end of March 2027.
Receiving the scholarship is conditional on the student fulfilling all admission criteria.

  • Extension of the scholarship to the second year is subject to maintaining an average grade of 8 (80%) during the first year.
  • Students must choose an MSc thesis project in the Microelectronics Dept, and if they receive a 9 (90%) or higher for the project, they will be offered a PhD position in the Microelectronics Dept.
  • The student must remain enrolled at TU Delft during the scholarship period.
  • Full details will disclosed in the award letter.

Student Experiences

I will always be deeply grateful to the Justus & Louise van Effen Foundation and TU Delft for believing in me and making my dream a reality. I hope to carry the confidence, knowledge, and values I have gained through this experience throughout my career and to contribute, in my own way, to the community that has invested so generously in my future. To the future recipients of the Justus & Louise van Effen Scholarship, I wish you the very best. I hope you make the most of this extraordinary opportunity, challenge yourself, explore beyond what you think is possible, and enjoy every part of the journey.

Kanza Zafar, ME, graduated 2026

Before coming to TU Delft, I had already heard about its excellent research and beautiful campus from people around me. After two years of studying here, I can say that those impressions were absolutely right. TU Delft gave me the opportunity to learn from knowledgeable and approachable teachers and make use of excellent academic resources. I met classmates and researchers from around the world and heard different perspectives through discussions and collaborations. Being part of such an inspiring academic environment has further strengthened my interest in research. The Justus & Louise van Effen Scholarship made all of this possible. It was much more than financial support. It gave me the freedom to focus fully on my studies, choose the courses that genuinely interested me, and complete my Master’s in two years.

Sarah Sultana, CEG, graduated 2026

This scholarship gave me more than just financial support. It gave me confidence to just say yes to opportunities as they came up. Being chosen for it made me feel like it was okay to leap into a field I'd never studied before. Looking back, it's not just that it supported my studies. It changed how brave I was willing to be.

Sejal Budholiya, IDE, graduated 2025

The Justus & Louise van Effen Scholarship gave me the chance to move abroad and pursue my master’s degree in aerospace engineering at TU Delft, which was an opportunity that ended up being about so much more than academics. Living in the Netherlands, meeting people from around the world, and challenging myself in a new environment helped me grow tremendously, both as an engineer and as a person. I’m incredibly grateful for the scholarship and for the experiences, friendships, and memories that came from my time at TU Delft.

Eden Pfanner, AE, graduated 2025

The scholarship gave me the confidence and space to believe that I could build expertise in a field I truly cared about. Today, that freedom has helped me start my own play design studio, Sticks & Stones, and I continue exploring how play can make a meaningful difference in education, mental health and people's everyday lives.

Swetha Krishnan, IDE, graduated 2024
