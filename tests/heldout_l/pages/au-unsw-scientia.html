Scientia PhD Scholarship Scheme

Browse Scientia PhD projects
Personalise

A special PhD scholarship scheme for domestic applicants with global ambitions

UNSW is looking for high-quality domestic applicants to work on a supervisor-defined PhD project with embedded global mobility opportunities. The UNSW Scientia PhD Scholarship Scheme underpins our mission to advance Progress for All by developing graduates who are globally focused and socially engaged.

These projects are fully funded, and successful candidates will receive a living allowance scholarship and a one-off Global Mobility Grant to carry out international engagement activities. A total of 20 scholarships will be available for commencement in 2027.
