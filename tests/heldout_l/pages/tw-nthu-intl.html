Eligibility for International Applicants (Taiwan Resident)
Nationality Applicants and their parents must all hold foreign nationality and have never held Taiwan Nationality.
Study Status in Taiwan Must not have studied in Taiwan under the status of an Overseas Chinese student.
Eligibility for Reapplication Those who have previously studied in Taiwan under the “Regulations Regarding International Students Undertaking Studies in Taiwan” may reapply and only once for NTHU undergraduate programs.
Applicants may not apply Applicants may not apply for programs in Medicine, Dentistry, or Traditional Chinese Medicine.
Graduate programs No such limitation applies to graduate programs.
Note: Foreign students currently studying in Taiwan who meet the above criteria may apply through the same admission process as overseas international applicants.
Declaration About International Admission
Handles the recruitment of international students without commissioning any external institution, legal person, group, or individual to handle related matters except publicity and promotion, and to assist students coming to Taiwan undertake the necessary related procedures.
