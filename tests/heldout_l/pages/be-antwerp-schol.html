Are you looking for financial support to study at UAntwerp? You're in the right place. Below you'll find an overview of all scholarship options — from programmes where UAntwerp selects you directly, to our own incentives for outstanding students, to external funding you can explore yourself.

What scholarships are available?

Scholarships where UAntwerp is involved

For these programmes, UAntwerp participates in the selection. You don't apply independently — contact us first.

  • Master Mind is awarded by the Flemish Ministry of Education to outstanding international Master's students. It covers a living allowance (€10 020 per year) and a reduced tuition fee (€139.40 for 2026–2027). UAntwerp submits applications centrally; deadline is 1 February. → Read more
  • Erasmus Mundus offers EU-funded scholarships for students in two joint Master programmes at UAntwerp: Applied Ecohydrology (MAEH) and Leading International Vaccinology Education (LIVE). → Read more
  • VLIRUOS awards scholarships to students from 29 eligible countries in Africa, Asia and Latin America, linked to five specific Master's programmes at UAntwerp. → Read more
  • Daughters For Life (DFL) offers scholarships for female candidates from Palestine, Israel, Egypt, Jordan, Syria, Lebanon, Iraq or Morocco, for Bachelor's and Master's studies. → Read more

UAntwerp's own initiative: High Potential Incentive

Outstanding non-EEA students admitted to a Master's programme may receive a tuition fee reduction — no separate application or action is needed. The incentive is granted after successful screening by the faculty. → Read more

External scholarships

Looking for other options? Check with the Belgian embassy in your home country, or browse our overview of external scholarships. → View external scholarships

What does it cost to study at UAntwerp?

Tuition fees at UAntwerp are reasonable by international standards, especially for EEA students. Non-EEA students pay a higher rate — which is exactly where scholarships and incentives make a difference. The estimated cost of living in Belgium is around €16 000 per year.

Frequently asked questions

Does UAntwerp offer scholarships for international students?

Yes - UAntwerp offers several options: programmes where we participate in the selection (Master Mind, Erasmus Mundus, VLIRUOS and DFL), our own High Potential Incentive for outstanding non-EEA Master's students, and links to external funding. Scholarships are available at Bachelor's, Master's and PhD level.

UAntwerp does not offer fully-funded scholarships. An Erasmus Mundus scholarship is a fully funded scholarship from the European Commission, awarded to students participating in an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master, in which the University of Antwerp is involved as a partner institution.​

Do I need to apply separately for a scholarship?

It depends on the scholarship. For Master Mind, VLIRUOS, Erasmus Mundus and DFL, a formal application is required. For Master Mind and VLIRUOS, UAntwerp submits that application on your behalf, so contact us early. For the High Potential Incentive, no additional application or action is required. The incentive is granted after successful screening by the faculty following your admission.

When is the deadline?

Deadlines vary by programme. For Master Mind, all applications must be submitted through UAntwerp by 1 February. For Erasmus Mundus and VLIRUOS, check the individual programme pages. If you're unsure, contact us at internationalstudents@uantwerp.be.

Can I combine a scholarship with other financial support?

Usually not within UAntwerp's own programmes — the Master Mind scholarship, for example, cannot be combined with other Flemish government grants or an Erasmus Mundus award. The High Potential Incentive cannot be combined with a payment plan. If you receive support from your home country or a private foundation, check with that provider.

Where do I start?

  • Check your eligibility first. Each scholarship has its own criteria based on nationality and study level.
  • Apply for admission early. For the High Potential Incentive, admission is all you need. For other scholarships, you'll need to be in contact with UAntwerp well before the deadline.
  • Explore external options in parallel. External scholarships can take time to process — start early.
  • Still unsure? Contact us: internationalstudents@uantwerp.be
