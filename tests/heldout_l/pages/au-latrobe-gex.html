FAQs

Eligibility depends on the individual scholarship and may be based on academic performance, course, discipline, campus or study type. Current students generally aren't eligible for a scholarship, but they may be eligible when applying for a new higher degree (for example, when graduating from an undergraduate course and applying for a postgraduate course).

Values vary from 20% to 100% in upfront* tuition fee reduction, depending on the scholarship. Check the relevant scholarship page to confirm the applicable value and conditions. Scholarships covered on this page apply to tuition fees only and do not cover living costs, accommodation or other expenses.

*Excluding Sydney campus.

Some scholarships have specific application requirements, such as a written statement or an early acceptance deadline, while others are based on meeting published eligibility criteria and will be automatically assessed on application. Always check the individual scholarship page to confirm whether a separate application, written statement or other action is required.

Some scholarships have specific application deadlines, while others are awarded through automatic assessment.

If a separate scholarship application is required, you can find the estimated outcome timeframe on the relevant scholarship page. If you are automatically assessed, scholarship outcomes will be included in your offer letter. Where a separate application is required, expected outcome timeframes are provided on the scholarship page.

No. Some scholarships are only available for selected courses, disciplines or campuses. La Trobe also offers scholarships for graduate research students. Check the relevant scholarship and course pages to confirm eligibility and availability.

Most scholarships cannot be deferred to a future intake, transferred to a different course or campus, or combined with other scholarships. However, the Early Acceptance Grant may be combined with selected 20%–30% scholarships. Always refer to the scholarship terms and conditions for details that apply to your award.
