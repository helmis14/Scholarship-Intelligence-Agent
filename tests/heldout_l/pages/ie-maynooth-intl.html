    International Office Scholarships and Awards

    At Maynooth University, we are proud to support talented students from across the globe in pursuing their academic ambitions and becoming part of a thriving, international community. Our International Scholarships provide both financial support and recognition to outstanding students, making a world-class education in Ireland more accessible and rewarding. These awards celebrate academic excellence, diversity, and the fresh perspectives that international students bring to campus. Explore our range of scholarships and funding opportunities to see how you can take the next step in achieving your academic and professional goals with us.

    VICE PRESIDENT INTERNATIONAL SCHOLAR AWARD

    The Vice President International Scholar Award offers a €7,500 tuition fee discount to two outstanding Postgraduate Taught international applicants who have demonstrated exceptional academic achievement in previous studies.

    Eligibility Criteria

    To be eligible for the award, applicants must:

    • Have accepted their offer and paid their deposit before 31st March 2026.
    • Be a Non-EU fee-paying student.
    • Apply for any Postgraduate Taught programme based on campus at Maynooth University.
    • Meet the Maynooth University English language entry requirements prior to application.
    • Hold an undergraduate degree from a university ranked in the top 500 in the QS or Times Higher Education Rankings.
    • Have achieved an undergraduate degree equivalent to a First-Class Honours in Ireland.

    Application Process

    • Applications open on 1st February and close on 31st March 2026.
    • Only applicants who meet the eligibility criteria are eligible to apply.
    • Applications must be submitted via the MS Form here.
    • Applicants are required to submit a Personal Statement at the time of application. This statement should highlight the candidate's academic achievements, personal qualities, leadership or community involvement, and how receiving the scholarship would support the candidate's educational and future goals.
    • Only completed applications submitted by the closing date will be considered.

    See full Terms & Conditions here.

    MAYNOOTH UNIVERSITY INTERNATIONAL EXCELLENCE SCHOLARSHIP

    The Maynooth University International Excellence Scholarship awards a €5,000 tuition fee discount (for one year) to six exceptional Postgraduate Taught international students. One scholarship is available for each of the following regions: North America, Latin America, South Asia, East Asia, Southeast Asia, and the Rest of the World.

    Eligibility Criteria

    To be eligible for the award, applicants must:

    • Be a Non-EU fee-paying student.
    • Have accepted their offer and paid their deposit by 31st March 2026.
    • Be applying for a full-time postgraduate programme.
    • Have achieved a minimum overall result of 80% in their primary degree.

    Application Process

    • Applications open on 1st February and close on 31st March 2026.
    • Only applicants who meet the eligibility criteria are eligible to apply.
    • Applications must be submitted via the MS Form here.
    • Only completed applications submitted by the closing date will be considered.
    • Applicants are required to submit a Personal Statement at the time of application. This statement should describe their prior academic performance and accomplishments and explain their motivation for choosing Maynooth University. The statement should demonstrate academic readiness and alignment with Maynooth University’s strengths.

    See full Terms & Conditions here.

    MAYNOOTH UNIVERSITY INTERNATIONAL LOYALTY SCHOLARSHIP

    The Maynooth University International Loyalty Scholarship offers a €1,500 tuition fee discount to international alumni returning to Maynooth University to pursue further full-time study.

    Eligibility Criteria

    To be eligible for the award, applicants must:

    • Must be a non-EU fee paying student.
    • Must be a Maynooth University international alumni.
    • Applicants must be applying for full-time on campus Postgraduate studies.
    • Applicants must have accepted their offer and paid their deposit before 30th June.

    Application Process

    • Applications open on 1st April and close on 30th June 2026.
    • Only applicants who meet the eligibility criteria are eligible to apply.
    • Applications must be submitted via the MS Form here.
    • Only completed applications submitted by the closing date will be considered.
    • Applicants must include a Personal Statement outlining why they have chosen to return to Maynooth University for further study, how their previous experience as a student influenced their decision and their future plans

    See full Terms & Conditions here.

     

    MAYNOOTH UNIVERSITY INTERNATIONAL MERIT AWARD

    The Maynooth University International Merit Award offers a €1,500 tuition fee discount to 25 students, recognising high-achieving international students who have accepted their offer to study at Maynooth University.

    Eligibility Criteria

    To be eligible for the award, applicants must:

    • Applicants must have accepted their offer and paid their deposit before 30th June.
    • Must be a Non-EU fee paying student.
    • Open to international applicants for full-time PGT and UG programmes.
    • PGT applicants must have a minimum overall result in their primary degree of at least 80%.

    Application Process

    • Applications open on 1st April and close on 30th June 2026.
    • Only applicants meeting the eligibility criteria may apply.
    • Applications must be submitted via the MS Form here.
    • Only completed applications submitted by the closing date will be considered.
    • Applicants are required to include a Personal Statement, sharing what attracted them to Maynooth University, how the chosen programme aligns with their academic goals, and what they hope to gain from their studies.

    See full Terms & Conditions here.

    DEAN OF THE FACULTY OF ARTS & HUMANITIES - INTERNATIONAL POSTGRADUATE SCHOLARSHIP

    The Dean of the Faculty of Arts & Humanities - International Postgraduate Scholarship offers a €2,000 tuition fee discount to 2 students, recognising high-achieving international students who have accepted their offer.  

    Eligibility Criteria 

    To be eligible for the award, applicants must: 

    • Applicants must have accepted their offer and paid their deposit before 30th May for a Faculty of Arts and Humanities Fulltime In-person Postgraduate programme 
    • Must be a non-EU fee paying student. 
    • Open to international applicants for full-time PGT Master programmes within the Faculty of Arts & Humanities. 
    • PGT applicants must have a minimum overall result in their primary degree that is equivalent to 65% in the Irish Higher Education System 

    Application Process 

    • Applications open 1st April and close 30th May. 
    • Only Applicants who meet the eligibility criteria may apply.  
    • Applications are submitted via this MSForm.  
    • Only completed applications submitted by the closing date will be considered. 
    • Applicants must include a Personal Statement outlining why the have chosen their programme of study at Maynooth University's Faculty of Arts and Humanities. 

    See full Terms & Conditions here. 

    PROGRAMME SPECIFIC SCHOLARSHIPS

    MBA Excellence Scholarship

    The MBA Excellence Scholarship is a €5,000 tuition fee discount available to applicants to the Master of Business Administration (MBA) at Maynooth University for the 2026–27 academic year. The scholarship is awarded on a competitive basis and is applied directly to the MBA programme fee. Full terms and conditions apply. Please see further details below: MBA Excellence Scholarship 2026-27

    • MA in TESOL and EMI
    • MA in Applied Linguistics and Intercultural Studies

    Students must first secure an offer for one of these programmes and then submit their scholarship application here. The deadline to apply is 30th April 2026. Full terms and conditions apply. Please see further details here: SMLLC Scholarships 2026.
