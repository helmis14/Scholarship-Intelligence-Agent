A global community of innovators

For nearly 100 years, the University of Hull has welcomed students from more than 100 countries. With world-class teaching, impactful research, and strong international partnerships, you’ll be part of a university that is shaping the future.

You’ll join a friendly and inclusive global community, with dedicated support every step of the way - from choosing a course to visas, scholarships, and settling into student life.

Why choose Hull

When you study at Hull, you’re more than just a number. You're part of a close-knit, supportive community where your lecturers know your name and everyone matters.

“Friendly” is the word most international students use to describe life in Hull – and it’s easy to see why.

Studying in Hull and the UK

We’re England's 14th oldest university and we’ve been helping people think differently for almost a century.

We’re rated Gold in the Teaching Excellence Framework (TEF 2023) – the highest ranking for teaching quality.

Your money goes further in Hull, most affordable city in the UK where rent is less than half the cost of London.1

Walk from your room to your lectures in under 10-minutes across our beautiful, safe campus.

Our researchers tackle world challenges. Join a university that is making a global impact – and become part of the solution.

From the legacy of William Wilberforce to a thriving arts, music, and festival scene, Hull is a city rich in history and culture.
