Scholarship Application
  • 2026 Chinese Government Scholarship (Scholarship for 2027 is coming soon)
    In order to strengthen mutual understanding and friendship between the Chinese and people around the world and promote China’s exchange and cooperation with other countries in various fields, the Chinese government sets up scholarships for talented students and scholars to pursue higher education or conduct research in Chinese universities.
  • 2026 Asian Future Leaders Scholarship Program (Scholarship for 2027 is coming soon)
    2026 Asian Future Leaders Scholarship Program（Scholarship for 2027 is coming soonAsian Future Leaders Scholarship Program (AFLSP)，offered by Bai Xian Asia Institute (BXAI), aims to promote cross-cultural understanding between the people of Asia and East Asian academic institutions.   • The Atomic Energy Scholarship Program of China
    The Atomic Energy Scholarship Program of ChinaProgram The Atomic Energy Scholarship (AES) Program of the China is jointly initiated by the China Atomic Energy Authority (CAEA) and the Ministry of Education of the People’s Republic of China (MOE) with the aim of fostering outstanding young talent working in the field of peaceful use of nuclear energy from developing countries.
  • Full Scholarships for Certain Programs
    Programs that provide full scholarships include Schwarzman Scholars Program, International Master's Program in Nuclear Engineering and Management, International Master's Program in Public Health, Master of Public Administration in International Development and Governance Program, International Master of Public Administration Program, International Master of Public Administration in the Belt and Road Initiative Program, International Master of Public Administration for Future Leaders Program, etc. You may consult relevant programs for further information about the scholarship application.
