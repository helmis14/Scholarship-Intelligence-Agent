Scholarships for fee-paying students

Scholarships are a great source of funding for prospective Master's students who are liable to pay tuition fees. Some of these are administered by Chalmers and some by other foundations and associations. Additional scholarships may be added to the list and you are therefore advised to check this webpage regularly.

Scholarships for Master's studies, administered by Chalmers

The scholarship page is not yet updated with the information regarding Chalmers scholarships offered in 2027 admission round. 

The application period for scholarships administered by Chalmers for Master's programmes will be open from late November 2026 until Mid-January 2027.

There is a separate application process for Chalmers-administered scholarships. You can only apply once you have submitted your programme application at  universityadmissions.se, as you will need to provide that application number.

You can apply for a scholarship before completing your programme application with the necessary documentation.
It is important to familiarise yourself with the key dates.
Please note that the scholarship deadline aligns with the programme application deadline, not the deadline for submitting supporting documentation.
Key dates for Master's studies

The scholarships listed below are for prospective students only. Applications must be submitted prior to the start of the first academic year at Chalmers as they are not open to current students. Scholarship offers are valid for two years of study under certain conditions. These conditions are outlined in the Scholarship Regulations.

Chalmers associated scholarship organisations

In addition to the scholarships administrated by Chalmers, we also cooperate with other associations providing scholarships for tuition fee-paying students.

External scholarships, administered by other organisations

There are also other organisations that provide scholarships that you can use to study at Chalmers. These organisations have their own timeline and application process, so make sure to check their website and contact them if you have questions about their scholarships.
