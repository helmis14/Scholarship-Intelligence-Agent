Find a scholarship for your study at Swinburne in Melbourne, Australia.

Make the most of your studies with a scholarship

Excited about moving here to study? We’re just as thrilled to welcome you! We value the vibrant contribution that international students make to our campus, culture and community. With an international scholarship, you can focus on experiencing all that Swinburne and life in Melbourne has to offer.

  • 2026 scholarships
  • 2027 scholarships

Swinburne International Scholarship

This scholarship is a merit-based scholarship offered to international students commencing their studies for the first time in 2026 at our Hawthorn campus in:

  • an undergraduate course
  • a postgraduate course
  • a pathway course (Swinburne Foundation Program, a UniLink diploma or Postgraduate Qualifying Program).


If eligible, you will receive 20 percent off your tuition fees for the standard course duration, calculated from the course start date to the course end date as stated in your letter of offer.

Excluded undergraduate courses

Bachelor of Aviation and Piloting (including the double degrees) and Bachelor of Nursing

Excluded postgraduate courses

Master of Physiotherapy, Master of Occupational Therapy, master degrees by research and doctorate degrees

George Swinburne International Excellence Scholarship

This scholarship will recognise a student’s high academic performance and is available to international students commencing an undergraduate or postgraduate course at our Hawthorn campus for the first time in 2026.

If eligible, you will receive 30 percent off your tuition fees for the standard course duration, calculated from the course start date to the course end date as stated in your letter of offer.

Excluded undergraduate courses

Bachelor of Aviation and Piloting (including the double degrees) and Bachelor of Nursing

Excluded postgraduate courses

Master of Physiotherapy, Master of Occupational Therapy, master degrees by research and doctorate degrees

ELICOS+ Scholarship

Are you applying for an English language course (ELICOS) plus an undergraduate or postgraduate (by coursework) course? You could get up to 10 weeks of your ELICOS tuition refunded. 

To be eligible for this scholarship, you must not satisfy the English language requirement of your principal Swinburne course. Once you have finished your ELICOS course and started your principal Swinburne course, a refund equal to 10 weeks of your ELICOS fees will be deducted from your tuition fees across the first three semesters of your undergraduate or postgraduate (by coursework) course. 

No extra application needed

When you apply to commence an intake in an eligible course for the first time in 2026 at Swinburne's Hawthorn campus, we automatically consider you for the available international scholarships based on the grades of your previous study.

If your application for study is successful, you will receive a letter of offer from us. In that same letter of offer, you will be notified if you have received an international scholarship. Visit our how to apply page for course application details.

Women in Engineering Leadership Scholarship

This scholarship supports female international students pursuing eligible engineering or construction courses while fostering professional development and career readiness through mentorship activities.

To be eligible, you must identify as a woman (as per your passport) and be commencing your studies at our Hawthorn campus for the first time in 2027 in one of the eligible courses. You must also meet the eligibility criteria for our scholarships.

If eligible, you will receive 30 per cent off your tuition fees for the standard course duration calculated from the start date to the end date as stated in your letter of offer.

The eligible courses are:

  • Bachelor of Construction Management (Honours)
  • Bachelor of Engineering (Honours)
  • Master of Construction and Infrastructure Management
  • Master of Engineering Science
  • Master of Professional Engineering

No extra application needed

When you apply to commence an intake in an eligible course for the first time in 2027 at Swinburne's Hawthorn campus, we automatically consider you for the available international scholarships based on the grades of your previous study.

If your application for study is successful, you will receive a letter of offer from us. In that same letter of offer, you will be notified if you have received an international scholarship. Visit our how to apply page for course application details.

  • “My scholarship is both a recognition of my achievements and an encouragement and incentive for my future studies.

    I have been given a real opportunity to realise my ambitions and reach my full potential in my field of study.”

    Lareina , Swinburne international student and scholarship recipient from China

Eligibility criteria

To be eligible for an international scholarship, you must:

  • be an international student (citizens of Australia or New Zealand and permanent residents of Australia are not eligible)
  • meet any course or scholarship-specific entry requirements, the terms and conditions of the scholarship, and the conditions outlined in your letter of offer
  • not be in receipt of a Swinburne tuition fee scholarship or reduced fee arrangement at any one time (Swinburne will apply the most favourable arrangement)
  • not be in receipt of another form of tuition fee scholarship or reduced fee arrangement from another organisation at the same time

Scholarship parameters

The scholarship applies to tuition fees only. It does not apply to any other fee, such as practicum training, Overseas Student Health Cover or other non-tuition fee costs.

Your letter of offer will include further details on the parameters and conditions for your scholarship.

Further criteria may apply to some scholarships. Contact our team at Swinburne or a representative in your country for specific information on your eligibility.
