Postgraduate Funding Opportunities

The Postgraduate Support Office (PSO) helps current and prospective students access funding for postgraduate studies and research by announcing calls for funding through the University website, social media platforms, faculties and information sessions. Funding opportunities are further communicated through the weekly UP research funding bulletin managed by the Research Capacity Development Unit within the Department of Research and Innovation (DRI). 

Different forms of funding are made available and administered through this division as per the below:

Internally funded opportunities
Externally funded
  • UP Doctoral Research Bursary
  • UP Postgraduate Bursary
  • UP Doctoral Commonwealth Bursary
  • NRF Postgraduate bursaries
  • DSI-CSIR
  • Mandela Rhodes
  • OWSD funding opportunities
