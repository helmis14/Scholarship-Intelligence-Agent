Call for international applications to participate in the scholarship programme in Mexico

8 June 2023
1,498 1 minute read

The Mexican Agency for International Cooperation for Development (AMEXCID) announces the opening of a call for international applications to participate in the scholarship programme in Mexico entitled “Excellence Scholarships in Mexico for Foreign Students”.

 This programme is intended for students and researchers registered in the various academic cycles (first, second and third cycles, post-doctoral research).

For more information on this program, the conditions of participation and the procedure for submitting applications, please visit the following website:

https://sigsa.sre.gob.mx

Application deadline before: July 21, 2023

It should also be noted that selected applicants will receive a scholarship ranging from 12,614 Mexican pesos ($700) to 15,768 Mexican pesos ($876) per month, with full coverage of transportation and treatement costs by the “AMEXCID” Agency. “. It is according to the criteria required on the site.

In addition, the Commitment and Acceptance Letter submitted by applicants must be written in Spanish and sent to the Programme Selection Committee through the following link. https://sigsa.sre.gob.mx To communicate with the stakeholders of this programme, AMEXCID provides people interested in this offer with the following e-mail address to request any information: infobecas@sre.gob.mx
