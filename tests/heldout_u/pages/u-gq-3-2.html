2025–2026 Scholarships Announcement for the People’s Republic of Bangladesh

  • December 4, 2025
  • Posted by: James Wotoe
  • Categories:
No Comments
Study Medicine & Dentistry in Bangladesh (2025–2026 Academic Year)
The Government of Liberia, in partnership with the Government of Bangladesh, invites qualified Liberians to apply for admission into Government and Non-Government Medical and Dental Colleges/Institutes in Bangladesh for the 2025–2026 academic year.
Important Information & Online Application:
Applicants must visit the official portals below for full details on requirements, deadlines, and procedures:
http://foreignstudents.dgme.gov.bd/login
www.dgme.gov.bd
www.mefwd.bd
www.dghs.gov.bd
Inquiries: 0776-871-512 / 0880-742-661
Monday–Friday | 9:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m.
Important Notice: Failure to meet the requirements or submit all required documents by the deadline will result in automatic disqualification.
Application Deadline: January 15, 2026
Eligible Liberians are encouraged to apply early and follow all instructions carefully
