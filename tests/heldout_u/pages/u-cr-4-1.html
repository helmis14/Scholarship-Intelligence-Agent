At Texas Tech University-Costa Rica, you can prepare for your place in the global economy.

And as a Texas Tech student or graduate, your learning, talent, and leadership skills contribute to the Red Raider community.

Leaders of Tomorrow Scholarships are for incoming students who are majoring in Restaurant, Hotel, & Institutional Management (RHIM) or Computer Science. Graduates in these fields can look forward to exciting career prospects!

Each Leaders of Tomorrow Scholarship covers up to $6000 of total tuition costs over a period of 4 years.
