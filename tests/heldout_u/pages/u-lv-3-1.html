The Government of Latvia offers scholarships to Korean students and researchers

   
Facebook X.com
30.01.2026. 10:46

To enhance student exchanges between Latvia and Korea countries, Latvian government offers three types of scholarships to Korean students and researchers for the academic year of 2026/2027:

  • Scholarships for studies at Latvian Higher Education Institutions (1 year bachelor’s, master’s or PhD studies). Application deadline: 1 April 2026;
  • Fellowships for research work in Latvian Higher Education Institutions (for academic staff up to 5 months) Application deadline: 1 April 2026;
  • Scholarships for participation in summer schools in Latvia in 2026. Application deadline: 1 April 2026.
