Введение

The Bolashak International Scholarship was established in 1993 to train qualified specialists at leading foreign educational institutions and organizations.

The program provides opportunities to pursue bachelor's, master's, doctoral, and residency programs, as well as professional internships.

Important: Bachelor's degree studies under the program are not available to all applicants. Since 2026, this has been a special competitive category for winners and prize-winners of international Olympiads in general education subjects and international scientific project competitions included in the approved list.

To participate in the program, applicants must meet the requirements of their chosen category and successfully complete the competitive selection process.

Who can win the Bolashak International Scholarship?

Citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan who meet the requirements and conditions of the competitive selection process may apply for the Bolashak International Scholarship.

The program offers the following study and training pathways:

  • Bachelor's degree programs — for winners and prize-winners (1st–3rd places) of international Olympiads in general education subjects and international scientific project competitions included in the approved list.

  • Master's degree programs — for applicants who have independently secured admission and certain professional categories specified in the Rules.

  • Doctoral programs — for applicants who meet the requirements for pursuing a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) or professional doctorate degree.

  • Residency programs — for applicants who meet the established requirements.

  • Professional internships — for employees belonging to categories determined by the Republican Commission for Training Personnel Abroad.

Requirements concerning educational qualifications, work experience, language proficiency, invitations from foreign organizations, and supporting documents vary depending on the applicant category.

The application submission periods and competitive selection dates are approved annually and published on the official website of the Center for International Programs JSC.

Definition of the category to be received

Before submitting documents, applicants must determine their intended study or internship pathway and verify that they meet the established requirements.

1. Bachelor's Degree Programs

Applicants who have achieved success in international Olympiads in general education subjects and international scientific project competitions included in the approved list may participate.

The main requirements include:

  • holding a diploma confirming first-, second-, or third-place winner or prize-winner status in the relevant international Olympiad or competition;

  • ensuring that the chosen field of study corresponds to the subject area of the Olympiad or competition;

  • meeting the admission requirements of a foreign higher education institution;

  • providing proof of the required level of proficiency in the state language;

  • submitting the documents specified in the Rules.

2. Master's Degree Programs

The following categories are provided for:

  • applicants who have independently secured admission;

  • applicants from rural settlements;

  • engineering and technical professionals;

  • healthcare professionals;

  • civil servants;

  • nuclear industry employees.

Certain professional categories are subject to requirements concerning work experience, diploma grade point average (GPA), proficiency in foreign and state languages, alignment of academic specializations, and verification of employment history.

Special admission requirements for foreign universities apply to applicants who have independently secured admission.

3. Doctoral and Residency Programs

Studies under doctoral and residency programs are available subject to compliance with the requirements of the Rules.

A separate category is provided for nuclear industry employees pursuing a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) or professional doctorate degree.

4. Professional Internships

The following employee categories have been designated for 2026:

  • engineering and technical professionals and employees of the agro-industrial sector;

  • nuclear industry employees;

  • users of artificial intelligence systems;

  • teachers and employees of educational institutions;

  • healthcare professionals;

  • civil servants;

  • media professionals;

  • employees in the fields of culture, tourism, and sports;

  • judges and court employees.

To undertake an internship, applicants must meet the requirements concerning professional experience, language proficiency, and the host organization abroad.

Important: Specific requirements should be verified against the current selection Rules and approved lists for the relevant year.

Preparation of necessary documents

The list of required documents depends on the chosen study pathway and applicant category.

Main Documents:

  1. Applicant questionnaire in the prescribed form.

  2. Identity document and passport.

  3. Educational qualification document with its supplement or transcript.

  4. Document confirming proficiency in the state language at the required level.

  5. Medical certificate in the prescribed form for persons traveling abroad.

  6. Motivation letter.

  7. Other documents specified in the Rules for the selected category.

Additional Documents Depending on the Category:

  • For bachelor's degree programs: a diploma from an international Olympiad or scientific project competition, documents confirming admission to a foreign university, and consent from a legal representative for applicants who are minors.

  • For applicants who have independently secured admission: confirmation of unconditional admission to a foreign higher education institution included in the approved list.

  • For professional categories of master's degree programs: employment records, information on mandatory pension contributions, a language proficiency certificate, and, where required, an application from the employer.

  • For doctoral programs: confirmation of admission and an approved individual study plan.

  • For internships: an invitation from the host organization, an internship program, documents confirming professional experience, and an application from the employer.

Documents issued in a foreign language must be accompanied by translations in the cases and manner specified in the Rules.

Submission of Documents

Applications to participate in the competition are submitted electronically through the eGov.kz portal.

Before applying, applicants must check the current list of required documents and the requirements for their selected category.

Choice of specialty and educational institution to study

How to Choose a Field of Study and a Foreign University

  1. Determine your intended field of study and eligibility category under the program.

  2. Review the list of priority fields of study approved for the relevant year.

  3. Check the list of related fields of study if it applies to your category.

  4. Select a foreign higher education institution from the approved list of recommended universities.

  5. Check the university's admission requirements, application deadlines, and required language proficiency certificates.

  6. Make sure the selected study program meets the requirements of the Bolashak International Scholarship.

Applicants for bachelor's degree programs must additionally verify that their chosen field of study corresponds to the subject of the international Olympiad or the field of the scientific project competition.

Conducting a competitive selection process

The competitive selection process for the award of the Bolashak International Scholarship is conducted in accordance with the Rules approved by Resolution No. 573 of the Government of the Republic of Kazakhstan dated June 11, 2008.

First Round — Comprehensive Testing

Applicants are assessed on their intellectual abilities, personal and professional competencies, and psychological readiness to study abroad.

Second Round — Interview

An independent expert commission conducts an individual interview to assess the applicant's professional qualifications, knowledge, and competencies.

Third Round — Decision of the Republican Commission

The Republican Commission for Training Personnel Abroad reviews the results of the competitive selection process and makes the final decision on whether to award or refuse to award the Bolashak International Scholarship.

Information on the results is published on the official website of the program administrator in accordance with the established procedure.

You won the Bolashak International Scholarship

After the Republican Commission decides to award the scholarship, the recipient must fulfill the requirements for arranging their studies or internship.

Step 1. Sign an Agreement

Contact the Center for International Programs JSC to conclude an agreement for studies or an internship.

Step 2. Arrange Security for Your Obligations

Depending on the applicant category and program conditions, collateral and/or guarantees may be required.

Specific requirements concerning the security of obligations, supporting documents, and deadlines are determined by the current Rules and the terms of the agreement.

Step 3. Prepare Documents for Travel Abroad

After signing the agreement, prepare the documents required for studying or undertaking an internship abroad, including visa documents and proof of funding where applicable.

Step 4. Comply with the Program Requirements

During your studies or internship, you must comply with the terms of the agreement, submit the required reports, and promptly inform the program administrator of any circumstances affecting your studies.

Step 5. Fulfill Your Obligations After Completing the Program

Upon completion of studies or an internship, the scholarship recipient must fulfill the terms of the agreement, including any applicable employment obligations.

For detailed information on the documentation process, security requirements, and communication with the assigned coordinator, please contact the Center for International Programs JSC.
