MSU Opens Applications for the MSU Inbound Scholarship 2026 – First Round

July 7, 2026MSU Scholarships, Newsby Nuntawat Pisaisawat1 Comment

Mahasarakham University is now accepting applications for the MSU Inbound Scholarship for International Students for Academic Exchange and Research Training at Mahasarakham University, Academic Year 2026, First Round.

This scholarship supports international undergraduate and graduate students who wish to join academic exchange, internship, or research training programs at Mahasarakham University. The program aims to promote international academic and research collaboration, strengthen multicultural learning, and expand MSU’s global academic network. The study or research period must be not less than 30 days and not longer than one academic semester. A total of five scholarships will be granted.

Selected students will receive a monthly living allowance. Students staying in university-arranged on-campus housing will receive 5,000 THB per month, while students who need to arrange off-campus accommodation because university housing is unavailable will receive 9,000 THB per month.

Applicants must be international undergraduate or graduate students currently enrolled at an accredited higher education institution in their home country. They must also have sufficient English proficiency, or another language required by the MSU academic advisor, and must hold valid health and accident insurance covering the whole period of stay in Thailand.

Required documents include the completed application form, academic transcript or proof of education, Statement of Purpose in English, research experience or research proposal if applicable, Letter of Acceptance from an MSU host faculty or unit, and proof of health and accident insurance coverage. The application form is available at https://inter.msu.ac.th/category/msu-scholarships/

Applications must be submitted to the host faculty or unit at Mahasarakham University. The host faculty or unit will then forward the complete application package to the Office of International Affairs, Division of Public Relations and International Affairs, Mahasarakham University, for further consideration. The application period is open from the date of the announcement until July 31, 2026. Eligible applicants and scholarship recipients will be announced in August 2026.

Scholarship recipients are required to submit a final report and presentation summarizing their academic exchange, research, or training activities before leaving Thailand. Any academic publication produced from the scholarship-supported activity must also acknowledge the MSU Inbound Scholarship.

For more information, interested applicants are encouraged to contact their prospective host faculty or unit at Mahasarakham University before preparing the application package.
