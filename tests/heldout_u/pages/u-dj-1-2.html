Scholarship from JetBrains Foundation

Scholarship from JetBrains Foundation
For the Program: MSc Advanced Software Technology (AST)
Image

The JetBrains Foundation offers full and partial scholarships for all applicants of the Advanced Software Technology (AST) program. 

  • Full scholarship – covers tuition fees and your first year of living expenses
  • Half scholarship – covers tuition fees only

The JetBrains Foundation is part of the JetBrains Group and is dedicated to supporting communities and advancing technology education worldwide. JetBrains tools are used by over 15 million developers, including teams at Fortune Global 100 companies, providing strong connection to cutting-edge industry and research environments.

More about MSc AST

Scholarship Application Process
  • Submit your program application by March 15, 2026.
  • Applications will be reviewed by a selection committee.
  • Shortlisted candidates will be invited to an interview with faculty members and program organizers.

Apply now

A strong application should
  • Be complete and well-structured;
  • Clearly highlight academic and research achievements;
  • Outline research interests and motivation;
  • Include a strong CV.

 

The final scholarship decision will be based on the interview results.

 

If you have any questions about the scholarship or program, feel free to reach out to Kristina Smolnikova at ksmolnikov@constructor.university.
