SRC Yi Bi Boa Scholarship Scheme

The SRC Yi Bi Boa Scholarship is a Scholarship scheme by the UGSRC which seeks to provide financial support to exceptional undergraduate students with higher financial needs of the University of Ghana.

Eligibility Criteria
  1. Be a SRC Dues paying student of University of Ghana (regular, fee-paying, city campus or a Distance education student).
  2. Be able to demonstrate financial need.
  3. A continuing student with a CGPA of 1.5 Or better
How To apply for the SRC YI BI BOA Scholarship Scheme
  • You are required to visit the SFAO website: https://www.ug.edu.gh/financialaid/
  • Go to Scholarships, then select SRC YI BI BOA SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME
  • Read the eligibility page and complete the ONLINE APPLICATION FORM with the required essays and documents.
  • In addition, applicants are required to PRINT OUT A HARDCOPY of the DECLARATION FORM and submit it to the Students Financial Aid Office on or before the deadline – Thursday, July 2, 2026.
Important Notice

1. If you are applying for financial aid for the first time or were unsuccessful with your previous application, please select New Application Submission under 'Application Type' and fill the online form.

2. If you have previously been awarded financial aid by the SFAO (and would like to renew it), then please select Renewal of Application under 'Application Type' and fill the online form.

For enquiries, please call the Students Financial Aid Office on 0302-945312/0205-186904/ 0206804077 or send an email to finaid@ug.edu.gh. You may also walk in during working hours to make enquiries.
