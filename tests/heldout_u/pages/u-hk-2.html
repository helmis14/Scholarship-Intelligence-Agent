Aim

The Sir Edward Youde Memorial Overseas Scholarships aim at financing outstanding students for overseas undergraduate studies.  They are expected to contribute significantly to the development of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (Hong Kong) after graduation.

Eligibility

  • Applicants must:
    1. be permanent residents of Hong Kong;
    2. have received at least five years of continuous full-time education in Hong Kong;
    3. fulfill all application procedures and admission requirements at the institution of their choices and be offered places subsequently; and
    4. be proficient in English and Chinese, and have a good mastery of the language in which their studies will be undertaken.
  • For further details, please refer to the Information Note of the application year.

Value of Awards

The maximum is HK$280,000 per year.  The Sir Edward Youde Memorial Fund Council may adjust downward the value by taking into account the actual university fees, necessary living expenses and other award(s) held concurrently by the scholar(s) to finance their overseas studies.

Method of Application (for 2027/28 academic year)

The study programme should only commence within one year after the formal announcement of the selection result in April 2027, i.e. between April 2027 and March 2028. During the application period, all application forms have to be completed and submitted online through “GovHK”. The signed Declaration (Annex E) (if any) and copy of relevant supporting documents should be submitted through the drop-in box at / by post to the Sir Edward Youde Memorial Fund Council Secretariat.

Commencement Date: 22 June 2026 (Monday)

Closing Date: 4 September 2026 (Friday)

All the above information is for reference only. Details will be announced when the awards are open for application.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need to be accepted by my chosen programme(s) at university(ies) / institution(s) outside Hong Kong before applying for the award?
    No. Applicants are only required to indicate up to ten combinations of universities / institutions and programmes as choices in their applications. In other words, it is not necessary for applicants to have obtained conditional or unconditional offers from the chosen university(ies) / institution(s) and programme(s) by the time they submit the applications.
  • If I am currently studying outside Hong Kong, am I still eligible to apply for the Fellowship / Scholarship awards?
    If applicants are currently studying outside Hong Kong, they can only apply for a proposed study programme other than the study programme being currently pursued. The proposed study programme should only commence within one year after the formal announcement of the selection result, which would normally take place in around the next April that follows the closing date of application.

    Please note that shortlisted applicants will be invited to attend in person selection interview(s) to be held between December and the next February following the closing date of application in Hong Kong.
