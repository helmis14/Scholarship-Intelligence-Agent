Adelphi was founded more than 125 years ago as the first coed private university on Long Island in New York. Today, we’re recognized across the nation—and around the world—for the quality of our academic programs, the support we give our students and the successful outcomes of our graduates.

Top Academics

There are many ways to measure the quality of academics. One is the annual set of rankings by U.S. News & World Report, which has consistently named Adelphi as one of the nation’s Best Colleges while recognizing many of our undergraduate and graduate programs. It also recognizes Adelphi as one of the country’s Top 100 in its new ranking of Best Private Universities.

Adelphi University has been named a Best College among National Universities for nine consecutive years. Adelphi remains the top-ranked private university on Long Island and places among the top 20 universities in New York State.
Celebrate Our Rise
Adelphi ranks No. 93 among private institutions in the country, a distinction that places it among the nation’s very best on U.S. News & World Report’s Top Private Schools list.
See How We Rank

Undergraduate Programs

U.S. News & World Report again ranks our undergraduate nursing program as one of the nation’s best. In addition, the undergraduate program offered by our Robert B. Willumstad School of Business appears in the prestigious Poets&Quants rankings.

Adelphi’s College of Nursing and Public Health climbed 14 spots to No. 172 in the annual list of Best Undergraduate Nursing Programs, placing it among the top 26% of universities nationwide and second among Long Island institutions.
Nursing Majors & Minors
Adelphi University holds the No. 1 spot among Long Island universities in the Poets&Quants 2026 Best Undergraduate Business Schools ranking, with our Robert B. Willumstad School of Business recording the largest gain on the list.
Adelphi Tops Long Island Universities

Graduate Programs

U.S. News & World Report ranks programs from five of our colleges and schools in its 2027 Best Graduate School rankings. All of these graduate programs and schools have appeared in the rankings for multiple years:

  • Best Part-Time MBA Programs
  • Best Nursing Schools: Master’s Degree
  • Best Nursing Schools: Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP)
  • Best Public Health School
  • Best School for Social Work
  • Best Psychology School
  • Best Clinical Psychology Doctorate Program
  • Best Speech-Language Pathology Program

Recognized Value

Top publications that focus their rankings on affordability, return on investment and career outcomes recognize Adelphi as one of the nation’s best colleges. For four years in a row, Forbes has ranked Adelphi as the best private university on Long Island in New York.

Forbes ranks Adelphi as one of America’s Top Colleges and as the top private university on Long Island. Its rankings recognize colleges that produce high-earning, influential graduates from all economic backgrounds, with less student debt.
Adelphi’s Ranking
Money magazine ranks Adelphi as one of the country’s Best Colleges for 2026. Its rankings focus on quality of education, affordability and student outcomes to help families make informed decisions about where to spend their tuition dollars.
A Top Value School
Adelphi earned national recognition in the 2026 Wall Street Journal/College Pulse rankings for its strong student outcomes. The University ranked highly in social mobility and best salaries, reflecting the lifetime return on investment it provides to students.
Ranked one of the best
Niche.com ranked Adelphi University in the top 8% of Best Value Colleges in America, the top 10% of Best College Locations in America, and the top 18% of Best Colleges for Nursing in America.
Ranked a Top College

Promoting Social Mobility and Student Success

Our annual ranking among the nation’s top performers in promoting social mobility powerfully reflects our commitment to our students’ success. So does our emphasis on career counseling—which starts as early as your first year.

Transforming lives at every income level, Adelphi University is ranked among the nation’s top social mobility performers, supporting students from all backgrounds in reaching their full potential.
Highly Ranked
Adelphi University earned a 2026–2027 Military Friendly® School with a Silver ranking—reflecting our strong commitment to supporting our military and veteran students.
Our Military Rating
Adelphi is recognized as a Career Development College of Distinction for the exceptional way that we equip students with skills to succeed in their lifelong career journey.
Expert Career Guidance

Supporting Transfer Students

Transfer students receive dedicated advising and a seamless credit process from day one, and Adelphi’s ongoing recognition by publications such as Colleges of Distinction reflects that commitment.

Adelphi is recognized by Colleges of Distinction for supporting transfer students through dedicated advising, a seamless transfer process and opportunities to thrive from day one.
Transfer Friendly
Named to the Transfer Honor Roll for the 10th consecutive year, Adelphi University is among just 275 colleges and universities nationwide recognized by Phi Theta Kappa for creating a supportive environment for transfer students and providing strong pathways to success.
A Top School for Transfers

An Athletics Powerhouse

Adelphi has established itself as a true powerhouse with conference and national championships. In the last three years, Adelphi :

  • Won two and appeared in a third NCAA Division II National Championships in men’s lacrosse
  • Appeared in two NCAA Division II Championship Finals in women’s lacrosse
  • Won three Northeast 10 Conference Presidents Cups (Overall Championship)

In Adelphi’s history, our record stands at:

  • 263

    NCAA Tournament Appearances

  • 45

    Northeast 10 Conference Championships

  • 71

    East Coast Conference Championships

  • 7

    Northeast 10 Presidents Cups

  • 5

    East Coast Conference Commissioner’s Cups

  • 19

    Division II National Championships

International Recognition

Adelphi’s reputation for excellence has spread beyond our borders and is now included in the prestigious Times Higher Education World University Rankings.

Times Higher Education ranks Adelphi in the top 7 percent of four-year universities in the United States and as one of the best universities in the world.
Global Recognition
Adelphi is recognized as a College of Distinction for the exceptional support services we provide international students at every stage of their academic journey.
Top Ranked in Global Belonging

Social Responsibility

Adelphi’s sustainability efforts start with the campus itself: Our 75-acre Garden City campus recently earned renewed arboretum status, and a $55 million modernization project is carrying that investment forward. We back it up with recognition as a Colleges of Distinction Vibrant Communities honoree for the inclusive activities we sponsor on and off campus.

Adelphi champions community engagement and sustainability by sponsoring inclusive activities for students and community members alike.
Great Campus Life
