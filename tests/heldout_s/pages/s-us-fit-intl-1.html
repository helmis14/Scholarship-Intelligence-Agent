types of aid

The benefits of private education are many: small class sizes, personalized attention from faculty who care, networking, research and other hands-on opportunities, and more. Private education is an investment in your future, and with the many financial aid options available to college students today, affording it is probably easier than you’d think.

There is a variety of financial aid available to help make your education affordable such as:

  • Florida Tech Scholarships and Grants
  • State Scholarships and Grants
  • Federal Aid
  • Private Scholarships and Loans

Florida Tech is also proud to be a Military Friendly University and help our veterans and active-duty service members earn a top-quality education. Please contact Military and Veteran Student Services for assistance using your military and veteran education benefits or the Department of Military Science for ROTC Scholarship opportunities.

University Scholarships and Grants

Florida Tech scholarships and grants are funds awarded directly by the university based on merit, financial need, or other criteria. 
Learn More About University Scholarships and Grants
State Scholarships and Grants

State scholarships and grants are financial awards provided by your home state government, typically based on academic achievement or financial need.
Learn More About State Scholarships and Grants
Federal Aid

Federal Financial Aid is available to US Citizens and eligible non-citizens who complete the annual Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). Types of aid include grants, loans, and work-study. opportunities.
Learn More About Federal Aid
Private Scholarships and Loans

Private scholarships and loans come from outside organizations or lenders. Scholarships don’t need to be repaid, but private loans must be repaid with interest.
Learn More About Private Scholarships and Loans
