    Student Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF)

    SURF Collection will occur October 14–16, 2026

    All SURF applications must be reviewed by a faculty/staff member, and SURF Collection will be your final opportunity. Please have all materials submitted into InfoReady prior to your SURF Collection appointment.

    • SURF Recipients

    What is SURF?

    The Student Undergraduate Research Fellowship (SURF) program is designed to allow sophomores, juniors, and seniors the opportunity to conduct in-depth research projects in their specific fields of study. Each student must have a faculty mentor, along with a well-developed proposal.

    All types of undergraduate research are eligible for support, and all academic disciplines are encouraged to apply. In addition to science, engineering, and mathematics projects, this program will support, for example, an art major who wants to sculpt a major project. Funds are provided for students to travel to conferences and present their accomplishments.

    Funding will not begin until January and may be up to $2,500 in stipend funding and $750 for travel funding for the student, plus $1,000 in student supply funding for the mentor, with lesser stipend amounts awarded depending on the grant period. If you are graduating in May, then your funding must end in May at graduation.

    Eligibility

    • U.S. citizen, resident alien, or national
    • Minimum of 30 hours of on-campus college credit at time of funding
    • You must be enrolled full time (Fall-Spring: 12 hours, Summer: 6 hours) every semester which you are funded.
    • Cumulative GPA of 3.25 or higher at time of application
    • Support from faculty mentor

    SURF Registration

    Applicants must complete and submit an online registration form prior to completing the SURF application. Upon registering, the student will receive a link to the SURF application packet, which includes the application form and detailed instructions.

    SURF Application

    Applications for SURF funding will be submitted electronically through InfoReady.

    Please consider attending an information session prior to beginning your application. If you cannot attend, please contact Dr. Halbert at mh171@uark.edu for more information.

    SURF Collection

    SURF Collection is a review of your application by a faculty/staff member. Please have all materials submitted into InfoReady prior to your SURF Collection appointment. All students will have their application collected before it is sent to ADHE. There are two options for SURF Collection:

    SURF Early Collection - prior to October 14, 2026

    • Students are encouraged to participate in an early collection. Please schedule an appointment with Matthew Halbert using this scheduling link if you would like your application reviewed and collected prior to the deadline.

    SURF Collection – October 14–16, 2026

    • Any registrants who have not participated in an early collection will have their application reviewed and collected by a University of Arkansas faculty or staff member during a Microsoft Teams appointment.
    • These appointments will be available to choose on October 1. If you have registered for SURF, you will receive an email with the opportunity to sign up for a collection appointment at that time.

    SURF Grant Timeline

    August 1 — SURF Registration Launch
    Complete the SURF registration form to receive application resources, announcements, and information about required review appointments. Registration is separate from the official application.
    September 1 — InfoReady Application Launch
    The official SURF application becomes available in InfoReady. Applicants may begin uploading documents and requesting recommendation letters.
    September — Information Sessions Begin
    Attend a SURF information session to review eligibility, application requirements, funding, and the review process. Applicants should also identify a faculty research mentor and begin preparing their materials.
    September–Mid-October — Application Preparation
    Work with your mentor to complete the research proposal, budget forms, student and mentor materials, transcript, résumé, and other required documents. Request recommendation letters early.
    Mid-October — SURF Collection
    Applicants must fully submit their InfoReady application—including recommendation letters—before their scheduled review appointment. Completion of a SURF review is required before an application can be forwarded to the state. Exact dates will be announced each year.
    Late October–November 1 — University Submission
    Following campus review and any required corrections, the Office of Nationally Competitive Awards submits approved applications to the Arkansas Division of Higher Education.
    December — Award Notifications
    The Arkansas Division of Higher Education notifies applicants of award decisions and provides acceptance instructions.
    January–Spring — Award Setup
    Recipients complete required acceptance documents, terms and conditions, compliance requirements, and university award-setup procedures before funding is released.

    Office of Sponsored Programs

    The Office of Sponsored Programs handles disbursement of awarded SURF funds.
