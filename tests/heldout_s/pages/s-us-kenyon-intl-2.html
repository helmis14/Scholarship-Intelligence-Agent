At Kenyon, mentoring and teaching go hand in hand, so our students come out on top — consistently earning national scholarships and prestigious postgraduate fellowships. What’s our secret? Exceptionally prepared students who give and receive great attention.

  • Fulbright Force

    Year in and year out, Kenyon is a top bachelor institution for producing Fulbright Fellows. Learn more about Fulbrights.

  • #2in the country among Division III institutions for producing NCAA Postgraduate Scholars.

  • Goldwater Glory

    Kenyon students are regularly recognized with Goldwater Scholarships, the most competitive undergraduate award in the sciences.  

  • 47NSF Graduate Research Fellowships have been awarded since 2010 to Kenyon alumni pursuing doctoral research.

We’re here to help.

Kenyon’s Office of National Fellowships & Scholarships helps current students and recent graduates identify and pursue prestigious fellowship opportunities and programs, providing resources and guidance throughout the application process and partnering closely with faculty mentors. Contact us to discuss which fellowship may be best for you.
