JMU Awards and Rankings

Awards and Prizes

Nobel Prize

A total of 14 Nobel Laureates have – at least temporarily – researched and taught at the Julius-Maximilians-Universität Würzburg.

ERC-Grants

The ERC Grants awarded by the European Research Council (ERC) are the most prestigious form of European research funding.

Leibniz Prize

    The German Research Foundation (DFG) awards the prizes every year to ten pre-eminent scientists.

Awards for Early Career Researchers

  • Heinz Maier Leibnitz Prize
  • Sofja Kovalevskaja Award

International Rankings

Academic rankings have repeatedly confirmed JMU’s strong national and international performance:

  • The internationally renowned Shanghai Ranking (2026) (German only) ranks the University of Würzburg among the three most research-intensive universities in Bavaria and the twelve best in Germany. In a global comparison, the University of Würzburg ranks 202nd.
  • The 2025 Times Higher Education (THE) World University Ranking currently ranks JMU 163rd globally and 16th nationally.
     
  • In the current Nature Index (2026) Research Leaders' Ranking JMU is among the top 220 universities worldwide. In a national comparison, it is among the top ten and therefore one of the strongest research universities in Germany.
