The Deutschlandstipendium scholarship

The University of Passau enjoys an excellent reputation in Germany and abroad owing to its consistently international approach and its focus on performance and practical relevance in research and teaching.

We expect a lot from our students, but at the same time we provide them with individual support and first-rate education – and give them the best chance for a bright future.

Through the Deutschlandstipendium scholarship programme, we support students at all stages of their degree studies who, in addition to excellent educational performance, have exhibited a strong social commitment and who have overcome hurdles in their own life and education.
