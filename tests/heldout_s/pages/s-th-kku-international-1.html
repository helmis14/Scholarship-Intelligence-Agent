Scholarship & Grant

  • Scholarship & Grant

KKU Inbound Visiting Scholar

(International Affairs Division)

  • The scholarship is for professors and lecturers from outside KKU to do onsite/online/hybrid exchange activity with KKU professors.
  • The scholarship provides up to 150,000 ฿ per project including travelling expenses, accomodation, visa, health insurance, and living allowance.
  • For more information : please visit https://iad.kku.ac.th/inboundvisiting/

KKU Outbound Visiting Scholar

(International Affairs Division)

  • The scholarship is for professors and lecturers from inside KKU to go abroad to do onsite/online/hybrid exchange activity with oversea professors.
  • The scholarship provides up to 150,000 ฿ per project including travelling expenses, accomodation, visa, health insurance, and living allowance.
  • For more information : please visit https://iad.kku.ac.th/outboundvisiting/

Abroad Research Presentation Scholarship

(the Research and Graduate Studies)

  • The scholarship is for professors and lecturers from inside KKU to go abroad to do onsite/online/hybrid research presentation (oral and poster).
  • The scholarship provides up to 70,000 ฿ per project including travelling expenses, accomodation, and registration fee.
  • For more information : please visit https://rad.kku.ac.th/Menu_support/presentation.php
