Meet With Us

Fund your passion and develop your competitive edge.

Applying for competitive scholarships and fellowships is more than just a pursuit of funding; it's a transformative experience that cultivates resilience, curiosity, confidence and self-reflection.

We understand the application process can seem complex and overwhelming, but you're not alone. The Office of Fellowships and Scholarships is dedicated to supporting you every step of the way—from identifying the right opportunities and refining their applications to securing strong letters of recommendation. Start by exploring the Find Funding page, checking out our advice on Building a Winning Application, or learning from the experiences of Our Scholars.

Featured Fellowships

Critical Language Scholarship

The Critical Language Scholarship Program (CLS) is a fully-funded overseas language and cultural immersion program for American undergraduate and graduate students.

  • Deadline: November.
  • Must meet with Megan Steward in advance of the deadline.
Learn more about the Critical Language Scholarship.  Link to the CLS webpage.

The Truman Scholarship

The Truman Scholarship seeks juniors with exceptional leadership potential who are committed to careers in government, the nonprofit or advocacy sectors, education or elsewhere in the public service.

  • Campus deadline: Nov. 15.
  • External deadline: February.
Learn more about the Truman Scholarship.  Link to the Truman Scholarship webpage.
