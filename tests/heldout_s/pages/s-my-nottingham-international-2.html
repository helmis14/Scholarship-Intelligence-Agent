Postgraduate Scholarships

Take the next step in your academic journey with UNM's postgraduate scholarships, providing financial support for those wanting to pursue a postgraduate programme with us.

  • Postgraduate Taught Excellence Scholarship
  • Corporate Partnership Postgraduate Taught Scholarship
  • University of Nottingham Malaysia Postgraduate Research Scholarship
  • Sibling Scholarship
  • Alumni Scholarship

 

Postgraduate Taught Excellence Scholarship
Description
This scholarship is offered to high achieving students enrolling into any UNM postgraduate taught programme, including master’s degrees, postgraduate certificates, and postgraduate diplomas.
Eligibility

 The following requirements must be met by successful candidates: 

  • Applicants must meet all entry requirements for their chosen postgraduate programme. 
  • Applicants must hold a strong upper second-class honours degree equivalent to a 65% average under the UK system or a CGPA of 3.4 out of 4 for Malaysian and US qualifications. 
  • Eligibility decisions are final and cannot be appealed.
  • Candidates applying through Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) are not eligible for this scholarship. 
Benefits

Eligible students will receive a 20% tuition fee reduction for the entire duration of their postgraduate taught programme, postgraduate certificate or postgraduate diploma.

To retain the scholarship
Students must successfully progress to the next academic year. 
Application process

No separate application required. Eligible offer holders are awarded automatically upon meeting the requirements, and the scholarship letter can be downloaded from the NottinghamHub portal.

Corporate Partnership Postgraduate Taught Scholarship
Description
The University of Nottingham Malaysia values its corporate partners and offers a scholarship for eligible staff members pursuing and Postgraduate Taught (PGT) programme at UNM. 
Eligibility
The applicant must be a current staff member of an organization listed under UNM's recognised corporate partners. 
Benefits
Recipients will receive 20% tuition fee reduction for the entire duration of their PGT programme. 
To retain the scholarship
The student must remain employed with the company recognized as a UNM's corporate partner throughout the study period. 
Application process
Once applicants receive an unconditional offer, they must submit a current letter of employment printed on the company's official letterhead and issued by the Human Resources Department. The letter should be addressed to the Sponsorship Unit.
University of Nottingham Malaysia Postgraduate Research Scholarship
Description

The University of Nottingham Malaysia Postgraduate Research Scholarship offers a competitive tuition fee reduction to outstanding students pursuing full-time postgraduate research (PGR) programmes. 

The scholarship is available to new postgraduate research students and aims to support high-achieving candidates with strong academic and research potential. 

Eligibility

Applicant must : 

  • apply for admission to a full-time postgraduate research programme at the University of Nottingham Malaysia 
  • hold at least a second-class undergraduate degree, or an equivalent qualification recognised as meeting the University of Nottingham's academic requirements 
  • meet all admission requirements, including the English language requirements 
  • demonstrate strong academic achievement and research potential 
Benefits

Successful applicants will receive one of the following tuition fee waivers: 

  • 50% reduction - available for all intakes, subject to scholarship availability and selection criteria 
  • 75% or 100% reduction - available for September intake only, subject to scholarship availability and selection criteria 

The scholarship is awarded for the normal duration of the programme: 

  • PhD - up to 3 years of the Research-Active Period 
  • MPhil - up to 2 years of the Research-Active Period
To retain the scholarship
The scholarship is awarded for the normal duration of the programme, provided recipients maintain satisfactory academic progress and continue to meet the University's postgraduate research progression requirements throughout their studies. 
Application process
  • identify a suitable research supervisor by contacting the relevant School through the Contact Research Supervisors webpage
  • submit an initial research inquiry together with the required supporting documents 
  • the School will assess your proposed research project, academic suitability, supervisory expertise, and the availability of research facilities 
  • if your proposed research is supported by the School and a supervisor is identified, you will be invited to submit a formal application via the NottinghamHub 
  • scholarship applications are considered as part of the admission process. As scholarships are highly competitive, meeting the minimum eligibility requirements does not guarantee the award of the scholarship
Sibling Scholarship
Description
When a student registers for any programme at the University of Nottingham, their second and subsequent siblings will be entitled to this scholarship. The award is applicable only in their first year of study at UNM and cannot be carried forward to subsequent academic years. 
Eligibility
The scholarship is awarded to the second and subsequent siblings of a registered UNM student. The siblings are not required to be enrolled at the University at the same time. 
Benefits
A 10% reduction on the first year's tuition fees.
To retain the scholarship
The scholarship is granted for one academic year only. 
Application process
Applicants are required to provide evidence of sibling relationship (e.g. birth certificate) and submit it to the Sponsorship Unit. 
Alumni Scholarship
Description
This scholarship offers UNM and UoN graduates an exclusive opportunity to pursue a second undergraduate degree or continue their studies at the postgraduate level with added financial support.
Eligibility
The UNM Alumni Scholarship is specially designed to support eligible Nottingham graduates who aspire to pursue another undergraduate, Master's programme or PhD at UNM. This scholarship caters to graduates who wish to continue their academic journey immediately after graduation, as well as those who have taken a break and are eager to recommence their studies.
Benefits
10% discount of the programme tuition fees.
To retain the scholarship
Progress to the next academic year. 
Application process
Automatic reduction will be granted once applicants submit their certificate of completion at the University of Nottingham.
