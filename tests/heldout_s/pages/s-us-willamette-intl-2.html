PNCA Oregon Arts Access Scholarship

  •  
Undergraduate Financial Aid
  • Equity Scholarships
  • Oregon Arts Access Scholarship
  • Equity Scholarships
  • Oregon Arts Access Scholarship

Pacific Northwest College of Art at Willamette University is proud to announce the new PNCA Oregon Arts Access Scholarship, a full-tuition scholarship designed to make a PNCA Bachelor of Fine Arts (BFA) education more accessible for Oregon for both first year and transfer students.

This scholarship provides eligible Oregon residents with coverage of remaining tuition costs after the application of eligible federal and state grants. Student loans are not considered grant aid and are not included in the scholarship calculation. The award is capped at the cost of full-time annual tuition. Any other eligible financial aid funds may continue to be used toward additional educational expenses beyond tuition.

Scholarship Details

To qualify, students must:

  • Be a resident of the state of Oregon.
  • Complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) or ORSAA no later than December 1st, 2026.
  • Demonstrate an adjusted gross family income of less than $80,000 annually based on FAFSA information*.
  • Have earned a minimum 3.0 cumulative weighted GPA.
  • Be admitted to Pacific Northwest College of Art at Willamette University.
  • Be pursuing their first bachelor's degree.
  • Qualify for Federal Pell and/or Oregon Opportunity Grants.

As a leading AICAD art and design college in the Pacific Northwest, PNCA is committed to expanding access to a high-quality arts education and supporting Oregon students as they pursue their creative and professional goals.

Application Deadline: December 1st, 2026

Students must submit both their PNCA application for admission and FAFSA by December 1st, 2026 to be considered for the PNCA Oregon Arts Access Scholarship. 

Willamette School Code: 003227

*The scholarship amount is capped at the cost of full-time tuition after eligible federal and state grants are applied. Scholarship funds may also be used toward other eligible educational expenses beyond tuition. Student loans are not considered grant aid and are not included in the scholarship calculation. The Student Aid Index (SAI) on the FAFSA must be 0 or less. In most cases, families with an adjusted gross income of less than $80,000 annually will have an SAI of 0 or less; certain uncommon circumstances may produce different results.
