Other Major Scholarships

University scholarships not only give recognition and encouragement to outstanding undergraduate and postgraduate students, but also provide financial support for students to participate in activities which are beneficial to their whole-person development.

University scholarships are usually granted by considering the following factors:

  • Academic merit
  • Community services
  • Leadership potential
  • Other non-academic performance, e.g. sports, innovation and creativity, music, arts

The University offers a wide range of scholarships and awards of which the major categories are as follows:

To be granted based on the academic excellence of the students, and may cover the tuition fee, academic expenses or living cost.  For example:

  • AIA Scholarships
  • Asian Future Leaders Scholarship Program
  • Croucher Studentships for Doctoral Study – Studentships in Hong Kong
  • The D. H. Chen Foundation Scholarship
  • The Hong Kong Jockey Club Compassionate Leader Scholarship
  • HSBC Hong Kong Scholarship
  • Link University Scholarship – Scholarship for Year 2-4 University Studies
  • Sir Edward Youde Memorial Fellowships for Postgraduate Research Students
  • Sir Edward Youde Memorial Scholarships (For Undergraduate Students)
  • Tung and Ngai Foundation Scholarship

To support students to participate in exchange, short-term overseas study programmes and other outreach activities.  For example:

  • HSBC Global Scholarship
  • Innovation and Technology Scholarship
  • Reaching Out Award

To support graduates to pursue a postgraduate degree, or even a second bachelor’s degree for some awards, at the overseas institutions.  For example:

  • Bei Shan Tang Foundation Scholarship – tenable at Stanford Graduate School of Education
  • Bei Shan Tang Scholarship – tenable at University of Pennsylvania
  • Chevening Scholarships – tenable at UK
  • Chiang Chen Overseas Fellowship – tenable at USA or Europe
  • Croucher Scholarships for Doctoral Study
  • Croucher Science Communication Studentships
  • The Esther Yewpick Lee Millennium Scholarship – tenable at University of Oxford (exclusively for CUHK graduates)
  • The Hong Kong Jockey Club Graduate Scholarship
  • The Hong Kong Jockey Club Music and Dance Scholarship
  • Hong Kong Scholarship for Excellence Scheme
  • Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship – Research Students – tenable at Japan
  • Knight-Hennessy Scholars Program – tenable at Stanford University
  • Kwok Scholarships – University of Oxford
  • Kwok Scholarships – Yale University
  • McCall MacBain Scholarships – tenable at McGill University
  • The Rhodes Scholarship – tenable at University of Oxford
  • Schwarzman Scholars Program – tenable at Tsinghua University
  • Sir Edward Youde Memorial Fellowships for Overseas Studies

To award students with enthusiastic participation in campus services and/or community services.  For example:

  • AIA Scholarships
  • CUHK Convocation Outstanding Services Student Awards (exclusively for CUHK undergraduates)
  • The D. H. Chen Foundation Scholarship
  • The Hong Kong Jockey Club Compassionate Leader Scholarship

To support outstanding young talents for participating in research at renowned institutions outside Hong Kong.  For example:

  • Croucher Fellowships for Postdoctoral Research
  • Professor Charles K. Kao Research Exchange Scholarships (exclusively for CUHK undergraduates)
  • To give due recognition to students for their creativity and stimulate their passion in innovation.  For example: Professor Charles K. Kao Student Creativity Awards (exclusively for CUHK students)
  • To award students who can demonstrate creativity and outstanding achievements in humanities and natural sciences respectively.  For example: CUHK Convocation Outstanding Creativity Student Awards (exclusively for CUHK undergraduates)

To encourage students who have demonstrated achievements or talent in (a) Sports and games; (b) Music and performing arts; (c) Culture, arts and design; or (d) Innovation, science and technology.  For example: Talent Development Scholarship

  • Some corporations offer both internship opportunities and scholarships to students. After completion of the internship, scholarship would also be granted to the students as encouragement.
  • To help awardees realize their career plans.  For example:
      • Innovation and Technology Scholarship
      • Tung and Ngai Foundation Scholarship
