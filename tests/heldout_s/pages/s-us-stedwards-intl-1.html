How Scholarships Work

You are automatically considered for merit scholarships when you apply to St. Edward’s. For most scholarship opportunities, an additional scholarship application isn’t needed. Students who receive athletic scholarships may not be eligible for all of the scholarships and grants listed below. Applicants should submit a completed application for admission by the priority application deadline in order to be fully considered for all scholarships and need-based grants.

Merit scholarships consider things like:

  • High school or transfer school GPA
  • High school class rank (if available)
  • Academic pursuits in high school or from a transfer school
  • Demonstrated ability to be successful at St. Edward’s

First-Year Student Merit Scholarships

At St. Edward’s, scholarships help reduce the average tuition paid by first-year students by more than half. All new freshmen admitted through regular admission will be considered for our merit scholarships, which range from $14,000 to $32,000 annually.

Students who graduate from a Catholic high school can qualify for the Catholic High School Scholarship, which provides an additional annual award of $5,000. Holy Cross high school students who qualify for the Sorin Award are not eligible for the Catholic High Scholarship. This scholarship is available to full-time, first-year and transfer students entering in the 2026-2027 academic year.

Students who graduate from an IDEA Public School can qualify for the IDEA Partner Scholarship, which provides an additional annual award of $5,000. This scholarship is available to full-time, first-year and transfer students entering in the 2026-2027 academic year.

Students who graduate from a KIPP Public School can qualify for the KIPP Partner Scholarship, which provides an additional annual award of $5,000. This scholarship is available to full-time, first-year and transfer students entering in the 2026-2027 academic year.

Students who transfer to St. Edward's from Austin Community College can qualify for the Austin Community College Scholarship, which provides an additional annual award of $5,000. This scholarship is available to full-time transfer students entering in the 2025-2026 academic year.

Students who are U.S. citizens and don’t live in Texas can qualify for the Edwardian Scholarship, which provides an additional annual award of $3,000. Recipients of the Holy Cross Sorin Award aren’t eligible for the Edwardian Scholarship. This scholarship is available to full-time, first-year students.

CPT James Ahn Special Forces Memorial Scholarship

Captain James Ahn, a graduate of UC Riverside and the Claremont McKenna College Army ROTC program, died in 2015 during a parachute training operation while participating with his Special Forces unit in Washington State. You are eligible if you complete the application by June 1 and are a:

  • Former Special Forces Service Member
  • Full-time student pursuing degree/certification completion at an accredited post-secondary institution including graduate school, university, college, community college, or vocational/technical institution
