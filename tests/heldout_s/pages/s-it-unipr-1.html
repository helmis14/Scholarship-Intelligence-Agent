Fees, scholarships and benefits for international students

This page provides all the information needed to calculate the financial situation for students from a household with income and assets abroad (also in part).

How it works

In the Italian system, a person's economic and asset situation is established using the ISEE (Indicatore della Situazione Economica Equivalente), which is calculated based on each person's income and assets.

On your economic and asset situation depends the tuition fees' amount and the possibility to obtain the scholarship, the accomodation and other benefits.

To apply for accommodation, scholarship and fee reduction, you will need to produce documentation showing the income and assets of your household, so that your financial situation can be assessed in accordance with the Italian system.

What documents you need

To calculate your financial situation in order to apply for the scholarship, the accomodation, the tuition fees' reduction, ecc. you must hold documentation showing the income and assets of your household.

You must upload the requested documents on the Er.go's website, the Regional Authority for Higher Education Rights.

Click here to get information about the documents to send.

Tuition fees

The tuition fees are the following:

  • first instalment or administration fee: 156 euros (regional tax and stamp duty)
  • annual tuition fees (personalized tuition fee): from 0 to about 2000 euros, depending on financial situation and area of study 

Once you have had your economic and patrimonial situation converted according to the Italian system, you can access all the information on university fees and contributions by clicking here.

For information on how to pay, please refer to the following page.

You can apply for tuition fees' reduction through this page on Er.go's website.

Then, you must submit the documentation to ER.GO using your Dossier Utente only, by uploading it in the Corrispondenza e Protocollo > Invia documentazione > Documentazione redditi esteri section, by the deadlines established for finalizing the application.

You can fill in the application even if you are not yet enrolled at the university (if you are a student applying for a visa, wait until you have obtained your acceptance letter).

For further information: sertasse@unipr.it 

Er.go's scholarship

The scholarship is a financial aid that you can use to pay for the expenses that you must face during your studies.

In Italy the benefits related to the right to education are provided by each Region. 
In Emilia-Romagna the Regional Authority for Higher Education Rights is called Er.go

Er.go and the University of Parma jointly administer benefits for students, including scholarship, accommodation and reduced university fees.

To apply, access to this page. Fill in the personal and financial data sections and the benefits application form. 

You can fill in the application even if you are not yet enrolled at the university (if you are a student applying for a visa, wait until you have obtained your acceptance letter).

You can start filling out the online application from the beginning of July, when the call for applications is published. 
On the Er.go website, you will find the full notice with all the useful details, including the deadline for submitting applications for each benefit.

Do not turn to intermediaries to help with your application, as they often charge for the service and may cause you to make mistakes.

For further information about scholarship click here.

Accomodation

If you meet the requirements set out in the competition announcement, you can apply for Er.go's accommodation as well.

To apply for accomodation, you must fill in the application on Er.go's website (the same to request scholarship and tuition fees' reduction) by ticking the corresponding item in the form "Benefici a concorso".

To get all information about Er.go's accomodation, visit this page.

If you don't succeed in obtaining Er.go's accomodation, you can browse our Vetrina alloggi and/or contact the Welcome office's staff (welcome@unipr.it). 

Do you have income both in Italy and abroad?

If your household has income and/or assets in both Italy and other countries, you will need to declare and document them all. Then follow the steps on this page.

More information

We invite you to carefully read the Er.go call for applications for the 2026-2027 academic year, which will be published on the Er.go website in early July, for all the details on the requirements for accessing the various benefits and all the deadlines.

If you require any information or clarification on the online application process or the required documentation, please visit the 'Contact' section of the Er.go website and join the Telegram and WhatsApp channels.

For information on tuition fee reductions, please email sertasse@unipr.it.
