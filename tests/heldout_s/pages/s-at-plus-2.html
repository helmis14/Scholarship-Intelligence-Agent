The University of Salzburg boasts six faculties with 34 departments and around 90 degree programmes in digital and analytical, natural and life sciences, social sciences and cultural studies, law and economics, and theology. Almost 18,000 students are enrolled in bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral programmes here.

Founded in 1622 by Prince Archbishop Paris Lodron and re-established in 1962, today the University of Salzburg is the largest educational institution in Salzburg.

The University of Salzburg is a research institution well established in both local and international networks. It is a high-profile institution boasting a diverse subject offer. Around 2,000 academics are responsible for shaping this profile through their excellent research work at the University of Salzburg.

Research-based teaching is one of the trademarks of the University of Salzburg. It combines the versatile research services with an excellent teaching offer for all students.

“The University of Salzburg is more than a place of knowledge – it is a place of exchange, growth, and diversity.
As Vice-Rector for Human Resources, it is particularly important to me that all staff members – whether in research, teaching, or administration – are able to contribute their skills, experience, and backgrounds, and are willing to take responsibility for our university.
We are committed to equal opportunities, sustainability, and a culture of mutual respect.
If you would like to become part of a forward-looking university in one of Europe’s most liveable cities, we warmly welcome you.”

– Univ.-Prof. Mag. Dr Elias Felten, Vice-Rector for Human Resources

FUNDING SCHOLARSHIPS

Scholarships for the promotion of scientific theses.

Amount of support grant: from € 750,00 to € 3.600,00

Requirements:

  • Application including description of work, financial plan and statement of costs
  • Present at least one expert opinion
  • Compliance with the requirements for the study programme, as well as the respective period of study plus an additional semester
  • Meet the proposal criteria
