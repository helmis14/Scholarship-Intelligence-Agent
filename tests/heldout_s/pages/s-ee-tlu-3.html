Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship programme writing camps 2025

Tallinn University (TLU) is searching for promising early-career researchers to join a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship (MSCA PF)! This fellowship offers 12-24 months of research funding, including a competitive salary, allowances, and research expenses.

Why choose Tallinn University?

  • Excellent Funding Opportunities: Even if you don't secure the MSCA PF, you might be eligible for alternative funding through ERA Fellowships or Estonian Research Council grants (for the candidates who have passed the 70% threshold in the evaluation).
  • Expert Mentorship: Work alongside experienced supervisors who will provide guidance and support.
  • Intensive Training: Participate in a 5-day hands-on training program designed to enhance your research skills (travel support, accommodation, and meals included!).

Who is eligible?

  • PhD holders with a maximum of 8 years of full-time research experience.
  • Researchers of any nationality who haven't lived or worked in Estonia for more than 12 months in the past 3 years.
  • Applicants must choose the TLU as their host institution.

Training and writing camps are organised at various times before the summer depending on which School is organising them.
