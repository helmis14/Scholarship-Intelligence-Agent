Saudi Arabia

Name of Program

Government of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia Scholarships for International Students and Researchers 2026/2027

Scholarship Offer

Type of Scholarships:
Language scholarships, student exchange programs scholarships, scholarships for short-term programs and whole degree programs

Programs for:
Undergraduates, Graduates, PhD students, researchers

Fields:
All fields of study

 

The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia offers government scholarships for international (non‑Saudi) students to study at Saudi public and private universities.

Scholarship Offer

For the 2026/2027 academic year 27 scholarships are offered to Switzerland. Scholarships are available for

  • academic degrees such as Bachelor's, Master's, and Doctorate;
  • advanced research opportunities in science, technology, and the humanities;
  • language study, and short-term and student exchange programs.
     

Eligibility Criteria

  • Applicants must be 16 years of age or older.
  • Applicants must hold a valid passport with at least six (6) months’ validity from the intended date of entry to the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
  • Applicants must provide proof of proficiency in the language of instruction of the chosen study programme.
    • Exception: applicants applying for Arabic language studies are exempt from this requirement.

Additional academic or programme‑specific criteria may apply and are determined by the host university.

Scholarship Benefits

Two categories of scholarships are offered:

Full scholarships

  • Cover all study‑related expenses in accordance with applicable Saudi regulations
  • The exact scope of benefits (e.g. tuition fees, living allowance, accommodation, health insurance, travel costs) is defined by the host university and applicable regulations.

Partial scholarships

  • Scholarships covering part of the tuition fees
  • The level and form of support vary by university and programme
  • Support may include:
    • tuition fee reductions, or
    • partial benefits related to educational services

Application Deadline

31.05.2026

Place of Submission

Applications must be submitted online through the official Study in Saudi Arabia platform https://studyinsaudi.moe.gov.sa.

Applicants may apply to multiple universities through a single application.

Remarks

Once the application has been submitted, it is sent to the educational institution(s) selected by the student. Admission decisions are made by the respective institutions. Following approval by the institution, the application is processed by the Ministry of Education and the institution to complete the final procedures.

Announcement and Further Information

Further information and application: https://studyinsaudi.moe.gov.sa

Romania

Name of Program

Scholarships of the Romanian Government 2026-2027

Scholarship Offer

Type of Scholarships:

Scholarships for whole degree programmes. 

Programs for:

Undergraduates, Graduates, PhD students. 

Fields:

All fields of study except medicine, dental medicine and pharmacy.

The Romanian State awards trough the Ministry of Foreign Affairs scholarships to foreign citizens for  studies in Romania to Foreign citizens from all non EU-countries. 


Eligibility 

To be eligible, the scholarship applicant must:

  • submit a complete file;
  • present study documents issued by accredited/recognized education institutions in the country where they were issued;
  • comply with the enrolment deadline;
  • have good results in education, respectively an average of the study years, for the last graduated school of at least 7 (seven) corresponding to the scoring system in Romania or the "Good" score, as the case;
  • comply with the application submission methodology.

Level of study 

What level of study does the scholarship cover?

The scholarship applicant can choose from any of the following three study cycles in accredited higher education institutions in Romania:

  • a)bachelor’s degree: bachelor's degree programmes are addressed to graduates of high school studies or pre-university studies at the end of which they obtained a baccalaureate degree or equivalent, as well as candidates applying for further university studies in Romania. The complete programme runs for a period of 3-6 years, depending on the specialization followed and ends with a bachelor's exam;
  • b) master’s degree: master's degree programmes are addressed to undergraduates, run for a period of 1 year, 1.5 years or 2 years and end with the dissertation exam;
  • c) PhD: doctoral programmes are addressed to graduate students or equivalent and run for a period of 4 years. In order to be admitted to doctoral studies and to obtain the scholarship, it is compulsory that, prior to submitting the application, the candidate should get the written agreement of the Doctoral Tutor, which must be a member of the doctoral school, and also that he/she passes the admission interview.

Language of study 

In order to promote the Romanian language and culture, the beneficiaries of the scholarships granted by the Romanian state for undergraduate and master's studies study only in Romanian. The exception is doctoral scholarship holders, who can opt for studies in Romanian or in a foreign language set by the doctoral school. For candidates who do not speak Romanian, a preparatory year is granted for the study of the Romanian language prior to the actual university studies, with the exception of doctoral scholarship holders who have opted for the form of education in a foreign language set by the doctoral school.
 

See the following website for the required documents and the scholarship benefits: https://scholarships.studyinromania.gov.ro/scholarship-about#docs

Program Description
The Darmasiswa Scholarship Program is a one-year, non-degree scholarship offered by the Government of Indonesia to foreign students to study Bahasa Indonesia, Indonesian arts, and culture at selected universities located in various cities across Indonesia.

Eligibility Criteria
Applicants must meet the following requirements:

  • Be a foreign national.
  • Have completed secondary education or its equivalent.
  • Be at least 18 years old and not older than 32 years (special age limit applicable for this year).
  • Have the ability to communicate in English, proven by an English Language Proficiency Certificate (TOEFL, TOEIC, IELTS, or other applicable certificates).
  • Be in good health, as proven by a medical certificate.
  • Have basic knowledge of the chosen field of study.
  • Demonstrate a strong interest in learning the applied field.
  • Not be a Darmasiswa Alumni.
  • Not be a Kemitraan Negara Berkembang (KNB) Scholarship Alumni.
  • Not currently be studying in Indonesia.
  • Not currently be working in Indonesia.

Scholarship Benefits
The scholarship provides the following benefits:

  • Monthly living allowance: IDR 4,700,000
  • Book allowance (one-time): IDR 2,000,000

Estonian Government Scholarships for international students, researchers and academic staff 2026-2027

The Estonian Ministry of Education and Research and Education and Youth Board offer scholarships to foreign nationals in the framework of the Estonian Scholarship Programme for international students, researchers and academic staff.

Scholarships for summer schools
The scholarship supports participation in short summer school courses of Estonian language and culture courses organized by the Estonian institutions of higher education as well as in summer schools related to the degree programmes taught in English.

  • Eligibility: BA, MA and PhD students enrolled at a higher education institution abroad who have completed at least one year of studies by the time of application. Pre-registration for the selected course is required. In the 2026 summer school application round, applications for online courses are also eligible.
  • Amount of scholarship: The scholarship covers a course fee of up to 700 EUR and accommodation costs of up to 25 EUR per night for a maximum period of four weeks. The Education and Youth Board transfers the scholarship funds directly to the university organising the course.
  • Duration: 1–4 weeks.

Grants for researchers and academic staff
Grants support academic activities such as research, teaching or collaboration with colleagues at Estonian higher education institutions.

  • Eligibility: Researchers and academic staff employed at higher education institutions abroad who hold an academic position at their home institution (e.g. professor, associate professor, lecturer, research fellow, assistant). PhD students can apply only under the student category. Grants for postdoctoral studies are not offered.
  • Amount of scholarship: Short-term grants (1–9 days) are funded at 45 EUR per day. Long-term grants (10 days – 10 calendar months) are funded at 660 EUR per month.
  • Duration: Short-term grants cover 1–9 days, long-term grants range from 10 days up to 10 calendar months.

Application Deadline

01.05.2026

Place of Submission

The application form with required documents must be submitted via email to rvo.dokumendid@clutterharno.ee.

Remarks

  • The decision to award a scholarship or grant does not guarantee admission to the host institution. Admission must be obtained separately and prior to the scholarship or grant decision.
  • Scholarship funds are generally transferred directly to the host institution and not to the individual applicant.
  • For research grants, the grantee must stay in Estonia for the entire duration of the grant period, which must fall within the relevant academic year.
  • For summer schools, only one course per applicant can be funded.

Within the framework of a bilateral exchange, the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports of the Czech Republic (MEYS) offers 2 to 4 scholarships to Switzerland.
The scholarships are designed for university undergraduate or graduate students and Ph.D. candidates who wish to undertake study or research visits to one of the Czech public institutions of higher education.

Scholarship-holders usually attend courses that do not lead to the award of a degree (non-degree study courses) or pursue an independent research activity (research visit), depending on their academic qualifications and preferences. Applicants are required to choose a field of study that is related to the field that they are studying or studied in the past at their home university/college.
Enrolment in standard degree study programmes, especially Ph.D. courses, for the period of scholarship is also possible. The feasibility of this option depends upon the host university.

The scholarships are not intended for intensive courses of Czech language and culture. A separate notification of scholarships for participation in the Summer Schools of Slavonic Studies is published on the Website of the Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports of the Czech Republic (Summer Schools of Slavonic Studies).


Scholarship Offer

  • Scholarships of a total of 18 months for the academic year 2026/2027, divisible into 9 and 4.5 months scholarships
  • monthly scholarship rate of CZK (Czech Koruna) 12,000 for Master's degree students and CZK 13,000 for holders of a Master's degree or equivalent
  • tuition waiver
  • medical benefits to the same extent as provided by health insurance in Switzerland

The cost of accommodation is included in the monthly scholarship rate. If applicants wish to be accommodated in a student dormitory, they must select this option on the application form under "PLANNED STUDY IN THE CZECH REPUBLIC".


Eligibility

  • Swiss citizenship (exception: foreign students who have completed their previous studies in Switzerland)
  • Undergraduate students at least in their 2nd Bachelor's degree year as of autumn semester 2026/2027
  • Graduate students and PhD candidates
  • All applicants must currently be enrolled at one of the universities or research institutions in Switzerland.

Please note:
Scholarships are offered for studies in the Czech language. Scholarship-holders can be admitted also to some English-language study programmes provided that the host university is willing to accept the candidate within the framework of the MEYS’ scholarship programme and to grant a tuition fee waiver for the English-language study programme. Candidates are advised to check with the desired host university at an early stage whether the university agrees to waive the tuition fees for the English-language study programme if they apply for a Czech Government Scholarship.

Overview
The Colombian Government, through ICETEX in cooperation with Colombian public and private higher education institutions, offers reciprocity scholarships to foreign citizens for postgraduate studies in Colombia, aiming to promote academic exchange and international cooperation.
Up to 70 scholarships are available under the 2026‑2 call. To promote geographical diversity, ICETEX may grant a maximum of five (5) scholarships per country of origin, subject to institutional criteria and budget availability. Scholarships are available for specialization programs, master’s degree programs, and, in specific cases, prior Spanish language courses.

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must:

  • Be foreign citizens, over 18 and under 50 years of age
  • Not hold Colombian nationality (including dual nationality)
  • Not reside in Colombia, nor have resided there in the six months prior to application
  • Not have initiated studies in Colombia at the time of application
  • Not have previously received a Beca Colombia scholarship
  • Not have a marital or de facto union with a Colombian citizen
  • Hold a completed undergraduate degree
  • Have a minimum undergraduate GPA of 4.0 out of 5.0 (or equivalent)
  • Hold final admission to one to three postgraduate programs listed in the official Academic Offer Catalogue 2026-2

Scholarship Benefits

The scholarship covers:

  • Full tuition waiver (100%), granted by the host institution
  • Monthly living allowance equivalent to two Colombian legal minimum monthly wages, paid quarterly in arrears (for in‑person programs only)
  • Health insurance allowance for medical and hospital care during the study period (subject to budget availability)
  • Courtesy Visa (Type V) issued free of charge for the duration of studies
  • One‑time allowance of COP 250,000 for the Foreigner Identification Card (Cédula de Extranjería)

Note: For virtual programs, only the tuition waiver is granted.

Duration of Scholarships

  • Spanish language courses: up to 6 months*
  • Specialization programs: up to 12 months
  • Master’s degree programs: up to 24 months
    • Master’s programs with three semesters are funded for up to 18 months

Programs exceeding these durations are not eligible.

*For applicants from non‑Spanish‑speaking countries, a Spanish language course of up to 6 months may be granted prior to the start of the academic program, provided that the applicant already holds admission to a master’s or specialization program listed in the Academic Offer Catalogue 2026‑2.

Application Deadline

05.06.2026 (17h00 Colombia time, GMT‑5)

Place of Submission

Applications must be submitted online on the ICETEX platform. All documents required for an application must be uploaded to the application platform.

Remarks

  • Each candidate must apply directly to the Colombian higher education institutions for admission to a Master's program.
  • The final letter of admission to the academic program must specify the start and end dates of the academic program. All letters of admission must be for programs beginning in the second semester of 2026. Deferral of the start of the academic program is not permitted under this call. If the program does not start in the second semester of 2026, the scholarship and its benefits will be withdrawn.
