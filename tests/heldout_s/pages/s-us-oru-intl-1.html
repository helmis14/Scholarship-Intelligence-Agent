ORU QUEST: Experience It!

You're invited to Oral Roberts University! This personalized, immersive campus experience lets you see what your future could look like as a Golden Eagle.

  • Compete for a full-tuition Quest Scholarship
  • Connect with current students and make new friends
  • Dive into your academic interests during open houses, class visits, and info sessions
  • Experience ORU’s spiritual life through Chapel
  • Explore opportunities to get involved on campus
  • Tour our NEW residence halls
  • Stay overnight in the dorms*
  • Take a campus tour 

Parents: We have prepared special moments throughout the event where you can learn about various aspects, such as college payment options, campus safety, student support, and more.

*Overnight visits are exclusive to students admitted to Oral Roberts University. Submit your application today!

FREE

Elevate your Quest experience and make lasting memories that will shape your future. *Overnight visits are exclusive to students admitted to Oral Roberts University.

Register Now

$25

This $25 one-day experience is designed for high school juniors, seniors, and transfer students interested in Oral Roberts University’s School of Worship, Media & Performing Arts. It includes an in-depth tour of ORU’s Peggy V. Helmerich Media Arts Center, discipline-specific workshops, ORU merchandise, lunch, the opportunity to audition for program admission and talent scholarships, free admission to a School of Worship, Media & Performing Arts event, and much more!

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

WHAT IS THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN THE ONE-DAY AND TWO-DAY OPTIONS?

Quest is a one-day, free experience that includes campus tours, academic open houses, Chapel, parent sessions, and access to next-step support. Quest Plus is a two-day, $50 experience that includes everything in the one-day option plus expanded class visits, additional campus life activities, and an exclusive overnight stay for admitted students.

HOW DOES SCHEDULING WORK FOR QUEST?

After registering, you will be able to review the full schedule of offerings and select the sessions you are most interested in attending—including academic open houses, class visits, interviews, tours, and campus activities—so you can shape your experience around your interests.

WHAT IS THE DRESS CODE?

The dress code will vary depending on the activity, but business casual attire is suggested for the competition. For a packing list for Quest Plus, click here.

WHAT ABOUT LODGING?

Admitted students (23 years and under) choosing the two-day option may participate in an exclusive overnight stay in the residence halls. Space is limited and requires advance registration. Parent/Sponsors will need to find overnight accommodations off-campus. Click here for a list of hotels in the area with an ORU discount.

CAN PARENTS ATTEND CLASS VISITS WITH THEIR STUDENT?

Due to limited classroom seating, class visits are intended primarily for students. However, if space permits, parents are welcome to join. Our team will be available on-site to help you navigate options throughout the event.

WHAT EVENT DO YOU RECOMMEND IF I DON’T KNOW IF ORU IS FOR ME YET?

If you're unsure about ORU, we recommend that you join us for our one-day Quest event! This will give you an opportunity to experience what ORU is all about without any financial commitment.

DOES ORU HELP PAY FOR CAMPUS VISITS?

Yes! ORU believes seeing campus in person makes all the difference — so we offer several ways to help make your visit more affordable. Click here to learn more.

WHERE CAN I FIND MORE INFORMATION ON THE FULL TUITION SCHOLARSHIP PROCESS?

For more information on the Quest Whole Leadership Scholarship, getting nominated, submitting a Quest Essay, when scholarship decisions are made, and other details, click here.

WHAT ARE THE TERMS OF THE VISIT AND FILE GRANT?

The Visit and File Grant is available to eligible students who submit a valid FAFSA or Financial Guarantee and complete a campus visit during the promotional period. Awards are subject to verification, institutional policies, and availability of funds. Completion of these steps does not guarantee admission or receipt of the grant, and the award is not offered as an incentive for enrollment.
