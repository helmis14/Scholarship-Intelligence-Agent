College and department scholarships

To be considered for college and department scholarships, new and continuing undergraduate and graduate students must complete the annual scholarship application in MyNEVADA.

The scholarship application for the 2026-2027 school year becomes available October 1, 2025 and is due February 15, 2026.

New students must complete all admission requirements by February 15 to be considered for scholarships.

In addition to the scholarship application in MyNEVADA, some academic departments may require a supplemental application to qualify for their scholarships. Please review the list below to see if you need to submit an additional scholarship application specifically for your department.

Colleges and departments requiring additional scholarship applications

  • Department of Art, Art History and Design Scholarships
  • School of Music Scholarships
  • Sanford Center for Aging (Gerontology) Scholarships
  • University of Nevada, Reno School of Medicine Scholarships
