EKÖP-KDP Scholarship 2025/2026

The draft of the call for University Research Fellowships – Cooperative Doctoral Programme (EKÖP-KDP) was approved by the NRDI on 13.05.2025. The call for applications is open from 13.05.2025 to 06.06.2025, 23:59.

The most important preliminary task for applicants is to find a supervisor approved by the Doctoral School related to their research topic with whom they can discuss their research idea/plan.

Who can apply?

Within the framework of the 2025/2026 EKÖP-KDP call for proposals, applications for individual research grants may be submitted for the following periods for doctoral students who:

  • had active doctoral student status in the 2024/2025 academic year, or applied for admission to a doctoral programme starting in the 2025/2026 academic year,
  • have not passed the complex examination yet, or passed the complex examination in the spring semester of the 2024/2025 academic year,

more detailed information in the call for application

How and where to submit applications (2025.06.04. 23.59pm)

All correspondence and requests for information relating to proposals should be sent to the contact e-mail address unkp_bce@uni-corvinus.hu

  • the application documents described in point 12 of the call for application must be submitted to one of the following thematic e-mail addresses, according to the category of the call for application, no later than 04 June 2025 – 23:59.pm:

ekop_kdp@uni-corvinus.hu

 

The above e-mail addresses are only used for sending (automatic saving) of the application documents.

Documents submitted electronically are accepted in the following format/extension: PDF

 

Expectations regarding manually or electronically signed documents:

  • the declaration must be signed with an electronic signature based on a qualified certificate, with at least an advanced level of security, or
  • the declaration must be authenticated using the ePapír service, through the use of Ügyfélkapu+ / DÁP
  • A scanned version of the printed and hand-signed document is acceptable.

In the case of a document in a foreign language (other than English), please submit a certified Hungarian translation in electronic format, too.

 

For the sake of the readability of your application, we recommend that you use one of the following to complete the annexes:

  • File (menu) / Save as PDF (*.pdf) or
  • File (menu) / Print by selecting Microsoft print to PDF
