Scholarships for Local JUPAS Students Admitted
on the Basis of HKDSE Results (2026 Entry)

The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) offers Admission Scholarships to outstanding local students admitted on the basis of the Hong Kong Diploma of Secondary Education (HKDSE) results through Joint University Programmes Admissions System (JUPAS). To be eligible for the University Admission Scholarships, local JUPAS students should have obtained Level 5** in at least one HKDSE subject. The amounts of University Admission Scholarships for local JUPAS students range from a minimum one-off amount of HK$8,000 to HK$704,500 covering full-tuition fees by instalments as well as a one-off study allowance and exchange scholarship for local and/ or overseas learning activities.

Students need not apply for the University Admission Scholarships. The University will identify qualified local JUPAS students and inform them of the scholarship offers after admission.
