Alumni scholarship

We’re committed to supporting our graduates with further opportunities to advance their careers and find work they love. That’s why we offer alumni scholarships to encourage you on your continuing journey through education.

Scholarship details

If you’re one of our graduates, you’ll already know we consider you part of our global community. But that’s not just a feel-good statement – we’d like to encourage you to continue pursuing your career goals with our support. That’s why graduates of Torrens University Australia are eligible to receive 25 per cent off total tuition costs as a fee-waiver alumni scholarship for their next qualification including diplomas, degrees, masters, graduate diplomas and graduate certificates.

To be eligible you need to be a domestic or an international student who completed the postgraduate or undergraduate degree.

To apply, contact our Future Student Advisors.

Frequently asked questions

  • What are the eligibility criteria for the scholarship?
    The eligibility criteria will vary depending on the scholarship you are applying for. For more information on your eligibility, please contact our Future Student Advisor.
  • What are the selection criteria?
    Depending on the scholarship, selection criteria may include academic merits, transcripts, portfolio submissions, written submissions and Torrens University internal applications.
  • When is the deadline to apply for a scholarship?
    You can apply for a Torrens University scholarship any time up to an intake date, to allow sufficient time to review and process the application.
  • Who should I contact if I have questions about the application process?
    Contact our Future Student Advisor with any questions related to your scholarship application.
  • Who is eligible to apply for a domestic student scholarship?
    To apply for a domestic scholarship, you need to be an Australian citizen, Australian permanent resident or New Zealand citizen, or hold a humanitarian visa.
