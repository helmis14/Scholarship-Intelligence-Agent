Scholarships for residents of Bahamas and Caribbean

Vantage Airport Group International Award

Students from the Bahamas (or Jamaica if there are no eligible applicants from The Bahamas) entering an undergraduate program in the Faculty of Adventure, Culinary Arts and Tourism or the Bob Gaglardi School of Business and Economics will automatically be considered to receive a $7500 award. Selection is based on the student's academic record and recommendations from their admission file assessment. If no applicants are available from The Bahamas or Jamaica, the next choice would be a student from the Caribbean.
