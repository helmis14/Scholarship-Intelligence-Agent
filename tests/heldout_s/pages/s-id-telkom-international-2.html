DARMASISWA SCHOLARSHIP 2024 AT TELKOM UNIVERSITY

Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 at Telkom University. Telkom University in 2024 welcomes all international students to participate in Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 at Telkom University. Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 is a one-year non-degree scholarship program that offers international students (non-Indonesian citizens) to learn Indonesian language, arts, music, crafts, and other specific subjects. Experience your once-in-a-lifetime academic study by studying Indonesian language and Textile and Craft in Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 at Telkom University, one of the best private universities in Indonesia. So, grab the opportunity now on Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 and choose Telkom University.

 

PROGRAM OF DARMASISWA SCHOLARSHIP 2024 AT TELKOM UNIVERSITY

Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 at Telkom University offers students to learn Indonesian language (BIPA), and Textile & Craft.

 

PROCEDURES OF DARMASISWA SCHOLARSHIP 2024 AT TELKOM UNIVERSITY

All international students (non-Indonesian students) who wish to apply for the Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 at Telkom University must register and submit all the required documents to Darmasiswa official website at https://apply.darmasiswa.kemdikbud.go.id and choose Telkom University for host university.

 

REQUIREMENTS OF DARMASISWA SCHOLARSHIP 2024 AT TELKOM UNIVERSITY

Here are some requirements of Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 at Telkom University:

  1. Having foreign nationality.
  2. Having completed secondary education or its equivalent.
  3. Minimum age 18 years old and maximum age limit of 32 years old (special for this year in 2024).
  4. Able to communicate in English, proven by English Language Proficiency Certificate: TOEFL/TOEIC/IELTS or OTHER CERTIFICATES if applicable).
  5. Physically and mentally healthy, proven by a medical certificate.
  6. Having basic knowledge of the applied field.
  7. Having strong motivation in learning the applied field.
  8. Not listed as alumni of Darmasiswa Scholarship Program.
  9. Not listed as alumni of KNB Scholarship Program.
  10. Not currently studying in Indonesia.
  11. Not currently working in Indonesia.

 

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS OF DARMASISWA SCHOLARSHIP 2024 AT TELKOM UNIVERSITY

  1. Curriculum vitae/ resume (template can be downloaded on the Darmasiswa website)
  2. Health/ medical certificate (in English)
  3. Passport that is valid for at least 18 months after application.
  4. Recommendation Letter from Education Institution/ Professional Institution on official letterhead and signature (in English)
  5. Recent academic transcript and academic certificates (in English)
  6. English language proficiency certificate (if applicable)
  7. Certificates related to the applied field (if applicable)
  8. Photo of passport (color)
  9. Essay containing applicant’s purpose of study (in English or Indonesian language maximum in 500 words).
  10. A two-minute video that shows applicant’s full body in describing her/his motivation to participate in the program and the next contribution as an ambassador of Indonesian culture after graduating in the program of Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 at Telkom University. The video must be in either English or Indonesian language.

SCHOLARSHIP COVERAGE OF DARMASISWA SCHOLARSHIP 2024 AT TELKOM UNIVERSITY

  1. Awardees of Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 at Telkom University will receive living allowance of IDR4,700,000/month, book allowance IDR2,000,000 (one-off), and settlement allowance of IDR4,700,000 (one-off).
  2. Awardees of Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 at Telkom University will be provided with accommodation and transportation during the Orientation Program if their time of arrival aligns with the schedule provided by the Ministry of Education, Culture, Research and Technology (MoECRT).
  3. Awardees of Darmasiswa Scholarship 2024 are advised to bring extra money (better in USD) for their personal unforeseen expenses in Indonesia, including processing fees for IMEI registration for phones priced above USD 500 (please refer to the FAQ for details).
