Scholarships with Specific Eligibility

Kevin and Michelle Clark Scholarship

Summary: Scholarships for CELCON students

Eligibility: Fulltime FAU Nursing student in good academic standing pursuing a nursing degree. Must be 1st generation college student in their freshman year of college.

 

Dr. Marlaine Smith Endowed Scholarship

Summary: Scholarships for CELCON students with financial need

Eligibility: Fulltime FAU Nursing student with demonstrated financial need



D. Burdella Cunningham Scholarship

Summary: Scholarships for CELCON students

Eligibility: Fulltime FAU Nursing student with demonstrated financial need

 

Helen Shoaf Memorial Nursing Scholarship

Summary: Scholarships for CELCON students

Eligibility: U.S. Citizen in their Sophomore year or higher in an FAU Nursing program with demonstrated financial need

 

Evelyn Weinberger Nursing Scholarship

Summary: To support annual undergraduate and graduate scholarships in the College of Nursing.

Eligibility: Awarded to 50% Undergraduate and 50% Graduate students registered as Fulltime FAU Nursing studetns

 

Dr. Nettie Birnbach Research Scholarship

Summary: To support doctoral level nursing students who are committed to conducting and completing nursing research as part of his/her degree program within the Christine E. Lynn College of Nursing.

Eligibility: Doctoral Nursing student committed to conducting nursing research as part of their degree program.

 

Masters Nursing Scholarship

Summary: To provide scholarships for students in the Master's program of the Christine E. Lynn College of Nursing.

Eligibility: Fulltime FAU Nursing Student in a graduate program

 

D’Avolio Family Scholarship

Summary: To provide funding for books for undergraduate nursing students

Eligibility: Fulltime FAU Nursing Student in an undergraduate program

 

Cross Country Healthcare Scholarship

Summary: To provide educational or research opportunities to CELCON students

Eligibility: Any fulltime FAU Nursing student in good academic standing

 

Hugh & Anny Godsey Scholarship

Summary: Scholarships for CELCON students

Eligibility: Fulltime students with at least a 2.75 GPA, making progress towards a degree

 

Esther Saylor Rothenberger Scholarship

Summary: For outstanding CELCON students who are either in an Undergraduate or Master’s program

Eligibility: Fulltime student in Undergraduate of Master’s program

 

Celia Lipton Farris and Victor W. Farris Foundation Nursing Scholarship

Summary: To support graduate nursing students, with preference for those specializing in mental health and/or suicide prevention. Scholarships may support student research projects related to mental health and/or suicide prevention in academic coursework or outside of coursework

Eligibility: Graduate students

 

Kay Carpenter Nursing Scholarship

Summary: Merit based scholarship for undergraduate BSN students

Eligibility: Students must be enrolled in the Freshman Direct, RN to BSN, or the Accelerated BSN program

 

Rhonda Goodman Caring Through Service Nursing Scholarship

Summary: Support for CELCON students who demonstrate caring through community service.

Eligibility: Applicants must provide confirmation of community service with their scholarship applications

 

DiPaula Family Scholars Program

Summary: Support for incoming Nursing Freshman

Eligibility: Fulltime enrollment in a Nursing program, incoming Freshman only

 

Lois M Galloway Memorial Nursing Scholarship

Summary: Support for Doctoral Nursing student

Eligibility: Fulltime enrollment in Nursing PhD Program

 

La Luz Roselia Inguanzo Memorial Scholarship

Summary: Scholarships for Nursing Students

Eligibility: Requires a one-page essay to be submitted on the impact of receiving the scholarship

 

Forrest C. and Frances H. Lattner Fund

Summary: Support Master’s and Doctoral level students who intend to become Nursing faculty

Eligibility: Fulltime Nursing Graduate student
