UP Scholarship 2026-27: Revised Last Date, Eligibility, Amount and Status

October 17, 2026 by Puneet Hooda
The UP scholarship application dates 2026 has been revised and Class 9 and 11 fresh applicants can now apply until October 17, 2026. Dashmottar (degree, diploma, ITI, B.Ed and other post-Class 12 courses) fresh applications are open until October 31, 2026.

UP Scholarship is the Uttar Pradesh government’s scholarship and fee reimbursement scheme for students from Class 9 up to postgraduate, diploma and ITI courses. It is open to General, SC, ST, OBC and Minority students who are permanent residents of Uttar Pradesh and whose family income is within the limit for their category, usually ₹2 lakh or ₹2.5 lakh a year. Applications are made only on the official portal, scholarship.up.gov.in, and the money is paid by DBT to the student’s Aadhaar-linked bank account.

UP Scholarship Registrations 2026-27 – OTR Process

One Time Registration (OTR) has been mandatory on the UP Scholarship Portal since the 2025-26 session and continues for 2026-27. An active mobile number and Aadhaar number are required. Only one OTR is allowed per student; creating multiple OTRs may lead to debarment.

Fresh applicants can follow these steps:

Step 1 – Visit scholarship.up.gov.in and select One Time Registration.

Step 2 – Authenticate the active mobile number and complete Aadhaar e-KYC.

Step 3 – Generate the OTR and save the number received on the registered mobile.

Step 4 – Open Student Registration and select the correct scholarship, category and study level.

Step 5 – Enter the OTR, complete UP Scholarship registration and save the session-specific registration number.

Step 6 – Log in under Fresh Login, fill in personal, academic, fee and bank details, and upload the prescribed documents.

Step 7 – Review every entry before final submission. Download and print the locked application.

Step 8 – Submit the printed form and supporting documents to the school or institution within the specified timeline.

UP Scholarship renewal applicants should use Renewal Login, update their present class or course, previous-year result, fee and bank details, complete authentication where required, and submit the form within the shorter renewal window.

Documents Required for UP Scholarship Application

Applicants applying for UP scholarship are required to keep the following documents handy while filling the UP scholarship application form –

1. Aadhaar card

2. UP domicile or residence certificate

3. Current income certificate

4. Caste certificate, if applicable

5. Previous qualifying examination marksheet

6. Current admission proof, student ID or bonafide certificate

7. Current-year fee receipt

8. Bank passbook showing an account in the student’s name

What After the UP Government Scholarship Application?

Your application passes through several offices before any money is released. A delay at any stage delays payment.

Stage Who acts What happens
1. Institution Your school/college Checks hard copy, records marks and attendance, does biometric authentication, forwards online
2. DIOS / University District Inspector of Schools (Class 9–12) or university/affiliating body (Dashmottar) Confirms genuine students and blocks ineligible students, courses or institutions
3. NIC scrutiny National Informatics Centre Automated data checks; mismatches are marked as suspect data
4. District committee District-level officers Lock verified data with digital signature
5. Demand creation Department Prepares the fund demand
6. Transfer Treasury via PFMS Amount credited to the student’s bank account by DBT

How to Check UP Scholarship Status 2026?

Students who have applied for UP Scholarship for this academic year can check their scholarship status by following the steps given below:

Step 1 – Visit the official UP Scholarship Portal.

Step 2 – Open the Status section and select the relevant academic session.

Step 3 – Enter the registration number, date of birth and captcha.

Step 4 – Review whether the application is pending, forwarded, verified, approved, rejected or marked for correction.

FAQs

1. What is the UP Scholarship last date 2026-27?

Under the revised timetable, Class 9 and 11 fresh applications will close on October 17, 2026, and Class 10 and 12 renewals closed on October 15, 2026. For Dashmottar courses, renewal closed on October 15 and fresh applications will close on October 31, 2026.

2. Has the UP Scholarship last date been extended in 2026?

Yes. In October 2026 the state revised the Class 9–12 timetable, extending fresh applications for Class 9 and 11 to October 17 and renewals for Class 10 and 12 to October 15. Dashmottar dates were not changed.

3. How do I check my UP Scholarship status?

Visit scholarship.up.gov.in, open the “Status” section, and enter your registration number and date of birth. After hard-copy submission, your institute verifies and forwards the application, so status updates happen in stages. Check regularly for corrections or payment updates.

4. When will UP Scholarship money come?

Phase 1 payments for Class 10 and 12 renewals run September 29 to October 5, 2026. Fresh school applicants are expected to be paid between January 25 and January 31, 2027. Dashmottar fresh payments are scheduled for 27–31 January 2027.

5. How do I apply for UP Scholarship renewal?

Renewal is for students who already received the scholarship last year and passed or were promoted. Use your previous year’s registration number to log in to the Renewal section, update your details, submit the form, and hand the printout to your institute.

6. What is the eligibility and income limit for UP Scholarship?

Students must be Uttar Pradesh residents studying in recognized institutions. Family income limits are generally ₹2.5 lakh per year for SC/ST students and ₹2 lakh for General, OBC, and Minority students. Aadhaar-linked bank accounts and OTR registration are mandatory.

7. Can I get UP Scholarship if I study outside Uttar Pradesh?

Yes, if you are a permanent resident of UP. Apply under the Post-Matric Other State scheme.
