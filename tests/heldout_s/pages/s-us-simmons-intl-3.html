Boston Scholarship

Undergraduate

We are deeply committed to Boston

As an institution deeply committed to the city of Boston, Simmons University strives to provide opportunities for local high school students to achieve success at the college level and beyond.

The Boston Scholarship is a four-year, full-tuition scholarship. Once at Simmons, recipients must maintain a 3.0 cumulative grade point average and are recommended to live on campus.

International students are not eligible for the Boston Scholarship.

Students who meet the following criteria are invited to apply for the scholarship:

  • Currently attend a Boston public high school or Boston charter school
  • Have at least a 3.0 cumulative grade point average (re-calculated)
  • Simmons is test optional so it is your choice if you would like to submit standardized test scores with your admission application. For those choosing to submit test scores, a 21 or higher is recommended on the ACT and a 1080 or higher is recommended on the SAT
  • Have demonstrated creativity, engagement, global perspective, leadership, resiliency, or other qualities indicative of the ability to contribute to Simmons' diverse community

The Application Process

Deadline: January 10

In order to be considered for this scholarship, applicants must submit their admission application along with their Boston Scholarship application materials by January 10. Applicants must first be admitted to the university by the scholarship deadline. Scholarship materials may be submitted either through the Common Application or through your applicant portal.

As part of the application process, applicants are required to submit an essay and participate in an on-campus interview.

Students should also complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA), if applicable, by February 1. The federal school code for Simmons is 002208.

Essay

The application consists of an essay in response to the prompt below. Students should submit their essays in PDF or Word Doc via email with the subject line "Boston Scholarship Application," by January 10. Each page of the document should include the student's full name and date of birth.

Please note: it is the student's responsibility to submit a working file in the proper format (PDF or Word) as we will not convert or accept incompatible files (i.e. Google Docs, Pages, etc.)

Essay prompt

Please choose ONE of the following essay prompts. Your response should be no more than 500 words.

  1. At Simmons, Boston is our home. Think about an issue or need you have observed in your community in Boston that is important to you. Describe why this is meaningful to you, and how you and others could make an impact or improvement on this issue.

    OR
  2. Think of a Bostonian who you admire. This person can be a famous figure or personal connection, and from the past or present. Identify what quality/qualities you admire about them, and explain how their example has influenced your own choices or actions. Use specific anecdotes and examples.

On-Campus Interview

Interview: Finalists will be invited via email to schedule an interview. We encourage students to schedule an on-campus interview, but there will also be an option to do them virtually. Interview days are typically held during February break. Finalists will receive a confirmation email with their interview date and time after scheduling.
