      Fellowships, Scholarships, and Grants

      Departmental Fellowships, Scholarships and Grants

      Department fellowships, scholarships and grants are awarded by each individual academic program. All applicants are considered for an award. A separate application is not required. Admitted students are typically notified at the time of admission of any type of award offer.

      Loyola College of Arts and Sciences

      Awards within Loyola College of Arts and Sciences are determined at the program level:

      • Biological Forensics
      • Data Science
      • Emerging Media
      • Forensic Pattern Analysis
      • Psychology
      • Speech Language Pathology
      • Theological Studies
