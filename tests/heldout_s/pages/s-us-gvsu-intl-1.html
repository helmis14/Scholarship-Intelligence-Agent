myScholarships – GVSU and External Scholarships

Grand Valley offers a scholarship-matching tool to help admitted students match with GVSU scholarships, as well as external scholarship opportunities. myScholarships will prompt you to answer questions to help match you to scholarships for which you might qualify. Every student, each year, should log into the scholarships system! By just logging in, you may match to scholarships that do not require an application. Scholarships will be available to view and apply to starting December 1. 

The FAFSA for the 2027-2028 academic year will be available in October! We recommend that you start your scholarship search early. If you are applying for a scholarship that requires you to show financial need, we will determine that after you file your FAFSA. For more information on the FAFSA, please visit Applying for Financial Aid.

Scholarship Information

GVSU Scholarships

Grand Valley offers 500+ donor and department scholarships. The majority of scholarship deadlines fall between February 15 and March 1. Students are encouraged to review their eligibility and submit applications for such scholarships by the posted deadlines. Students can begin to submit applications for the 2027-2028 academic year on December 1. These scholarships vary and may be based on interests, academic performance, special talents or major. Many of these scholarships are made possible through generous donations.

Each year, you will need to log into the system. By just logging in, you may automatically match to scholarships that do not require an application!

Starting December 1 each year, a comprehensive list of all GVSU scholarships can be viewed without logging in here. 

External Scholarships

These scholarships are provided by agencies, foundations, or other providers not affiliated with Grand Valley State University. There are several types of scholarships available based on your major, minor, GPA, hobbies, interest, and more. These scholarships are from local, state or national organizations. The scholarships in this database have been vetted and come from a reliable source.

These scholarships are available all throughout the year. Be sure to continue to log back into the system multiple times throughout the year to see new scholarships that are added or that you have been matched to.

Scholarship Matching Steps & Timeline

After logging into myScholarships, you will be asked to complete a series of questions to create your student profile. These questions, along with information from Grand Valley (major, GPA, class level, FAFSA, etc.), will help to match you to scholarships for which you might qualify. Remember, you will still want to review the qualifications of the scholarships before submitting an application to make sure that you fully qualify for the scholarship. The majority of GVSU scholarships will be available from December 1 through March 1, while external scholarships are available year-round. Grand Valley has been notified that some external scholarships have updated eligibility criteria and application deadlines. Students are encouraged to review their eligibility and submit applications for scholarships by the listed deadlines.

We recommend reviewing the myScholarships User Guide for step by step instructions.

Scholarships Disclosure

Please read the disclosure prior to logging into the system.

  • By logging into the scholarship system, I grant Grand Valley State University and members of scholarship review committees complete access to the records and information contained within my student file, including information from my academic record, admissions application, FAFSA, financial aid record, and any other information to help determine scholarship eligibility.
  • By logging into the scholarship system, I acknowledge that as a scholarship award recipient it is my responsibility to submit a thank you letter to my scholarship donor(s) upon request from the Office of Financial Aid & Scholarships.
  • By logging into the scholarship system, I consent the release and publication of my name and pertinent criteria information as a scholarship award recipient to the appropriate scholarship donor(s), as well as any local media outlets, including those affiliated with GVSU. I retain the right to submit a written withdrawal of this consent at any time.
  • By logging into the scholarship system, I acknowledge that all scholarship awards are subject to the availability of funding and applicable institutional policies and may be modified or rescinded accordingly.
  • By logging into the scholarship system, I acknowledge that, as a scholarship recipient, I may be contacted by University Development to meet with the scholarship donor.
  • By logging into the scholarship system, I hereby verify that the information I provide in the system is true and correct, to the best of my knowledge. I understand that withholding or falsifying information may result in revoked consideration of any scholarships I apply to.

Scholarship Review Process

Grand Valley participates in the practice of blind reviewing to allow for unbiased review of scholarship applications. This means that the Office of Financial Aid & Scholarships does not share your name, student ID number, photo, or other identifying factors with reviewers when they review your application. In order to continue to keep your identity anonymous, we ask that you do not include any of these items in your essays, short answers, or uploaded documents.

The scholarships team is here to help you! If you would like to schedule an appointment for assistance with your scholarship search and to receive feedback on applications, please email [email protected].

myScholarships FAQ

Do I have to be a student at Grand Valley State University to use this database?

Yes. You must be an admitted student at Grand Valley State University with a network ID and password to apply for scholarships through the myScholarships system. If you are a new student, your network credentials will be emailed to you after acceptance to the university. 

Is a FAFSA required for scholarships?

Not all scholarships require a FAFSA or financial need to be eligible, however filing your FAFSA can increase the number of scholarship opportunities you are matched to.

The 2026-2027 FAFSA is now available! You can file your FAFSA here: https://studentaid.gov/. Once you file your FAFSA, it will take time for the Department of Education to process your FAFSA and send it to Grand Valley. We will determine your eligibility for any need based scholarships you applied to (scholarships that require you to show financial need through the FAFSA), as well as any that you are currently eligible for once we receive your FAFSA.

How many questions do I need to answer to complete my student profile?

There is no specific amount. However, the more questions you answer, the more scholarships you could be matched with now and in the future. If you have new profile questions to answer, you will see a gray bar on your Dashboard that states "You still have outstanding questions that may qualify you for more opportunities! Click here to continue answering more matching questions."

You can also view the questions you have already answered and update them by clicking on the Questions tab from your Dashboard on the left side of your screen.

When should I start searching for scholarships?

To be considered for as many scholarships as possible, start your search at least one year in advance. The majority of scholarship deadlines will be between December and March for the upcoming year. Our scholarship timeline (see the Scholarship Application Steps & Timeline above ) includes important dates for GVSU Scholarships. You may consider inputting these dates into your calendar with a yearly repeat, as many of these dates remain the same every year. Start early and stay organized.

How often should I visit myScholarships?

You should log in periodically to check and see if there are any new scholarships you have matched to, or if there are new questions to answer to help match you to more opportunities. Scholarships are added throughout the year, so be sure to continue to log in.

How do I see the scholarships I matched to?

After answering the questions, click on the “Scholarships” tab on the left menu to see the list of GVSU scholarships and external scholarships to which you have matched. You can then view the application if one is required or for external scholarships, you will be able to click on the link to be re-directed to that scholarship's website where you can complete the application for that opportunity.

What does "Internal requirement managed by institution" mean?

Many scholarships use data from our Student Information System, which includes details like your admissions status, GPA, and major. Since we already have access to this information internally, you don’t need to include it in your application. Questions about criteria can be emailed to [email protected]

My scholarship application requires a resume, where can I go for help in creating or reviewing a resume?

While a resume is not required for all scholarships, you may be asked to upload your resume for some specific scholarship opportunities. If a resume is required, the scholarship committee will be able to view your submitted resume. You can use this resume to further capture your accomplishments, academic achievements, and community involvement. The GVSU Career Center provides an extensive resume guide with examples and offers personalized resume reviews.

Can I update my application once it's been submitted?

In many cases, yes. To edit an application, click View Application. You will know if you are able to edit your application if the Edit button is enabled to take action. If the button is disabled, the scholarship has reached a point where it can no longer be edited. If the application requires a document, you can replace the document after you submit your application with an updated version if it is before the deadline. We highly recommend thoroughly reviewing your materials and/or answers before submitting your application.

Can I verify my application was submitted?

Yes. You will receive an email once you submit an application for a GVSU scholarship. You can also click the Applications tab to see if your application is Ready to Start, In Progress, or Submitted.

My parent is a GVSU alum. Does that mean I automatically qualify for a scholarship?

To receive the Alumni Heritage Scholarship, you must be a new, incoming first-year student and log into MyScholarships to provide your alumni parent's name and graduation year on the Alumni Heritage Scholarship application. Award amounts are based on the number of applicants and available funding. Deadline to apply is July 1. 

How do I know the difference between internal and external scholarships?

Scholarship types are indicated by symbols to the left of the scholarship name. For internal scholarships (these are GVSU specific opportunities) you will see a building, and for external scholarships you will see a globe. If you have questions, please contact the Scholarships Team at 616.331.3234 or at [email protected]. 

Can I view a list of available Grand Valley Scholarships without logging into the scholarship database?

Yes, a link is available in the Scholarship Information section above to view a comprehensive list of all GVSU scholarships.

I have questions on an external scholarship, who do I contact?

Grand Valley is providing access to these scholarships as an additional resource to assist in your search for scholarships, but these are external scholarships. If you have questions on scholarship criteria, deadline, etc. please contact the scholarship provider. If you have questions or trouble navigating the system, please contact the Office of Financial Aid & Scholarships at [email protected].

What are external scholarship opportunities?

Scholarships provided by agencies, foundations, or other providers not affiliated with Grand Valley State University.

How do I apply to an external scholarship?

Once you match to a scholarship, you will be able to click on each one and be re-directed to that scholarship’s website where you can complete the application for that opportunity. If there is no link provided or the link is having issues, type the scholarship name and provider into a search engine and navigate to their application.

When will I find out if I received a scholarship?

Be patient. Sometimes it will be months before you know you have been chosen to receive a scholarship. GVSU myScholarships awards are typically made in late spring/early summer prior to the upcoming academic year. If you are awarded a Grand Valley scholarship, you will be contacted via email. You can check the status of your applications anytime in the myScholarships system under the 'Awards' tab.

For external scholarship opportunities, it depends on the agency or company offering the scholarship. For information on the timeline for external scholarships, please view the website where you applied or reach out to that agency or company. 

Should I write a thank you letter?

Always and some scholarships require it! Scholarship donors are generous individuals who give to support the educational endeavors of students like you. Receiving a well written thank you from a student is always special and lets the donor know that their scholarship was greatly appreciated.  We encourage you to take the time to express your gratitude and remind them why students appreciate their gifts! If you are awarded a Grand Valley scholarship that requires a thank you letter you will be able to complete the letter after you accept your award. This can be done under the Action Required section of your dashboard in myScholarships. For scholarships that require a thank you, you must first complete the letter and have it approved before the scholarship will pay on your account. Thank you letters are shared directly with the scholarship donors.

I'm interested in pursuing a fellowship. Where can I find more information?

The Frederik Meijer Office of Fellowships supports student scholars in pursuit of nationally competitive award opportunities through advising services and programming, they are located in 230 Mary Idema Pew Library.

 

Why don't I have a general application to complete?

We have updated the myScholarships database! Due to this update, we no longer have a general application for you to complete. You will now answer your profile questions and those, along with information from your FAFSA and from Grand Valley, will help match you to scholarship opportunities.

I am having technical difficulties with myScholarships. Who do I reach out to?

For any technical difficulties, we recommend you reach out to the myScholarships Support Team by using the Feedback feature in your myScholarships profile. Please be as specific as possible in your request as this makes it easier for the support team to resolve the issue. This team is not Grand Valley staff members, so make sure your issue is technical.

Why am I a partial match for a scholarship?

If you are a partial match for a scholarship, you will see this by clicking ‘Scholarships’ then clicking ‘Partial Matches’. If you have a scholarship under this tab, additional questions need to be answered to determine if you are eligible. In some cases, the information from the FAFSA or from Banner will automatically answer questions for you. If you have not yet filed your FAFSA for the upcoming academic year, we encourage you to complete it as soon as possible. Once all questions for a scholarship are answered the scholarship will move to the ‘Matches’ or ‘Non-Matches’ tab.

*Note- the more questions you answer, the more scholarships you could be matched to now and in the future.

Why am I a non-match for a scholarship?

If you are a non-match for a scholarship, this means that you did not match any of the requirements of the scholarship criteria. If you have questions about a specific requirement, please email [email protected] .

Faculty or Staff member looking for more information?

If you are a faculty or staff scholarship reviewer who is looking for more information on myScholarships, please click here to access the reviewer website with further information on the review process. Please log in with your Grand Valley credentials to access the page.
