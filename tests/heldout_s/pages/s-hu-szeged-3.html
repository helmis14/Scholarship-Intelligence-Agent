Hungarian DIASPORA

Hungarian DIASPORA

2021. November 11.
2 perc

The Hungarian Diaspora Scholarship is available for bachelor’s, master’s, one-tier master’s, doctoral, non-degree programs (preparatory and specialisation courses) and postgraduate specialist training courses.

The Hungarian Diaspora Scholarship offers a wide range of study opportunities in Hungary, including bachelor’s, master’s, one-tier master’s, doctoral, postgraduate specialist training, and non-degree programmes, such as preparatory and specialisation courses.

The scholarship programme aims to strengthen the connection between Hungary and Hungarian diaspora communities worldwide by providing young people of Hungarian heritage with the opportunity to pursue higher education in Hungary. It supports those who wish to rediscover and strengthen their connection to their Hungarian heritage, while gaining professional knowledge and skills that can contribute to the development and prosperity of their diaspora communities.

Who can apply?

The programme is open to applicants from all countries of the world, with the exception of EU Member States, the Republic of Serbia, and the Zakarpattia Oblast of Ukraine.

The Hungarian Diaspora Scholarship is overseen by the Minister of Foreign Affairs and Trade and managed by the Tempus Public Foundation.

For detailed information about eligibility, application requirements and the application process, please visit the official Hungarian Diaspora Scholarship website

Summer Courses

Make your summer in Hungary an experience to remember!

Do you have Hungarian heritage and want to reconnect with your roots? Would you like to explore Hungarian language and culture, discover music and dance, or deepen your knowledge in a particular field?

The Hungarian Diaspora Scholarship Summer Courses offer an exciting opportunity to spend your summer in Hungary while learning, making new connections and experiencing Hungarian culture firsthand.

Whether you are interested in language, music, dance, science or cultural heritage, these courses provide a unique and enriching summer experience - and may even be the first step towards pursuing your future studies in Hungary.

Discover the summer courses and start planning your Hungarian adventure!
