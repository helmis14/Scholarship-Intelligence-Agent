Book, Print and Media Awards

ALA and its member units honor books, print, and other forms of media through a variety of awards. These awards recognize the publications, as well as the authors, illustrators, and publishers who create them.

 

See all Awards

Historic Book, Print & Media Awards

ALA has a long history of recognizing exemplary media works, including books, published research, and other media. Although these Historic Awards are no longer offered, they have helped establish ALA as a leader in emphasizing the crucial role books and media play in the advancement of lifelong literacy.
