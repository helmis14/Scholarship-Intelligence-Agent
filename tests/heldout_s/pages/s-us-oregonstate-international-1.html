Graduate Scholarships For International Students

Graduate programs: Graduate Pathway (GPW) or Master's International Direct (MID)

Continued Success Scholarship
  • Group: GPW, MID
  • Amount: $3,000-$5,000
  • Duration: Up to 2 years (renewable) 
  • Eligibility/More Info: 

    • Successful completion of Graduate Pathway or Master's International Direct and matriculation to OSU

    • Achieve a minimum GPA 3.5 in all coursework taken during Graduate Pathway or Master’s International Direct to qualify for $3,000 per year

    • Achieve a minimum GPA 3.75 in all coursework taken during Graduate Pathway or Master’s International Direct to qualify for $5,000 per year

    • Maintain consecutive enrollment at OSU as a full-time degree seeking student in an approved graduate program

    • Renewable as long as the student remains in good academic standing, maintaining a GPA 3.5+

    • Cannot be combined with graduate tuition remission or OSU assistantships 

Regional Scholarship
  • Group: GPW, MID
  • Amount: $1,000-$7,500
  • Duration: One time during the first year/term 
  • Eligibility/More Info: 

    • Limited availability; first come, first served

    • Applicable to Graduate Pathway program enrollments

    • Personal statement or scholarship interview may be required for some reason regions

    • Contact your into regional staff or OSU representative for more information
