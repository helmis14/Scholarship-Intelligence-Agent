Major Grants

  • September 25, 2026

    RIT chemical engineering students reshaping particles to help build the materials of the future

    Chemical engineering student Natalie Bedolla-Mortensen is playing a role in reengineering tiny particles that could one day become cell tissues of 3D-printed organs. Her research is part of Rochester Institute of Technology’s work in the growing field of soft matter development.

  • August 27, 2026

    RIT researchers study teamwork skills in engineering education

    The new research hub will improve engineering education to close skills gaps and address how artificial intelligence is impacting the workplace.

  • May 5, 2026

    RIT partners with Army to strengthen nation’s future workforce

    The U.S. Department of the Army Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics has awarded RIT a premier cooperative agreement to establish and lead the Army STEM Education Consortium.
