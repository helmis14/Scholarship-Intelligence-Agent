The Japan Foundation Awards

Every year since 1973, the Japan Foundation has presented the Japan Foundation Awards to individuals and organizations that have made significant contributions to promoting international mutual understanding and friendship between Japan and other countries through academic, artistic and other cultural pursuits. Fiscal year 2026 marks the 53rd anniversary of the Japan Foundation Awards.

The Japan Foundation Awards (2026)

  • SANAA Sejima and Nishizawa and Associates [Japan]
