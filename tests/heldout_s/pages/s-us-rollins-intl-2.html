Visit Rollins Scholarship

Earn a $4,000 scholarship by visiting America’s most beautiful campus

As you’re searching for your right-fit college, there’s simply no substitute for experiencing a campus in person. A visit to our lakeside campus lets you sit in on classes, sample campus dining, and see firsthand what it’s like to live and learn at Rollins. But we know visiting requires time and resources. That’s why we’ve created the Visit Rollins Scholarship, a new $1,000 annual scholarship—totaling $4,000 over four years—for rising seniors who complete an official campus visit between June 1, 2026, and December 18, 2026, and apply for admission as a first-year student by November 15, 2026.

Visit Rollins Scholarship Eligibility

To be eligible for the Visit Rollins Scholarship, a student must:

  • Apply by Nov. 15

    Apply for admission for fall 2027 as a first-year student by November 15, 2026

  • Visit by Dec. 18

    Take a formal campus visit—a daily campus tour or an in-person Discover Rollins event—between June 1, 2026, and December 18, 2026

  • Enroll for Fall 2027

    Enroll as a first-time student for the fall 2027 entering class

Additional Details

  • The Visit Rollins Scholarship is renewable for four years, totaling $4,000 over four years
  • Students who attend Rollins through a scholarship or grant program that meets or exceeds tuition and housing may not qualify for the Visit Rollins Scholarship. Such programs may include, but are not limited to, the Alfond Scholars, Rosen Scholars, Boys & Girls Club Scholars, full athletic scholarships, tuition remission benefits, tuition exchange, and veteran administration benefits/military programs.
  • All Visit Rollins Scholarships are guaranteed for four years or completion of a bachelor’s degree—whichever comes first.
  • The Visit Rollins Scholarship is not duplicated if a student has visited multiple times.

Book Your Campus Visit Today

  • Daily Campus Tours

    Experience our personalized learning environment firsthand, get a feel for our tight-knit community, sample campus cuisine, and so much more.

  • Discover Rollins Open House Events

    Explore everything from academics and student life to admission and financial aid during our half-day open-house experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is the Visit Rollins Scholarship worth?

The scholarship is $1,000 per year, renewable for up to four years, for a total of $4,000.

Do I need to apply for the grant separately?

No. Take an official visit, apply by November 15, 2026, and enroll for fall 2027. The scholarship is automatically added to your award.

Who qualifies for the Visit Rollins Scholarship?

The scholarship is available to rising seniors who apply for fall 2027 admission as first-year students by November 15, 2026, complete an eligible campus visit between June 1, 2026, and December 18, 2026, and enroll at Rollins as first-time, first-year students.

Which campus visits qualify?

A daily campus tour or an in-person Discover Rollins event, completed between June 1 and December 18, 2026. Register through Rollins so your visit is on record.

If I visit more than once, can I earn the scholarship more than once?

No. One qualifying visit is all it takes, and the scholarship isn't duplicated for additional visits.
