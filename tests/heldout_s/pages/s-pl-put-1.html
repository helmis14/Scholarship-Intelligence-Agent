STER - Stypendia / Scholarship

Task 2. Scholarships for the best PhD students of PUT Doctoral School implementing doctoral projects in international cooperation

 

The task will be to select, in a competition, the best foreign and Polish PhD students of the PUT Doctoral School, conducting doctoral projects in international cooperation and aiming at obtaining a joint degree or double degree. The beneficiaries of the competition will be paid doctoral scholarships for a period of 6 months, after the approval of the individual research plan. The scholarship obtained within the Project will replace a standard doctoral scholarship.

PhD students will be able to take part in subsequent competitions regardless of having received a scholarship before.

The main objective of the activities will be the promotion of international cooperation in the implementation of doctoral projects and the acquisition of a greater number of supervisors from abroad. They are expected to contribute to stronger cooperation between scientists from PUT and foreign academic and research institutions.
