COIMBRA scholarship 2024/25: 2 candidates selected

  •  05, 31, 2024
  • Category Borse di Studio Eng, EnglishNews, News
  • Posted By Ferrari

 

 

Based on the results of the evaluation of the committee, two candidates have been selected and awarded with the COIMBRA scholarship for the year 2024/2025. The committee evaluated the 21 applications received, 10 from Sub-Saharan African countries, 11 from Latin American countries, noting the excellent level of the proposed research projects.

For the Sub-Saharan countries Chimwemwe Msosa has been selected to join the department of Civil Engineering and Architecture

For the Latin American countries Cínthia Oli

veira Demaria has been selected to join the department of Brain and Behavioural Science

We congratulate the two researchers who will join our professors in Università di Pavia soon and wish them fruitful collaboration and a joyous time at our university.
