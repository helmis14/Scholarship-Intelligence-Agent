Research fellowships

Research Fellowships are awarded to carry out research activities. They allow holders to collaborate in the implementation of a research programme or a step of it.

As required by University Regulation on research fellowships, the selection procedures may include PhD’s graduates or Master’s degree graduates and an appropriate professional scientific curriculum. The duration of the research fellowship may vary from a minimum of one to a maximum of three years.

Calls for research fellowships are published on the following Link identifier #identifier__150516-1page (Italian version).

For further information please consult Link identifier #identifier__8446-2Area Personale and theLink identifier #identifier__35599-3 Departments issuing the research fellowship.
