TYPE A

Chinese Government Scholarship-Bilateral Program

Who can apply?

Applicants for undergraduate, postgraduate and scholar programs.


Where to apply?

Dispatching authorities in your home country, such as:

- Educational and Cultural Office of Chinese Embassy/Consulate

- Department of Education, etc.


Tips:

No application fee;

Do NOT apply to HUST or the China Scholarship Council (CSC);

Please refer to the notice released by the dispatching authorities for application details.

Pre-admission documents are preferred.


How to apply for the Pre-admission Letter:

Step 1. Log in to the HUST Online Application System at http://admission.hust.edu.cn

Step 2. Click Only for Pre-admission Letter

A prospective supervisor’s acceptance letter may also be submitted to the authorities as the pre-admission document.


Agency No.

Please consult the dispatching authorities.


TYPE B

Chinese Government Scholarship - Chinese University Program

Who can apply?

Applicants for postgraduate programs ONLY.

Applicants should have an excellent academic record and demonstrate strong academic potential.


Chinese Government Scholarship - Silk Road Program

Who can apply?

Applicants from the 10 designated countries:

Russia, Malaysia, Mongolia, Laos, Indonesia, Cambodia, Thailand, Bangladesh, Vietnam, Pakistan.

in 5 specialties: 

Bachelor's Programs (Chinese-medium instruction ONLY):

- Architecture

- Software Engineering

Applicants should have passed HSK 4 (score ≥ 180);

Master's & Doctoral Programs (English-medium instruction):

- Computer Science and Technology

- Civil Engineering

- Chemistry.


Where to apply?

International Admission and Cooperation Office,

School of International Education,

Huazhong University of Science and Technology


Tips:

Application fee: 800 CNY (Non-refundable).

The results of academic review, scholarship nomination, and admission will be communicated via email.


How to apply:

Step 1. Log in to the HUST Online Application System at http://admission.hust.edu.cn

Step 2. Click Apply for Admission
