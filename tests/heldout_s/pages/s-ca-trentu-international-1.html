Review key information on tuition, taxes, making a deposit to Trent, earning money while you study, and scholarships for international students.

Estimate Your Costs & Potential Awards

Planning for university is an important step, and we're here to help. Use this calculator to get a personalized estimate of your tuition and ancillary fees based on your program of interest, residency status, and academic average. You may also see an estimate of entrance scholarships for which you could be eligible.

Please note: This calculator provides an estimate only and is intended for planning purposes. Tuition, ancillary fees, scholarship values, eligibility requirements, and university policies are subject to change without notice. Scholarship estimates are based solely on the information you provide and do not constitute an offer of admission or financial aid. Your official fees and scholarship eligibility will be determined upon admission and registration.
