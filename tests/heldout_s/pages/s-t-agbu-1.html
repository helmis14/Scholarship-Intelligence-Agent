    Creative Armenia - AGBU Fellowships

    Defining Armenia’s cultural future

    The Creative Armenia-AGBU Fellowships support visionary and boundary-pushing talents across all artistic fields who demonstrate both creative excellence and a bold commitment to their craft at catalytic moments in their careers. Through this year-long program, fellows receive a comprehensive package of funding, mentorship, and promotion, individually tailored to best serve each fellow’s goals.

    From visual arts to literature, from film to music, the 2026 Fellowships offer funding, mentorships, and promotion to 5 exceptional artists as they join the already 38 Fellows driving a powerful movement for Armenia's creative future.

    As a Fellow, you get:

    Funding

    $5,000 to bring your creative project to life

    Mentorship

    Connections to leading professionals in your field

    Promotion

    Marketing support to reach new audiences

    How did the Fellowships start?

    Two partner organizations came together to discover and champion the most brilliant emerging minds of our culture.

     

    Read

    The Fellowships represent the first collaboration of AGBU and Creative Armenia, which entered into a strategic partnership in May 2018. Creative Armenia, a global arts foundation for the Armenian people, pursues a mandate to discover, develop, promote, and produce innovative talent. To learn more about our partner go to www.creativearmenia.org.
