International Scholarships and Aid

Learn about the scholarships and loans available to international students, designed to help make your education here more affordable.

Montclair State University is dedicated to helping international students from every corner of the world access an affordable, high-quality education. While we’re not able to offer need-based financial aid if you are studying on an international visa, we do provide opportunities for merit- and talent-based scholarships. You’ll also find information about loans for international students below. Count on our support every step of the way as you plan for your studies and future with us.

Financial Assistance for International Students

Merit-based Scholarships

Montclair State University offers merit-based scholarships to freshman international students.

Qualified students can receive our Presidential Scholarship. Students who qualify will be automatically awarded this prestigious scholarship. 

This scholarship can be renewed for up to four years and is offered to students who meet certain GPA criteria. 

Students will be required to maintain a minimum GPA to continue to receive this award. More information is provided upon acceptance.

Talent-based Performing Arts Awards

Students who are accepted into select performing arts programs may be considered for a talent-based scholarship, awarded by the faculty in recognition of an exceptional audition. These scholarships are open to in-state, out-of-state and international students, and are applied directly to your educational costs. Please note that they can’t be exchanged for cash, and recipients must remain enrolled in the performing arts program to which they were admitted. 

Your award can support up to eight consecutive full-time fall and spring semesters toward your first undergraduate degree. If you complete your degree in fewer than eight semesters, the remaining funds simply won’t be used. The scholarship amount may be adjusted if your residency or enrollment status changes, and it will be offered in addition to any other merit-based scholarships you receive.

ScholarshipUniverse

As part of our commitment to providing you with a high-quality and affordable education, we are pleased to offer access to ScholarshipUniverse. This online tool collects many external scholarships in one convenient place, helping you find as much financial support as possible. After you create your profile, the system will match you with the scholarships that fit you best. 

Please note that this tool is available for deposited students only (NetID will be required for access). 

Loans for International Students

Many lenders specialize in lending to international students and you can find them listed on ELMSelect.com, as well as on other lending sites. 

We encourage you to review all the lenders that may be available to you as more lenders are adding products for international students. This policy applies to loan certification requests for international students submitted to financial aid. 

Montclair State University receives certification requests for students who are entering school with, but not limited to, F or J visas. 

To certify these loans, Montclair must ensure that the student is meeting the following minimum registration requirements as set out by U.S. federal regulations: 

  • Graduate Degrees other than the Artist’s Diploma: enrollment in a minimum of 9 credits
  • Artist’s Diploma: enrollment in a minimum of 6 credits
  • Undergraduate Degree: enrollment in a minimum of 12 credits 

In addition, Montclair will only certify the private educational loan if the student is registered and an accurate Cost of Attendance/Budget is calculated. 

  • Montclair will certify loans based on the academic year which begins with fall.
  • Cost of Attendance is based upon semester enrollment.
  • A student seeking a loan for a full year in January will have a loan certified only for the terms within the academic year in which the student will be enrolled (spring or spring/summer). A new loan must be submitted for the next fall/spring.
  • Prior to certification, students need to complete a Precertification Form for review. Students must be logged into their @montclair.edu email account to respond.
  • The student who borrows from an exclusively international lender will have an opportunity to respond with a Cost of Attendance Re-evaluation Request (with supporting documentation) before the loan is sent to the lender for final processing. A link to the Cost of Attendance Re-evaluation Request Form is provided on the Precertification Form.
     
  • Prior to loan disbursement, registration will be monitored for all students with a private educational loan.
    • If a student withdraws from courses prior to the end of the Add/Drop period or they defer their enrollment to a later date, the private educational loan will be cancelled and/or rejected via ELM.
    • Records of these transactions will be maintained within Financial Aid for a period of three years.

Assistantships for Graduate Students

A limited number of assistantships are available for master’s and PhD. students. Students interested in applying for an assistantship for the fall must complete their application prior to March 1. Assistantships for spring are limited.

Please note that assistantships are highly competitive. Students should be prepared to finance their own education through personal/family/sponsor funds or loans obtained in their home country.

Learn more about assistantships from The Graduate Center on Inside Montclair
