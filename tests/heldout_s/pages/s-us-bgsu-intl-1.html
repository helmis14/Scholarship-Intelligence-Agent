One of the most common questions families ask us is "How can we make college more affordable?"

BGSU is committed to providing you with a high-quality education while helping you reduce student debt. 8 out of 10 BG Campus undergraduate students receive financial assistance to help lower the cost of their education. 

Learn how a BGSU education may cost less than you think. Our financial aid team is here to help you understand your options and make a plan that works for you.

Scholarships

The surest way to get a scholarship offer is to simply apply to BGSU! Once you’re admitted, BGSU will begin making scholarship offers based on your credentials. Scholarships are free money and can make a big difference in reducing your tuition bill. And we make it easy to apply, too.
