Research & Scholarship

At Goucher, learning extends beyond the classroom as students and faculty pursue questions that matter. Through hands-on research, creative scholarship, and collaborative projects, they explore new ideas, contribute to their fields, and make a difference in their communities and beyond.

Faculty Scholarship & Achievement

See how Goucher faculty are investigating new ideas, sharing their expertise, and making an impact through research, scholarship, and creative work.

Faculty Scholarship & Achievement

Goucher Symposium

Every spring, the Goucher Symposium celebrates student learning and scholarship. Explore the research, creativity, and projects Goucher students bring to life through in-person and livestreamed presentations and performances.

Goucher Symposium

Summer Science research

Goucher’s summer research program brings students and faculty together for six to eight weeks of hands-on scientific research, discovery, and collaboration in a range of disciplines.

Summer Science Research Program

Kratz Summer Writing Fellowship

Goucher students can apply for summer writing fellowships to support travel or research, a writing-related internship, or attendance at a conference or workshop.

Kratz Summer Writing Fellowship

TORCH

The TORCH project brings together Goucher faculty and outside partners to develop teaching tools that incorporate climate science into life science and health science courses.

The TORCH Project

Access to PUIs

The ACCESS to PUIs program provides aspiring faculty with mentorship, teaching observation and feedback, and panel discussions on life at a primarily undergraduate institution.

Access to PUIs

Center for the Advancement of Scholarship & Teaching (CAST)

CAST supports Goucher faculty through programs, consultations, and resources related to teaching, scholarship, and professional development.
