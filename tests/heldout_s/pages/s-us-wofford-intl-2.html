The Rosalind Sallenger Richardson Fine Arts Endowed Scholarship Fund

The Rosalind Sallenger Richardson Endowed Scholarship Fund was established in 2016 by the family of Mr. and Mrs. Ashley Richardson Allen. The endowment fund is in honor of Ashley Allen’s mother, Rosalind Sallenger Richardson, for her interest in the performing and visual arts. What our students see in the world is influenced dramatically by what we show them. This scholarship honors the artistic spirit of Rosalind Sallenger Richardson. Through her creative hands, artistic eye, and graceful, gentle heart, she lovingly showed her children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren the beauty surrounding them. In doing so she unveiled the wonder of art within our souls and forever enriched their lives.
