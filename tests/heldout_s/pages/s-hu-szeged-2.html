Stipendium Hungaricum

Stipendium Hungaricum

2016. March 17.
2 perc

Stipendium Hungaricum is a scholarship program coordinated and financed by the Government of Hungary.

The Aim of the Program

The Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship Program was launched in 2013 by the Hungarian Government. The core mission of the program is to increase the number of foreign students in Hungary and to encourage Hungarian higher education institutions to attract top foreign students.


Scholarship Covers

Although the scholarship covers tuition fee, health insurance, accommodation support and a monthly stipend, students will need other financial source to cover all the expenses while studying at SZTE. Click here and read Provisions Covered section of the official website of Tempus Public Foundation. For further information about the cost of living in Szeged, visit this page.

 

Language of the Program

Most of the international programs are offered in English, but many of them are available in French, German, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Turkish, or in Hungarian.

 

Preparatory Program

The Hungarian Studies Center of the University offers an intensive two-semester preparatory program in Hungarian as a foreign language for international students who wish to study at a Hungarian university. For more information, click here.


Application Process

Find the details of the application process of bachelor’s, master’s, one-tier master’s, doctoral, non-degree programs, and postgraduate specialist training courses here. When applying, please ensure that you submit your application online via this platform and indicate the University of Szeged as your first choice.
