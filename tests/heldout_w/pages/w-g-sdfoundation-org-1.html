Community Scholarship Program

Providing financial support to help San Diego students pursue and complete higher education.

Give Close to Home Common Scholarship Application

Helping Students Pursue Their Dreams

When every child has the chance to learn, San Diego grows with them — but the cost of college keeps too many local students from finishing what they started.

Since 1997, the Community Scholarship Program has awarded $65.2 million to more than 16,200 students, making it the largest non-university scholarship provider in San Diego County.

By offering financial assistance, the program helps students dedicate more time to their studies, stay enrolled and complete their degrees.

These scholarships exist because donors like you make them possible. Individuals, families and fundholders across San Diego come together, year after year, to support students who share their values, interests or life experiences.

“I’m bettering my education so that I can come back and help my community more.”

–Michelle Caro, Scholarship Recipient

Give Close to Home

San Diego is the home we all share. And when our neighbors need us, we show up. A gift to San Diego Foundation is how we take care of each other – one family, one neighborhood, one gift at a time.

Give Close to HomeGive From Your Fund

The Community Scholarship Program helps to:

Provide Need-Based Support: Reduce the cost of higher education so students can focus on academic success rather than financial stress.

Support Degree Attainment: Help students persist through college by easing financial pressures that often interrupt or delay graduation.

Build Resilient Communities: Support students who contribute their skills and education to San Diego’s economy and communities.
