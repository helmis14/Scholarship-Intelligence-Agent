Call for Applications – Algerian Visiting Research Fellowship 2026-2027

الاثنين 13 جويلية 2026 15:00 الأربعاء 2 سبتمبر 2026 14:24

As part of the Emir Abdelkader Algerian Program, the University of Algiers 2 – Abou El Kacem Saadallah informs its faculty members and researchers that applications are now open for the Algerian Visiting Research Fellowship for the 2026-2027 academic year, organized by the Oxford Centre for Islamic Studies.

This fellowship offers the selected researcher the opportunity to join the Centre’s multidisciplinary academic community for a period of up to one full academic year to conduct an independent research project in one of the fields related to the study of Islam and the Muslim world, with a particular focus on the following disciplines:

  • Anthropology
  • Economics
  • Geography
  • History
  • International Relations
  • Law
  • Literature
  • Philosophy
  • Political Science
  • Religious Studies
  • Sociology
  • Science and Society

Eligibility Requirements

  • The applicant must be an Algerian academic who normally resides in Algeria.
  • The applicant must have made distinguished scholarly contributions in their field of specialization.
  • The applicant must hold a PhD (postdoctoral level).

How to Apply

Applications must be submitted through the Visiting Fellowship page of the Oxford Centre for Islamic Studies.

Application Deadline: 30 July 2026

For more information: Please visit the official website of the Oxford Centre for Islamic Studies.

http://www.oxcis.ac.uk
