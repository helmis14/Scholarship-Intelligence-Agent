Scholarship Programs offered by the Romanian Government for Foreign Citizens
  • Home
  • news
  • Scholarship Programs offered by the Romanian Government for Foreign Citizens
news
Comments Off on Scholarship Programs offered by the Romanian Government for Foreign Citizens
Posted By

Jonee Joseph

The Ministry is pleased to announce scholarship programs made available through the Embassy of Romania for the 2026–2027 academic year.

These programs provide support for international students pursuing studies in Romania at the Bachelor’s, Master’s, and Doctoral levels. Scholarships are offered across a wide range of academic disciplines; however, programs in medicine, dental medicine, and pharmacy are not included in this initiative.

Please note that the programs will be instructed in the Romanian Language, and as such, successful applicants are required to undergo 1 year of language learning. It is important to highlight that the scholarship covers registration fees, tuition fees, a monthly stipend, and accommodation expenses. Please note that round‑trip airfare to and from Romania is the responsibility of the applicant.

Applications must be submitted online via the official platform https://scholarships.studyinromania.gov.ro/.

Applicants will be required to upload the following documents:

  • Copy of diplomas
  • Copy of academic transcripts
  • Copy of birth certificate
  • Copy of the first three pages of a valid passport
  • Curriculum vitae (CV)

Candidates applying for PhD studies must also submit a Letter of Intent outlining:

  • List of scientific works
  • Specialized publications
  • Detailed description of the proposed research project
  • The written agreement of a supervising tutor, who must be a member of the chosen doctoral school.

All application documents must be apostilled at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs prior to online submission.

Following the completion of the online application, candidates are also required to submit physical copies of all documents to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

The deadline for submitting online applications is 31 March 2026.
