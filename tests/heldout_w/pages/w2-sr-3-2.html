Yunqi PhD Scholarship in Science and Technology 

The scholarship is targeted at students who are interested in pursuing a full-time PhD at NTU in the areas of Science and Technology. 

  • All nationalities are eligible for the scholarship. 
  • Applicants must meet the general admission requirements for the PhD programme. Refer to the respective programme for any additional requirements.  

Applicants can apply for the scholarship directly through the NTU application portal by indicating their interest in the “Scholarship” section of the application. Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an interview and be evaluated by the University as part of the selection process. 

Supporting documents to be uploaded in the online application: 
  1. Identification documents 
  2. Education qualifications (in both original language and official translation in English) 
  3. Research proposal  
  4. Two referee reports  
  5. Other supporting documents:
    - Resume
    - Research publications
    - Test scores (i.e. IELTS/TOEFL/GRE/GMATE/GATE), where applicable 

 

The details of the scholarship are as follows: 

  1. The scholarship is tenable for one year in the first instance and thereafter may, subject to the Scholar’s satisfactory progress, be renewed each semester at the University’s discretion. 

  2. The maximum period of the Scholarship is four years for PhD candidates. 

  3. The scholar may not find any form of employment, or hold any other scholarship, award, fellowship or allowance during the period of the scholarship without the prior approval of the university.
