About Scholarship

The Bakala Foundation Scholarship Program enables recipients to achieve their educational dreams by helping to fund bachelor’s or master’s degrees at foreign universities.
In the 16 years of the program’s existence, we have supported 233 students with their studies at 69 universities in 12 countries. Join them!

Find the best university for your academic and personal growth. The choice of international university and field of study is yours - we are here to support you.

Who can apply?

Applicants must be:
  • Under 34 years of age as of the application deadline;
  • In the final year of high school or currently enrolled at a university;
  • Citizens or permanent residents of the Czech Republic;
  • Holders of an excellent academic record;
  • Unable to fully fund foreign university tuition and other expenses due to their financial situation;
  • Planning to pursue, or currently pursuing, a bachelor's or master's degree at a foreign university;
  • Seeking a rigorous academic experience to foster intellectual growth, rather than primarily a cultural exchange or language improvement;
  • Motivated to drive positive change through their studies and future career.
Have you ticked all the boxes? If so, then our program is for you!

Our scholars' stories

During my undergraduate studies in the Czech Republic, I became interested in military topics, especially ongoing armed conflicts, and dreamed of a career in the defense sector. Although I studied political science, I tried to choose subjects that would be at least somewhat close to that, and I wrote my bachelor’s thesis on the dynamics of Czech military modernization.
Eliška Filová Ellison
