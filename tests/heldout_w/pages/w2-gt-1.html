      Guatemala launches national scholarship call to pursue graduate studies at CATIE

      • Posted by Karla Madrigal P.
      • Categories News, Noticias Guatemala, Noticias Posgrado
      • Date 25 May, 2026
      • The first 2026 call of the Scholarships for Our Future program will remain open until May 31 and will allow Guatemalan professionals to access master’s and doctoral studies at CATIE, Costa Rica.

      CATIE (Tropical Agricultural Research and Higher Education Center) invites Guatemalan professionals interested in continuing their academic training to apply for the first annual call of the Scholarships for Our Future program, an initiative promoted by the Government of Guatemala to expand opportunities for access to higher education and specialized training.

      This program emerged from the National Scholarship Fund, created on March 11, 2025, as one of the national priorities of President Bernardo Arévalo’s government, with the objective of supporting Guatemalan students in starting, continuing or completing higher and technical education studies, both in Guatemala and abroad.

      In this context, CATIE positions itself as an international academic alternative for those seeking specialization in areas related to sustainability, natural resources, territorial development, agricultural production and climate change.

      The call is aimed at Guatemalan professionals who have already completed their university studies and wish to pursue a master’s degree or doctorate. Selected applicants may apply for full or partial scholarships to undertake graduate studies at CATIE, located in Turrialba, Costa Rica.

      The scholarships cover expenses associated with the academic program, including tuition, study fees, research and other costs directly related to professional training. They also include a stipend for complementary expenses such as accommodation, meals, books, materials and transportation. Full scholarships cover all program costs, while partial scholarships may finance between 25% and 75% of study-related expenses.

      Requirements to apply include being Guatemalan and residing in the country, submitting academic records, personal essays, references and demonstrating social or community commitment, in addition to information related to the applicant’s socioeconomic situation.

      Within its academic offerings, CATIE provides on-site, virtual and hybrid programs aimed at training professionals capable of responding to the environmental and productive challenges of Latin America and the Caribbean.

      The on-site master’s programs, which will begin in March 2027, are Agroforestry and Sustainable Agriculture, Management and Conservation of Tropical Forests and Biodiversity, Sustainable Tourism in a hybrid format, and Economics, Development and Climate Change.

      Likewise, in September 2026, a new cohort of virtual master’s programs will begin in Sustainable Agribusiness and Market Management, Watershed Management and Governance, and Sustainable and Regenerative Coffee Production. A program in Sustainable Livestock Production will also open soon. The institution also offers international doctoral programs.

      CATIE has consolidated agreements with organizations and institutions throughout Latin America that facilitate funding opportunities for graduate studies, expanding access to quality higher education for professionals in the region.

      The call will remain open until May 31, 2026, and interested applicants may complete their application through the official Scholarships for Our Future program website.
