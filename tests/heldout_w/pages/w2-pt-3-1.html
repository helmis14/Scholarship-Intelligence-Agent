    Graduate Excellence Scholarship

      Graduate Academic Excellence Scholarship

      This scholarship is designed to support exceptional university students looking to pursue graduate studies at Modul University. This competitive, merit-based scholarship aims to recognize outstanding academic achievements and potential across various fields. 

      If you have any questions at all about finances, tuition fees or payment options, please contact our Student Recruitment Team

      Eligibility Requirements

      Amount:  Depending on your grade point average (GPA) 
      • GPA>90%: 15% of tuition
      • GPA>85%: 10% of tuition
      • GPA>80%: 5% of tuition

      GMAT Score

      • > ‎675: 15% tuition reduction
      • 625 - 665: 10% tuition reduction
      • 535 - 615: 5% tuition reduction


       

      Our Graduate Academic Excellence Scholarship specifically supports students enrolling in the following graduate programs at Modul University:
      • MSc in Management
      • MSc in International Tourism Management
      • MSc in Sustainable Development, Management, and Policy
      • MSc in Data Science for Sustainability
      • Master of Business Administration

      Limited scholarships are available, so apply soon!

      Successful applicants can receive a tuition fee reduction of up to 25% for the MSc programs and up to 50% for the MBA program, based on their academic qualifications and achievements. Take advantage of our graduate scholarships—apply now before they’re gone

      IMPORTANT INFORMATION

      • The scholarship award covers tuition fees only

      • To qualify for this scholarship applicants must have a strong academic record

      • This scholarship cannot be combined with other grants or scholarships, except for the Full Tuition Fee Payment Reduction and Academic Year Tuition Payment Reduction

      • Applications are open throughout the year. Limited spaces are available for each intake


      For more information about the Modul University Graduate Academic Excellence Scholarship, please contact recruitment@modul.ac.at
