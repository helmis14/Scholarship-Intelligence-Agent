Walter Williams Scholars Award (Non-Missouri residents; U.S. citizens)

Once you apply and are admitted to the University of Missouri as a journalism major, the School of Journalism provides an automatic $1500 merit-based award — Walter Williams Scholars Award — to non-Missouri residents (U.S. citizens). Award details listed below apply to first-time college freshmen.

This merit-based award may be combined with other automatic MU Awards and is renewable for up to four years (eight semesters) provided students remain in good academic standing in the School of Journalism. (3.0 cumulative GPA or higher)  

No Application is required. For more information, please see the School of Journalism’s Walter Williams Scholarship page.

Priority is given to students who apply before Dec. 1. For more information call 573-882-1908 or email journalism@missouri.edu
