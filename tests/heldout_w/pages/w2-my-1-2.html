SASS Visiting Fellowship Program

The SASS Visiting Fellowship program (funded/self-funded) aims to nurture and grow SASS international research networks by supporting scholars to spend up to three months at the School working on a collaborative project with one or more SASS researchers. The collaboration may be in the form of a joint publication, a grant proposal, or a major international event. Fellows will be expected to deliver a public lecture and a master class for graduate students.

Successful applicants are provided with an office space, and IT access. Additionally, funded visiting fellows will be provided with full up to 3 months accommodation, and a one-way return airfare.

Application requirements:

  • alignment to the strategic goals of Monash University and the research themes of thriving communities, geopolitical security and climate change.
  • alignment to the research strengths of the School of Arts and Social Sciences and the theme of Social Transformation in Southeast Asia.
  • a track record of collaboration with the proposed collaborator, or strong potential to develop an ongoing collaborative program of work.
  • strong potential to strengthen SASS international research networks by building cross-institutional relationships with leading research institutions globally.
  • submission of a strong proposal for a program of work, including evidence of a track record of research on the proposed topic and outputs that can feasibly achieved by the conclusion of the fellowship.

*Note: Applicants are required to contact current/potential collaborators before submitting an application

Kindly visit this page to submit an application.
