National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship (GRFP)

Congratulations to the awardees for the 2026 cycle! Information about the awards can be found at NSF GRFP. Thank you to all the applicants!

The Michigan Tech Graduate School provides dedicated resources, workshops, and one-on-one writing support for students applying to the National Science Foundation’s Graduate Research Fellowship Program (NSF GRFP).

Program Overview

The NSF GRFP recognizes and supports outstanding graduate students in NSF-supported science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) disciplines who are pursuing research-based master's and doctoral degrees.

Fellowship Benefits

  • Duration: Three years of financial support over a five-year fellowship period
  • Stipend and allowance information.

Note: NSF GRFP Fellowships are open to U.S. citizens, nationals, and permanent residents only.

Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for the NSF GRFP, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Be a U.S. citizen, national, or permanent resident.

  • Have never previously applied to the GRFP while enrolled in a graduate degree program.

  • Have never earned a master’s or professional degree in any field.

  • Have completed no more than one academic year in a graduate degree-granting program.

For full program guidelines and field-specific eligibility details, visit the official NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program Website.

Application Deadlines

  • How to Apply: Application information and resources 

  • Deadlines Begin: October 19 (Deadlines vary by discipline/field of study)

  • Reference Letters Due: Check official NSF GRFP site for exact submission cutoffs.

Applicants are strongly encouraged to review the official NSF deadlines for their specific field of study well in advance of the deadline week.
