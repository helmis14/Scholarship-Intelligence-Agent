PhD in Computational Sciences (Quantum Computing)

The Cyprus Institute
The Cyprus Institute (CyI) is a non-profit research and educational institution with a strong scientific and technological orientation, emphasizing international collaborations, cross-disciplinary research, innovation, and post-graduate education. The CyI, through its established research centres, addresses societal challenges that are important at both the regional and international levels. Many of its research activities are being carried out in partnership with leading institutions in the respective thematic areas.

The selected candidate will be working at the Computation-based Science and Technology Research Center (CaSToRC) of The Cyprus Institute. CaSToRC acts as the National HPC Competence Center for Cyprus. It pioneers in the introduction, development and employment of intense computational methods and data to advance scientific and technological disciplines. CaSToRC aspires to cultivate the use of High Performance Computing (HPC), Simulation, Machine Learning and Data Science in Cyprus and the Eastern Mediterranean and to serve the needs for computational and data intensive applications for academia, government and industry. In addition to offering training and user support, it pursues its own research areas, which currently focus on scientific computing and Chemical Dynamics and Kinetic Modelling, Climate Modelling, Computational Engineering, Quantum Computing, Machine Learning/Artificial Intelligence and Nuclear and Particle Physics.

The Graduate School
The Graduate School was established in 2010 and is the educational arm of The Cyprus Institute. It is a fully accredited, degree-granting institution of higher education offering exclusively postgraduate programs. It maintains a small number of students that are fully integrated into the research activities of the institute. This provides the students with the opportunity to acquire hands-on training in the use of the latest methods and techniques, have access to state-of-the-art infrastructure and conduct research with internationally recognized research teams.

The successful applicant will be based at the Graduate School of The Cyprus Institute and at the Computation-based Science and Technology Research Center (CaSToRC). The selected person will be enrolled in the PhD program: Computational Sciences.

Description of the PhD Research Project
The Computation-based Science and Technology Research Center (CaSToRC) at The Cyprus Institute invites applications for a PhD fellowship in quantum computing and its applications.
Applicants should hold a BSc or Master’s degree in quantum computing, mathematics, physics, computer science, or a related field.
Previous research experience — particularly in quantum algorithms, cryptography, quantum information theory or mathematics —is not required but will be considered an advantage.
The doctoral research will be carried out at The Cyprus Institute in Cyprus. The research programme is supported by the European Commission under the ERA Chair “Quantum Computing for Excellence in Science and Technology (QUEST)” project.
Quantum Computing has seen significant progress over the last 20 years in the development of quantum devices which offer a fundamentally new computing architecture compared to classical computers. Motivated by the rapid advances in quantum computing, QUEST aims to establish a Quantum Computing Hub in Cyprus to exploit Quantum Computing applications in science and technology.

Required Qualifications
Successful applicants should have (or be about to earn) a Master’s degree (or equivalent) in Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field. English language proficiency is also required (see details in the How to Apply Section below for details on application requirements).

How to Apply
Applicants should refer to the CyI website under the PhD in Computational Sciences Admissions section for additional information about the application process and apply online by December 1, 2026.
For details on the admission progress see: https://www.cyi.ac.cy/index.php/education/phd-programs/computational-sciences/phd-admissions.html

When applying through the online application system, applicants must select the specific PhD Project Code: GS-COS-QC-26-1 in order to be considered for the position.

Application Deadline
The application deadline is 1 December 2026. Please note that, due to the expiry of the relevant early application and external migration deadlines, this position is currently open only to candidates who do not require a visa to study and reside in Cyprus.

Financial Support
The successful candidate will be employed as Graduate Research Fellow or receive a stipend in accordance with the current regulations at the Graduate School of the CyI and of the LU.

The exact salary / stipend level to will be confirmed upon appointment.

Additional and Contact Information
The position will commence in February 2026 and will be funded until the end of the project in December 2028. An extension beyond this period may be possible until the completion of the PhD studies, subject to satisfactory performance and the availability of funds.
The successful candidate will be admitted to the PhD programme in Computational Sciences at The Cyprus Institute, commencing in the Spring 2027 semester (1 February 2027).
