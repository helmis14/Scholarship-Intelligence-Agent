Scholarship for Students from Selected SAARC Countries

As part of its commitment to internationalization and cultural diversity,  The NorthCap University (NCU) offers a special scholarship for students from selected SAARC countries.

This initiative is designed to attract academically talented students from neighbouring nations and provide them with access to quality education in India.

Students from Nepal, Bhutan, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh are eligible for this scholarship. Under the scheme, selected candidates benefit from domestic fee rates, making education at NCU more accessible and affordable.

Scholarship Criteria

Scholarship Type Eligibility Criteria (Qualifying Examination) Applicable Programs Scholarship Limit
100% Scholarship Above 90% Any Undergraduate, Postgraduate or PhD Program One student per program
50% Scholarship Between 80% to 90% One student per program
25% Scholarship Between 70% and 80% One student per program
The number of scholarships is capped at one student per program per scholarship category. The qualifying examination criteria will be relaxed by 5% for female students.
