What is the FPU Programme?

The grants for the Training of University Academic Staff (FPU) aim to train doctoral researchers by funding predoctoral contracts, enabling recipients to carry out the research needed to submit a doctoral thesis while acquiring the university teaching competences required to join the higher education system.

Launched in the 1990s by the Ministry of Education, the FPU programme is managed from 2026 by the State Research Agency (AEI), under Royal Decree 265/2026 of 1 April. The integration aims to unify the management of doctoral training grants from the Ministry of Science, Innovation and Universities, simplify the procedure and align FPU programme management with the AEI's standard processes.

How it works in 3 steps

The FPU is a collaboration between the PhD student and an R&D centre that employs you. Without a confirmed centre, there is no application to submit.

1
PhD Student

Find a research group

Use the search tool to find an R&D group you want to work with. Talk to them first. Without a confirmed centre, there is no application.

2
PhD Student

Register and complete your section

Register with the Unified Applicants Registry (RUS), complete the electronic application form and sign the documents generated by the system.

3
R&D Centre

The centre submits the application

The legal representative of the centre completes their section, validates and submits the full application to the AEI electronically.

What has changed compared to previous years?

The 2026 FPU call features a new managing body, application procedure and evaluation criteria. These are the three structural changes to understand before starting.

AEI

Now managed by the AEI

For the first time, the FPU call is fully managed by the State Research Agency (RD 265/2026), following its standard procedures for award, justification and monitoring.

Centre submits

The centre submits the application

The PhD student completes their section of the application form, but the final application is signed and submitted by the centre where the thesis will be developed, which acts as the beneficiary entity.

75 + 25

New 75 + 25 criteria

The PhD student's academic track record will be assessed (75 points, with a threshold of 60), alongside the scientific and technical track record of the team where the grant will be developed (25 points).
