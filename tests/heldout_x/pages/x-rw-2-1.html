About this scholarship

The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program partners with the University of Rwanda to award undergraduate scholarships based on academic merit, demonstrated economic disadvantage and leadership potential. Applications are assessed using a standard weighting scale:

Academic Talent30%
Economic Disadvantage45%
Leadership Potential25%

Important information

Equivalence

Applicants with foreign academic qualifications must obtain an equivalence from Rwanda’s Higher Education Council (HEC) and the National Examination and School Inspection Authority (NESA).

Visa on arrival

An entry visa is granted on arrival at Kigali International Airport to citizens of all countries.

International student visa

International students must apply for a study visa within 15 days of arrival, with a police clearance and a valid passport. Scholarship holders from outside Rwanda need this visa to study.

Smartphone

We encourage every student to have a smartphone to support blended learning until they receive the laptop provided by the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at the University of Rwanda.
