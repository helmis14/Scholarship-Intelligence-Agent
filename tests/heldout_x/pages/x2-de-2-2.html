2025-2026学年ZUST国际学生优秀学历生奖学金申请指南 | Excellent Degree Students Scholarship Application

Jun 26, 2026    Hits：


Excellent Degree Students Scholarship aims to encourage diligent and hardworking international students pursuing degree at ZUST.

The applicant must meet the following requirements:

Officially registered self-funded international students of ZUST who finish all the fee payment in the past, not applied for suspending schooling, have not lost student status nor have delayed graduation.

The applicant should have the right attitude towards study, be diligent and have received good marks (GPA≥3.0) during this academic year.

The applicant's attendance absences should be less than 20 teaching periods and absences of spot check should be less than 4 teaching periods within an academic year.

The applicant should actively participate in the social service activities and other social activities organized by the university.
