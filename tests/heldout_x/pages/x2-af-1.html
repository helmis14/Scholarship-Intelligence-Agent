e-Scholarship scheme for Afghan Nationals for UG & PG level online programmes/courses for the A.Y. 2026-27. The dates for accepting applications are from 15 July to 27 July, 2026.

Indian Council for Cultural Relations

Enrolment Notice

Online UG & PG Programmes for Afghan Nationals — A.Y. 2026-27, July Session

Enrolment for Online Undergraduate and Postgraduate Programmes/Courses for Afghan Nationals for the Academic Year (A.Y.) 2026-27 commencing from July Session 2026 through e-VidyaBharati (e-VB) Network Project (iLearn Portal)

We are pleased to invite applications for 1000 e-Scholarships from Afghan Nationals for pursuing Undergraduate (UG) and Postgraduate (PG) level online courses at selected Indian Universities/Institutes from 15 July, 2026. Interested students can enrol through the e-VidyaBharati Network Project (iLearn Portal).

The scholarship will cover the Tuition Fee/OCF Fee payable to the University/Institute. No stipend or any other dues will be payable.

Apply via iLearn Portal https://afghan.ilearn.gov.in/
Last Date for Submission of Applications: 27 July, 2026
