About the scholarship

Summary

The Merna Mattsson Scholarship for Women in the Arts, Social Sciences and Humanities is made possible by a generous legacy donation from the Mattsson family in recognition of Merna's contribution as a long serving staff member in the Swinburne Library.

The scholarship was established to support female students from disadvantaged backgrounds to undertake and/or continue tertiary education in the Arts, Social Sciences and Humanities to achieve their full academic potential, by reducing the need to work outside of study.

Value
One-off payment [AUD]
$4,000

Eligibility and selection criteria

Eligibility

Applicant must:

  • identify as female
  • be 25 years of age or older
  • be an Australian or New Zealand citizen, Australian permanent resident, or Australian permanent resident with humanitarian visa.
Enrolment criteria

Applicant must be:

  • a commencing first year or continuing second year and above Swinburne student in Semester 1, 2027
  • enrolled full time in any Swinburne undergraduate or postgraduate course in the School of Social Sciences, Media, Film and Education. Online students are also encouraged to apply.
  • studying on-campus or online.
Selection criteria

Applicant must have:

  • experienced an educational disadvantage.
Study areas

This scholarship is related to the following study areas:

  • Education
  • Film and Television
  • Psychology
  • Media and Communication
  • Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences
Supporting documentation

Personal statement

Submit a personal statement of up to 500 words demonstrating a commitment to study. For example, returning to study after an extended period of time following a break:

  • to raise or take care of a family
  • medical necessity
  • other pressing circumstances.

Educational disadvantage

Provide evidence of educational disadvantage which may include:

  • personal information and location
  • financial hardship
  • living with a disability or medical condition
  • difficult family and life circumstances.

Written reference

Provide a written reference from a teacher, community leader, or employer. Note: referees cannot be a family member or friend.
