Grow PA Scholarship Grant

  • Top of page

The Grow PA Scholarship Grant Program, which began in the 2025-26 academic year, continues in the 2026-27 academic year. The application for new students and those who wish to renew is available on the PHEAA website.

Through this program, Pennsylvania resident students enrolled in Penn College's majors who have not yet completed the requirements for a bachelor-degree program are potentially eligible for grants of up to $5,000 per year for a maximum of four academic years!

Most Penn College associate's and bachelor's degrees pre-qualify.*

Grant recipients must commit to live and work in Pennsylvania after graduation, within the in-demand occupation, for the number of years equal to the number of academic years for which they received the grant.

It is important to understand that grant recipients who do not satisfy the work, residency and graduation requirements will be required to repay the entire Grow PA Grant.

* Individual Studies, General Studies, Applied Technology Studies, and Allied Health are excluded.

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a first-time or a renewal award, recipients must:

  • Have submitted the 2026-27 FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid)
  • Be a current Pennsylvania resident enrolled in an eligible program of study
  • Maintain Financial Aid Satisfactory Academic Progress (for federal programs)
  • Have yet to complete the requirements for a bachelor's degree at any college
  • Maintain at least half-time enrollment (6 or more credits/semester)
Learn more from PHEAA

How Do I Apply?

Funding is limited, so don't delay! If you meet all eligibility criteria and will be enrolled in an eligible associate- or bachelor-degree program at Penn College during the Fall 2026 and/or Spring 2027 semesters, start your application as soon as possible from PHEAA's Grow PA Grant site. Once you complete the application, submit it via PHEAA's Account Access site. You will need to create an account on the site if you don't already have one.

The application will close on January 7, 2027 and includes a Master Promissory Note (MPN) specific to the Grow PA Grant Program. You must agree to repay all Grow PA Grant funds received plus any interest if the work or graduation requirements are not met.

PHEAA recommends students who meet all eligibility criteria to submit their 2026-27 FAFSA before they apply for the Grow PA Grant.

2026-27 Award Processing

During the summer of 2026 PHEAA began selecting renewal applicants (2025-26 Grow PA Grant awardees) for 2026-27 awards followed by selection of first-time applicants. PHEAA will email students who are selected.

PHEAA notifies colleges of all 2026-27 awardees via certification listings. Colleges are required to review all awardees to ensure that they meet all eligibility criteria and then return the listings to PHEAA.

At some point after it receives certification listings, PHEAA notifies colleges of disbursement approval. For the Fall 2026 semester, disbursements for eligible students enrolled at Penn College began in October 2026.

Reasons Why Repayment is Needed

PHEAA provides the following reasons why a Grow PA Grant will need repaid:

  • The recipient shall enter repayment status should they fail to satisfy their degree requirements. Recipients who fail to satisfy their degree requirements shall be provided a 6-month grace period.
  • The recipient shall enter repayment status should they either fail to document or fail to satisfy their employment service obligation within the time period allotted. The recipient shall enter repayment status on the first day of the next calendar month (or no earlier than 30 days from conversion) after the Pennsylvania Higher Education Assistance Agency verifies that they have failed to fulfill the employment service obligation.
  • The recipient shall enter repayment status should they request to convert their grant to a loan because they decided they will not enter employment in an in-demand occupation, will not be a Pennsylvania resident, or will not be employed within the Commonwealth.
