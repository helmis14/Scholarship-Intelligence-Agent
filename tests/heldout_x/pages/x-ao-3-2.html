USJ Community Fellowship

The USJ Community Fellowship Programme is intended to support the financial needs of promising students pursuing their studies at our university. The fellowships are donated by various prominent local institutions and individuals to students with outstanding academic performance. Interested students should read the Information Sheet before submitting your application.

 

2026/2027 USJ Community Fellowship

 

Application Period: 1 April to 8 May 2026

Deadline to submit supporting documents: 12 June 2026

Eligibility

1. USJ students who are not in their final year of study. 
Note: The fellowship subsidy will be awarded to successful applicants in the form of a deduction on the tuition fee in the Spring Semester of 2026-2027.
2. Students who are in active status and with no overdue tuition.
3. Cumulative GPA of 14/20 or above and attendance above 80% in all modules in 2025/2026.
4. Students who need to retake courses in 2026/2027 are not eligible to receive the Fellowships.
5. For local students only: Students should also apply for the Financial Aid offered by Education and Youth Development Bureau (DSEDJ) and provide the application number afterwards.

How to apply?

Step 1: Fill in the Online Application Form on or before 8 May 2026.

Step 2: Submit the supporting documents to the Office for Student and Alumni Affairs (OSAA) in person on or before 12 June 2026.

Please click HERE to see the list of supporting documents required(Access the list with USJ email).

Important Note

    • All submitted information will be kept confidential. The USJ Scholarship and Fellowship Committee will evaluate the applications on a case-by-case basis.

    • As there is a limited number of community fellowships, there is no guarantee that every candidate will be awarded a fellowship.

    • Students experiencing emotional distress due to financial difficulties are welcome to contact the Counselling Team or OSAA for further support.
