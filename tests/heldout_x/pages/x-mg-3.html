Guidelines

How to Apply

Scholarships are awarded based on a 550-word essay that outlines your academic and professional goals and describes how your personal experiences will help you achieve them.

Explore the information below to learn about eligibility requirements, application tips, and how our scholarships can support your academic journey.

Application Timeline
  • Opens: September 15, 2026
  • Closes: November 15, 2026 (11:59 p.m. CST / 05:59 a.m. GMT)
  • Review Period: December 2026 through January 2027
  • Recipients Announced: June 2027
Essay Requirements
  • Length: 550 words or fewer.
  • Content: Clearly detail your goals and how your experiences support them.
  • Format: Submit in English as a plain-text entry (no formatting or markup).
  • Originality: Your essay must be your own work.
  • Anonymity: Do not include your name, address, or any other identifying information in your essay.
Additional Documentation
  • Proof of Enrollment: If awarded a scholarship, provide documentation confirming your intent to enroll in an accredited educational institution for the 2027-28 academic year (e.g., acceptance letter or enrollment verification).
Application Submission
  • Complete the online application form via the Mensa Foundation’s Award Spring scholarship portal.
  • Ensure all required fields are accurately filled out.
  • As part of the submission process, you must certify that your essay and all information provided is your own work.

Judging

Evaluation Criteria

Understand how your essay will be evaluated to maximize your chances of success.

01
Goal Statement

Clearly articulate your academic and professional goals. Include specific steps or experiences that demonstrate your commitment to achieving these goals.

02
Persuasiveness

Explain how your past achievements, personal experiences, and future plans increase the likelihood of reaching your goals. Make a compelling case for why you will succeed.

03
Writing Quality

Your essay will be evaluated on grammar, spelling, punctuation, organization, and overall clarity.

04
Originality and Authenticity

Ensure your essay is original and reflects your unique perspective. The work must be your own, as certified in the application process.

Become a Mensa Foundation Scholarship Judge

Shape the future by evaluating scholarship essays from bright, motivated students.

Why Become a Judge: Your expertise can help identify and support the next generation of leaders by playing a crucial role in assessing students’ aspirations through their essays. Judges need only a passion for education and a desire to help students succeed. Mensa membership not required.

What’s Involved: As a scholarship essay judge, you will read and evaluate essays based on set criteria. We provide training and guidance to ensure consistent and fair assessments, helping you make a meaningful impact.
