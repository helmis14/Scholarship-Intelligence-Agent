🎓 Call for Applications – EGYAID Scholarships 2026–2027

EGYAID Scholarships – Egypt

The University informs students and interested applicants that applications are now open for the EGYAID Scholarships for the 2026–2027 academic year.

📅 Application deadline: 15 September 2026

Applicants are invited to consult the information regarding the application requirements and submit their applications through the official platform indicated in the announcement.

🔗 Application platform:

admissions.study-in-egypt.gov.eg

📌 Interested applicants are encouraged to carefully review the official announcement and submit their applications before the deadline.
