Erasmus KA171 scholarship University of GRANADA

adminoran2 Etudiant أيضا يتوفر:       27 تشرين1/أكتوير 2024 الزيارات: 690
As part of the cooperation between the University of Oran2 and the University of Granada, we are pleased to launch the call for applications for mobility within the framework of the Erasmus KA171 programme. 
 
This call is intended for a single Master’s student enrolled at the University of Oran 2 for the academic year 2024-2025 in the discipline of Spanish Language
 
Number and duration of mobilities:
Student Mobility for Studies (SMS): 01 students for a period of 5 months
Date to remember: 30 October 2024 Deadline for applications on the following link:
