José Padín Scholarship

The Dr. José Padín Scholarship is awarded annually to two students who are residents of Puerto Rico. The scholarship supplements Haverford’s need-based financial aid by replacing loans, work-study, and summer earnings expectations with additional scholarship funding.

This page includes eligibility requirements, application instructions, important deadlines, and frequently asked questions. Scholarship applications can be submitted online and are due February 8, 2027. The scholarship application must be submitted in addition to your previously submitted application to Haverford.

The Admission Committee will notify applicants of their admission decisions in early March. Scholarship finalists will be invited to participate in José Padín Scholarship Weekend.

Apply by: February 8, 2027

Apply to José Padín Scholarship

Scholarship Overview

What is the José Padín Scholarship?

  • The Dr. José Padín Scholarship is awarded annually to two students who are residents of Puerto Rico and have demonstrated outstanding academic achievement, extracurricular involvement, and a strong commitment to the future of Puerto Rico.

Established in 1967 by Mrs. Pauline Padín in memory of her husband, Dr. José Padín (Haverford Class of 1907), the scholarship honors his lifelong dedication to education, public service, and the preservation of Puerto Rico's cultural heritage.

The scholarship supplements Haverford's need-based financial aid package by replacing student loans, work-study, and summer earnings expectations with additional scholarship funding.

What is the award amount?

Padín Scholars receive:

  • Additional scholarship funding that supplements Haverford's need-based financial aid
  • No student loans in their financial aid package
  • No work-study expectation
  • No summer earnings expectation
  • Additional funds during the first and junior years to help cover educational and living expenses

The Padín Scholarship does not change the family contribution calculated as part of Haverford's financial aid package.

Who sponsors the scholarship?

  • The scholarship was established by Mrs. Pauline Padín in memory of her husband, Dr. José Padín, and is administered by Haverford College.

Eligibility

Who can apply?

Applicants must:

  • Be a resident of Puerto Rico
  • Apply for admission to Haverford College
  • Demonstrate outstanding academic achievement
  • Show strong commitment to the future of Puerto Rico
  • Demonstrate leadership, social and cultural awareness, and political engagement

What are the academic requirements?

Successful applicants have:

  • Excellent academic achievement
  • Strong extracurricular accomplishments

Community service and extracurricular activities are valued but are not required.

Residency/citizenship requirements

  • Applicants must be residents of Puerto Rico.
  • Being of Puerto Rican descent, Latine, or Hispanic is not required to be eligible for the scholarship.

Enrollment requirements

  • Applicants must complete an application for admission to Haverford College, as scholarship consideration is contingent upon applying for admission.

How to Apply

To be considered for the Dr. José Padín Scholarship:

  1. Apply for admission to Haverford College

    • Complete and submit your application for admission to Haverford College.

  2. Submit your Padín Scholarship essay

    • Write an essay in English explaining why you are well-qualified for the Padín Scholarship. Essays may be any length and should honestly describe your qualifications, goals, and commitment to Puerto Rico.

    • Submit your essay by uploading it through your application or emailing it to admission [at] haverford.edu (admission[at]haverford[dot]edu).

  3. Meet the application deadline

    • Completed scholarship applications and all required materials must be submitted by February 8, 2027.

Selection Process

The Admission Committee reviews all eligible applications. Finalists are invited to participate in José Padín Scholarship Weekend before scholarship recipients are selected.

About Dr. José Padín and the Scholarship

Dr. José Padín

Dr. José Padín graduated from Haverford College in 1907 and later served as Puerto Rico's Secretary of Education from 1930 to 1936. During his tenure, he successfully restored Spanish as the language of instruction in Puerto Rico's schools, helping preserve the island's linguistic and cultural heritage.

After leaving public office, Dr. Padín worked in the United States as a translator and associate editor before returning to Puerto Rico, where he served on the Council of Higher Education until his death in 1963.

The Padín Scholarship Legacy 

In 1967, Dr. Padín's wife, Pauline Padín, established the scholarship in his memory. She envisioned a scholarship that would support students from Puerto Rico who would benefit from Haverford's academic excellence while using their education to serve Puerto Rico.

Today, Padín Scholars continue that tradition through leadership, scholarship, and service.

Puerto Rican Students at Haverford College

For decades, Padín Scholars and other students from Puerto Rico have enriched the Haverford community by: 

  • Mentoring and tutoring students in Philadelphia and surrounding communities
  • Supporting courses and programming focused on Latin America
  • Assisting the Office of Admission in connecting prospective students from Puerto Rico with Haverford
  • Contributing to campus life through leadership and community engagement
