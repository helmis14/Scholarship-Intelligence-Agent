Scholarship Programme for Diaspora Children (SPDC) scheme for the academic year 2025-26.

December 30, 2026

The Embassy of India in Djibouti invites applications from Persons of Indian Origin (PIO) and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) residing in Djibouti for the Scholarship Programme for Undergraduate Courses in India under the Scholarship Programme for Diaspora Children (SPDC) scheme for the academic year 2025-26 The last date for submission for duly filled-in application forms in the prescribed format is 30th January 2026. Application can be submitted to Embassy of India, Djibouti or emailed at - cul[dot]djibouti[at]mea[dot]gov[dot]in
For more details, click on the link below: https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1eC_XdvLsWv2VqMBpSQxwYpbYxEaQ0OKC?usp=sharing
