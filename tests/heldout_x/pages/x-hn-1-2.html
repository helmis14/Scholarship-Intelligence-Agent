KNB Scholarship

KNB Scholarship Overview

Kemitraan Negara Berkembang Scholarship, popularly known as KNB Scholarship, is a financial assistance offered by the Indonesian Government to prospective international students coming from developing countries to pursue their degree (Bachelor, Master, or Doctorate Degree) at one of the universities in Indonesia. 

The scholarship was conceived in the 10th Conference of Heads of States of Non-Aligned Movement (NAM) countries, 1st–6th September 1992. The Government of the Republic of Indonesia began offering the scholarship to potential students from NAM member countries the following year.

KNB offers an experience of studying in top universities in Indonesia and at the same time living in one of the world’s most diverse and vibrant societies. By getting the scholarship, students will experience quality postgraduate programs managed by the 47 (forty-seven) host university including Universitas Andalas (UNAND).

Vision and Mission

  • To contribute to the human resource development in the developing countries.
  • To promote deeper cultural understanding and to strengthen the relationship between developing countries.
  • To improve Indonesian higher education institutions’ research capacity as a part of the national strategies in increasing the number of world-class universities in Indonesia.

Program Structure▾

1. Indonesian Language Training Program (BIPA)

All KNB Scholarship awardees are required to undertake Indonesian Language (Bahasa Indonesia) training for a maximum of one (1) academic year. All academic activities, including lectures, theses, and dissertations, will be conducted in Bahasa Indonesia. The BIPA Program is provided by Universitas Andalas.

2. Preparatory Program

A preparatory program may be conducted before the academic program in accordance with Universitas Andalas’ academic policies and program planning.

3. Academic Program
The academic program (lectures, thesis, or dissertation) is conducted in Bahasa Indonesia with the following maximum scholarship duration:

  • Doctoral Degree Program: up to 8 semesters
    Two schemes are available: Doctoral Program by Research & Doctoral Program by a combination of Coursework and Research
    An extension of up to 2 (two) additional semesters can be granted on a case-by-case assessment of the awardee's individual case.
  • Master's Degree Program: up to 4 semesters
  • Bachelor's Degree Program: up to 8 semesters
How To Apply▾
  1. Download the KNB Scholarship Offering Letter from the KNB Scholarship website: https://knb.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/
  2. Submit the Offering Letter, Official Passport, English Proficiency Test, (Applicant from non-English-Speaking Countries), Recommendation Letter from the Indonesian Embassy, Recommendation from Employer/Immediate Supervisor, Academic Recommendation Letter from Previous Schools, Academic Certificates and Academic Transcripts from Previous Degree, Recommendation Letter from the potential research supervisor in one of the Universities providing partner of the KNB Scholarship (for Doctoral applicant only), Statement of purpose of their studies in Indonesia (for Doctoral applicant only) in website KNB Scholarship;
  3. Complete the online application process at https://knb.kemdiktisaintek.go.id/
  4. Selection process.
  5. The Selection Results will be broadcast online through the KNB Scholarship Website and officially announced through the Indonesian Embassy publication network.

Applicant's Eligibility▾
  • Bachelor's Degree Scholarship
    1. The maximum age to apply for the scholarship is 21 years old, no more than December 31st, 2026.
    2. Hold a High School degree. Please be advised that a bachelor's degree holder is not eligible to apply for the bachelor's degree scholarship.
    3. Provide a curriculum vitae in English or Bahasa.
    4. Have the English Proficiency Test Score minimum as follows: TOEFL ITP of 477, TOEFL IBT of 53, IELTS of 5,5, TOEIC of 510 or equivalent (the certificate must be obtained within the last 2 Years). Participants of foreign nationality who come from English-speaking countries, or participants from a university who are enrolled in programs in English (stated in the participant's diploma/transcript), are not required to submit the English Proficiency document by attaching official proof from their home university.
    5. Provide the recommendation letter to apply for the KNB scholarship from the Indonesian Embassy or the Indonesian Consulate General in English or Bahasa. The recommendation letter must be from the applicant's country of origin, not the country of domicile.
    6. Provide a recommendation letter to apply for the KNB scholarship from the employer/immediate supervisor in English or Bahasa. If currently unemployed, can provide a statement letter.
    7. Provide an Academic Recommendation Letter from previous schools in English or Bahasa.
    8. Provide The Scanned Academic Certificate and Academic Transcripts Copy of the high school degree in English or Bahasa, that has been legalized by the Ministry of Education and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the applicant’s country.
    9. Proof of citizenship by submit Oﬃcial Passport, which is still valid for at least December 31st, 2027. An official passport is a non-Diplomatic Passport or Work Passport.
    10. Provide a video with a duration maximum 5 minutes in English or Bahasa and submit the video to your youtube and the link of the video must be upload in your KNB Scholarship account. The video explains about;
      - Profile the applicant;
      - Why you choose studies in Indonesia and by KNB Scholarship; and
      - Why you choose studies in campus and study program that you choose.
    11. Provide a certified medical report including: physical examination, Infectious disease test (Tuberculosis, syphilis, HIV/AIDS, Ebola, Mpox), mental health test, and a negative drug test (screening for amphetamine, methamphetamine, marijuana, morphine, and benzodiazepines). All documents must be in English or Bahasa and issued within last 3 (three) months;
    12. Willing not to marry/or be married to, engage/be engaged to a de facto person who holds or is eligible to hold Indonesian citizenship or permanent residency or with other scholarship recipients;
    13. Willing to not be pregnant during scholarship period;
    14. Not currently or will be undergoing military service until the end of the scholarship period;
    15. Complete the online application form in https://knb.kemdiktisaintek.go.id

    *Note :
    1. Please be advised that documents (c),(e),(f),(g),(h), and (k) must be in English or Bahasa;
    2. Willing to attach Yellow Fever vaccination certificate for applicants who come from and/or reside in Africa.
