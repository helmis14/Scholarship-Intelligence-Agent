The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) Regional Office Jakarta has officially announced the opening of the scholarship applications for the DAAD University Summer Courses 2027. This fully funded program invites foreign students to participate in intensive language, cultural studies, and specialized technical language courses hosted directly at renowned German universities during the summer period.

Designed to offer students a firsthand experience of academic and cultural life in Germany, the program is primarily open to currently enrolled Bachelor’s and Master’s degree students from all academic disciplines who are at least 18 years old. Final-year Bachelor’s students who plan to continue their studies toward a Master’s degree are also encouraged to apply. To qualify, candidates must demonstrate a minimum German language proficiency level of B1 through recognized certificates such as onSET, TestDaF, Goethe-Institut, DSD, DSH, telc, or ÖSD. Generally, official language certificates must not be older than one year at the time of application.

Interested applicants from Universitas Jember should take note of key selection criteria and requirements:

  • Eligibility: Candidates must be enrolled at a university outside Germany throughout the application process and course duration.
  • Award Limit: The DAAD University Summer Course scholarship can only be received once per individual recipient.
  • Commitment: Selected scholars are expected to demonstrate a personal commitment to environmental sustainability throughout the program.

Prospective applicants can access detailed guidelines, course options, and portal requirements by visiting the official program page at University Summer Courses Offered in Germany for Foreign Students and Graduates. Additional consultations and information sessions can also be booked through DAAD Indonesia.

Application Deadline All application materials must be submitted through the official DAAD Portal no later than Friday, October 30, 2026.

For further queries regarding the program, eligibility details, or application procedures, students may directly visit us at International Office of the University of Jember (Pusat Layanan dan Kemitraan Internasional, LPMPP lantai 3).
