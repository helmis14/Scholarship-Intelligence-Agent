NTU Centennial Fellowship

To celebrate its centennial anniversary, National Taiwan University proudly introduces the NTU Centennial Fellowship, a prestigious scholarship program for outstanding students from top international partner institutions. This initiative seeks to deepen global academic partnerships, promote cross-border exchange, and support talented individuals in pursuing master’s or doctoral degrees at NTU. Through this fellowship, NTU aims to attract promising international scholars to engage in cutting-edge research and transformative learning, as we embark on the journey toward the next century of excellence. Please check details on the Scholarships page.

The Office of International Affairs is here to help! It is our responsibility to ensure that every international student’s needs are met, and that you get the most out of your stay at NTU.

 

Drop-in consultation hours: Monday to Friday, 1:30 PM to 4:30 PM

 

National Taiwan University conducts its international student admissions in accordance with the regulations of the Ministry of Education. The University does not collaborate with any study abroad agencies or external institutions for recruitment purposes. Requests from such organizations for campus visits, meetings, or recruitment cooperation are, in principle, not accommodated.

All official admissions information is published on NTU’s Overseas Student Admission website. The campus remains open to the public; those who wish to explore are welcomed to visit—no prior application is required.
