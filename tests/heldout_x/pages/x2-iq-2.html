Applications for 2027-2028 Chevening Scholarships are now open!

  • Print
  • Email
 

Applications for 2027-2028 Chevening Scholarships are now open!

Chevening is the UK Government’s prestigious international scholarships programme. Funded by the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office, and partner organisations, we offer exceptional future leaders the opportunity to further their education in the UK so that they can drive impact in areas of shared priority for the UK and their home countries. 

Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research Office of Envoys and Cultural Relations, Master's Scholarship in the UK, Teachers, employees, and citizens of the Kurdistan Region are informed that the Chevening Scholarship is open for the academic year 2027–2028 The program is funded by the Government of the United Kingdom (UK), through the Department for Foreign Affairs, Partnership and International Development (FCDO).

https://www.chevening.org/scholarship/iraq/
