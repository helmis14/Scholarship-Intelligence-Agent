Vice-Chancellor Excellence Scholarship

50% reduction in tuition fees for the duration of the course.

Requirements:

  • Undergraduate applicants require an equivalent minimum ATAR 95 or above
  • Postgraduate applicants require an equivalent of 6.5 GPA or above

Selection process:
These scholarships are competitive awards and are confirmed through a selection process. For those who have been shortlisted, you will be required to complete a video submission outlining your suitability for the scholarship.

Applicants will be notified of the scholarship outcome as part of the selection process. Applicants who are eligible for an alternate scholarship will be issued an offer, including a scholarship at the time of application. Successful recipients will be required to participate in the Flinders International Ambassador Program and actively support Flinders University in promoting Flinders globally.

Scholarship eligibility

  • Available only to full fee-paying international applicants of any nationality (citizens of Australia and New Zealand and Australian Permanent Residents or other permanent visa holders are ineligible for the Scholarship).
  • Available to new international commencing students only. Commencing students who were required to defer their offer due to visa application refusal or delays are still considered commencing students for this scholarship if it is still available by the intended commencement semester/study period.
  • Applicants must meet the application eligibility criteria for entry into the course they wish to study to be considered for the scholarship.
  • Scholarships are not available to applicants transferring to Flinders with more than one third of the course total unit value of credit from studies completed at another institution.

Scholarship exclusions

The following courses are excluded from the Global and Global Excellence Scholarships:

  • Bachelor of Clinical Science/Doctor of Medicine
  • Bachelor of Nursing (Graduate Entry)
  • Graduate Certificates
  • Graduate Diplomas
  • Doctor of Medicine
  • Master of Psychology (Clinical)
  • Master of Physiotherapy
  • Master by Research
  • Doctor of Philosophy

How to apply

Review terms and conditions located on your scholarship page

Review the terms and conditions listed under your desired scholarship section.

Check the requirements

Check and confirm you meet all entry requirements for your chosen course.

Check eligibility

Make sure you meet the scholarship eligibility requirements above before you apply. Please note that we do not provide pre-assessment.

VC Excellence Scholarship requirements

For students who meet the requirements for Vice-Chancellor Undergraduate or Postgraduate International Scholarships, complete the online Vice-Chancellor Scholarship Application Form as advised in your Offer Letter before the application deadline. 

Are you a new applicant to Flinders University?

Submit an application and you will be assessed, and advised within your Letter of Offer. If you are eligible you simply need to accept your offer for the scholarship to be awarded.
