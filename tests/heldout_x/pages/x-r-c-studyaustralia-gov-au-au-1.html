Scholarship Information

Level of study: Postgraduate, Undergraduate / VET

There’s more than one path to university. As an international student in Australia, you may be eligible for the Alternative Study Path Scholarship if you have completed studies onshore, or if you are in Australia and in the process of applying for a student visa. This scholarship covers 15 percent of your total course tuition.
