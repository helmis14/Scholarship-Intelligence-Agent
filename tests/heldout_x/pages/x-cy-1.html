Scholarship scheme offered in cooperation with the ‘CyprusAid’ of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Cyprus

Tweet
Share

23/05/2025 07:30

Νέα-Ανακοινώσεις

The Cyprus University of Technology (CUT), in collaboration with the ‘CyprusAid’ of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of the Republic of Cyprus, is offering scholarships for undergraduate and postgraduate studies to interested students from Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, South Africa, Ghana, Egypt, Lebanon, Palestine, Jordan, Bahrain, Ethiopia, Madagascar, Mauritius, Seychelles, the Comoros,  Papua New Guinea, Fiji, Kiribati, Marshall Islands, Nauru, Samoa, Solomon Islands, Tonga, Tuvalu, Vanuatu for studies commencing in September 2025.

*Applicants must hold a valid passport or ID Card proving that they have one of the eligible nationalities and be permanent residents of one of the eligible countries.

Click here to see an overview of the Scholarship Scheme 

Which programs do these scholarships cover?

The scholarships apply to the following programs:

Undergraduate

Postgraduate

Bsc in Nursing*

All Phd Programs

Higher Diploma in Culinary Arts**

Higher Diploma in Hotel and Tourism Management**

*Prior to commencing the 4-year undergraduate studies in the Bsc in Nursing program, successful applicants are required to complete a one-year Greek language course at the Language Centre of CUT in Limassol. The scholarship terms also cover the duration of this preparatory year.

**These are 3-year programs, taught in English, at the University’s Campus in Paphos and lead to the acquisition of a Higher Diploma.

What do these scholarships include?

For all the programs mentioned above, the scholarships for successful candidates include:

  1. Full tuition coverage
  2. Accommodation in university dormitories for the full duration of studies*
  3. A monthly stipend of €400 for the full duration of studies:
  4. Provision of a laptop up to the value of €500
  5. Application fee for a permit to enter Cyprus (as determined by the state)
  6. Compulsory medical examinations for entry permit up to €150
  7. Airfare to visit home country up to €1,000*
  8. Application fee for issuance/renewal of a residence permit in Cyprus (as determined by the state)
  9. Compulsory medical examinations for residence permit up to €150
  10. Mandatory health certificate issued by a public hospital
  11. Individual insurance contract up to €180
  12. Special medical examinations up to €180
  13. Miscellaneous expenses up to €100

*Note: The living allowance is not provided during the summer months (July and/or August) if the student returns to his or her home country.

Who is eligible to apply, and what documents are required?

Applicants for the undergraduate programs must meet the following requirements and provide the necessary documentation:

  • An official certificate of citizenship (copies of ID and valid passport) from one of the eligible countries
  • A Leaving Certificate from Secondary Education from one of the eligible countries
  • A certificate demonstrating proficiency in English

Applicants for the postgraduate PhD programs must meet the following requirements and provide the necessary documentation:

  • An official certificate of citizenship (copies of ID and valid passport) from one of the eligible countries
  • Photocopies of university degrees (BSc Degree and a MSc Degree from recognized universities in relevant fields)
  • Detailed academic transcripts.
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV).
  • English Language Proficiency Certificate: Acceptable certificates include G.C.E/I.G.C.S.E O level exams with a minimum grade of C, or IELTS/TOEFL scores with a minimum of 6.5 or 580 (paper-based test)/72 (internet-based test). Graduates from English-speaking university programs are exempt from this requirement

Applicants will be electronically notified of the outcome of their application via the email address they have provided during the submission process and through the University’s Online Portal.

  • Application Type:

Scholarships ‘CyprusAid’ (UND) or Scholarships ‘CyprusAid’ (PHD)

  • Application Deadline: June 15th, 2025
