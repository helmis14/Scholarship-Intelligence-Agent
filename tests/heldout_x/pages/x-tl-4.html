UNS Scholarship for August 2027 Intake

Dipublikasikan pada 2026-06-08 • Admin

Universitas Sebelas Maret (UNS), Indonesia, invites prospective international students to apply for Bachelor's, Master’s, and Doctoral programs through the UNS Scholarship for the August 2027 intake.

SCHOLARSHIP TYPES

  • Full Scholarship

Scholarship coverage

  1. Tuition and instituional fee waiver
  2. Free Indonesian Language Course
  3. Health Insurance 
  4. Settlement allowance: IDR 1.000.000 
  5. Monthly Living Allowance
    • Bachelor's Degree: IDR 1.500.000 
    • Master's Degree: IDR 1.750.000 
    • Doctoral's Degree: IDR 2.250.000
  • Partial Scholarship

Scholarship coverage:

  1. Tuition and institutional fee waiver
  2. Free Indonesian Language course
  3. Health Insurance

Notes:

  • Scholarships are open for all study programs. Quotas for the Faculty of Medicine or Medical Sciences are limited.
  • Airfare tickets and student visa/stay permit costs are to be borne by students.
  • Health insurance coverage is subject to the policy of the insurance provider. See here for the detail https://uns.id/healthinsurance

SCHOLARSHIP DURATION

  • Bachelor: 8 semesters
  • Master: 4 semesters
  • Doctoral: 6 semesters

RESPONSIBILITIES OF AWARDEES

  1. Comply with Indonesian laws and university regulations
  2. Attend all academic activities
  3. Maintain required GPA (minimum 3,00 for social sciences and 2,75 for engineering and natural sciences)
  4. Graduate within the scholarship duration
  5. Follow terms stated in the scholarship agreement

Scholarship may be revoked in cases of violation or underperformance. Resigned awardees must reimburse received funds.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Applicants must:

  1. Be non-Indonesian citizens
  2. Meet the following age requirements (when submitting application):
    • Bachelor's Degree: Maximum 21 years 0 month old 
    • Master’s Degree: Maximum 35 years 0 month old
    • Doctoral’s Degree: Maxiumum 40 years 0 month old
  3. Not hold refugee status or have been convicted of a any serious crime
  4. Demonstrate English language proficiency: TOEFL: min. 450 (Engineering & Natural Science) / 500 (Social Science), Duolingo English Test min. score 100, or Medium of Instruction Letter from previous school/university
  5. Demonstrate a strong interest in studying at UNS.
  6. Be willing to attend an online interview and test
  7. Have financial support
  8. Complete all required documents

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

  1. Recent formal photo (JPG/PNG, max. 300 KB)
  2. Valid Identity Card (Citizen ID card)
  3. Passport (valid for at least 18 months)
  4. Graduation certificate & academic transcript (English or Indonesian)
  5. Recent health certificate including: physical examination, infectious disease test (Tuberculosis, syphilis, HIV/AIDS, Ebola, Mpox), and a negative drug test (screening for amphetamine, methamphetamine, marijuana, morphine, and benzodiazepines). All documents must be in English or Bahasa and issued within last 3 (three) months
  6. Curriculum Vitae (UNS template)
  7. Financial Statement (UNS template)
  8. Personal Statement (UNS template)
  9. English language proficiency certificate
  10. Indonesian language certificate (optinal but preferred)
  11. Recommendation letter from the Indonesian Embassy
  12. Two recommendation letters from academic supervisors/lecturers or immediate working supervisor (for Master & Doctoral only)
  13. Preliminary research proposal (for Doctoral only)

Document templates are available at: https://uns.id/admissiontemplate

 

HOW TO APPLY

  1. Download Letter of Offer and cover letter to request recommendation from the Indonesian Embassy
    - Download the Letter of Offer at https://uns.id/offer2027
    - Download the cover letter here
  2. Explore available programs/majors and the curriculum
    https://uns.id/listofcourses 
  3. Visit the Indonesian Embassy/Consulate or contact them via email or other online platforms
    Request a recommendation letter by presenting:
    • Letter of Offer from UNS
    • Cover letter to request recommendation
    • Passport
    • Graduation Certificate
    • Academic Transcript
  4. Register at UNS International Admission Portal
    • Create an account or log in if you already have an account
    • Complete your personal information
  5. Upload all required documents
  6. Print registration card
  7. Selection Stages
    • Administrative screening
    • Computer-based test
    • Interview 
  8. Admission Notification
    Successful candidates will receive an notification with a Letter of Acceptance, Scholarship Contract, and Visa Invoice.
  9. Confirm and Proceed
    • Send confirmation to study at UNS
    • UNS will apply for your study permit and student visa
    • Depart to Indonesia upon receiving official notification
