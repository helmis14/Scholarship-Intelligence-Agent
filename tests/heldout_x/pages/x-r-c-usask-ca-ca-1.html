International Students from British Columbia Award

Value: $10,000
Number offered: 1
Eligibility: Open to international students residing in British Columbia who apply to enter any direct-entry undergraduate program. Students will be considered automatically for this award based on academic achievement.
Selection criteria: Academic achievement
Deadline: Apply for admission by May 1
