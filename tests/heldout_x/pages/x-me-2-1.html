정부초청장학 (Global Korea Scholarship) 선발일정

학부과정 Undergraduate(GKS-U)  

Application Schedule
Schedule Dates Notes
Application Period Mid Sep. ~ Late Sep. by online (https://www.studyinkorea.go.kr/)
Embassy Track 3rd Round Review Early Dec. ~ Late Dec. Including Department Review (Interview)
Embassy Track Screening Result Announcement Late Dec. 3rd Round Selection Result
NIIED Final Result Announcement Early Jan. Final Result

대학원과정 Graduate(GKS-G)

Application Schedule
Schedule Dates Notes
Application Period for the University Track Early Feb. ~ Mid Mar. by post or in person
Documents Screening Late Mar. ~ Early April Office of International Affairs
Department Screening ~ Mid April Document Screening Interview
Candidate Announcement for the University Track Late April 1st Round selection Result
Embassy Track Screening Result Announcement Early June 3rd Round selection Result
NIIED Final Result Announcement Late June Final Result
