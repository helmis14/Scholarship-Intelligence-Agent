Emirates Leadership Initiative Fellowship

Earn a fully funded Harvard master’s degree & leadership training with the ELI Fellowship.

Apply now Get the fellowship one-pager

Key Fellowship Information

Admission Application Deadline

December 1, 2026 at 12 p.m. (noon) ET

Financial Aid Application Deadline

January 15, 2027 at 12 p.m. (noon) ET

Funding Amount 

Full tuition scholarship, health insurance, and living stipend

Questions?

Admissions and Financial Aid: admissionsandaid@hks.harvard.edu

Fellowship Program Manager: saida_abdalla@hks.harvard.edu

 

Shape the future of the Arab region, be a force for change in an evolving global arena.  

Now more than ever, modern leadership requires the ability to work across cultures and sectors—to think globally and act locally. The Emirates Leadership Initiative (ELI) Fellowship equips students with the tools to be a powerful force for change.

Generously supported by a gift from the Department of Government Enablement – Abu Dhabi Government, the Emirates Leadership Initiative Fellowship is a collaboration between the Center for Public Leadership, the Middle East Initiative, and Executive Education at Harvard Kennedy School. The fellowship provides advanced training in public management and leadership development to emerging public and private sector leaders who are from, have worked significantly in, or are deeply committed to building a better future for the Arab region.

Who Should Apply? 

You may be a strong fit for the ELI Fellowship if you: 

  • Have spent a significant portion of your career working in the Arab region, or have demonstrated a deep commitment to building a better future for the region. 
  • Demonstrate strong academic credentials, a deep commitment to public service, leadership potential and a clear vision for creating impact in the Arab region, and a collaborative spirit.
  • Are ready for full-time study on the HKS campus in Cambridge, Massachusetts.

How to Apply

To apply for the ELI Fellowship, there are two steps:  

  1. Complete the application for admission to your selected HKS master’s degree program. This application is currently open and will be due on December 1, 2026.
  2. Complete the HKS financial aid application after applying for admission, which will be due January 15, 2027.

To get started on this process, you can visit our How to Apply page or fill out the Request for Information form to get more information on the degree programs application process.

Master’s Programs

Application and Fellowship Program Information

Step 1

Apply to the Harvard Kennedy School master’s degree program of your choice before December 1, 2026 at 12 p.m. (noon) ET.

Step 2

Applicants for the Emirates Leadership Initiative fellowship must submit through the financial aid portal before January 15, 2027 at 12 p.m. (noon) ET:

  • A biographical profile, not to exceed 150 words (This profile should be short, concise and in the third person.) Please include the following highlights: an introductory sentence including your name; the degree you are pursuing at Harvard Kennedy School in addition to any joint degree at Harvard or concurrent degree program at another graduate or professional institution; where you are from and where you currently reside; an overview and highlights of your relevant work/professional experience; significant leadership achievements including awards or certifications; and educational credentials with the degrees earned and institutions attended.)
     
  • A document containing written responses to the following two essay prompts:
    • Please discuss a topic you are passionate about as it pertains to leadership in the Arab world and your experiences as it relates to this topic. Please include specific examples in your response (500 words or less).
    • Please describe areas of leadership growth or opportunity you hope to pursue while a student at HKS (200 words or less).

Selection

Finalists for the Emirates Leadership Initiative fellowship will be interviewed in late February by members of the fellowship selection committee.

Upon admission to HKS, applicants will be notified if they are selected for the Emirates Leadership Initiative fellowship.

Eligibility

The Emirates Leadership Initiative Fellowship is open to newly admitted degree program students at Harvard Kennedy School who are from, have spent a significant portion of their careers working in, or have demonstrated a deep commitment to building a better future for one or more countries in the Arab region. Successful candidates will also demonstrate strong academic credentials, a deep commitment to public service, leadership potential and a clear vision for creating impact in the Arab region, and a collaborative spirit.

What We Offer

The fellowship provides:

  • Financial support toward a master’s degree program at Harvard Kennedy School plus professional development opportunities
  • A comprehensive co-curricular program to augment your leadership development
  • A network of exceptional peers and mentors who will enhance your ability to impact the communities you serve

The Emirates Leadership Initiative Fellowship provides a full tuition scholarship, health insurance, and living stipend for the academic year. The fellowship funding covers the full length of the degree program. For the one-year MC/MPA program, funding covers the full academic year. For two-year degree programs, funding covers both years. Financial support is only provided for the cost of attendance at HKS.

The Fellowship Experience

Fellows will participate in a yearlong co-curricular program designed by Harvard Kennedy School’s Center for Public Leadership. This not-for-credit program is intended to inspire, to provide concrete opportunities for leadership skill-building, and to connect fellows with an interdisciplinary network of like-minded peers and mentors that will last a lifetime. The fellowship co-curricular program includes dinner seminars, an annual retreat, and experiential learning grounded in discussions with visionary leaders whose work has had significant impact in the world. The program will also focus on strengthening the capacity for public leadership through reflection, mentorship, and skill building.

The Fellows will create a vibrant network of students and alumni committed to advancing knowledge, collaborating on solutions to pressing public policy problems, and building leadership capacity in their communities, the Arab world, and globally.

Note: Before submitting your application, we expect that you have thoroughly reviewed all pertinent information related to the Emirates Leadership Initiative Fellowship, including but not limited to: program requirements, eligibility information, and the background and purpose of the fellowship. Being a CPL fellow carries with it certain responsibilities including maintenance of good academic standing as required by your degree program; attendance at all fellowship programming for the entire duration of the fellowship; leading program sessions and service activities and responsible conduct in representing CPL and Harvard University. Please review our Fellowship FAQ for more information.

Commitment to Nondiscrimination

As a matter of commitment, policy, and law, Harvard Kennedy School does not discriminate on the basis of gender, gender identity, color, race, age, sexual orientation, religion, national or ethnic origin, political beliefs, intellectual focus, disability, or veteran status. The School strongly believes diversity enhances the value of all its activities, from research and teaching to degree programs and public events. The School encourages applications from candidates who are interested in careers in public service and acknowledges a special responsibility to train effective leaders in public policy and management.
