Scholarship

We provide high-quality, affordable education..

See Details

The Libyan International University announces the launch of the [4th] edition of its scholarship program for the academic year 2026-2027, as part of its commitment to achieving social responsibility and striving to develop the education and health services sectors, particularly in the field of nursing, which is considered a fundamental pillar of medical services.

This scholarship is designated for students wishing to enroll in the Specialized Nursing Science Program at the Faculty of Medical and Health Sciences for the academic year 2026-2027. The number of available scholarships is [12] full scholarships.

First: Academic Benefits

  • Full coverage of tuition fees throughout the program’s duration, which is four academic years in addition to the internship year.
  • Obtaining a Bachelor of Science in Specialized Nursing (BSN) degree with 240 credit hours according to the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS).
  • Studying across all nine nursing departments: Pediatric Nursing, Internal Medicine and Surgical Nursing, Emergency and Intensive Care Nursing, Community Health Nursing, Psychiatric and Mental Health Nursing, Obstetrics and Gynecology Nursing, Dental Nursing, Geriatric Nursing, and Nursing Services Management.

Second: Modern and Distinguished Teaching Methods

  • A curriculum that combines theoretical education and clinical application, relying on active learning, problem-solving, and creative thinking.
  • Training students to use research evidence and evidence-based practice in nursing decision-making.
  • Simulation-based learning, case studies, Problem-Based Learning (PBL), and blended e-learning.
  • Faculty members with academic and clinical experience providing supervision, academic guidance, and individual follow-up for students.
  • Developing communication skills with patients and the healthcare team in both Arabic and English.

Third: Practical Training and Hands-On Education

  • Training in laboratories, clinical skills labs, and simulation labs prior to transitioning to actual practice with patients, enabling students to master skills in a safe environment.
  • Field training in public and private hospitals and health centers, such as the University Medical Center, the University Dental Center, the Clinical Skills Center, and the Simulation Center, allowing exposure to diverse work environments and specialties.
  • Training in primary healthcare and community health centers alongside hospitals and medical centers, so that students learn to provide care for individuals, families, and the community.
  • The internship year, an integrated practical training year that refines professional competence before graduation.

Fourth: Employment Opportunities and Advancement After Graduation

A specialized nursing qualification increases employment opportunities in:

  • Public and private hospitals, specialized centers, intensive care units (ICU), emergency departments, operating rooms, dialysis units, oncology centers, primary healthcare centers, and community health.
  • Maternal and child healthcare facilities, geriatric care, mental health facilities, dental clinics, and specialized centers.
  • A career path toward supervisory, leadership, and nursing service management positions.
  • The possibility of continuing postgraduate studies (Master’s and Ph.D.) and working in university teaching and scientific research.
  • Opportunities to work within Libya in different cities and institutions, as well as in health and humanitarian organizations, while benefiting from credit hours under the European system.
  1. The applicant must be under 25 years old at the time of application submission.
  2. The applicant must possess good academic achievements.
  3. Proficiency in the English language.
  4. Experience or participation in voluntary community service activities (if any).
  5. The family’s financial income does not permit covering the cost of private university education in this specialization.
  6. Fulfilling all academic and technical admission requirements for the program, including holding a High School Diploma (Scientific Track) or its equivalent, and passing both the personal interview and medical examination.

  Submission must be online via the dedicated system on the university website, by the announced deadline.
