Scholarship to study in Ukraine

State Scholarships for International Students

Ukraine offers international students a unique opportunity to study in Ukraine on scholarship through state-funded programs provided by the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine. These scholarships are available to foreign citizens from partner countries that have signed international agreements with Ukraine in the field of education.

Each year, hundreds of international students receive a scholarship to study in Ukraine, allowing them to obtain a high-quality European education at Ukrainian higher education institutions free of charge.

Who Can Study in Ukraine on Scholarship?

A scholarship to study in Ukraine is available to citizens of countries that have bilateral or multilateral educational agreements with Ukraine.

You can check if your country is eligible to study in Ukraine on scholarship:

  • List of eligible countries: https://mon.gov.ua/en/ministry/activity/international-cooperation/possibilities-for-obtaining-an-educational-grant
  • Interactive map: https://uploads.knightlab.com/storymapjs/c23c420c05abad72a17b1def2003893e/possibilities-for-obtaining-an-educational-grant/index.html

What Does a Scholarship to Study in Ukraine Cover?

Students who study in Ukraine on scholarship receive the following benefits:

  • Free education at Ukrainian higher education institutions
  • Monthly allowance (if provided by the international agreement)
  • Medical care in accordance with Ukrainian legislation
  • Accommodation in a student dormitory under the same conditions as Ukrainian citizens
  • One-year Ukrainian language preparatory course (free of charge)

What Is Not Covered by the Scholarship?

While a scholarship to study in Ukraine covers tuition and basic academic support, it does not include:

  • Recognition of foreign educational documents (nostrification)
  • Annual medical insurance
  • Visa fees and travel expenses
  • Issuance of a temporary residence permit

These costs are paid by the student independently.

Language of Study for Scholarship Students

All students who study in Ukraine on scholarship are enrolled in Ukrainian-language programs.

Before beginning the main degree program, students complete a preparatory year of Ukrainian language study at the host university. This preparatory education is free of charge; however, a monthly allowance is not provided during this period.

Academic Year in Ukraine

The academic year for students who study in Ukraine lasts from:

September 1 to June 30

How to Get a Scholarship to Study in Ukraine

The process of obtaining a scholarship to study in Ukraine is conducted through the applicant’s home country authorities, not directly through Ukrainian universities.

Key Timeline:

  • By December 15 – Ukraine informs partner countries about available scholarships, requirements, and documents
  • By March 31 – Partner countries submit selected candidates
  • By August 1 – Final decisions are announced by the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine

Step-by-Step Guide: Study in Ukraine on Scholarship

  • Contact your national education authority

Learn about opportunities to study in Ukraine on scholarship, application deadlines, and selection criteria.

  • Prepare your documents

To apply for a scholarship to study in Ukraine, you will need:

  • Passport copy
  • Diploma or certificate with academic transcripts
  • Motivation letter
  • Research proposal (for PhD candidates)
  • Completed application form
    All documents must be translated into Ukrainian.

  • Submit your application

Submit your documents to the authorized institution in your country within the official deadline.

  • Pass the competitive selection

Candidates are selected through a national competitive process.

  • Receive nomination

Successful applicants are nominated to the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine.

  • Await for the final decision by Ukraine

The Ministry reviews applications and awards the scholarship to study in Ukraine.

  • Receive confirmation and invitation

Approved candidates receive:

  • Official scholarship confirmation
  • Study invitation from a Ukrainian university

  • Legalize educational documents

Certify your academic documents according to national legislation.

  • Apply for a student visa

Contact the Ukrainian Embassy to apply for a student visa.

  • Inform your university and arrive in Ukraine

Coordinate arrival dates or agree on starting online learning with your university.

  • Final formalities

Further procedures (residence permit, dormitory accommodation, document recognition) are handled directly with the university.
