Corporate Scholarships

Αt Alba we are continually striving to support education and personal development and remain committed to helping executives through training, in order to develop themselves personally and professionally.

Since 2011, we have offered employees of Greek companies and their children numerous Corporate Scholarships for post-graduate studies. During the 14 years since the Program’s launch, companies from shipping to banking and pharmaceutical, to energy industries have come onboard and we look forward to welcoming more! 

It’s worth mentioning that Alba provides scholarships annually, with Corporate Scholarships being the most significant.

For the academic year 2026-2027, the postgraduate programs, which participate in the Corporate Scholarships’ scheme, are the following:

  • The Alba MBA (part-time mode)
  • MBA with Shipping (part-time mode)
  • MSc in Management
  • MSc in Finance
  • MSc in Shipping Management
  • MSc in Marketing
  • MSc in Strategic Human Resource Management

What we offer through the Corporate Scholarships is:

  • A 20% Corporate Scholarship on the tuition fees, if two (2) employees/children of your employees/combination of the two register in our various programs
  • A 30% Corporate Scholarship on the tuition fees, if three (3)  or more employees/children of your employees/combination of the two register in our various programs

If you want to learn more on the Alba Corporate Scholarships scheme, please download the full announcement (in Greek).
