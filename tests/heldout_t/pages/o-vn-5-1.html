Overview

This year, there are 21 scholarships on offer to currently enrolled RMIT Vietnam undergraduate students who have achieved outstanding academic results.

The scholarships are awarded semesterly in Semester 1, 2 and 3 of 2027, as follows:

School

Number of scholarships and value per semester
The Business School 01 x 50% and 02 x 25% of the remaining program tuition fees
School of Communication & Design 01 x 50% and 01 x 25% of the remaining program tuition fees
School of Science, Engineering & Technology 01 x 50% and 01 x 25% of the remaining program tuition fees
Note

RMIT Vietnam reserves the right to adjust the values and allocation of scholarships at its discretion.

Eligibility

To be considered for this scholarship, the applicant must:

  • Be an RMIT Vietnam student currently enrolled in a Bachelor’s program;
    •  Students who already receive a 50% scholarship are not eligible to apply for this scholarship.
  • Have completed at least 96 credit points;
    • Study from non-award programs or from universities other than RMIT Vietnam is not counted.
  • Have achieved a cumulative GPA of 3.4 (on a 4.0 scale) or above.

Eligibility will be assessed based on available information as at the application deadline, therefore may not take into account results from the semester in which the application is made.

Selection criteria

  • Applicants are assessed and ranked by cumulative GPA and credit points of RMIT Vietnam Higher Education study following the eligibility criteria.
  • Where applicants have the same cumulative GPA, preference will be given to the applicant with the higher credit points. Where applicants have the same GPA and credit points earned, their academic performance of the latest semester will be considered.
  • Available scholarships in each respective semester are awarded in order of rank, starting with 50% scholarships, followed by 25% scholarships.

Click the “Apply online” below and submit the online Scholarship Application Form prior to the scholarship deadline:

Note

  • Before applying for the scholarship, we recommend applicants to read the Scholarship Terms and Conditions (PDF, 768kb, 6p).
  • The scholarship application process is highly competitive with a limited number of scholarships available. Unfortunately, not all eligible applicants will receive one.
