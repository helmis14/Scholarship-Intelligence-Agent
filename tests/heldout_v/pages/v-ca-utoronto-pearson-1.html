Scholarships Admission Awards

Admission awards are scholarships for students starting their first year of undergraduate studies. These awards recognize your academic achievement and are available to students from across Canada and around the world.

Our admission awards recognize academic achievement and are available to students from across Canada and around the world. You'll automatically be considered for a variety of entrance scholarships when you apply to U of T. No separate application is required for most admission awards.

Some admission awards are available to Canadian students based on financial need, Indigenous identity, first-generation student status, leadership, community involvement or other criteria. These awards require a separate application through the Awards Profile.

Automatic consideration

Admission awards are based on academic performance and are automatically assessed as part of the admissions process.

Who's eligible?

High school students entering their first year of undergraduate studies at U of T, including international applicants.

Spotlight awards

Here is a sample of some of our admission awards that your application will automatically be considered for:

President's Scholars of Excellence Program

Approximately 150 of the most highly qualified students applying to first year of direct-entry, undergraduate studies will be distinguished as President's Scholars of Excellence (PSEP). Outstanding domestic and international secondary school students are considered automatically for PSEP upon admission.

Learn more

U of T Scholars Program

Outstanding domestic and international secondary school students are considered automatically for these awards on the basis of their average at the time of admission. In some cases a U of T Scholars award is tenable in conjunction with other admission awards that students may receive from their campus, faculty or college. 

Learn more

U of T Mississauga Automatic Entrance Scholarship

U of T Mississauga offers three levels of automatic entrance scholarships to all domestic and international students upon admission to their campus with the admitted averages starting at 90.0%.

Learn more

U of T Scarborough Automatic Entrance Scholarship

Domestic and international students applying from secondary school who are admitted to U of T Scarborough will automatically be considered for entrance scholarships.

Learn more

Frequently asked questions

  • If you are selected for an admission award, you'll typically be notified about the award when you receive your offer of admission. Details will be in your offer letter and in your applicant portal.

  • Yes, it's possible to receive multiple awards. However, eligibility and combinations depend on the specific award terms.

     

  • Some awards are one-time entrance scholarships, while others are renewable for multiple years. Check the specific award terms in your offer letter and on your applicant portal.
