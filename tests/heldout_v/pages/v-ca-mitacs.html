Mitacs Model

A simple but powerful approach

Mitacs supports business–academic research collaboration through paid placements for undergraduate to graduate students and postdoctoral fellows.

We work closely with post-secondary institutions to match students with partnering Canadian enterprises who co-fund the placements. This includes private sector businesses, not-for-profits, municipalities, and health-sector organizations.

The Mitacs Difference

Building Canada’s competitive advantage in ways no other organization can

Mitacs powers what’s next by:

  • Unlocking access to deep, cross-disciplinary talent and R&D capacity through an unmatched network of universities and colleges

  • Meeting Canada’s businesses where they are and equipping them for what’s next

  • Moving ideas from lab to market at the speed of today

  • Supporting job creation and readying students and postdoctoral researchers with industry experience and connections to professional networks

  • Bringing Canada to the world through international partnerships, global networks, and talent attraction
