Graduate Sponsorship Research Award (GSRA)

Graduate Sponsorship Research Award (GSRA) Turn Your Capstone Project into Graduate Success with UDST!

The GSRA empowers you to transform your bachelor’s capstone project into a stepping stone toward graduate excellence. Join graduate programs at UDST and seize this exclusive scholarship opportunity. This prestigious initiative nurtures future faculty and researchers through two tracks: Local (study within Qatar) and International (study abroad for Qatari nationals and applicants with Qatari mothers).

Who Can Apply

  • Under 40 years old at the submission deadline.
  • Employed in Qatar.
  • Accepted into or applying for full-time graduate programs starting the next academic year.

Funding and Benefits

GSRA offers comprehensive support:

  • Master’s degree: Up to 2 years; Ph.D.: Up to 4 years.
  • Tuition fees: Fully covered.
  • Conference allowance: Up to QAR 18,000/year.
  • Health insurance and settlement allowance for international awardees.
  • Annual air ticket for international awardees.

Key Dates

  • Deadline to submit application: 21 April 2025, noon (Doha Time).
  • Research Office Vetting Deadline: 28 April 2025, noon (Doha Time).
  • Announcement: July 2025.

For more information, contact Applied Research and Graduate Studies office: +974 44952618 or Email Abitha John: abitha.john@udst.edu.qa
