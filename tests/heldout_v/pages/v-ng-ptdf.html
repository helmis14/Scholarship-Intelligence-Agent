UGSET - General Shehu Musa Yar'Adua University of Geological Sciences and Engineering Technology

A premier tertiary institution established by the Petroleum Technology Development Fund (PTDF) to enhance indigenous human capacity for the oil and gas industry. Following Federal Executive Council’s approval, physical developments commenced in 2009 with the award of various contracts under Building, Civil, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering Services.

Through strategic initiatives, PTDF has revolutionized Nigeria's oil and gas industry, significantly enhancing productivity and fostering a culture of continuous improvement and innovation.

Our Mandate

Building Capacities and Capabilities in Nigeria’s Oil and Gas Industry through the development of Human Capacities, Institutional Capacity Development as well as the promotion of Research and Acquisition of relevant technologies.

Our Mission

To train Nigerians to qualify as graduates, professionals, technicians and craftsmen in the field of engineering, geology, science and management in the oil and gas industry in Nigeria and abroad.

Our Vision

To serve not only as a vessel for the development of indigenous manpower and technology transfer/acquisition in the oil and gas industry but to make Nigeria a human resource center for the West African sub-region and the Gulf of Guinea.

Our Core Values

Our Core Values are Self-less service, Integrity & Transparency, Commitment & Team Spirit, Staff Learning & Development and Respect for the Individual

Unlocking the Future of Nigeria’s Oil & Gas Industry, Empowering Tomorrow’s Experts Today!

The Petroleum Technology Development Fund (PTDF) is dedicated to enhancing local manpower and technology in Nigeria's oil and gas industry. Our key initiatives include offering scholarships for advanced studies, funding research and development, and fostering collaboration between academia and industry.
