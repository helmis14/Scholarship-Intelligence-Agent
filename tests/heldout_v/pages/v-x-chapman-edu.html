»Financing for International Students

Stay in TouchVisitApply

This page provides undergraduate cost and financial information specifically for international students. This should help you: 

  • Understand the cost of attending Chapman as an international student.
  • Learn more about financing options for international applicants.
  • Plan for future years at Chapman.

Estimated cost of attendance 

Chapman University is a need-aware institution. The total annual cost is the minimum amount of financial verification required for Chapman University to issue an I-20 or DS-2019 for the upcoming academic year.

$67,874 - Tuition and fees
$18,438 - On-campus room and board
$2,321 - Health Insurance (mandatory)
$5,600 - Books, supplies, and personal expenses
$94,233 - Estimated total cost

All international students studying in the U.S. are required to have health insurance. You can learn more on our Student Health Insurance page. You can view our undergraduate Cost of Attendance page for a complete 2025-2026 academic year breakdown. All rates are subject to change.

If you plan to bring family to the United States, you must verify additional funds for their expenses. The estimated expenses for dependents are $5,000 for the first dependent and $2,500 for each additional dependent per academic year.

Scholarships for international students

International students are eligible for merit and need-based scholarships. Merit-based scholarships can be as high as $42,000 per academic year for first-year students and up to $15,000 per academic year for transfer students.

  • There is no additional application for scholarship consideration. All admitted students are automatically reviewed for merit awards. Merit scholarships are based primarily on a student’s high school academic achievements and overall strength of the application. Merit-based scholarships are awarded at the discretion of the Admission Committee. Additional scholarships are available through individual departments.
  • Chapman offers limited need-based aid of up to $15,000 per year for international applicants. To be considered for need-based aid, you must submit the FAFSA (if eligible) and the CSS Profile.
  • U.S. citizens and Permanent Residents living or studying outside the U.S. are eligible and encouraged to apply for the  FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid).
  • We encourage international students to search for outside scholarships. You can contact the EducationUSA Advising Center nearest you and/or conduct a search online (keep in mind that some scholarships require U.S. Citizenship).

How we can help

We are happy to help as you consider Chapman and work on your application! If you have any questions or want to learn more about the university, you can:

  • Email the Office of Admission: admission@chapman.edu
  • Visit campus: If you will be in Southern California, we encourage you to schedule an in-person tour of Chapman!
  • Visit virtually: We also offer many virtual opportunities to connect, including tours and information sessions.
