College of Education Scholarships

The College of Education awards scholarships at both the departmental and college levels each year. Eligibility for individual awards is determined by the College of Education based on a student’s fall semester classification. All information submitted will be verified, and only complete applications will be considered.

Applications will be accepted through September 19th, 2026 in Scholarship Universe.

Eligibility

Juniors, seniors and graduate students officially admitted to the College of Education and enrolled in coursework are eligible to apply for College of Education scholarships. Undergraduate teacher preparation majors must have passed all sections of the FTCE – General Knowledge test in order to apply.

Please note that some awards require applicants to demonstrate financial need. If you wish to apply for any of these scholarships, you must have a current FAFSA on file with the Office of Student Financial Aid.

Email Us: coescholarships@fau.edu

We thank our donors for their continuing support.
