2026 Beijing Government Scholarship Applications Are Now Open

for Current International Students of Peking University

 

This notice is to announce the opening of 2026 Beijing Government Scholarship Applications for Peking University current international students. Eligible applicants are welcome to submit applications within the specified timeline.

 

I. Eligibility:

1. The applicant should be full-time foreign undergraduate, master's and doctoral student of the second year or above from Peking University;

2. The applicant has not received any other scholarship from the Chinese government, social organization or Peking University (with the exception of one-time non-renewable merit awards and Beijing Government Scholarship Type C);

3. The applicant should be friendly towards China, and abide by Chinese laws and regulations as well as school rules and regulations;

4. Undergraduate students shall not exceed 25 years old at the time of enrollment; master's degree students shall not exceed 35 years old at the time of enrollment; doctoral students shall not exceed 40 years old at the time of enrollment;

5.Only for programs with regular tuition fees.

 

II.Scope of the Scholarship

1. Type A: full tuition waiver, housing, living stipend, and medical insurance.

2. Type B: full tuition waiver, housing, and medical insurance.

3. Type C: full tuition waiver and medical insurance.

 

III. Scholarship Duration

The academic year 2026-2027

 

IV. Application Time

Open now until Beijing time at 9:00 a.m. on September 11th. Late submissions will not be accepted.

 

V. Application Procedure

Log onto Peking University International Student Service System (http://www.studyatpku.com), select Home - Scholarship Application - Application for Current Students, fill in personal information, upload the official transcript at PKU and other supporting documents, and submit the application.

 

VI. Note

  1. Applicants must ensure that the submitted application materials are truthful and valid. Any false material will lead to disqualification for the scholarship.
  2. The final scholarship award decision is expected to be announced by email in October 2026.
  3. The official transcript at PKU can be obtained by searching for "Electronic Proof Materials Application" on the portal. Providing unofficial transcripts such as screenshots will affect the scholarship result.
  4. Students who have received Beijing Government Scholarship Type C and are currently in the second year or above can apply to upgrade to the Type B or Type A scholarship.
