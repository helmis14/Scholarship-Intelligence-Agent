The mission of Undergraduate and Prestigious Scholarships is to provide prospective and enrolled students with information and access to internal and external financial resources, as well as to steward the campus's philanthropic undergraduate student support funding while increasing the visibility and reputation of the university. Undergraduate and Prestigious Scholarships believes in the critical need for student financial support and in the university's guiding principle of providing equitable access to higher education for the residents of California and beyond.
