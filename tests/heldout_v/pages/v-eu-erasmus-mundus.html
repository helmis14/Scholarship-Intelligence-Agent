Erasmus Mundus Catalogue

Master’s opportunities for all

Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters

Erasmus Mundus Joint Master programmes are delivered by multiple higher education institutions across various countries. They are renowned for their academic excellence and strong integration. Master's level students from all over the world can apply. Additionally, scholarships are available for outstanding students. 

Read more to find out if an Erasmus Mundus Joint Master is the right fit for you. Then, explore the Erasmus Mundus Catalogue, which is updated annually and lists the master’s programmes currently supported by the European Union. While many programs offer Erasmus Mundus scholarships, some do not, as they may be reaching the end of their funding period or have been granted a temporary use of the Erasmus Mundus name after funding. 

Most master’s programmes require applications to be submitted between October and January for courses commencing the following academic year. Interested students should visit the program's official website and directly contact the consortium for information on courses, application procedures, and scholarship availability. Project overview links will instead direct you to the Erasmus+ project results platform, which showcases the details and outcomes of projects funded under Erasmus+.
