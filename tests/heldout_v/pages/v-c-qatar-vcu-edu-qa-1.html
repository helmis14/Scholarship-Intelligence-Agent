Undergraduate Merit Scholarship

  • Overview
  • Important Dates
  • Eligibility Requirements
  • What the Scholarship Includes
  • FAQs
  • Additional Questions

VCUarts Qatar is committed to supporting exceptional students who join our University. 

The VCUarts Qatar merit scholarship is designed to attract and recognize outstanding students who demonstrate exceptional academic and creative potential. This merit-based scholarship evaluates students based on a holistic view of both their academic achievements and creative excellence.

All applicants to our undergraduate programs (BA and BFA) will be automatically considered for the merit scholarship upon submission of their application. 

Important dates

Applications open September 15

Applications to our undergraduate programs open September 15.

Applications close January 15

Since there is no separate application process for the scholarship, the deadline is the standard VCUarts Qatar admissions deadline.

Eligibility requirements

Applicants who will be considered for the merit scholarship will have an essay (for BA applicants) or a portfolio (for BFA applicants) that demonstrates excellence in arts and design.

Considered applicants will also meet the following academic requirements:

  • GPA: 3.30 minimum (on a 4.0 scale)
  • English proficiency: A minimum TOEFL (iBT 4.5+ if taken on or after January 21, 2026; 94+ if taken prior to January 21, 2026), IELTS (7.0+) score or Duolingo (130-135).

Only incoming students are eligible to be considered for the merit scholarship.

What the scholarship includes

Recipients of the merit scholarship will be given up to $40,000/year, which can cover annual tuition as well as on-campus student housing. 

Scholarship funds will be directly applied to tuition fees (up to $37,642/year), and if provided, the remaining amount will also be directly applied to Qatar Foundation student housing costs (which range from $2,774 to $7,604/year, depending on the room type).
