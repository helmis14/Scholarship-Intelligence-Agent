Exchange Programs
Study abroad at one of CSULB’s university partners for a semester or year.

Semester Programs
Study abroad with a CSULB professor in Florence, Japan, London, South Korea, or Spain for a semester.

Short-Term Programs
Study abroad on a CSULB faculty-led Summer or Winter course.

CSU International Programs
Study abroad through one of the CSU IP systemwide programs for a year or semester.

Independent Programs
Study or intern abroad through an affiliate partner of CSULB for a Semester or Summer.

Search using our Advanced Search feature for more options based on parameters like major, location, program type, or GPA requirements.

After determining which program you have a preference for. Open the link to the brochure.

Image

You will then be able to view the brochure and apply from there for the program of your liking. Make sure to check GPA, Major, and Languague Requirements in the Academics Tab. Deadlines are also posted.

Image

Once starting your application you will be eligible to request an appointment via email at studyabroad@csulb.edu.

  • The email must include:
    • your major
    • the country or region where you hope to study abroad
    • the semester or year when you plan to go abroad.
  • A Study Abroad team member will reply with instructions on scheduling the advising appointment.

Meet with your major/minor Academic Advisor to discuss CSULB course equivalencies for the program you have selected and complete the online application.

Application Deadlines

  • March 5, 2027, for CSULB Fall 2027 semester & academic year programs
  • October 2, 2026, for CSULB Spring 2027 semester & calendar year programs (except Japan, Spain, and Denmark Programs are September 1, 2026)

See individual Study Abroad program deadlines for CSU IP, Summer, Winter, and independent program deadlines.
