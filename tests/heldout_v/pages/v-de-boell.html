Tailwind for Talents: Scholarships for Graduates and PhD students

In our effort to foster equal opportunities and equitable access to education, we support graduate students and PhD students from DAC countries who intend to pursue a Master's degree or PhD in Germany.

Contact

Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship Department
Schumannstraße 8, 10117 Berlin, Germany
T +49 (0) 30 285 34-400
F +49 (0) 30 285 34-409

Telephone office hours:

Funded by the Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF) and the Federal Foreign Office (AA).

Info flyer scholarship

Our next application deadlines:

Spring 2027: 15 January 2027 until 1 March 2027 
Fall 2027: 15 July 2027 until 1 September 2027 

The online application portal opens on January 15, 2027, and closes on March 1, 2027.

Who can apply?

Students: 
International students who have completed their first degree abroad can apply prior to starting a Master's degree program in Germany. 
Priority is given to applicants from DAC countries who have not yet taken up residence in Germany at the time of their application. 

PhD students: 
International PhD students who have completed their graduate degree abroad may apply for a PhD program in Germany. 
Priority is given to international applicants from DAC countries who have not yet taken up residence in Germany at the time of their application.
