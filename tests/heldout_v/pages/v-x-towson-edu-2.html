Financial Aid

Federal financial aid (grants and loans) can be applied toward TU exchanges, TU programs, and approved non-TU programs but there may be some restrictions depending on the type of aid you receive. Students can request additional loans to help cover study abroad/away costs by emailing a Study Abroad/Away Budget to the TU Financial Aid Office.  Questions about financial aid for study abroad or away? Email the TU Financial Aid Office. 

IMPORTANT: Tuition remission can only be applied to TU Exchanges.

  • Study Abroad/Away Aid Requirements and Procedures

Scholarships

Each year, Towson University students receive scholarships for study abroad and away experiences from TU and external organizations and providers. Reach out to our office or refer to the organization or provider’s website for more information on the scholarships below. 

TU Study Abroad and Away Scholarships

Open to TU full-time undergraduate and graduate degree-seeking students participating in an approved study abroad program or exchange, TU study away (domestic) faculty-led program, or the National Student Exchange. Recipients are required to participate in a study abroad/away related service activity or project during or after their experience. Fall, spring, Spring Break, Minimester, summer, year.

Deadlines: March 15 (summer, fall, and academic year); October 15 (Minimester, spring, Spring Break, calendar year). Students applying early for study abroad may also apply for the scholarship in the prior cycle.

How To Apply:

  1. Review the the TU Study Abroad & Away Scholarship information in the Portal
  2. Click the "Apply Now" button
  3. Select the term that you're applying for
  4. Complete and submit the application
  5. Questions? Email: studyabroad AT_TOWSON

TU Study Abroad & Away scholarships include the TU Institutional Study Abroad Scholarship, Angela Gerwig Harold Memorial Endowed Scholarship, COFAC Study Abroad & Away Scholarship, Dr. Armin and Marlene Mruck Scholarship (CLA Student Exchange Program), Dr. John M. Govern Memorial Scholarship, Frank J. Sanders Scholarship, Sae Joo Chang Endowment for International Scholars and Program Development Scholarship, TU in Italy American University of Rome Scholarship, and the TU in Italy Lorenzo de Medici Scholarship.

TU Angela Gerwig Harold Memorial Endowed Scholarship

The Angela Gerwig Harold Memorial Endowed Scholarship is open to full-time, undergraduate degree-seeking students majoring in a Foreign Language who are studying abroad. Fall, spring, Spring Break, Minimester, summer, year. 

TU Honors College Study Abroad Scholarship

The TU Honors College Study Abroad Scholarship is open to all TU full-time undergraduate degree-seeking members of the TU Honors College who are studying abroad. Fall, spring, Spring Break, Minimester, summer, year. Students must submit an Honors College Study Abroad Scholarship Application Form in order to be considered. 

Deadlines: April 1 (summer, fall, and academic year); November 1 (Minimester, spring, Spring Break, calendar year). 

Non-TU Scholarships

benjaMIN A. Gilman INTERNATIONAL Scholarship

Gilman Scholarships are open to full-time undergraduate degree-seeking students who are Pell Grant recipients. Fall, spring, Spring Break, Minimester, summer, year. See website for deadlines.

Boren Fellowships 

Boren Fellowships are open to U.S. graduate students studying less commonly taught languages in world regions critical to U.S. interests. Students studying abroad in Western Europe, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand are not eligible to apply. Boren Fellowships are awarded for experiences abroad ranging from 12 weeks to 12 months. 

Boren Scholarships 

Boren Scholarships are open to U.S. full-time undergraduate degree-seeking students studying in world regions critical to U.S. interests and/or studying less commonly taught languages. Students studying abroad in Western Europe, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand are not eligible to apply. 12 weeks minimum program lenght with preference for two semesters (minimum 8 weeks for STEM majors). Fall, spring, summer, year. See website for deadlines.

Bridging Scholarships 

Bridging Scholarships are open to U.S. full-time undergraduate degree-seeking students studying abroad in Japan. Minimum 3 months. Fall, spring, year. See website for deadlines.

BUTEX Scholarship

BUTEX (British Universities Transatlantic Exchange Association) Scholarships are open to U.S. full-time undergraduate degree-seeking students studying abroad at a BUTEX member university in the UK. Fall, spring, year. See website for deadlines.

Critical Language Scholarship Program 

The Critical Language Scholarship (CLS) Program offers fully funded intensive summer language institutes overseas in critical need foreign languages for U.S. citizen undergraduate, Master's and Ph.D students. Summer. See website for deadlines.

DAAD Awards 

DAAD grants are open to U.S. full-time undergraduate and graduate degree-seeking students for study or research in Germany. See website for deadlines.

DIVERSE INTERNATIONAL WOMEN OF COLOR STUDY ABROAD SCHOLARSHIP 

The Diverse International Women of Color Study Abroad Scholarship is intended to assist women of color who are exceptional in their studies and in need of financial assistance to study abroad. Fall, spring, summer, year. See website for deadlines.

Freeman-ASIA Scholarship

Freeman-ASIA Scholarships are open to U.S. full-time undergraduate degree-seeking students with demonstrated financial need for study abroad in East or Southeast Asia. Program must include intensive language study. Fall, spring, summer, year (minimum 8 weeks for summer, 12 weeks for semester). Internship based programs are not eligible for award. See website for deadlines.

Fund for Education Abroad 

Fund for Education Abroad Scholarships support undergraduate degree-seeking students who are underrepresented among the U.S. study abroad population. No previous study abroad experience. Fall, spring, Spring Break, Minimester, summer, year (minimum 14 days). See website for deadlines.

John S. McCain International Scholarship for Military Families

The Gilman-McCain Scholarship is open to U.S. undergraduate degree-seeking students who are child or spousal dependents of active or activated United States military personnel. Applicants must be recipients of any type of Title IV federal financial aid during the time of application or provide proof that they will be receiving Title IV federal financial aid during their study abroad program or internship. Fall, spring, Spring Break, Minimester, summer, year. See website for deadlines.

JOY worldwide study abroad scholarship

The Joy Worldwide Study Abroad Scholarship is open to African American undergraduate degree-seeking students. Fall, spring, summer, year (minimum 3 months). See website for deadlines.

National Italian American Foundation

National Italian American Foundation Awards are open to U.S. undergraduate degree-seeking Italian American students, and students from any ethnic background majoring or minoring in Italian language, Italian studies, Italian American studies or a related field. Fall, spring, Spring Break, Minimester, summer, year. See website for deadlines.

National Society of Collegiate Scholars

The National Society of Collegiate Scholars offers multiple types of scholarships to full-time undegraduate and graduate degree seeking student NSCS members. Eligibility requirements vary. See website for deadlines.

Oldenburg Contact Grants

Oldenburg Contact Grants are open to full-time undergraduate and graduate degree-seeking students who are Maryland residents studying abroad at Carl Von Ossietsky University in Oldenburg, Germany. €200 per month stipend. To apply, submit the following to the TU Study Abroad & Away Office: one page essay describing your degree program including your major and courses taken; official transcript, a letter from your German language professor describing your language ability; a letter of recommendation from a professor. Spring, fall, year. Application deadlines: October 15 for spring, March 15 for fall.

Turkish Coalition of America SCHOLARSHIPS

The Turkish Coalition of American Scholarships are open to full-time undergraduate or graduate student degree-seeking students for study abroad in Turkey, the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus, or Bosnia and Herzegovina. Fall, spring, Spring Break, Minimester, summer, year. See website for deadlines.

U.S. Japan Council Toshizo Watanabe Study Abroad Scholarship

The Toshizo Watanabe Study Abroad Scholarships are open to undergraduate and graduate degree-seeking students. Fall, spring, Spring Break, Minimester, summer, year. See website for deadlines.
