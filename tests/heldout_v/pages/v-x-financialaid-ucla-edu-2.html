Department Awards

This page includes resources for units making financial aid awards to UCLA students.

Welcome UCLA professional staff members! We look forward to assisting you so that you can assist our students. On this page, you will be able to find information on processing departmental awards. Please reference the UCLA Payment Guide for Student Awards to find answers to frequently asked questions about awarding.

If you will be making awards for your department, you will need to become a member of the UCLA Strategic Scholarship Stakeholders listserv, by contacting our scholarships unit at UCLAscholarships@fas.ucla.edu. We meet several times each year to discuss UCLA policies on awarding, to provide awarding materials, emails with deadline reminders, etc. so you can help UCLA meet its goals to recruit and retain our students throughout their academic careers.

Below is a brief summary of the multiple awarding methods described in our Payment Guide, above. These are based on the award recipient’s status as a matriculated or Non-matriculated UCLA student.

Forms:

AID ID Request for New Scholarship (Revised April 2026)

Award Submission for Prospective Undergraduate Students

Departments should use the Bruin Financial Aid Letter (BFAL) Upload Form below to award scholarships to admitted students that will be included on the BFAL.

Email the form to Financial Aid and Scholarships (FAS) at UCLAscholarships@fas.ucla.edu and our office will award each student and the system will generate a revised BFAL within 1-2 business days.

(Note: Blackbaud Award Management (BAM) cannot currently be used for BFALs.)

  • UCLA BFAL Scholarship Award Form (Awards for prospective students)

Award Submission for Matriculated Undergraduate Students

Please submit your awarding requests via the Departmental Scholarship Awarding form. In order to access the form please use your @ucla.edu email to complete the SSO login process.

All UCLA Award applications should be collected and reviewed in the UCLA Financial Aid and Scholarships Registered Student Application

This application, also known as Blackbaud Award Management (BAM), allows our students to find the maximum number of potential awards, while completing fewer external applications spread across various UCLA websites. Any external applications should be moved to BAM as soon as possible.

The Blackbaud Award Management (BAM) portal allows UCLA to collect scholarship applications, review them securely, make award offers, and/or collect post-acceptance responses and thank you notes to create donor reports.

All Undergraduate awards will be submitted within BAM, even if sent via upload form.

Each school is limited to a maximum of two administrators, which all schools currently have. Staff are required to complete training on the BAM system as well as UCLA awarding policies, before they are given access.

Note: Currently enrolled students in any UCLA program may not be administrators.

Award Submission for Matriculated Graduate and Professional Students

UCLA Go Grad is the administrative portal for the UCLA Division of Graduate Education at UCLA.

In order to have access to this service, you must have a UCLA Logon ID. You can create your UCLA Logon ID at https://accounts.iam.ucla.edu/. Accounts require a departmental authorization and Graduate Division approval. For additional information on awarding graduate and professional students, please contact the Office of Fellowships & Financial Services at askgrad@grad.ucla.edu.

Award Submission for Non-Matriculated UCLA Program Participants

Awards for Non-Matriculated UCLA Program Participants are processed by Accounts Payable or UCPath

NON-MATRICULATED UCLA PROGRAM PARTICIPANT AWARDS ARE NOT ELIGIBLE TO BE PROCESSED THROUGH BLACKBAUD AWARD MANAGEMENT (BAM) AND WILL NOT BE PROCESSED BY THE FINANCIAL AID & SCHOLARSHIPS OFFICE

Effective with the 2024-2025 awarding year, the Financial Aid and Scholarships division will no longer process payments for visiting non-matriculated students. This includes, but is not limited to Scholarships, Fellowships, Prizes, and Service awards. To pay UCLA visitors, please visit the BruinBuy Plus training information at https://purchasing.ucla.edu/bruinbuy-plus-hub. There is also a Quick Reference Guide which can be accessed at https://ucla.app.box.com/s/iuz1tb09ngrodcwd7cyaqv3ivw8nexoz.
