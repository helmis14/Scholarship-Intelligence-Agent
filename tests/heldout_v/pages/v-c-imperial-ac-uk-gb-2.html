The Aziz Foundation: Imperial Environmental Technology or Conservation Science Scholarship

Scholarship overview

  • Degree level

    Postgraduate taught, Master's research

  • Value

    Full Home tuition fee scholarship

  • Number of awards

    1

  • Academic year

    2026/2027

  • Tuition fee status

    Home

  • Mode of study

    Full time

  • Available to

    Prospective students

  • Application deadline

    01/05/2026 Closed

  • Additional information

    Scholarship page

    enquiries.env@imperial.ac.uk

  • Available to applicants in the following departments

    • Centre for Environmental Policy

Eligibility criteria

Applicants must:

  • have submitted a complete application to the MSc Environmental Technology or Conservation Science programme

  • be active in their local and/or faith community and show a desire to develop intellectual skills in a multi-faith or secular environment

  • be able to demonstrate a long-term commitment to community or societal development and good relations through public leadership

  • be Zakat eligible, in financial need, and unable to cover the cost of tuition fees

  • have an excellent understanding of the field and how to progress within it, with relevant prior experience and/or achievements

  • have knowledge of how the programme will enable them to generate social impact

  • be a UK national and eligible for Home fee status at the University

  • commit to joining and engaging with the Foundation’s alumni support programme and to paying forward through widening participation activity at their university, volunteering, or acting as a mentor

  • submit a statement of no more than 1,000 words setting out how they meet the criteria above, including how they hope to use the scholarship in their future work

Please note: This scholarship is available to new entrants only. Students already studying the course are not eligible.

Course specific information

This scholarship is available for students applying to the MSc Environmental Technology or MSc Conservation Science programme only.

Application process

To apply for this scholarship, you must first submit a complete application to the MSc Environmental Technology or MSc Conservation Science programme.

 

You must then submit a statement of no more than 1,000 words setting out how you meet the scholarship criteria and how you hope to use the scholarship in your future work.

 

Please submit your statement to enquiries.env@imperial.ac.uk by 01/05/2026. Remember to include your full name and CID in your email.

Additional information

One scholarship is available to provide a unique opportunity for an exceptional Muslim student active in British Muslim communities and keen to build bridges with all parts of British society.

The successful student will have the commitment and intelligence to support and represent Muslim communities in wider society, as well as the credibility to bring and explain new learning experiences back to the community. In doing so, they will help create mutual understanding between Muslims and others and contribute to a more cohesive society.

Contact

If you have any additional questions, please contact us at enquiries.env@imperial.ac.uk.
