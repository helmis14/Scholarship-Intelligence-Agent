Scholarship pathways

Designed for merit, need, access, and national development.

Each programme area gives applicants a clearer route to support, whether studying in Ghana, pursuing strategic training abroad, or applying through district-level channels.

National Merit Scholarships

Recognition for high-performing students with strong academic records, leadership potential, and the drive to contribute to Ghana's future.

Local Tertiary Support

Funding support for qualified students in accredited Ghanaian institutions, helping reduce financial barriers to higher education.

Foreign Scholarships

Bilateral and international study opportunities in priority disciplines that strengthen Ghana's human capital.

District Access

Decentralized interviews and validation that bring scholarship access closer to communities across the country.

About the Authority

Public service with a sharper standard of access.

The Ghana Scholarships Authority operates with a mandate to award scholarships to brilliant but needy Ghanaians, strengthen tertiary participation, and support skills development for national progress.

The work combines national policy, district validation, institutional confirmation, and transparent application processes so deserving students can move with confidence.

Legal mandate and institutional network

Coordinating public scholarship support under one transparent standard.

Scholarship delivery depends on clear regulation, verified institutions, public coordination, and local validation across Ghana.

Ghana Scholarships Authority Act, 2025

A single public mandate for transparent scholarship administration.

An Act to establish the Ghana Scholarships Authority to regulate the administration and award of Government scholarships in a transparent and equitable manner and for related matters.

Application journey

Four steps from interest to consideration.

Applicants should use only the official application portal and follow the published requirements for their scholarship category.

1

Create your account

Register on the official application portal with accurate personal information.

2

Complete your profile

Fill the application form and upload required academic documents.

3

Take assessment

Complete the online aptitude test when required for your scholarship category.

4

Attend interview

Appear before the panel in your selected district or announced selection channel.

Use only official channels.

Apply through the verified portal and ignore agents, unofficial payment requests, or social media accounts claiming guaranteed scholarship placement.

Prepare before applying

Keep your documents ready before you begin.

  • Admission letter or proof of enrolment
  • Academic transcripts or results
  • Ghana Card or valid national identification
  • Passport photograph and contact details
  • Proof of need where the category requires it
  • Programme-specific documents listed in the notice
