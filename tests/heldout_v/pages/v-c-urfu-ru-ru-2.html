About the Russian Scholarship Project

Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project is your chance for an early admission to a tuition-free program at one of the leading universities in Russia!

Who can participate:

  • Foreign citizens;
  • Stateless persons;
  • Compatriots living abroad.

Format of the competition: online at all stages.

Essential requirements for each track:

  • For Bachelor's track – a completed general secondary education;
  • For Master's track – a higher education degree (at least a Bachelor's);
  • For PhD track – a higher education degree (at least a Master's or a Specialist's);
  • Participants of the postdoctoral track should have a PhD or a candidate/doctor of science degree.

Registration period: August 20 - November 1, 2026.

Stages of Open Doors Competition

Stages of Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project Competition

August, 20

Registration on the website https://od.globaluni.ru.

August 20 – November 1

Registration and portfolio submission

November 1

Deadline for registration and portfolio submission

Announcement of the first stage results

November 13 – December 21

The second stage: completion of tasks for bachelor's, master's, doctoral (PhD) and post-doctoral tracks of the competition

November 16

The second stage registration deadline

December 21

Announcement of the second stage results

December 22 – February 26

The third stage (ONLY for participants of the doctoral (PhD) and post-doctoral tracks)

January 10

The third stage registration deadline

February 26

Announcement of the third stage results (winners of doctoral (PhD) and post-doctoral tracks)

Winners & Runners-up

Participants who get a sufficient number of points after 2 stages and are among 25% of the best, fall into the list of winners and runners-up of the Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project.

The winners of the Open Doors have the right to tuition-free education.

Runners-up of the Open Doors have the right to tuition-free education, if there are winners who have not submitted necessary documents or refused to study at the 2nd stage.

Winners and runners-up can choose a program in English or Russian available for foreign citizens in the area corresponding to the profile of the Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project.

Privileges for Participants of Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project Final Stage

Ural Federal University provides participants of the final stage of the Open Doors Russian Scholarship Project with the opportunity to receive a 25% discount on their tuition-fee.

About Ekaterinburg

Ekaterinburg is the 4th largest city in Russia, located on the geographical and civilizational border between Europe and Asia in the heart of the country.
Ekaterinburg is one of the largest economic centers in the world ranked 3rd in Russia, after Moscow and St. Petersburg. This is a modern and dynamically developing metropolis, industrial, business and cultural center of the Urals.

Еkaterinburg has a world-class infrastructure for business, education, tourism, culture and entertainment. This is also a scientific city, where the Ural Branch of the Russian Academy of Sciences is based, and dozens of research institutes work.
