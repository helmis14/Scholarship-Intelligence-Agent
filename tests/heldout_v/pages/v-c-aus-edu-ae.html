    President’s Scholarship at AUS

     

    We are happy to share exciting news from American University of Sharjah (AUS) in the United Arab Emirates. As part of our ongoing commitment to attract exceptional applicants from around the world, AUS has launched the President’s Scholarship for international students. The scholarship aims to attract and retain academically qualified and talented students to join our academic community. AUS offer 32 majors and 46 minors at the undergraduate level through its College of Architecture,  Art and Design, College of Arts and Sciences, College of Engineering, and School of Business Administration.

    The President’s Scholarship reflects the commitment to excellence of our university president, Her Excellency Sheikha Bodour bint Sultan Al Qasimi, who leads several Sharjah, UAE, regional and global institutions and initiatives to foster socio-economic development, support cultural industries and heritage, and catalyze multi-stakeholder partnerships for sustainable development. The scholarships will be granted to up to 20 applicants every year and are open to those who completed their secondary education outside the UAE.

    What's included?

    • 100% tuition and lab fees
    • Monthly stipend
    • On-Campus housing
    • Three round-trip tickets during their years of study
    • Student medical insurance
    • Application fee waiver


    The AUS campus is not only a center for academic excellence but also a place of remarkable beauty. Designed to blend tradition and modernity, our stunning campus features striking architecture, lush green spaces and state-of-the-art facilities that create an inspiring environment for learning and community engagement.

    We invite you to explore the AUS campus from wherever you are. Take a virtual tour and discover the heart of our university by visiting www.aus.edu/tour.


    With our campus situated in Sharjah, home to a number of key interconnected research and entrepreneurship institutions, our students are part of a larger ecosystem that cultivates talent and is committed to fostering innovation through research and technological advancement. Not only are our students receiving a world-class education at AUS, they also benefit from an environment where they can learn, innovate and grow. 

    Beyond Sharjah, the UAE offers unparalleled opportunities for students. As a global hub for business and innovation, the UAE is home to numerous multinational companies across industries such as technology, finance, healthcare and sustainability. This dynamic landscape provides AUS students with exceptional opportunities for internships, networking and full-time employment after graduation.

    AUS has cultivated strong partnerships with leading companies across the UAE, connecting students with valuable work experience through internships and collaborative projects. These partnerships open doors for students to gain practical knowledge and establish meaningful connections with industry leaders, ensuring they are well-prepared to launch successful careers in a competitive global market.

    This scholarship is offered to qualifying students in their final year of secondary education who plan to begin their studies at AUS in the fall semester. The selection process is based primarily on academic excellence and other criteria that highlight students’ achievements. The President’s Scholarship is renewable for students who continue to meet the scholarship requirements. To know more about the requirements for the President’s Scholarship, please click here.

    Thank you for your continued support in guiding students towards their educational aspiration. We would greatly appreciate it if you could share information about the AUS President's Scholarship with qualified applicants in your school and encourage them to apply through apply.aus.edu. Applicants will need to create an account by clicking on "Create an Account" under "Sign In."

    If you have any questions or require further information about our scholarship opportunities, please do not hesitate to contact the Scholarship Office at scholarship@aus.edu.
