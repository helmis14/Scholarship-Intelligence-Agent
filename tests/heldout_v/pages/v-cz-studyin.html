Student Experiences 🌟

Hear from international students who chose Czechia for their studies.

Dominik

Škoda Auto Vysoká škola

The connection with practice is, in my opinion, one of the biggest advantages of ŠAVŠ. The mandatory six-month internship is also a huge plus. It gave me a significant advantage at the start of my career because, after my final exams, I did not enter the workplace as a complete newcomer but already knew how things worked in the corporate world.

Sharon

Masaryk University

Tereza

Masaryk University

I had been looking for a flexible job alongside my studies for a long time, and I no longer wanted to do work that had nothing to do with my field. I owe Masaryk University the opportunity to work as a student ambassador and a Science Slam coordinator. Thanks to these opportunities, I get to do what I enjoy and what is connected to my studies.

My Journey Learning Czech

It’s 2014 and I’m standing in a small shop in Long Island City, New York - Slovak Czech Varieties. Offering products from the two countries that previously made up Czechoslovakia, they have a modest collection of Czech and Slovak books. I pick one up. I think, maybe I can identify at least one of the of words I know? Žaba, tráva, kráva, pes, brambor, opice, Akta X? I open the book. “Wtonkg woeikl kje oweiuot.” Maybe the next page? “Lkjkdgsik lksdjgls kiotwgndkjhg.” Nope - just gibberish. Am I ever going to learn this language?
