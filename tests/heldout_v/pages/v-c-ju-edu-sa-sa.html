Deanship of Admission and Registration at Jouf University Announces Admission for International Scholarships (1448 AH)

The Deanship of Admission and Registration at Jouf University announces the opening of admission for internal and external scholarships for international students for the academic year 1448 AH through the “Study in Saudi Arabia” platform.
Applicants are invited to review the admission requirements and complete the application process إلكترونيًا عبر the official platform, ensuring accuracy and completeness of submitted information.
The Deanship confirmed that the application deadline is Thursday, May 21, 2026, and emphasized the importance of meeting all requirements within the specified timeframe.

About Us

Since its establishment in mid-1426 AH, Jouf University has achieved a number of accomplishments and successes. Its journey of growth has continued across various administrative, academic, and educational fields, as well as in construction projects within the university city. These developments have received strong support from the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques and His Royal Highness the Crown Prince—may God support them—as well as ongoing follow-up by His Highness the Governor of Al-Jouf Region and His Excellency the Minister of Education.
