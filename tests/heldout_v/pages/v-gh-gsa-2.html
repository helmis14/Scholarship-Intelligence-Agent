SCHOLARSHIP OPPURTUNITIES IN THE KINGDOM OF SAUDI ARABIA

Applications are invited from interested and qualified candidates, who wish to be considered for scholarship awards at selected universities in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

PARTICIPATING UNIVERSITIES

The scholarships are available at the following universities.

  1. University of Taif                    IV.   University of Najran                       
  2. University of Madinah           V.   University of Tabuk
  3. University of Jazan               VI.    Fahad Bin Sultan University

 

ELIGIBILITY:

Candidates must satisfy the following:

 

a)   Be Ghanaian citizens resident in Ghana

b)   Must have a genuine birth certificate

c)   Must have Passport

PROGRAMMES AVAILABLE

The scholarships are available in the various fields,

               I.         Technology Management                   VI.    Technology

              II.         Business Management                         VII.    Medicine

             III.         Islamic Economics                                VIII.    Education

           IV.         Marketing                                                IX.    Architecture

            V.         Engineering                                             

                                                        

MODE OF APPLICATION

Interested applicants must visit https://studyinsaudi.sa/en to apply.

Applicants are advised to carefully review the eligibility requirements, terms and conditions before applying.

          

 

            SIGNED

       DIRECTOR-GENERAL

Before applying

Follow the notice sequence.

Review the official notice carefully. Where institution-specific admission links are listed, complete those steps before submitting evidence through the Authority portal.

  • Confirm eligibility and deadline.
  • Apply through any institution links stated in the notice.
  • Submit evidence through the official Authority portal.
