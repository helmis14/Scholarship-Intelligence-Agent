The PSECU Scholarship 

The PSECU Scholarship program allows students to focus on their education rather than their finances. Winners receive a $2,000 scholarship every year over the next four years for a total scholarship value of up to $8,000 per student. Scholarship winners must remain enrolled as active students throughout their postsecondary education to maintain and receive their scholarship funds.

Scholarship Eligibility:

Must Be a PSECU Member

Before you apply, you must be a PSECU member. Not a member? Become a member today - let's get started.

(Individuals with custodial accounts are eligible to apply. Please note that joint owners are not considered members.)

Must Be a High School Senior

You must be a graduating high school senior attending a two- or four-year post-secondary school. The school does not have to be in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. Students attending Thaddeus Stevens are eligible!

About the Scholarship

As a not-for-profit credit union, we believe that education is key to a better life. Our scholarship program helps graduating high school seniors reduce the financial burden of postsecondary education. Since 1997, we’ve awarded nearly $1.2M in scholarships to more than 168 students. Three of the six scholarships awarded each year are named for the people who represented the credit union’s ideals and commitment to service.

Honoring Our Past

Three of the six scholarships we award each year are named for people who, through word and deed, represented the credit union’s ideals and commitment to service:

  • PSECU John J. Marisic Memorial Scholarship - John was a longtime member of the PSECU Board of Directors and served as the Vice Chairman for some time. He was an active Board member and was very involved in community events. He was the embodiment of the mission of credit unions to serve the greater good. This scholarship is awarded to the top applicant each year in honor of John's dedication to PSECU and commitment to improving the community.
     
  • PSECU Frank J. Breiner Memorial Scholarship - Frank served on the PSECU Board of Directors for 52 years, including in the roles of Treasurer, Executive Committee Member, and Secretary/Assistant Treasurer. His years of service are shining examples of devotion to community and represent the roots of what PSECU stands for as an organization. This scholarship is awarded in Frank’s honor to an applicant who demonstrates exceptional dedication to extracurricular and/or community service activities.
     
  • PSECU Michael A. Gottshall Memorial Scholarship - Michael was an employee of PSECU for over 12 years. He was known for being giving, genuine, and always putting others before himself. He believed in "living life today" and was often described as someone you were proud to introduce as your friend. This scholarship is awarded to an applicant in honor of Michael's commitment to service before self.
