Provided our application turns out to be successful, the programme consortium may be able to offer an opportunity to the best candidates from V4, EaP and the Western Balkans to apply for the International Visegrad Fund scholarships covering costs of study and expenses. Eligible candidates will be notified about such an opportunity by mid-September.

Tuition fees for the cohort starting in the academic year 2026/2027: 

EU students: 9,400 EUR 
 
Non-EU students: 12,900 EUR 
 

Tuition fees for the cohort starting in the academic year 2025/2026: 

EU students: 9,400 EUR 
 
Non-EU students: 12,900 EUR 
 

Tuition fees for the cohort starting in the academic year 2024/2025: 

EU students: 9,000 EUR

Non-EU students: 12,500 EUR 

 

We proudly announce that thanks to the generous support of the International Visegrad Fund  "Visegrad Scholarships - Joint Degree Program 2026-27" are available.

For the candidates applying to the Joint Degree Programme MA in International Relations: Europe from Visegrad Perspective for 2026/2027, IVF offers a total of eight (8) scholarships.
 
Eligible first-year students who will begin their study programme on October 1, 2026, will be awarded scholarships covering the costs of their studies (four semesters, subject to two application rounds) as well as tuition fees.
 
Scholarships cover mobility costs (3500 EUR per semester) and the tuition fee for the programme.
 
Scholarship Application Deadlines:
 
  • Starting date: April 15, 2026
  • Closing date: June 15, 2026 (noon)
 
The scholarships will be awarded to the best-ranked students enrolled in the programme for the 2026/27 academic year upon results obtained in the application process and scholarship interviews as reflected in the ranking list.
 
Scholarship Availability:  A total of eight (8) scholarships will be awarded to first-year students.

Eligibility Criteria:
 
Applicants must be the nationals of:
  • Visegrad Group (Czechia, Hungary, Poland and Slovakia)
  • EU Eastern Partnership and Western Balkans (Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Moldova, Ukraine, Northern Macedonia, Montenegro, Serbia, Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo).
Detailed information on documents to submit and procedure will be provided shortly.
