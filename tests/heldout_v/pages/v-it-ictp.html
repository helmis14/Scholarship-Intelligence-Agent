About ICTP

DISCOVER





ICTP is a unique institution that explores fundamental scientific questions at the highest level, promotes active engagement with scientists in developing countries, and advances international cooperation through science.

ICTP

Research at ICTP has led to major breakthroughs in areas ranging from the unification of fundamental interactions and the theory of neutrino oscillations to climate modeling. More recently, cross disciplinary research is paving the way for advances in quantum technologies and artificial intelligence.
