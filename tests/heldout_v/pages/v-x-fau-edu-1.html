Demand The Limits Injury Attorneys Scholarship $5,000 Must be majoring in Criminal Justice with junior or senior standing. Graduation semester of Spring 2028 or later and full-time enrollment in Fall 2027 (those graduating in Fall 2027 are not eligible). Have a demonstrated financial need for scholarship funds at the time they are disbursed (Spring 2028). Submit a minimum 500-word essay (no longer than 1,000 words) describing what inspired you to pursue a degree in criminal justice, why you chose the School of Criminology and Criminal Justice at Florida Atlantic University, and how receipt of this scholarship will help you persist to graduation. September 1 – October 12 Mid November Scholarship Universe
