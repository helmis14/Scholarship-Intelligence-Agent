TitleMinerals Research Institute of Western Australia (MRIWA) 2027 PhD Scholarships round
Description

The program supports outstanding postgraduate research aligned to key priorities for Western Australia's minerals industry, including safer and more efficient mining, critical minerals, decarbonisation and green steel development. By promoting this opportunity, you will help attract the next generation of researchers and strengthen the future workforce of WA's resources sector.

MRIWA is offering five PhD scholarships in 2027:

One MRIWA Odwyn Jones PhD Scholarship

Precision and Low Impact Mining:

Supporting research that advances safer, more efficient mining through precision technologies and reduced environmental impacts.

Two MRIWA PhD Scholarships for Women

Any Focus Area:

Open to the highest-ranked female applicant with a research proposal aligned to any MRIWA focus area.

Net Zero Emissions Mining:

Open to the highest-ranked female applicant with a research proposal focused on accelerating mining and mineral processing decarbonisation through clean energy and low-emissions technologies.

Two MRIWA PhD Scholarships

Alternative Use of Tailings and Waste:

Supporting circular economy solutions that improve resource efficiency and reduce waste in the critical minerals industry.

Net Zero Emissions Mining and Green Steel:

Supporting research that advances decarbonisation through clean energy, innovation and low-emissions technologies.

Opportunity statusOpen
Close date02 Nov 2026
Eligibility

We encourage domestic and international students to apply, particularly those enrolled or intending to enrol in a PhD program at a Western Australian university.

Round closes: 11.59pm AWST, Monday 2 November 2026.

Application assessment commences once the round has closed. Applicants will be notified of the outcome in January 2027.

How to apply

Further details, including eligibility criteria and application forms, are available on the MRIWA website: MRIWA PhD Scholarships Program
