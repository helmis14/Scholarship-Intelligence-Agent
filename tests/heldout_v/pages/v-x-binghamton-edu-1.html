External Scholarships

Exploring Scholarship Opportunities

At Binghamton University, we encourage students to seek out external scholarships to help cover the cost of attending college. These scholarships can make a big difference, reducing financial stress by covering things like tuition, books, and living expenses. 

Why Apply for External Scholarships?

  • More Financial Help: Scholarships can close the gap between what financial aid covers and what you still need. This can help you graduate with less debt.
  • Broader Options: Some scholarships are designed for specific groups, including those related to your background, career interests, or unique experiences. These can give you more opportunities, such as study abroad or internships.
  • Stand Out: Winning scholarships not only helps with finances but also looks great on your résumé. It shows you’re dedicated, proactive, and able to compete for competitive awards.

Tips for Finding Scholarships

  • Start Early: Many deadlines are months before the school year starts, so begin your search as soon as possible.
  • Look Locally: Your community—whether through hometown organizations, religious groups, or your family's workplace—may have scholarships that are easier to win.
  • Apply Often: Even smaller scholarships add up. Don’t hesitate to apply for as many as possible!

Where to Find Scholarships

  • External Scholarships and Undergraduate Research Center: Located on campus, this center can help you find scholarships that match your goals.
  • Fastweb & Scholarships.com: These are popular scholarship search engines where you can create a profile and find opportunities tailored to you.
  • College Board, ScholarshipOwl, Peterson’s, Niche, and Unigo: These websites offer databases of scholarships based on your academic background, interests, and more.
  • Scholly® Scholarships by Sallie Known for student loans, Sallie Mae® also provides a vast database of scholarships and a free scholarship search tool.
  • The U.S. Department of Labor’s Scholarship Finder: A government-run search tool that offers access to thousands of scholarships, fellowships, grants, and other financial aid opportunities.

ROTC Scholarships

If you’re interested in a military career, Binghamton offers Army and Air Force ROTC scholarships. These not only help cover the cost of college but also offer valuable leadership training and career opportunities.

Beware of Scholarship Scams

While there are many legitimate scholarships, it’s important to be cautious. Never pay to apply for a scholarship, and don’t share personal financial information unless you know the provider is reputable.
