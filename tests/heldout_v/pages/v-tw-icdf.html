                The 204 new students gathered at the TaiwanICDF's "International Higher Education Scholarship Program" orientation, marking a new beginning and a fresh start for them as they journey from Taiwan to the world!
                The TaiwanICDF Advances International Health Cooperation through Medical Capacity Building and Health Personnel Training Programs
                Twenty-eight healthcare professionals from Taiwan’s diplomatic allies and friendly countries gather in Taiwan for professional training at partner hospitals.  
                Mandarin Education Takes Root in Haiti
                Through the continued dedication of TaiwanICDF teachers, Mandarin education has taken root in Haiti. This year, as the political situation eased, 38 students registered for The Test of Chinese as a Foreign Language (TOCFL), highlighting strong local interest and tangible learning outcomes.
                Expand Employment Opportunities in Belize
                TaiwanICDF has partnered with IDB to strengthen Belize’s public employment services and expand skills training in priority sectors, helping increase the supply of skilled labor with a particular focus on women, youth, and migrants.
                The Public Health Emergency Response System Enhancement Project in Somaliland: Lighting the Way Forward
                The TaiwanICDF and NTUH Yunlin Branch come together to reflect on the project's achievements and share their journey and experiences.
                TaiwanICDF Announces the 2026 Scholarship Recipients for the International Higher Education Scholarship Program
                TaiwanICDF is pleased to announce 2026 TaiwanICDF Scholarship recipients list. 
                Forum on “Data Empowerment for Collective Good”
                The TaiwanICDF will host a forum titled “Data Empowerment for Collective Good: Smart Healthcare Implementation and Public Health System Upgrading” in Geneva on May 19th to promote universal health coverage and strengthen public health governance.
                Integrated System under the Somaliland Health Information System Enhancement Project Officially Launched
                The TaiwanICDF-supported Somaliland Health Information System has been officially launched at Hargeisa Group Hospital, integrating eight core modules to enhance health information management.  
                Taiwan–Europe Scholarship Advancing Tech Talent
                Building on TaiwanICDF’s strong academic partnerships, the Taiwan–Europe Scholarship develops tech talent through industry-aligned master’s programs. Scholars gain cutting-edge knowledge and hands-on experience within Taiwan’s dynamic tech ecosystem. Applications close March 15, 2026—apply now to advance your career.
