Warwick Scholars

Warwick
Scholars

Over the past decade, Warwick’s Scholars programme has ensured that talented undergraduate scholars from disadvantaged backgrounds can take up a place at university. With your donations and investment from the University, we're currently supporting 447 scholars in various years of study.

Unlike traditional scholarships, Warwick Scholars creates a level playing field for all. Before a student even has a place at university, the award-winning initiative encourages under-represented young people to apply. During their year 13 studies, we support students to realise their ambitions, achieve their academic potential and develop vital skills, empowering young people to achieve their dreams of a university education.

Scholars at Warwick benefit from academic support, networking opportunities and a bursary of up to £2,000 per year to help fund the costs of living and essential educational resources. In addition, students receive a 50% tuition fee discount for each year of study and academic support throughout their A-Levels.

But we can still do more. We believe that access to a world-leading university should be open to people from all backgrounds. To make this a success, we will need the support of our alumni community and friends. Your gifts fund more scholarship bursaries and enable expansion of the programme, ensuring that more young people have a chance to succeed.

Your gifts fund more opportunities, ensuring access to a world-leading university is open to people from all backgrounds.
By supporting Warwick, you can ensure everyone has a fair chance at achieving their goals regardless of where they started from.
