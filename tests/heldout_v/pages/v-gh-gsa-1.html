Local tertiary scholarships

Apply locally. Study confidently. Build Ghana's future.

The Local Tertiary Scholarship supports academically qualified Ghanaian students pursuing accredited tertiary education in Ghana, with district-level review to improve fairness, access, and accountability.

Start Application View Process
Application window Usually announced each academic year through the official portal.

Applicants should rely on official portal notices and district guidance for exact dates.

Purpose

Supporting Ghanaian students where opportunity begins.

The programme helps brilliant but financially challenged students continue their tertiary education at home. It connects online applications, district verification, academic merit, and public funding into a more accessible scholarship pathway.

  • Reduce financial barriers for brilliant but needy Ghanaian students.
  • Improve access, retention, and completion in local tertiary institutions.
  • Support applicants through district-level assessment and verification.
  • Align scholarship support with national human capital priorities.
District access Applications are reviewed closer to the communities applicants come from.
Requirements

Know what qualifies before you apply.

Applicants should prepare early, confirm the accreditation status of their institution, and make sure their documents match the information provided on the portal.

Eligible institutions

The scholarship applies to recognised tertiary study in Ghana.

  • Accredited public and private universities
  • Accredited technical universities
  • Colleges of Education
  • Nursing, midwifery, and health training institutions
  • Other accredited tertiary institutions in Ghana

What it may cover

Support can be full or partial depending on approval, need, and available funding.

  • Academic user fees or tuition support
  • Approved academic facility user fees
  • Examination fees
  • Library and ICT levies
  • Accommodation support in selected cases

Eligibility criteria

Applicants must satisfy the core citizenship, academic, and need requirements.

  • Must be a Ghanaian citizen with a valid Ghana Card or national ID.
  • Must have admission into an accredited tertiary institution in Ghana.
  • Must demonstrate academic merit through records or admission documents.
  • Must demonstrate financial need through district-level vetting.
  • Must not be receiving another government scholarship at the time of application.
Documents

Prepare clear documents before the portal opens.

Uploads should be readable, accurate, and consistent with the information entered in the application form. Original copies may be requested during district review.

01 Admission letter
02 Academic records or certificates
03 Ghana Card or national ID
04 Passport-sized photo
05 Proof of need or supporting evidence
06 Any additional document requested on the portal
How to apply

A clear process from portal registration to award notification.

The process is designed to combine online submission with local verification through the District Scholarship Review Committee.

01

Confirm Eligibility

Review the citizenship, admission, academic merit, and financial need requirements before starting.

02

Create Portal Account

Visit apply.scholarships.gov.gh, choose Local Tertiary Scholarship, and register with a valid email and phone number.

03

Complete Application

Enter accurate personal, academic, admission, financial, and district information in the online form.

04

Upload Documents

Upload clear scanned copies of all required documents and check that every file is readable.

05

Attend District Interview

Applications are reviewed through the District Scholarship Review Committee. Bring original documents if invited.

06

Track Notification

Successful applicants are notified by SMS, email, or portal dashboard, subject to merit, need, and quota availability.

07

Award and Disbursement

Approved scholarship support is processed to beneficiary institutions according to award terms and funding schedules.

Application window

Use the current official dates before you apply.

Application dates may change each academic year. Applicants should always confirm the opening and closing dates on the official portal before starting or submitting.

Current window: check official portal

The Authority announces active application windows through the official application portal and public communication channels. Do not rely on unofficial dates.

When to prepare

Prepare your admission letter, academic records, Ghana Card, proof of need, and contact details before the portal opens so you can apply without rushing.

Status tracking

Understand what happens after submission.

After submitting, your application moves through verification, district review, and award decision stages. Keep checking your portal dashboard, phone, and email.

01

03

District review

What to expect from the District Scholarship Review Committee.

Local tertiary applications are reviewed through district structures to support fairer access and local verification. If invited, attend on time with original documents that match your portal submission. The committee may confirm your identity, district residence, admission status, academic history, financial need, and the accuracy of the documents uploaded online. Applicants should answer questions clearly, be honest about their circumstances, and understand that the interview supports verification rather than guaranteeing an award.

  • Watch for invitation notices Interview invitations may come by SMS, email, phone call, or portal notice.
  • Bring original documents Carry original copies of uploaded documents for verification.
  • Prepare your explanation Be ready to explain academic background, financial need, and district residence.
  • Understand award decisions Final awards depend on verification, merit, need, and available quota.

Before you apply checklist

  1. Confirm you meet the citizenship, admission, merit, and need requirements.
  2. Use the same name, date of birth, and ID details across all documents.
  3. Scan documents clearly before upload; avoid blurry or cropped files.
  4. Choose the correct district of residence for review and interview.
  5. Keep your phone number, email, and portal password active and accessible.
  6. Review every field before submitting; avoid duplicate applications.

Common mistakes to avoid

  1. Uploading unreadable documents or screenshots with missing edges.
  2. Selecting the wrong district or using inconsistent residential information.
  3. Using different names across admission letter, Ghana Card, and certificates.
  4. Submitting without checking phone number, email, and portal login details.
  5. Applying while already benefiting from another government scholarship.
  6. Waiting until the final day before preparing required documents.
Portal support

Having trouble with the application portal?

Use the portal recovery options for password issues first. For unresolved login, submission, or upload problems, contact the Authority through official support channels and include your name, phone number, email, and a clear description of the issue.

Official caution

Apply only through official channels.

The Authority does not require applicants to pay unofficial agents for scholarship consideration. Use the official application portal and keep your login details private.

FAQ

Quick answers for local scholarship applicants.

These answers help applicants avoid confusion before submission, district review, and award notification.

Can private tertiary students apply?

Yes, if the institution is accredited and the applicant meets the published eligibility requirements.

Does submission guarantee an award?

No. Awards depend on eligibility, merit, need, verification, district review, and available quotas.

Where does the interview happen?

District-level interviews are normally coordinated through the applicant's District Scholarship Review Committee.

How will applicants be notified?

Applicants may receive updates by SMS, email, phone call, or through the portal dashboard.

Can documents be changed after submission?

Applicants should submit carefully. Changes may depend on portal rules and guidance from the Authority.

What if I forget my portal password?

Use the portal recovery option first. If the issue continues, contact official support channels.

Ready to begin your local scholarship application?

Use the official portal only, submit accurate information, and keep your phone and email active for district review and award notifications.
