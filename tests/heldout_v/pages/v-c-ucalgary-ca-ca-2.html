Chris Kirby - Always Safe Always Professional (ASAP) Bursary

  1. Info for Students
  2. Grants, Funding, and Scholarships
  3. Chris Kirby - Always Safe Always Professional (ASAP) Bursary

This award was established to honour Chris Kirby and their contributions to STEP Energy Services.

Award

Value: $1,200 for OHS Diploma; $600 for OHS Fundamentals and/or OHS Advanced per year
Number: One for OHS Diploma; Three for OHS Fundamentals and/or OHS Advanced per year
Application Deadline: August 1

Eligibility Criteria

Students must meet the following requirements:

  • Currently enrolled in one or more Occupational Health and Safety programs:
    • Occupational Health and Safety Fundamentals Certificate
    • Occupational Health and Safety Advanced Certificate
    • Occupational Health and Safety Diploma
  • Have registered in the first course of their currently enrolled Occupational Health and Safety professional certificate or professional diploma
  • Canadian citizen or permanent resident
