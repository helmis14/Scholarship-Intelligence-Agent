Cook Islands Scholarship Programme

Te Reinga Akataunga’anga Scholarship Programme

Since 2017, the Ministry of Education has administered the Te Reinga Akataunga’anga scholarship programme, enabling Cook Islanders to pursue tertiary studies both locally and abroad. The programme offers various funding streams to support students at different stages of their educational journey.

Scholarship Application Form 2026 (closes 28 November 2025 )

Scholarship Categories

Starter Pack

For first-year students (school leavers or mature students) commencing a programme of study.

International Full Scholarships

For students who have successfully completed their first year of study overseas with academic merit.

Domestic Full Scholarships

For recipients wishing to commit to full-time study while remaining in the Cook Islands.

In-Country Study Grants

For recipients who wish to remain in-country and undertake study while continuing to work.

First Year Fees Free

Waiver of fees for selected first-year students enrolled in undergraduate programmes.
