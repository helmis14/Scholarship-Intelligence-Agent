Financial Support and Scholarships

Net Price Calculator

The Office of Financial Support and Scholarships is committed to making higher education accessible by minimizing financial barriers for students so they may realize their educational goals. We strive to provide services of the highest quality to support the academic mission and goals of the university and its students.

Our office is committed to the highest standards of professional conduct.

Read the University of Nebraska Code of Conduct policy.
