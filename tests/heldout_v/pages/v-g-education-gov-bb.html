National Development Scholarships for 2026

Scholarship Details

Applications are invited from suitably qualified Barbadians for the 2026 National Development Scholarships at undergraduate and postgraduate levels.

Further information pertaining to the scholarship and application forms can be obtained from the Ministry’s website at www.education.gov.bb or

The Tertiary Section,
Ministry of Education Transformation,
Elsie Payne Complex,
Constitution Road,
St. Michael
535-0863/850-4802 or tertiary@mes.gov.bb.

Incomplete applications will not be accepted.

Who is eligible?

Applicants must:

  • be citizens of Barbados
  • be between the age of eighteen (18) and forty (40); and
  • have been accepted at an Internationally accredited University.
What does the scholarship cover?
  • one (1) direct economy class ticket to the country of study at the commencement of the course, and one (1) return ticket to Barbados on completion of the programme;
  • full tuition fees, payable directly to the University;
  • a book allowance of $ 1,000.00 (once);
  • a clothing allowance of $736.00 (once), applicable for students pursuing studies in cold countries; and
  • Living allowance as follows:
    • USA. $6,300.00 per annum;
    • CAN.$5,880.00 per annum; and
    • United Kingdom £7,560.00 per annum.
What is my obligation?

Successful applicants will be required to enter a bond with two (2) sureties to return and work in Barbados on completion of the course, on the basis of one year for each year of the award.

How do I apply?

The application form may be obtained using the link below or for further information, you may visit the Ministry of Education Transformation, Elsie Payne Complex, Constitution Road, St. Michael.

All forms must be completed and submitted no later than June 17, 2026.
