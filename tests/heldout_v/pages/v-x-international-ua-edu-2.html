Login Education Abroad

So you may be asking yourself, “How do I pay for study abroad?” Funding is a very important factor in planning your education abroad experience. Our office is here to help! There are tons of resources for students looking for study abroad funding, but you can also use your existing financial aid, scholarship, and other methods of payment here at UA.

Check out our funding resources and FAQs below and let us know if you have any questions. Remember, your Education Abroad Advisor can work one-on-one with you to determine how best to fund your international experience.
