International financial support

International scholarships and funding

Birkbeck International Excellence scholarship (January)

Scholarships of £3,000 are available for international students starting a postgraduate programme in January 2027.

Birkbeck International Excellence Scholarship (autumn intake)

Scholarships of £1,000-£5,000 are available for students starting a undergraduate or postgraduate course in autumn 2026.

GREAT - Birkbeck Scholarship - Indonesia 2026

This scholarship offers Indonesian students £10,000 towards their tuition fees for a wide range of one-year taught postgraduate courses in the 2026-27 academic year.

Birkbeck Global Future scholarship

This scholarship will be applied as a discount to the tuition fee for the first year of your full-time master's course, to bring it in line with the Home tuition fee amount.

Vice Chancellor's Scholarship

This scholarship is available for international students domiciled in Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Pakistan and Vietnam.

Chevening scholarship

Chevening Scholarships are full cost, covering both tuition fees and living costs. Available for full-time master's students on a taught courses.

Commonwealth scholarship

Commonwealth citizens can apply for a number of different funds under the Commonwealth Scholarship.

Birkbeck-OnCampus Progression Scholarship

This scholarship is designed to support international students who have completed a foundation programme with OnCampus in continuing their studies at Birkbeck.

Accommodation scholarship

A number of organisations offer external scholarships that international students studying at Birkbeck can apply for.

Loans for Canadian students

Birkbeck administers a range of loans for students from Canada. Find out how to apply and whether you're eligible.

Financial aid for American students

Birkbeck administers a range of loans for students from the United States. Find out how to apply and whether you're eligible.

Faculty and school funding

Acker postgraduate psychology bursary

The bursary looks to support talented students who come from a low income background or demonstrate financial difficulty studying in the School of Psychological Studies.

AHRC Landscape Award Studentships

This award will provide up to three fully funded studentships per year, over five cohorts.

Birkbeck Mimizuku Award in Japanese Studies

This £5,000 scholarship is open to students studying BA Japanese Studies, BA Japanese Studies with Foundation Year or BA Japanese Studies with International Experience.

Earth and Planetary Sciences Enabling Fund

Students studying the subject areas of earth and planetary sciences can apply for financial assistance for fieldwork and non-fieldwork costs.

Emily Paige Short Fund

An award of up to £1,000 for students studying earth and planetary sciences.

Greta Dexter Exhibition Bursary

This £1,000 scholarship is open to students studying French or French Studies.

MRes Philosophy Bursary

The School of Historical Studies will award a limited number of bursaries to students embarking on the MRes in Philosophy.

Peltz studentship

Five scholarships are available for part-time postgraduate students studying in the School of Historical Studies, the School of Natural Sciences and School of Psychological Sciences.

Sasakawa Japanese Studies Postgraduate Studentship

The Sasakawa Postgraduate studentships is available to students studying a postgraduate taught or research degree with a focus on Japanese studies.

Women in Sport scholarship

This award aims to foster women’s participation in the sport industry and aims to benefit a female student enrolled on a sport programme (MSc).

Will Vaughan Scholarship

This is a partial fee waiver for a student on MA History of Art, MA History of Art with Collections Management or MA History of Art with Curating.

Birkbeck Lifelong Learning Guarantee

We offer all graduates a discount when continuing their studies at Birkbeck.
