Scholarships incoming students Mastermind ICP Master of Statistics ICP Master of Transportation sciences Watch out

There are various scholarships for international students, depending on their country of origin, study programme,... . Below you can find more information about the scholarships.

Mastermind

The MasterMind call for the 2027–2028 academic year is not yet open. This call will be available exclusively to students interested in the Master of Interior Architecture and the Master of Statistics and Data Science programmes.

  • Further information will be provided once the call opens.
  • Contact UHasselt: Niels Lambrichts

 

 

ICP Master of Statistics

The International Course programme (ICP) is a 2-year international and multidisciplinary training in statistics. It combines a solid study of fundamental methodology with up-to-date training. 

More information: Master of Statistics and Data Science - ICP.

ICP Master of Transportation sciences

The International Course programme (ICP) is a high-quality multidisciplinary international master programme of 2 years (on-campus) with a unique focus and approach to Road Safety in Developing Countries. The programme is supported by VLIR UOS and has 10 scholarships available each year..

More information: Master of Transportation sciences - ICP

Please be careful when applying for other scholarships. An admission letter to study at Hasselt University always comes directly from Hasselt University and from an official e-mail address ending with @uhasselt.be.
