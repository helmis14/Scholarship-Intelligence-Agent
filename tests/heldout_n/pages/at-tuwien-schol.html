Grants and Scholarships

DEKANAT
  1. DEKANAT /
  2. Grants and Scholarships /

Funding grants and merit scholarships for the following courses can be applied for at the Dean’s Office of the faculty of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering:

  •  Mechanical Engineering
  • Mechanical Engineering - Management
  • Manufacturing and Robotics
  • Chemical and Process Engineering
  • Material Sciences 

Funding grant

Funding grants are used to fund the scientific or artistic work of students studying for a degree (diploma theses and dissertations).

Level of bursary: €750 to €3,600

Information sheet, opens an external URL in a new window (PDF)
Application for a funding grant , opens an external URL in a new window(PDF)

Next application deadlines:  April 30th, 2026 and October 15th, 2026

Merit scholarship

Merit scholarships are designed to fund students and graduates studying for a degree in recognition of their outstanding academic performance.

Level of bursary: €750  to €1,500

Information sheet , opens an external URL in a new window(PDF)
Application for a merit scholarship , opens an external URL in a new window(PDF)

Next application deadline:  October 15th, 2026

 

The Dean of Studies decides whether to award a merit scholarship and the amount of the scholarship. Unfortunately, the number of scholarships available is limited, so not all students who apply for a scholarship will receive one. Scholarships are awarded on the basis of ECTS performance, ECTS-weighted grade point average and length of study. There is no legal entitlement to a Merit Scholarship. 

The documents for submission can be handed in personally at the Dean's Office or sent by e-mail!
