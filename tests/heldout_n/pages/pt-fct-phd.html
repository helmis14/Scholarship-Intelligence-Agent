Studentships for Doctoral Studies

Doctoral Studentships are intended for candidates enrolled in a program leading to a doctoral degree, or candidates who meet the necessary conditions to enroll, and who wish to conduct research leading to the award of such a degree in all areas of knowledge.

Calls

  • Studentships all scientific fields

    The Call Studentships for doctoral studies in all scientific fields is held annually and is part of the FCT's major Calls

  • Studentships Maria de Sousa

    The Call Maria de Sousa PhD Studentships is aimed at acquiring new knowledge in the field of virology.

Calls

  • Closed on
    March 2026

    2026 Call for PhD Studentships – Regular Application Process

    PhD studentships aim to fund research activities leading to the award of the PhD degree.
  • Closed on
    March 2026

    2026 Call for PhD Studentships – Specific Line of Application in a Non-academic Environment

    PhD studentships, to be awarded in the Specific Line of Application, aim to fund research activities carried out in close articulation with non-academic institutions, leading to the award of the PhD degree.
  • Closed on
    Apr 2025

    2025 Call for PhD Studentships – Regular Application Process

    PhD studentships aim to finance research activities that lead to obtaining the academic degree of doctor.
