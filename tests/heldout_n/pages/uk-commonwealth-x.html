Membership

The ACU has over 400 member universities in more than 40 countries across the Commonwealth. 70% of our members are in low and middle income countries, and we are represented in more than 50% of Commonwealth large ocean states.

Our network covers over 10 million students and more than 1 million academic and professional staff.

Our work

The Association of Commonwealth Universities is a global network of universities with a shared  commitment to building a better world through  international collaboration in higher education. As the world’s first and oldest international university  network, we have been bringing universities together  from across the Commonwealth for more than 100 years.

Projects and programmes

We have extensive experience of co-designing and delivering programmes that help our members to develop their capacity for education, research, and innovation.
International mobility

We build enduring international connections and understanding between individuals, institutions, and nations.
Policy and advocacy

We provide an influential voice for universities in international policy forums and work to ensure that our members' needs are at the forefront of education policy and decision-making.
Networks and communities

Through our networks and communities we leverage the strengths of a global network to support on strategic priority areas and share expertise and resources.
