International Scholarships

We are proud to support our high-achieving international students with scholarships which offer a discount on tuition fees. Find a list of our current scholarships for students from overseas below.

Scholarships
Partnership scholarships

British Council GREAT Scholarships

In partnership with the British Council and the GREAT Britain Campaign, the University of Salford is offering two GREAT Scholarships: one for a student from India and one for a student from Vietnam. The scholarships are available for eligible one-year postgraduate programmes, including MSc Managing AI in Business or MSc Diabetes Care for students from India, and MSc Data Science or MSc Microsystems and Nanoengineering for students from Vietnam.

Learn more about British Council GREAT Scholarships

Chevening scholarships

Chevening is the UK Government’s international awards scheme aimed at developing global leaders.

Learn more about Chevening scholarships

NCUK scholarships

We are an owner-member of the Northern Consortium of UK universities (NCUK). NCUK delivers pathway programmes that lead to degrees at member universities. To support students who progress from NCUK, we offer a number of high value scholarships to outstanding applicants for undergraduate or master's programmes.

Learn more about NCUK scholarships

Commonwealth scholarships

Commonwealth Scholarships enable talented and motivated students to gain the knowledge and skills required for sustainable development.

These scholarships are funded by the UK Department for International Development (DFID) and offered through the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission in the UK (CSC).

Learn more about Commonwealth scholarships

Salford/EATL Data Science scholarships 

We have joined together with Ethical Advanced Technologies Limited (EATL) Innovation Hub in Bangladesh to support students from Bangladesh who aspire to contribute to the development of their country through data science and AI.  

We have 6 part-funded scholarships to support students MSc Data Science students from Bangladesh. At least 3 of these will be awarded to female applicants to support our Women in STEM agenda.

Learn about the Salford/EATL Data Science scholarships

Salford/International Nursing College scholarships

We have joined together with International Nursing College in Bangladesh to support nursing students who want to develop their nursing skills and return to Bangladesh to contribute to the development of the health care sector.

The scholarships are part of the University’s wider engagement in Bangladesh that includes work to support the development of the nursing curriculum, and teacher training and research methodology masterclasses for healthcare professionals.

We have 10 part-funded scholarships to support students on MSc Nursing and BSc Nursing Studies (top-up).

Learn about the Salford/International Nursing College scholarships

Global Gold Excellence Scholarship

Learn more about the Global Gold Excellence Scholarship

Learn more about the Global Silver Excellence Scholarship

Learn more about the IFY Excellence Scholarship.

Other discounts

Loyalty discount

If you have completed an eligible programme of study here, you may be entitled to a discount of 20% off your fees if you take another programme with us. For more details please see the information on our loyalty discount. 

Please note, you may apply for the loyalty discount if you have already been awarded a scholarship, however, in line with our terms and conditions, you can only receive one scholarship or discount (the one with the highest value).

Prompt payment discount

If you pay your fees in full, either on before or at enrolment, you can receive a prompt payment discount of 3%. This is applied to the net fee remaining after any scholarship has been applied.

Terms and conditions

Before applying for an international scholarship, please read our terms and conditions. Unless specified, our international scholarships are available to full-time, on-campus, self-funded students who have been assessed as international students for fee purposes.
