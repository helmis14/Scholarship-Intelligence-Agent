Workshops

CPGS Workshops

CPGS offers more than 30 workshops each year to support your research. Our workshops are offered by experts in their fields. Some workshops are in person others online. Join the CPGS mailing list([email protected]) to be kept informed of that week's events. More workshops are added throughout the year.
