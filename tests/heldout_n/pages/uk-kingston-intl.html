Become an international scholar

Being awarded a scholarship is a great achievement

A scholarship not only provides financial support. It also offers a rich student experience unlike any other.

At Kingston, you'll become part of our international scholars community, giving you the opportunity to:

  • attend our annual scholars' event alongside senior staff and academics
  • boost your career prospects through valuable networking opportunities
  • meet other scholars from around the world
  • enhance your CV and impress future employees with your award
  • work as a TAP ambassador for Kingston University, with opportunities to contribute to marketing material and in-country activities.

International Merit-Based scholarships

At Kingston University, we offer a small number of merit-based scholarships for international students. You must already have an offer to study an undergraduate or postgraduate (taught) course at Kingston University. These scholarships are not available to students who are currently enrolled on a Kingston University degree or foundation course.

The scholarships are worth £5,000 towards your first year tuition fees. If you are awarded the scholarship and pay the full tuition fees in advance, you should deduct the £5,000 from your total fees. If you are paying by instalments, you will receive the £5,000 deduction on your final instalment.

Please apply using the application form which usually opens in March and November each year. You will need to upload your offer email and a 300-word personal statement, to support your application. Your personal statement should address the following questions:

  • Why do you want to study at Kingston University?
  • How do you intend to use your experience and knowledge in the future?
  • Why are you a good champion for Kingston University?

How to apply for an International Merit-Based scholarship

International Merit-Based scholarship application are now closed for September intake. For the January intake, applications will open in November.

International Success Support Scholarship

The International Success Support Scholarship provides £2,500 towards tuition fees for eligible international students. This award empowers international students to succeed at Kingston University.

Postgraduate students receive £2,500 in their first year.

Eligible postgraduate courses starting in January 2027 

  • International Business Management MSc
  • Accounting and Finance MSc
  • User Experience Design MSc
  • Data Science MSc
  • Data Science Conversion MSc
  • Renewable Energy Engineering MSc

Eligible postgraduate courses starting in September 2027 or January 2028

  • All courses in the Faculty of Business and Social Sciences (excluding the MBA)

  • All courses in the Faculty of Engineering, Computing and the Environment
    Explore all eligible courses in the Faculty of Engineering, Computing and the Environment >
  • All courses in the School of Life Sciences, Pharmacy and Chemistry

You are eligible for an International Success Support Scholarship if you:

  • Have received an offer to study on one of the eligible courses at Kingston University.
  • Are assessed as an overseas student for fee purposes.

You do not need to apply for an International Success Support Scholarship. If you are eligible, the award will be automatically applied to your tuition fee invoice – no separate application is required.

FAQs about our international scholarships

We understand that you probably have a lot of questions when it comes to funding your studies at Kingston. We have collated some common answers below to help you:

The International Merit Based Scholarships will be awarded on the following basis:

  • Your reasons for wanting to study at Kingston University
  • Your ability and enthusiasm to be a champion for Kingston and support us in our recruitment and marketing work

Applications are not on a first come, first served basis, so take your time.

Use your personal statement to demonstrate your enthusiasm. Remember, your personal statement should answer:

  • Why do you want to study at Kingston University?
  • How do you intend to use your experience and knowledge in the future?
  • Why are you a good champion for Kingston University?

International Success Support Scholarships are awarded based on the course you apply for and are automatically applied to the tuition fee invoice for eligible students. 

Yes, you can apply for more than one Kingston University scholarship.

However, if you are awarded more than one Kingston University scholarship, you will only be able to accept one Kingston University scholarship with the exception of an International Success Support Scholarship which can be combined with an International Merit Based Scholarship.

Students who receive an International Scholarship may also apply for a Loyalty Bursary. Full details, and the application form, are available on My Kingston (the University intranet) after enrolment.

It is also possible to combine a scholarship from Kingston University with an externally awarded scholarship as well.

International Merit Based Scholarships

If you are awarded the scholarship and pay by instalments, you will receive the £5,000 deduction on your final instalment.

If you are awarded the scholarship and are paying the full tuition fees in advance, you should deduct the £5,000 from your total fees (e.g. if your fees are £13,500 and you are awarded a £5,000 scholarship, you should pay £8,500).

Find out more about how to pay your fees on the international student finance page.

International Success Support Scholarships

An International Success Support Scholarship worth £2,500 will be automatically deducted from your tuition fee invoice and applied towards your tuition fees if you meet the eligibility criteria. 

  • Undergraduate students on eligible courses can receive £2,500 per year for up to three years.
  • Postgraduate students receive the award for their first year of study.

The scholarship must be used for the course and intake you include in your International Merit Based Scholarship application.

If you change your course or defer, you will lose your International merit based scholarship.

International Success Support Scholarships are only available for 2026 entry on selected courses. You can defer your place from January 2026 to September 2026 and remain eligible, but any other deferral or course change may affect your award.

You are required to pay a minimum of 50% of the published tuition fee in order to enrol. The £2,500 scholarship will be deducted from the remaining balance when your invoice is raised.

Please note if you are a postgraduate applicant, a non-refundable £5,000 deposit is required to apply for your CAS. This deposit forms part of the 50% minimum payment and is not reduced by the scholarship.

Loyalty bursaries and discounts

If you are an international ('overseas fee' paying) student, you may qualify for one of our bursaries or alumni discounts:

Postgraduate Progression Scholarship

If you have completed an undergraduate course at Kingston University and are progressing to a postgraduate degree, with no gap in study, you might be entitled to a 15% discount on postgraduate tuition fees.

How to apply for a Postgraduate Progression Scholarship

You do not need to apply for a Postgraduate Progression Scholarship. If you are eligible, it will be applied automatically to your course fees invoice. If you’re paying via instalments, the discount will be apportioned evenly across all of your instalments.

For more information, view our Postgraduate Progression Scholarship page.

Alumni discount

The alumni discount is a 10% tuition fee discount for all Kingston University undergraduate students who are progressing to study one of our postgraduate courses. These include postgraduate taught, postgraduate research, PGCE, and MArch in Architecture courses.

How to apply for the Alumni discount

You do not need to apply for this discount. If you are eligible, it will be applied automatically to your course fees invoice. If you’re paying via instalments, the discount will be apportioned evenly across all of your instalments.

For further information on the Alumni discount, visit our postgraduate finance page.

Terms and Conditions

Full terms and conditions for the Postgraduate Progression Scholarship and Alumni discount can also be found on our postgraduate finance page.

The family bursary is a 10% tuition fee discount for international students with a direct family connection (defined as a sibling, parent, child or spouse) to a current or previous student enrolled at Kingston University. Both family members are entitled to receive the bursary, if they are studying at the same time.

Applications for the Family Bursary will open in September.

How to apply for the Family Bursary

If you are eligible for the Family Bursary, you can only apply after you have fully completed enrolment.

To apply, go to My Kingston > International students > International loyalty bursaries. You will need to submit the application form with evidence of your family connection. For example, a marriage certificate (for spouse) or birth certificate (for parent, sibling or child). 

You will also need to provide evidence your relative studied at Kingston University. For this, you will need to provide their graduation certificate or Kingston ID number.

If approved, the discount will be apportioned evenly across all of your instalments (or refunded in the case of a full tuition fee payment).

This bursary is a 10% tuition fee discount for international students who previously participated in a Study Abroad, International Summer School, or Exchange programme at Kingston University and are returning to do a full-time undergraduate or postgraduate degree course. Please note that preparation courses, such as pre-sessional English or short courses, are not eligible. 

How to apply for Study Abroad, Summer School and Exchange Student Bursaries 

Eligible students can apply after they fully complete enrolment, via My Kingston > International students > International loyalty bursaries. Submit the application form to the International Office, along with the academic transcript/certificate from your Study Abroad, International Summer School, or Exchange programme.

If approved, the discount will be apportioned evenly across all of your instalments (or refunded in the case of a full tuition fee payment).

  • The 10% Family and Study Abroad, Summer School Exchange bursary applies to International fee status students.
  • Final decisions on eligibility will be determined by the University.
  • The discount cannot be combined with any other alumni or loyalty discount offered by Kingston University, including faculty, department or school discounts that may be available.
  • The discount can be combined with an International Scholarship.
  • If you do not provide appropriate evidence as part of your loyalty bursary application, you will not receive the discount.
  • Preparation courses (e.g. short courses and pre-sessional English courses) are not eligible for the 10% discount.
  • The 10% discount does not apply to any undergraduate or postgraduate placement year fees (the 10% discount is only applicable for 'taught' years of study).
  • The 10% Family bursary will only be applied to both family members if they are fully enrolled (‘current students') and studying at the same time. At the date of enrolment these criteria need to be met and cannot be reviewed retrospectively.

External scholarships for students from outside the EU

There are external institutions that offer scholarships for international students who wish to study in the UK.

Please note, scholarships from partner institutions are not administered by Kingston University. Make sure you use the correct application form when you apply.

Chevening Scholarships are the UK government's global scholarship programme, funded by the Foreign and Commonwealth Office (FCO) and partner organisations. 

These scholarships are for students with an offer to study for a one-year master’s degree, who have been identified as potential future leaders across a range of fields, including:

  • Academia
  • Business
  • Civil society
  • Media
  • Politics
  • Religion

Please visit the Chevening website for more information about eligibility criteria and how and when to apply.

Through the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (CSC), the British Government provides over 800 scholarships and fellowships for postgraduate study and professional development each year to applicants from Commonwealth countries.

These awards can cover:

  • Master degrees
  • Split-site scholarships for PhD candidates

All awards are made strictly on the basis of academic merit and the potential to contribute to the needs of the applicants' home countries. For further information, visit the GOV.UK website.

There are approximately 20 awards available each year for US citizens to study in the UK. These include scholarships for postgraduate study and scholarships and fellowships for lecturing and/or research.

British Marshall Scholarships are funded by the Foreign and Commonwealth Office  to help to finance Americans of high ability to study for a degree in the UK. Up to 40 scholarships are awarded each year for study at any UK university. 

They cover two years of study, in any discipline, at either undergraduate or postgraduate level, including:

  • Tuition fees
  • Cost of living expenses
  • A books grant
  • Research and daily travel
  • Air fares
  • A contribution towards the support of a dependent spouse (if applicable)

For further information, visit the Marshall Scholarships website.

Additional information

Our scholarships are very competitive and we receive many applications every year. If you require a Student Visa, please proceed as soon as possible with further steps. For example, if you are a postgraduate student, you should pay your deposit to secure your CAS in time for your visa application.

Kingston University reserves the right to withdraw or amend a scholarship or bursary without prior notification. Scholarships and bursaries are offered subject to funding and availability.
