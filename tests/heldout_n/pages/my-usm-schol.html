The 2025 Malaysia International Scholarship is Now Open!

Go to https://biasiswa.mohe.gov.my/INTER/index.php to apply.

🎓 Malaysia International Scholarship (MIS) 2025 🇲🇾

A golden opportunity for international students to pursue a Master’s or PhD in our school — fully funded!

✅ Who Can Apply:

  • Citizens from eligible countries. Details are at the link.
  • Age ≤ 40 (Master’s) / 45 (PhD)
  • CGPA ≥ 3.0
  • Proficient in English (IELTS ≥ 6.0 / TOEFL ≥ 550 / English-medium degree)
  • Must submit a research proposal (for research-based programs)

🎯 Scholarship Benefits:

  • Full tuition fees
  • RM1,500 monthly allowance

📝 How to Apply:

  1. Go to: https://biasiswa.mohe.gov.my/INTER
  2. Fill in the online form
  3. Upload documents (passport, transcripts, English test results, CV, recommendation letters, admission letter, research proposal)

📅 Apply before the deadline: 30 June 2025! Go to https://biasiswa.mohe.gov.my/INTER/index.php to apply.
