Why Loran

Loran builds meaningful, positive change across Canada and beyond by empowering young people with curiosity, integrity, and the dedication to uplift their communities.

We believe that identifying such promising young people means looking beyond grades to find students with character—those who show courage by pushing boundaries and challenging the status quo; display a commitment to service that improves the lives of others; and demonstrate intrinsic leadership potential.

The Scholar Experience

Being a Loran Scholar is more than receiving an undergraduate award—it’s belonging to a community that will empower you on a journey of growth and a lifelong pursuit of purpose.

Leadership Enrichment

Leadership Enrichment

  • Skill and capacity building 
  • Experiential learning through work placements across the public policy, enterprise, and community development sectors
  • Immersive learning in different parts of Canada and abroad

Inspirational Community

Inspirational Community

  • One-on-one mentorship from a business or community leader 
  • Scholar gatherings and community-building programming
  • A diverse network of supportive scholar and alumni peers
  • Lifelong membership in a community of values-driven individuals

Financial Support

Financial Support

  • An annual stipend of $12,000
  • A full tuition waiver annually, tenable at one of our partner universities
  • Up to $14,000 for the Summer Experiential Learning Program
