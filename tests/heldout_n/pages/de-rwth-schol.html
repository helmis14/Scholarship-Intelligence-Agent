Scholarships for Short Courses

We believe that high-quality education and making intercultural experiences should be available to everybody, regardless of their financial situation. So we are glad to announce that we award scholarships for selected on-campus Summer & Winter Schools every year. Our different scholarships with reduced program fees increase the chance for students to attend our Short Courses, independent of their background.

We are awarding scholarships to particularly qualified applicants on a rolling basis. As scholarship places are limited, your chances increase the earlier you apply.

Country Scholarship 2026

We love the country mix! As RWTH International Academy, we stand for diversity and intercultural exchange. Therefore, we want to give students from as many different cultural backgrounds as possible the chance to get a taste of studying at RWTH Aachen University. For the 2026 Summer Schools, we are again issuing Country Scholarships applicable for all open Summer Schools taking place on campus.

Scholarship holders receive 50% off the program fee.

What we expect from our scholarship holders

In addition to the usual application requirements, as a scholarship holder, you should have outstanding grades, achievements, references and motivation.

As a participant, you will be supporting us as our ambassador to reach out to students from all over the world.

  • During your stay, you will be featured in media appearances.
  • After your stay, you will provide us with a testimonial.
  • Back at home, you will share your experience with your student community (presentation, blog, social media).

How to become a scholarship holder

If you study in one of the below listed countries, you are eligible for our scholarships. In addition to your usual application documents, you need to:

  • upload a scholarship motivation letter in which you state why you are the perfect candidate to fulfill the above explained expectations.
  • include a letter of recommendation to your application.

You can submit all necessary documents via the application form.

Eligible Countries

If you are currently studying in one of the following countries, you can apply for the scholarship:

Scholarships for Master Applicants

Are you planning to study a post-graduate degree and applying for one of your Master’s degree programs? Check out our exclusive scholarship portfolio to support different student groups with financing their studies!

Women in Engineering Scholarship

This scholarship was created to enable for more qualified female engineering students to pursue a Master’s degree program at RWTH International Academy. Furthermore, it is intended to create gender equality among the international students of English-taught Master’s degree programs in engineering.

  • PDF Download Regulations

High Potential Student Grant

This scholarship was created to enable highly qualified students to complete their Master’s degree program at RWTH Aachen, offered by RWTH International Academy. This scholarship can grant admitted students a reduction of up to 25% of the tuition fees charged by RWTH International Academy.

  • PDF Download Regulations

Global Talent Scholarship

This scholarship was established to enable promising students from around the world to pursue a Master's degree offered by RWTH International Academy, thus making further education more accessible. It supports bachelor graduates from across the globe, check the regulations for a detailed list of eligible countries. The GTS scholarship covers up to 35% of the program tuition fees after successful admission.

  • PDF Download Regulations

You can apply for these scholarships through our official application portal RWTHonline as part of your regular master’s application. The application deadline for these scholarships corresponds with the regular deadline for master’s application, March 1st for Non-EU applicants and July 15th for EU applicants.

Qualification Programs: Outstanding Performance Award

This scholarship honors talented students who have successfully completed our German Engineering College (GEC) in engineering. Their remarkable performance during the GEC will be rewarded with a reduction of up to 50% of the tuition fees charged by RWTH International Academy.

  • PDF Download Regulations

The program manager will select suitable participants of one of the mentioned qualification programs, who can then submit an internal application towards the end of the program.

Scholarships for our Medical Programs

Scholarship for Employees of RWTH Aachen University or RWTH University Clinic

This scholarship supports employees of RWTH Aachen University or of the University Clinic of RWTH Aachen University. It grants admitted students a reduction of 100% of the tuition fees charged by RWTH International Academy.

Please submit a proof of membership and a meaningful personal statement. First, you need to apply for your chosen master program via RWTHonline. Afterwards, you can apply via our website of M.Sc. Applied Health Informatics & Digital Medicine or M.Sc. Periodontology.

Scholarship of the RWTH International Academy

This scholarship was created to support applicants with promising prior experience in a relevant field to take part in our “M.Sc. Applied Health Informatics & Digital Medicine” or "M.Sc. Periodontology". The achievements in the first degree as well as type and content of relevant professional experience are decisive. This scholarship grants admitted students a reduction of 50% of the tuition fees charged by RWTH International Academy.

Please submit a proof of experience and a meaningful personal statement. First, you need to apply for your chosen master program via RWTHonline. Afterwards, you can apply via our website of M.Sc. Applied Health Informatics & Digital Medicine or M.Sc. Periodontology.

Scholarships for Enrolled Students

In this section we present you our Scholarships for our enrolled students at RWTH International Academy.

German Language Merit Scholarship

RWTH International Academy actively encourages student‘s efforts and dedication towards learning German.When planning a career in Germany, proficiency of the German language is one of the skills to put you ahead other applicants when applying for jobs. This merit–based scholarship was created to reward qualifying RWTH International Academy students who pursue a higher German proficiency through extracurricular German courses offered by the RWTH Language Center.

The GLMS application period runs from 15 April to 30 April and from 15 October to 30 October each year.

Next application period October 2026
  • PDF Download Regulations

Social Responsibility Award

The Social Responsibility Award was established to honor deserving RWTH International Academy students who demonstrate their awareness and responsibility for our society and environment by regularly contributing to communal or creative commitments.

The application period for the Social Responsibility Award opens 15 January and closes 15 February each year.

Next application period January 2027
  • PDF Download Regulations

Outstanding Academic Performance Award

Formerly known as the Best of Class Award, this recognition is intended to honor talented students with outstanding academic achievements – recognizing students of our engineering and humanities programs alike.

The Outstanding Academic Performance application period runs from 01 June to 30 June for engineering students and from 01 December to 30 December for students enrolled in our MPA program each year.
