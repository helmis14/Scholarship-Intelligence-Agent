    International Student Scholarships

    These scholarships are available to international and EU students.

    About the scholarships

    Unlock your potential with our International Student Scholarships.

    International scholarships can provide students with some financial assistance to support diversity, whilst recognising academic excellence. They celebrate achievement, promote global education, and create opportunities for students to thrive in their choice of study.

    UWE Bristol offer up to £4,000 (first year only) for students joining us on undergraduate courses and postgraduate taught courses.

    Eligibility

    To be eligible you must be an international fee payer.

    A number of different areas will be considered when offering scholarships including but not exclusive to:

    • course choice
    • personal statement
    • academic references
    • academic performance
    • interviews
    • skills and experience
    • domicile.

    These scholarships are awarded entirely at the University's discretion.

    How to apply

    No application is required for an International Student Scholarship. Successful candidates will be contacted and offered an award once an offer is made.

    Note for successful candidates: you will need to prove when accepting the scholarship that you satisfy the UKVI criteria in terms of being able to cover living costs and the remainder of the fee.

    Read our full scholarship terms and conditions.

    For more information please email international@uwe.ac.uk.
