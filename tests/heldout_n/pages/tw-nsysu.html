(Deadline 2023/2/9)111-2博士學位生獎助學金申請Application for International Elite Doctoral Student Scholarship

請欲申請本校111-2學期博士學位生獎助學金之同學，請務必先上網填寫資料

(https://reurl.cc/E7qpZa)，並於112年2月9日(四)前將下列表件送至系辦公室，謝謝！

(一)申請表。

(二)中文歷年成績單1份【於教務處申請正本，新生第一學期免附】。

(三)學生職涯發展與學術研究規劃(外國學生免填)

(四)研究計畫書1份。

(五)優異表現證明(如發表論文、得獎紀錄、課外活動等)及其他有助審查之文件【無則免附】。

 

For the International Elite Doctoral Student Entrance Scholarship provided by the Office of Academic Affairs, NSYSU, please read the regulations carefully and make sure you are qualified for applying this scholarship again. If you would like to apply for this scholarship, please fill in an online form (https://reurl.cc/E7qpZa). Then, prepare all the required documents on the application form and submit to Physics department office directly before Feb. 9th, 2023. It’s very important to submit them on time, otherwise you will not be the candidate of Elite Doctoral Student Scholarship. Thank you very much!

i. Application form【has been attached in this email】

ii. One copy of complete official transcript in Chinese 【Please apply for transcripts at the Office of Academic Affairs, transcript not required for entering students in their first semester.】

iii. One copy of research plan

iv. Supporting documents (published papers, awards, extracurricular activities, etc.) that may aid the review process 【not mandatory】
