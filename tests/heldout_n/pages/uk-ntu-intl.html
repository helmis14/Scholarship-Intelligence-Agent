NTU International Scholarships

Our scholarships range from £2,000 up to 50% off your tuition fees. They are applied as tuition fee reductions once you enrol on your course at NTU.

These are awarded to Bachelors and Masters students who can demonstrate excellent academic achievement and the potential to contribute to the University community.

Last year we awarded over 1,400 scholarships to students from more than 80 countries.

Scholarships for January 2027

These scholarships are open to offer holders starting at NTU in January 2027.

Scholarships for September 2027 entry will be published on this page a little later in the year.  Please use this list of January scholarships as a guide but note that they may be subject to change.

Our NTU Excellence Scholarships are competitive and will be awarded based on the strength of your application.

You could get 50% or 25% off your first-year fee. Undergraduate students will also get £3,000 off each subsequent year of your course.

  • 50% scholarship – Depending on the fee for your course you would get between £9,150 and £12,500 off your first-year fee. A significant saving meaning you will only pay between £9,150 and £12,500 for your course.
  • 25% scholarship – Depending on the fee for your course you would get between £4,525 and £6,250 off your first-year fee. This means you will only pay between £13,725 and £18,750 for your course.

How to apply

Scholarship applications for January 2027 are open.

To apply, you must have an offer to study at NTU.

You only need to submit one application form and you will be considered for all the scholarships and awards you are eligible for.

Our deadlines for January 2027 are:

  • Wednesday 30 September 2026
  • Final deadline: Saturday 31 October 2026, 16:00 (GMT)

Please note, if you are already studying at NTU, you are not eligible to apply for a scholarship.

Scholarship Application Form

To be eligible for an NTU International Scholarship award, you must:

  1. Have an offer of a place on an eligible course at Nottingham Trent University.
  2. Be classed as an overseas student for tuition fee purposes (‘international student’). Please visit ntu.ac.uk/scholarships for information on scholarships for UK students.
  3. Be due to enrol on the first year of a new course of study (or year 2 or 3 of an undergraduate course in the case of advanced entry) in January 2027 or September 2027. International students enrolled before September 2027 and due to progress onto the second, third or fourth year of their course of study in September 2027, or repeating the first year of study, are not eligible to apply.

Terms and Conditions for International Scholarships

  1. Nottingham Trent International College (NTIC) progression students are not eligible to apply for an NTU International Scholarship award.
  2. International students who receive sponsorship (your tuition fees are being paid by an employer, government, or funding body in full or in part) are not eligible to apply for an International Scholarship.
  3. If you are studying at an institution that has a partnership agreement with NTU you are eligible for either an NTU International Scholarship award (if your scholarship application is successful) or the Partner Progression Discount (the higher amount would be deducted from your tuition fees).
  4. If you are awarded a Scholarship by an external body, you can only be awarded an NTU International Scholarship award if the total combined amount of both awards is not higher than 50% of your first year’s tuition fees. Please check the Terms and Conditions of the external award to make sure any award from NTU would not impact your external award.
  5. Applicants may only apply for an NTU International Scholarship award once per academic year per offer of a place on a qualifying course.
  6. Applications received after 16:00 GMT (4 pm) on each deadline will automatically be considered for the next available deadline. For example, application forms submitted after the first deadline and before the second deadline, will be considered for the second deadline.
  7. Applications received after the final deadline will not be considered.
  8. This application form is for eligible undergraduate and postgraduate taught courses only. Please do not use this form to apply for research funding.
  9. If you are awarded any other scholarship, bursary and / or tuition fee reduction from NTU you will only be eligible for the higher award (except for candidates who are awarded a Sport Scholarship or if you are eligible for the Alumni discount and the combined awards are not higher than 50% of your first year’s tuition fees).
  10. If you receive an award for each year of study, the award will be deducted from your tuition fee at the start of each year. You must successfully complete each year of the course to continue to receive this. Scholarships are not applied when a placement year is undertaken but will resume for your final year of study.
  11. NTU Scholarship awards can be transferred between courses within the same academic School and academic year. In the case of a 50% Excellence award your scholarship would be reviewed by the course team for your new course. Scholarships cannot be transferred to a different academic School or academic year.
  12. Applicants will be informed whether they have (or have not) been awarded an NTU International Scholarship award (and of the value of the scholarship award if they have been awarded) within five weeks of the application deadline.
  13. The scholarship will be applied to your tuition fee when you accept your scholarship offer, accept and meet the conditions of your course offer and you are eligible to enrol on your course.
  14. Scholarships are competitive and selection is based on the information provided on the scholarship application form. It is particularly important that you give your scholarship statement careful thought and preparation, and that you refer to the criteria provided.
  15. If applying for the High Achievers Award, you must be able to provide a graded transcript in order to evidence that you meet the criteria of the High Achievers award.
  16. If your scholarship application is successful and you enrol at NTU, we may ask you to become a student ambassador, or to appear in online and / or printed marketing materials for NTU. For example, providing a student profile and a photograph which may be used to promote NTU and NTU’s scholarship schemes and courses. Your name and course may be printed in any Scholarships Awards event materials. If you do not wish us to use your data in this manner, please notify us by email at international@ntu.ac.uk.
  17. If you fail to complete your course and choose to retake the course or undertake another eligible course, any scholarship you were originally awarded will not be applicable.
  18. If you fail to complete your course and the scholarship was not applied (i.e., you did not pay the tuition fees, or the tuition fees were reimbursed) and you choose to retake the course or undertake another eligible course you may re-apply for a scholarship provided you still meet the eligibility criteria. Consideration of any new scholarship application will be in accordance with the stated criteria and there is no guarantee that the application will be successful.
  19. If you withdraw from your course while owing a proportion of your tuition fees to NTU, application of the scholarship shall be at the discretion of NTU, and the scholarship may be withdrawn.
  20. Eligible postgraduate Masters courses are Master level awards (full-time and part-time only).  Excluded courses: LLM BTC, LLM LPC (full-time, part-time, distance learning), LLM (distance learning), MSc Human Resource Management (part-time), MA Social Work and MA Advanced Social Work Practice (if you have access to NHS funding), MSc Biomedical Science (flexible learning), SQE courses, all PGCE courses. See our scholarships for Professional Law courses. If any queries please contact ntu.scholarships@ntu.ac.uk to confirm whether your course is eligible for the scheme. NTU reserve the right to exclude other courses at their discretion.
  21. NTU may close International Scholarship award applications to individual courses at any time.
  22. We cannot accept scholarship applications including AI generated content. The application form must be completed by individuals using your own words.
  23. NTU’s decision in all matters relating to the scholarship is final.

Scholarship Application Advice

Our scholarships are competitive so it is important to make yourself stand out from the crowd. Here are some of our top tips for completing your application:

  • Spend good time on the application – you only have 500 words so make sure you use them wisely.
  • Express your interests and your achievements - we want to know all about you and your goals for the future. We aren't looking for perfect grades, we just want to hear your authentic voice.
  • Believe in yourself – you are good enough to apply so why not give it a go.

You may be tempted to use AI tools for your scholarship essay. Our advice:

  • Do - Use AI tools to help you check spelling or to edit and refine your essay.
  • Don't - Use AI tools to create your essay. We want to hear your authentic voice and this is an important part of the marking criteria.

If you are applying for a merit-based award only, you don't need to complete the full application. Just fill in your personal details and tick the box stating you wish to be considered for the Merit-based Awards.
