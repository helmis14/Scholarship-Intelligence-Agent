Scholarship & Financial Aid

Shanghai Jiaotong University(SJTU) provides different kinds of scholarship for excellent international applicants, such as Chinese Government Scholarship, Shanghai Government Scholarship, SJTU Scholarship, etc. For international graduate students, SJTU Global College(GC) added the KLA Scholarship on the basis of SJTU Scholarships. Recipients of renewable scholarship awards must meet renewal requirements each year.
