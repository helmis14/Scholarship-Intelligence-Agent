    INDIVIDUAL PHD GRANTS

    Overview
    • Career Stage(s):
      PhD Candidate (with Masters)
    • Funding Type(s):
      PhD Grant
    • Deadline: 11-03-2026 14:00 CET
    • Call launch: No further calls will be launched.
    Home Funding Instruments AFR

    This programme is discontinued, the information on this page is only for ongoing projects.

    All currently running beneficiaries of an AFR grant, including those funded under the 2026 call, continue under the AFR terms and conditions that were in place at the time of their award for the full duration of their grant. In addition, specific continuity rules apply for some beneficiaries: PhD students who were already receiving State financial aid for higher education before the current law entered into force will continue to receive this support under the same conditions as before. For full details, please refer to the relevant legal provisions.

    Find out more about “Aides à la formation doctorale (AFdoc)”
