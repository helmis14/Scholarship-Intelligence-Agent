Scholarships

Find out more about our scholarships below.

  • Global Citizens Scholarship

    Scholarships for all international students worth up to £18,000 plus free accommodation for at least one year.

    Find out more

  • International Accommodation Award

    Option of free accommodation for one academic year.

    Find out more

  • Aber Grad Global Scholarship

    Rewarding Our Graduates with £2,000 Tuition Fee Discounts for Postgraduate Study

    Find out more

  • International Family Award

    £1,000 per year tuition fee reduction for friends and family of Aber alumni.

    Find out more

  • Caru Aber International Award

    £1,000 Tuition Fee Reduction for Returning Exchange, Study Abroad, Summer School Students, and Alumni of the University who graduated more than one year ago.

    Find out more

  • Music Scholarships

    £650 per year for musical excellence, open to applicants for any course

    Find out more

  • The Keates-Lee Educational Trust Bursary

    £4,000 tuition fee reduction for undergraduate students from African nations or refugees domiciled in the UK.

    Find out more

  • David Game College Scholarship

    £2,500 tuition fee reduction for the International Foundation Programme's Computer Science & Technology pathway.

    Find out more
