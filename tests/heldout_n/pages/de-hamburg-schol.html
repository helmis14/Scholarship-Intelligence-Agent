Merit scholarships for international students enrolled at Universität Hamburg (currently no further calls for applications)

With its merit scholarship program, Universität Hamburg supports outstanding international students in all subjects and at all degree levels. The awarding of a merit scholarship allows recipients to concentrate fully on their studies and gives them the opportunity to develop their skills.

You meet the application requirements if you

  • have been enrolled in your subject at Universität Hamburg for at least 2 completed semesters and are pursuing a degree at the time of application. Students in 2-year master’s degree programs can apply after 1 completed semester. Doctoral researchers are unfortunately excluded from applying;
  • do not hold German citizenship;
  • are not eligible for the federal student loan scheme (International students fulfilling the requirements of Section 8 of the German federal training assistance act (Bundesgesetz über individuelle Förderung der Ausbildung, BAföG) cannot be funded)

Funding amount and funding duration

The maximum monthly funding sum is €992.

Merit scholarships are awarded for 2 semesters (total of 12 months). You can reapply for a merit scholarship. The maximum funding duration is 2 years.

Application deadlines

No further calls for applications are currently planned for the merit scholarship program.

Procedure of Merit Scholarships

Application

Follow the link for your chosen application period and log in using your STiNE ID:

Application form (deadline: 15 April)

After following the link for your chosen application period and logging in using your STiNE ID, you will be invited to register and to complete your application. Register with your student email address and enter your student email address, not your private email address, in your online application.

Please note that only complete applications submitted by the application deadline will be considered. Missing documents will not be requested and incomplete applications will be rejected. Therefore, we strongly recommend that you compile your application documents early and upload them to the application portal in a timely manner.

You must upload the following documents to the online application portal:

  • CV
  • letter of motivation: the template form (PDF) must be used
  • record of achievements:
    • Students in a Bachelor's degree program and students in a degree program concluding with a state examination ("Staatsexamen") need to submit either the STiNE printout of their "Leistungskonto" or a current transcript of records.
    • Students in a Master's degree program  need to submit their most recent degree certificate with grades in either English or German and additionally either the STiNE printout of their "Leistungskonto" or a current transcript of records. 
  • scan of your residence permit or, in the case of EU citizens, your ID and the registration certificate of residence ("Meldebescheinigung") in Germany
  • evaluations from 2 full-time teaching staff members with doctorates at the University of Hamburg. Please note the information "Who can issue an evaluation?" in the FAQ. The template evaluation form (PDF) must be used.
  • proof of subject-related additional achievements and academic awards (if applicable)

Only applications completed in full and submitted by the application deadline can be considered. Therefore, begin compiling your application documents early and have these ready to upload to the application portal.

Selection procedure

Merit scholarships are awarded by a committee comprising members of faculties and ASTA (student council).

Once the selection procedure has been completed, you will receive a notification by e-mail.

Selection criteria

  • good to excellent academic performance
  • additional subject-related achievements and academic awards (e.g., publications, prizes)

Disbursement of scholarship

Merit scholarships are disbursed on the first day of each month of funding. Funding commences on 1 October for the application round ending on 15 April.

Scholarships cannot be paid into a foreign bank account. Academic leave is not permitted during the period of funding either.

FAQs

Can I apply if I have both German and foreign citizenship?

No. You cannot, as you are eligible for BAföG in this case

Who can issue an evaluation?

  • full-time professors or other university teachers holding a doctoral degree at University of Hamburg.
  • professors emeriti of the University of Hamburg who are active in teaching
  • acting professors (Vertretungsprofessorinnen und Vertretungsprofessoren) at the University of Hamburg
  • senior physicians at the University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf (for students in a medical degree program)

How can I obtain an evaluation?

Reach out to lecturers from your department. Ideally, they should know you well—you attended one of their seminars or wrote a term paper for them, for example. Contact your evaluators at least 6 weeks before you apply for funding to ensure the evaluations are prepared in time. Give them the evaluation template to use.

Will my application be accepted if I only submit 1 evaluation?

No. Your application is only complete when it includes 2 evaluations. 

Is it allowed to work part-time while receiving  a merit scholarship?

During the period in which you receive the scholarship, you may earn a maximum of 603 euros per month additionally.

As a scholarship holder, am I allowed to spend a semester abroad or time outside of Hamburg during the lecture period?

Unfortunately, it is not possible to receive funding through the merit scholarship program during a semester abroad. Please reach out to us if you are planning a longer leave during the lecture period.

Are merit scholarship holders able to apply for further funding?

No, additional funding from other sources is not permitted.

How can I upload my application documents?

You first need to fill out and submit the application form. Afterwards, you will receive an email with your login information for the Mobility-Online portal, where  you can upload your application documents.

Can the funding be extended?

You can reapply after receiving a one-year scholarship. To do so, you must resubmit all updated documents online by the relevant deadline (see ‘Application’ section for more information). Your documents will not be kept. The maximum funding period is 2 years.

How can I submit my application?

You only need to ensure that you have uploaded all required application documents correctly in the application portal.

What are my chances of receiving a merit scholarship?

Unfortunately, funding for the merit scholarships program is limited. Therefore, it may happen that even very good applications cannot be considered in case of a high number of applications.
For your orientation, here are the funding rates from the last three application rounds:

  • April 2025: 11%
  • October 2024: 18%
  • April 2024: 28% 

Can doctoral researchers apply for a merit scholarship?

No, doctoral researchers cannot apply for a merit scholarship. If you are a doctoral researcher, you can find information about scholarships and other funding options on the website of the Hamburg Research Academy.

I have not been awarded  a merit scholarship, but I need financial support for my studies. Who can I contact?

The Campus Center provides a detailed overview of options for financing your studies.

The Counselling Centre for Study Financing advises students on funding their studies—for example, by working part-time or applying for financial aid through the federal student loan scheme.

The Counselling Centre for Social and International Affairs can answer questions about part-time jobs and health insurance. 

Is there an age limit?

No. As of the Winter Semester 2015/16, there are no age restrictions.
