Scholarships for prospective master's students

Find out about scholarship opportunities provided by Karolinska Institutet, the Swedish Institute and other scholarship providers.

Financial circumstances play an important part in planning for your studies at Karolinska Institutet (KI). Investing time and effort in researching and applying for scholarships and grants should therefor be part of your plan early on.

Scholarships provided by KI

The KI Global Master's Scholarship for fee-paying students

Karolinska Institutet offers around 10 scholarships per year to excellent students who have been admitted to one of our global master’s programmes. The scholarship is distributed across all the global master’s programmes. The competition is high with approximately 700 international students applying for the scholarship annually.

Apply for the KI Global Master's Scholarship

KI-DIS Fellowship

The KI-DIS Fellowship is a co-funded, merit-based scholarship covering the tuition fees to any of KI’s global master’s programmes. The scholarship is available annually to a DIS alumnus who studied in Stockholm or Copenhagen. DIS is a study abroad foundation targeted at undergraduate students from North American colleges and universities.

Apply for the KI-DIS Fellowship

Scholarships for bachelor's and non-fee paying students

KI does not offer any scholarships for studies on bachelor’s level or for EU-citizens/non-fee paying students. 

Scholarships provided by the Swedish Institute

SI Scholarship for Global Professionals

The Swedish Institute (SI) offers several fully funded scholarships for fee-paying master's students. The scholarship covers the tuition fee, a monthly allowance and a travel grant. In 2025, Karolinska Institutet received 18 scholarship awardees through the SI Global Professionals programme. 

For the 2026 intake, all KI global master's programmes are eligible for this scholarship except Erasmus Mundus Joint Master's Degree in Public Health in Disasters.

SI Scholarship for Global Professionals

SI Scholarship Pioneering Women in STEM

The Swedish Institute (SI) offers the Pioneering Women in STEM scholarship, a fully funded opportunity for fee-paying master's students. The scholarship covers the tuition fee, a monthly allowance and a travel grant. In 2025, Karolinska Institutet received 2 scholarship awardees. 

For the 2026 intake, five master’s programmes at Karolinska Institutet are eligible:

  • Biomedicine
  • Health Informatics
  • Nutrition Science
  • Toxicology
  • Translational Physiology and Pharmacology

SI Scholarship Pioneering Women in STEM

External scholarships

There are several external scholarship opportunities that could be available for Karolinska Institutet's global master's programmes. Students from the listed nationalities are encouraged to explore these scholarships further to determine their eligibility.

COLFUTURO 

Each year, a number of admitted students independently choose to apply for COLFUTURO funding. If you need an extended tuition fee deadline or a letter of acceptance for this purpose, contact tuitionfees@ki.se. 

LPDP (Lembaga Pengelolaan Dana Pendidikan)

Each year, a number of admitted students independently choose to apply for the LPDP scholarship. If you receive the scholarship, send your Letter of Guarantee to tuitionfees@ki.se for further assistance. 

Bolashak International Scholarship

Each year, a number of admitted students independently choose to apply for the Bolashak International Scholarship. If you need an extended tuition fee deadline or a letter of acceptance for this purpose, contact tuitionfees@ki.se. 

SECIHTI

Each year, a number of admitted students independently choose to apply for SECIHITI funding. If you need an extended tuition fee deadline or a letter of acceptance for this purpose, contact tuitionfees@ki.se. 

 

Other scholarship opportunities

There are many Swedish, European and international foundations that award scholarships and grants. They can have a significant impact on the actual financial cost of your education. Study in Sweden lists various types of scholarships, including Swedish Institute scholarships, university scholarships, and other scholarships. Use the filters to narrow down the options based on your eligibility and preferences.

Student loans

Karolinska Institutet has been granted 'Deferment Only' status by the US Department of Education. This status means that US students may defer making payments on existing federal student loans while enrolled at Karolinska Institutet.

You submit an In-School Deferment request form to Karolinska Institutet. After our certification, you submit the form to your servicer.

For more information, please contact tuitionfees@ki.se

 

Alberta Student Aid

Karolinska Institutet is designated by Alberta Student Aid. To learn more about the requirements and the application process, please see Apply | Alberta Student Aid.

Ontario Student Assistance Program (OSAP).

Karolinska is approved for the following cycles in the Ontario Student Assistance Program (OSAP).

  • 2024-25 OSAP
  • 2025-26 OSAP

To learn more about the requirements and the application process, please see How to apply for OSAP.

Student Aid British Colombia 

Karolinska Institutet is a designated institution for British Columbia student loans. 

You apply online through StudentAid BC Dashboard. Before your student loan can be released, Karolinska Institutet must confirm that you are enrolled in the confirmation of enrolment form.

For more information about Canadian student aid, please contact tuitionfees@ki.se
