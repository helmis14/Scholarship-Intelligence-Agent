    BAföG

    The BAföG (Federal Training Education Act regulating student loans) is intended to support young people in financing their studies. Students are eligible for BAföG if their own financial means and those of their parents or spouses/partners are insufficient for this purpose.

    Support Foundations for Gifted Students

    Scholarships and financial aid are not only available to highly gifted students. Support from various funding programs is also available forother groups such as STEM talents, first-time students or students with children.

    Read more Support Foundations for Gifted Students

    Further information

    The German National Association for Student Affairs (DSW)  provides information on the various sources of funding: parental financial support, BAföG, student loans and jobs.

    In addition, there are the many foundations and organisations for promoting talented students, which apart from providing financial assistance, concern themselves primarily with offering non-material and personal support.

    In the current semester, 300 top-performing and particularly dedicated students are being supported through the Deutschlandstipendium programme.

    The International Office advises on various funding options and awards scholarships to TUD students who spend a semester abroad as part of programs such as ERASMUS and PROMOS.

    Further funding measures that are subject-specific or specific to certain occasions are also available.
