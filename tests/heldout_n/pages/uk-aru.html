International scholarships

Get help with the cost of study. Find out more about our generous scholarships and how to apply for them.

How it works

  • Apply

    Apply to study a course at ARU. You do not usually need to apply for scholarships (but check individual scholarship pages for details).

  • Review

    We review your application and check if you are eligible for any ARU scholarships.

  • Confirm

    If you are eligible, we will offer you the highest value scholarship. You cannot usually combine scholarships.

  • Discount

    You may also be able to get a Full Payment Discount of £1,000.

Other sources of funding

The UK Council for International Student Affairs (UKCISA) has information about funding for international students looking to study in the UK.

Postgrad Solutions offers 15 bursaries across a number of subject areas. Apply now.

The Global Study Awards encourage young people to study abroad, and explore new countries, cultures and languages. You can get up to £10,000 to expand your horizons. Maximum of nine awards available.

Chevening is the UK Government’s international awards scheme aimed at developing global leaders. It offers Chevening Scholarships and Chevening Fellowships. Recipients are selected by British embassies and high commissions around the world. To apply, visit the Chevening website.
