Visiting Student Program

As one of the leading universities in Korea, Hanyang welcomes all international students who want to explore and experience Korean culture while pursuing their academic growth with our world renowned faculties.

International Summer School

HISS is one of Korea’s leading summer programs. Every year, around 2000 students spend 4 weeks at one of Korea’s leading private universities. HISS offers world-class education combined with a variety of unique cultural experiences.
