Scholarships for international students

Find out more about the funding options available to you as an international student at Plymouth
To reward outstanding achievement and to help ease any concerns you may have about funding your studies at the University of Plymouth, we are pleased to offer a number of international scholarships.
Before you apply please read our policy and procedure guidance. It may save you time and it will ensure that you are informed of our processes.

Undergraduate international scholarships
Postgraduate international scholarships
Please note: 
  • Students who are eligible for multiple scholarships, awards or discounts by the University of Plymouth will only qualify for the highest amount awarded. Where a student may qualify for the alumni discount and a scholarship then the highest award only will be applied. Scholarships are awarded to eligible students who commence their first year of study at the University of Plymouth, regardless of level/stage. Foundation Year students may also be eligible for scholarships.
  • Scholarships are not available for courses within the Peninsula Medical School and Peninsula Dental School.
  • Scholarships are not available for online courses (distance learning).
