Overview of the MEXT Scholarship Program

The Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship is a program that provides outstanding international students with opportunities to study at Japanese universities and other educational institutions.

The scholarship covers application fee, admission fee, and tuition fee, a monthly Stipend, and travel expenses to and from Japan.

 

The MEXT Scholarship offers two selection pathways:

University Recommendation, in which applicants are nominated by a Japanese university,

Embassy Recommendation, in which applicants are selected through a Japanese embassy or consulate abroad.

University Recommendation *October entrance only

The University Recommendation system allows universities to apply to MEXT for scholarships on behalf of outstanding international students who are newly arriving in Japan from countries that maintain diplomatic relations with the Japanese government. There are two main types of university recommendation scheme: General Selection and Special Selection.

*University Recommendation is for October enrollment only.

Embassy Recommendation

The Embassy Recommendation is a primary pathway for the MEXT Scholarship, where Japanese diplomatic missions abroad handle the recruitment and initial screening.

It is widely open to international students from countries with diplomatic relations with Japan, covering various levels from undergraduate to research (graduate) studies.

Undergraduate Level

Embassy recommendation selections for undergraduate level are conducted by the Japanese diplomatic missions in each country and MEXT. For details, please contact a embassy or consulate in your country. 

 

NOTE：Deadlines vary by embassy, typically around April.

A List of Japanese Diplomatic Missions (external link) 

Graduate Level

■Intensive Japanese Language Program ※only to graduate-level students

 

MEXT students have two options for studying Japanese upon arriving in Japan. First, students may participate in a six-month Intensive Japanese Language Program as research students, prior to beginning their research activities in their respective labs. During this period, students are required to focus exclusively on learning Japanese; if they do not pass the program, they will not be admitted to the graduate school.

The other option is to enroll as degree course students or research students and engage in laboratory activities while taking some Japanese language courses. After obtaining permission for a provisional acceptance letter, students can consult with their prospective supervisor whether the six-month Japanese program is necessary for them.

Application Types

- Reservation Program (EJU)

*This program is for privately financed international students who have achieved excellent results on the Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students (EJU) and plan to enroll as a regular student in a Japanese university undergraduate school.

- Reservation Program by Pre-arrival Admission

*This program is intended for privately financed international students seeking admission to a program that conducts the selection process and grants admission without requiring the applicant to visit Japan prior to enrollment. Whether or not this system is carried out depends on the graduate school. Please refer to the school list on the website to check which programs offer this option.

University Scholarship and Other Financial Aid

University Scholarship

 

‣　Financial Support for For undergraduate, master's programs, professional degree students

Tohoku University will establish a new scholarship program, the Tohoku University International Excellence Scholarship, beginning in the 2027 academic year.

This scholarship program is designed to provide focused financial support to outstanding international students enrolled in undergraduate programs, master’s programs, and professional degree programs. Recipients will be selected based on entrance examination results or academic performance after enrollment, together with other relevant criteria. Approximately the top one-third of eligible students in each program are expected to receive support.

Please visit here for further details.

 

‣　Financial Support for PhD Students
Tohoku University newly established LEAP Program to improve PhD students’ research environment and to dispel the anxieties about the economic conditions, by offering the monthly stipend and research grant. Please visit here for further details.

Fee Waivers

Students enrolled in degree programs with outstanding academic records may be eligible to apply for exemption from the admission fee and tuition. Please note that not all applicants will qualify for these exemptions.

* Non-degree students are not eligible for the waivers.

Please visit here for further details.

Scholarships Provided by Private Foundations in Japan

Some private foundations or other organization in Japan offer scholarships for international students. These scholarships vary with regards to recruitment procedures, eligible fields of study and amount. Some may be granted before admission, but in many cases, application shall be made through Tohoku University after admission.
