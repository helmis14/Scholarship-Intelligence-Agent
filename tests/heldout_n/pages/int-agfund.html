Investing in people

To create sustainable development

The Arab Gulf Programme for Development (AGFUND) was established in 1980 upon the initiative of His Royal Highness Prince Talal bin Abdulaziz Al Saud, may Allah have mercy on him, with the support of the leaders of the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) States. Through its strategy, AGFUND addresses the root causes of human development challenges, targeting all segments of society without discrimination.

What we do

Focus Areas

AGFUND believes in the sustainability of human development and poverty alleviation, working to enable vulnerable groups — women, children, people with special needs, displaced people and refugees — to exercise their rights and lead a dignified life. Our efforts concentrate in five areas.

Early Childhood Development

The Arab Council for Childhood and Development was founded in 1987 in a strategic partnership with UNESCO, advancing childhood and development issues across the Arab world.

Civil Society Development

The initiative was launched in 1989, followed by the formal establishment of the Arab NGO Network for Development (ANND) in Beirut in 2002 during a conference chaired by HRH Prince Talal bin Abdulaziz Al Saud, under the patronage of President Rafik Hariri — may Allah have mercy on them both.

Launch of the Arab Forum for Land and Climate and the Arab Climate Adaptation Lab.

Establishment of the largest Arab library featuring 56 specialized publications on civil society and development.

Open Education

The Arab Open University was established in 2002 as a non-profit educational institution at the initiative of Prince Talal bin Abdulaziz Al Saud — may Allah have mercy on him — in partnership with The Open University in the United Kingdom, to provide flexible, high-quality higher education for all across the Arab world.

Financial Inclusion

In 2006, AGFUND’s efforts led to the establishment of the first bank for the poor in the Arab world in Jordan under the name National Microfinance Bank. This was followed by the establishment of AGFUND Financial Inclusion Banks across several countries.
