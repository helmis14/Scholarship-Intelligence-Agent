Choose Glasgow Caledonian

We are Scotland’s largest and leading modern university – a vibrant, values-led community with campuses in the heart of Glasgow and London.

We’re incredibly proud to be recognised as:

  • the UK’s leading modern university
  • ranked in the top 40 universities nationally (Times and Sunday Times Good University Guide 2026)
  • the UK’s University of the Year for Graduate Jobs (Daily Mail University Guide 2026).

As an international student, you can expect an exceptional academic experience, outstanding facilities, exemplary teaching standards, and a career-focussed education. Glasgow Caledonian has 90% of graduates in employment and/or further study, outperforming the Scottish sector average by +2 percent (HESA, 2026).

University for the Common Good

We go beyond the traditional role of a university and aim to make a positive impact in local, national and international communities. We design all our courses and the wider student experience with the Common Good in mind. We’re the only Scottish university with EcoCampus Platinum status and strive to keep higher education accessible and affordable.

Scholarships and funding

If you have Indian or Nigerian nationality and are applying for a full-time postgraduate course, you may be eligible for a tuition fee discount of up to £3,000. There is no need to apply for this scholarship as your eligibility will be assessed on application. The tuition fee scholarship will be applied at enrolment.

GCU Plus: Students with Indian and Nigerian nationality who have a strong academic background and statement of purpose, or who have a high score in one of the GCU-approved English Language tests, may also be eligible for an additional £1,000 scholarship in the form of a tuition fee discount.

These scholarships are available for on-campus study in Glasgow and London only.

Visit the New to GCU Postgraduate Scholarship page.

International students with the following nationalities: Bangladesh, Canada, Ghana, Hong Kong, Indonesia, Kenya, Malaysia, Nepal, Philippines, Singapore, Sri Lanka, Thailand and Vietnam, who study a full-time postgraduate taught course from the academic year 2026-2027 are eligible for a £3,500 scholarship in the form of a tuition fee discount. There is no need to apply for this scholarship separately as your eligibility will be assessed on application. The tuition fee scholarship will be applied at enrolment, should you be successful in your application to your chosen course.

This scholarship is available for on-campus study in Glasgow and London only.

Visit the Regional Postgraduate Scholarship page.

Fee discounts are applicable to self-funded undergraduate international students only.

Undergraduate students receive £2,500 tuition fee discount towards their annual fees each year of their degree. Up to £10,000 for a four-year programme or £12,500 for a five-year course.

*Note that these scholarships are available for on-campus study in Glasgow and London only. Distance learning or part-time and research courses are not eligible for this discount.

This is a full tuition fee scholarship for one-year master's course.

You must have an unconditional offer to study for a master's degree at GCU (Glasgow or London campus)  and be able to detail how you will cover your living costs in line with UKVI visa regulations.
