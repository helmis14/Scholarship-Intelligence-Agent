Humanarium

Humanarium is the latest initiative from RCSI aimed at bridging science, expertise and society. This new public exhibition space and café in 118 St Stephen's Green will offer a rolling programme of free exhibitions and events exploring health sciences and medical research. It’s a place for everyone to join conversations about how to live longer, healthier and happier lives.
