    Postgraduate bursaries and scholarships (International)

    The awards listed on this page are the for 2025/26 and 2026/27 academic year.

    If your course started in January 2025 then it falls in the 2024/25 academic year. Please see our 2024/25 bursaries and scholarships archive for relevant information.

    Please note that these awards are available to international fee paying students only. Home postgraduate funding pages can be found here.

    Terms and Conditions

    Please note that our bursaries and scholarships are subject to Terms and Conditions and each award has its own eligibility criteria. Further information about eligibility can be found using the individual award links above.
