International scholarships and financial support

We offer a range of scholarships and financial support to help our international students with the costs of studying at Oxford Brookes University. You can find out more about some of these schemes below.

Make sure you also visit our funding finder tool to find out what funding support you may be eligible to apply for. 


Search Funding Finder

£2000 International Student Scholarship - January 2027 entry

Are you an international student joining us in January 2027? You could qualify for a £2,000 scholarship.

We know that moving to a new country can be a challenge, and we believe that early preparation is an important factor in student success. In preparation for their new life in the UK, we are offering a £2,000 scholarship to support students who make their deposit payment by a relevant deadline.
