Beinecke Scholarships

Since 1975, more than 750 scholars from more than 120 colleges and universities have pursued advanced study in the arts, humanities, and social sciences with support from the Beinecke Scholarship.

Another 50 conservation leaders have earned graduate degrees and returned home to shape sustainable futures across the African continent through the Beinecke African Scholarship.
