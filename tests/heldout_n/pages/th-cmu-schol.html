SCHOLARSHIPS

Explore various scholarship opportunities designed to support and empower our students across different needs and achievement

Type of Scholarships

  • Scholarship for Financially Challenged Students
  • Scholarship for Academic Excellence
  • Work-Study Scholarship
  • Emergency Scholarship
  • Scholarship for Academic, Extracurricular, and Sports Activities
  • Scholarship for Student Academic Competition

Details of Each Scholarship

1. Scholarship for Financially Challenged Students

Purpose: Support students facing economic hardships.

  • 📅 Duration: Up to 2 regular semesters
  • 💰 Amount: Up to 60,000 Baht/year
  • 🧑‍🎓 Who Can Apply: Students with proven financial need
  • 📋 Conditions: Students must assist with faculty duties (up to 8 monthly installments)

2. Scholarship for Academic Excellence

Purpose: Encourage academic dedication and performance.

  • 💰 Amount: 16,000 Baht/year
  • 📈 Eligibility:
    • Regular students in years 1–3
    • GPA ≥ 3.00
    • Top-ranked in the program
    • No disciplinary record, F, or U grades
  • 📌 Selection: Based on academic year GPA

3. Work-Study Scholarship

Purpose: Offer financial aid through part-time student work.

  • ⚙️ Type of Work: Academic and general administrative tasks
  • 🧑‍🎓 Eligibility: Physically healthy and responsible students
  • 💰 Amount & Duration: Case-by-case basis
  • 📋 Conditions: Tasks must not affect academic performance

4. Emergency Scholarship

Purpose: Assist students during unexpected financial crises.

  • ⚠️ Eligible Situations: Accidents, family loss, natural disasters, etc.
  • 💰 Amount: Determined case-by-case
  • 🧑‍🎓 Who Can Apply: Students facing sudden, serious financial challenges

5. Scholarship for Academic, Extracurricular, and Sports Activities

Purpose: Promote student participation in activities representing the faculty.

  • 🎯 Eligibility: GPA ≥ 2.00 and committed participation
  • 📝 Requirement: Submit a proposal and participate in approved events
  • 💰 Amount: Case-by-case

6. Scholarship for Student Academic Competition

Purpose: Encourage participation in academic competitions.

  • 🧠 Scope: Local, national, or international academic competitions
  • 📝 Requirement: Submit work and represent the faculty officially
  • 🧑‍🎓 Who Can Apply: Students with a strong commitment to academic contests

How to Apply

  • Visit the CM-IES Office or check the นักศึกษาทุน วิศวะ มช Facebook page for announcements.
