Fulbright Postgraduate Scholarships to the UK

As part of the Fulbright US Student programme, our Postgraduate Student Awards provide the only scholarships available for academic work in any graduate programme at any recognised UK university.

Applications for US Postgraduate Awards are closed for 2027-28. 

We offer a variety of awards in a range of disciplines in partnership with universities across the United Kingdom. Additionally, we offer an Open Award for postgraduate study or independent research projects in any recognized UK university. 

Sign up to our mailing list for the latest updates on news, opportunities and advice.

Our postgraduate awards generally include: 

  • Tuition waiver
  • A personal living allowance (stipend)
  • Sickness and accident coverage
  • Cultural enrichment programmes and events
  • Membership to national arts and culture organisations
  • Access to a global alumni network of collaborative engagement and cultural diplomacy, with over 300,000 academically and professionally accomplished members

The personal living allowance is intended to help cover the cost of living in the UK as a student and is subject to annual review.

Applications for 2027-28 are now closed.

To stay up to date, join our mailing list.

To be eligible to apply, applicants must:

• Be a US citizen and a non-UK resident
• Hold a Bachelor degree or equivalent professional training/experience in a relevant field before departure to the UK

Dual US-UK citizens are eligible to apply.

The US-UK Fulbright program aims to foster cultural understanding between our two countries. Preference is therefore given to applicants who do not have extensive experience (six months or longer) in the UK.

Applicants with extensive UK experience should explain in detail how further experience in the UK will fulfil the Fulbright mission in ways that their prior visits did not.

Part time and distance learning masters/ research degree courses are ineligible for the Fulbright US Postgraduate awards to the United Kingdom.

The US-UK Fulbright Commission is committed to academic freedom and a rigorous, merit-based selection process. Candidates should, however, note, that final approval for Fulbright grants is made by the Fulbright Foreign Scholarship Board (FFSB), which is appointed by the President of the United States and ensures that selected projects are aligned with Administration priorities, including executive orders issued by the US President. A situation may arise in which, despite a positive nomination by the Fulbright Commission in the UK, a project may not be approved by the FFSB.

What we're looking for

We are looking for applicants who can engage with the UK in an atmosphere of openness, academic integrity and intellectual freedom, thereby promoting mutual understanding.

We assess more than your academic record. The best applications are from well-rounded students who can demonstrate:

  • Ambassadorial skills
  • Intercultural sensitivity 
  • Genuine desire to learn more about the UK and to share aspects of American culture
  • Extracurricular activities
  • Community involvement
  • Leadership potential
  • Plans to further the Fulbright mission and give back to the US upon returning

How to apply

Choosing an award

There are dozens of awards available to graduate and recently graduated students each year. The UK is the most competitive Fulbright programme in the world, so it's important to apply for an award that best fits your experience and interests.

Selection committees and interview panels will be looking for clear reasons for selecting your UK university preference. We expect candidates to conduct a thorough university search and to identify institutions that are a strong fit for their academic, personal and professional goals.

Review the available awards through the main Fulbright US Student programme portal.

You can also search for a Fulbright programme adviser at your institution or alma mater, to discuss which Awards are a good match for you.

Applicants are strongly encouraged read carefully through the eligibility criteria on the United Kingdom section of the IIE website. 

Application instructions

Applications for 2027-28 are now closed. Sign up to our mailing list for the latest updates on news, opportunities and advice.

Fulbright Postgraduate Student Award applications for US citizens are facilitated by the Institute of International Education (IIE).

1. Follow the IIE Fulbright US Student Program guidance, including timelines and components and award-specific instructions.

Slate, the online application, is used by Fulbright programmes around the world, and Awards differ greatly from country to country. 

You must make a separate application to your chosen UK institution, the timeline, and deadlines for which will vary from the Fulbright application.

Application process

Interviews

Shortlisted applicants will be invited to interview during February and early March 2027. Postgraduate Student Award interviews are by video call and cannot be rescheduled.

Notification

All applicants will be informed of their status by late April 2027. The Fulbright Commission cannot comment or provide feedback on individual applications.

Postgraduate Awards to the UK timeline

In general, the Fulbright Postgraduate Student Award competition for US citizens follows the timeline below. Candidates must make their own independent applications to UK institutions.
