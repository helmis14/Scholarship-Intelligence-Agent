## English

**Who we are.** [Operator name], [address], [contact e-mail] ("we"). Contact for privacy requests: [privacy e-mail].
[Data protection officer, if appointed: name and contact.]

**What we collect.**
1. Account: your e-mail address, a protected form of your password (we cannot read it), the time you accepted these
   terms, your choices about AI drafting, and a record of account events such as logins and password changes (without
   your IP address).
2. Applicant profile, only what you enter: name, the phone number asked when you create the account (shown on your
   CV, never shared), citizenships, country of residence, education, grades and tests, work and volunteer experience,
   achievements, languages, study plans and preferences.
3. Your applications: which scholarships you track, their status, your notes, checklists, document drafts and outcomes.
4. Reports you send about mistakes in scholarship information (without your account; we remove e-mail addresses and phone
   numbers from the text).

Please do not enter health information or other sensitive details unless they are needed for an application. If an essay
describes financial or family circumstances, we store it encrypted and use it only to show it back to you.

**Why we use it.** To provide the service you asked for: matching you with scholarships, explaining eligibility, tracking
deadlines and storing your drafts ([legal basis per law, to be set by counsel: contract / consent]). To keep accounts
secure and prevent abuse ([legitimate interest / legal obligation]). We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use
your profile to train AI models.

**AI.** Scholarship information is checked by an AI model reading public web pages; this involves no personal data. If you
turn on AI drafting, the facts you confirmed in your profile are sent to our AI provider [Anthropic, PBC, United States]
to draft documents for you. You can turn this off at any time.

**Who receives it.** Our hosting provider [name, country] and, if you use password reset, our e-mail provider [name].
Each processes the data only for us under a written agreement. [Transfers outside your country and their safeguards, to be
completed by counsel.]

**How long we keep it.** Until you delete your account. Earlier versions of your profile: the last 20. Backups: up to
[N] days after deletion. Login sessions end after 30 days or 7 days without use.

**Security.** Your profile, notes, documents and e-mail address are encrypted at rest (AES-256); passwords are stored as
scrypt hashes; access uses expiring sessions over HTTPS.

**Your rights.** You can see and download all your data (Account → Export), correct your profile, delete your account and
all your data in one step (Account → Delete), withdraw your AI-drafting consent, and contact us or the data protection
authority [name] with a complaint. We answer requests within [time required by law].

**Children.** The service is for people aged 18 or over [or: younger users with a parent's or guardian's consent, as
counsel decides].

**Changes.** We will tell you before we change this notice in a way that affects you.
